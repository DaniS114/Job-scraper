Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
We are looking for an experienced Senior Game Developer to join our online slot game development team. In this role, you will be writing code, architecting high-performance games, helping to shape technical direction of our internal game SDK, and setting the standard for quality and stability. You will act as the main responsible for delivery of specific game titles, taking them from concept to deployment independently, while also contributing to the core libraries that power our entire portfolio.
​
Key Responsibilities
•
End-to-End Ownership:
Be able to independently lead the development of high-quality online slot games using TypeScript, handling everything from state management to complex animations and UI.
•
Architecture & SDK Evolution:
actively contribute to and improve our shared game client SDK.
•
Performance Optimization:
Ensure games run smoothly across all devices (desktop and mobile).
•
Technical Leadership:
Make architectural decisions, select appropriate design patterns, and ensure maintainability and scalability.
​
