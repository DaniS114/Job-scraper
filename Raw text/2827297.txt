Company info
QBCS is an international consulting company specializing in Oracle Retail-based enterprise solutions. The company focuses on transforming retail operations through deployment, improvement, and optimization of Oracle Retail ERP systems. QBCS operates with a multinational background and offers international opportunities, emphasizing professional training and development within a young team environment.
