Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced
ServiceNow Team Leader
to join our growing team – someone who combines strong technical expertise with inspiring leadership and a passion for building high-quality solutions.
This is not just another senior role. It’s an opportunity to shape a team, influence technical direction, and actively contribute to how we move forward as a company.
What will you do?
Lead, mentor, and develop a team of 8–12 ServiceNow developers and consultants
Define and drive the technical vision and quality standards on the ServiceNow platform
Actively contribute to implementation projects (analysis, solution architecture, development, code reviews)
Act as a trusted partner for clients, project managers, and internal stakeholders
Establish and continuously improve best practices, processes, and technical standards
Support the growth of your team members through mentoring, feedback, and knowledge sharing
Be part of the management circle and actively participate in decisions and processes that shape the company’s future
Who are we looking for?
A seasoned ServiceNow professional with deep knowledge of the platform (ideally across multiple modules – ITSM, ITOM, HRSD, CSM, or custom applications)
Strong technical foundation – JavaScript scripting, integrations, and solution architecture
Proven experience leading a team or a natural leadership mindset with the ambition to grow into a key leadership role
Experience in project delivery and direct client communication
Advanced English (B2+/C1) for daily communication
German is a strong advantage
What do we offer?
The opportunity to lead a stable, skilled, and motivated team
A real impact on technical direction, internal processes, and company growth
Involvement in the management circle with space to co-create and influence decisions
Exciting international projects
Flexibility and trust-based culture
Support for continuous learning and ServiceNow certifications
Competitive compensation based on your experience and impact
