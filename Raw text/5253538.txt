Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced Linux System Administrator who wants to work on business-critical infrastructure with a strong focus on automation, stability, and continuous improvement. In this role, you will join an international agile team and help shape secure, high-performing Linux environments that support essential services at scale.
Our client operates in the banking / financial services industry.
Tasks:
- Administration, configuration, and maintenance of Linux servers (RHEL 7–10)
- Automation of processes within Linux environments (Bash, Shell, Python, Salt, Terraform)
- System monitoring, performance tuning, and capacity management
- Patch management and system updates (RHEL Satellite)
- Troubleshooting OS, service, and application issues
- User, group, and permission management
- Backup and recovery operations (TSM)
- Documentation of system configurations and procedures
- Supporting security standards and compliance (CIS, PCI DSS)
- Participation in vulnerability remediation and audit activities
