Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design and implement reliable, scalable system services for edge and physical AI workloads.
Build runtime infrastructure that supports deployment, lifecycle management, and updates of AI-powered components on-device.
Develop abstractions that decouple applications from underlying AI frameworks, models, and hardware accelerators.
Create APIs, SDKs, and developer tooling that streamline how engineers build and integrate AI-powered features.
Improve end-to-end developer workflows, from local development to on-device deployment and updates.
Build observability and diagnostics capabilities to help developers understand performance, failures, and system behavior.
Design software that performs reliably under edge constraints such as limited resources, real-time requirements, and heterogeneous hardware.
Contribute to system-level performance optimization across CPUs and AI accelerators.
Ensure systems are maintainable, testable, and robust in production environments.
Work closely with architects, product managers, and AI engineers to shape platform capabilities and developer-facing abstractions.
Collaborate across teams to design clean APIs, service boundaries, and integration points.
Contribute to reference implementations, documentation, and example workflows.
Requirements
Strong experience designing and building systems software, platform services, or distributed systems in production.
Proficiency in C/C++, Rust, Go, or Python (language choice depends on component).
Solid understanding of Linux-based systems, process isolation, IPC, and service lifecycles.
Experience with API design and versioning.
Experience building developer platforms, SDKs, or internal tooling.
Strong fundamentals in algorithms, data structures, and performance optimization.
Ability to work across multiple layers of the stack, from APIs down to system behavior.
Excellent problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.
Nice-to-have
“Nice to Have” Skills and Experience: Experience with container technologies and modern packaging/deployment approaches., Familiarity with edge computing, embedded Linux, or real-time systems., Exposure to AI/ML inference systems, even from a platform or infrastructure perspective., Experience with heterogeneous computing (CPUs, GPUs, NPUs, DSPs)., Background in robotics, vision systems, or physical AI applications., Contributions to open-source systems or developer tooling projects., Familiarity with security concepts such as secure boot, isolation, or attestation.
