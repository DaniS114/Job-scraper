Responsibilities
At Computacenter, we empower people to make a real impact. As a Finance Analytics Engineer, you’ll drive process improvements and automation within the Finance Reporting & Business Process Development Team. This Team Leader–equivalent role has no direct people‑management duties, but you’ll hold strong technical influence and act as a trusted advisor in key transformation initiatives.
On a daily basis:
​Design and Build: Develop robust Power BI dashboards and data models (using DAX and Power Query) to replace legacy manual reporting.
​Automate Workflows: Eliminate repetitive manual tasks and excel-based processes using the Power Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps), VBA, or Python.
​Drive Innovation: Act as the hub for Low-Code and AI adoption within Finance, helping us deploy intelligent agents and automated workflows.
Translate & Bridge: Partner with Finance stakeholders to translate complex business requirements into precise technical specifications for data engineering.
​Data Stewardship: Act as the guardian of the "Single Source of Truth" for Working Capital and Financial data, ensuring backend integrity and accuracy.
Requirements
You have a Hybrid Profile: A solid background in Finance/Controlling combined with a "Builder" mindset and a passion for technology.
​You have Technical Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Power BI (with DAX) and Excel is a must; knowledge of SQL and Data Modeling are advantages.
You are an Automation Pro: You have tangible experience automating business processes using low-code tools (Power Automate, Power Apps) or scripting (VBA, Python).
You are AI-Ready: You have an interest in Generative AI and how to apply it to financial workflows (e.g., Copilot, Prompt Engineering).
You have Fluent English knowledge (oral and written) to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
You have excellent Problem-Solving Skills: You don't just report errors; you engineer solutions to prevent them from happening again.
