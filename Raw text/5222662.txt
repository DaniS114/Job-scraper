Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
As a growing team, your contributions will have visible impact from the very beginning. Client prioritize speed where it counts, encourage experimentation, and gradually scale their solutions to support ambitious goals. Building AI awareness and capability across the company is an ongoing journey, and you will play an important role in shaping it.
What You Will Do
Translate complex financial problems into effective AI-driven solutions.
Design and implement AI models that improve efficiency and personalization in banking.
Develop conversational AI features and multi-agent workflows for advanced use cases.
Integrate AI capabilities into our digital banking products to ensure reliable operation.
Establish solid technical foundations for scalable and future-ready AI systems.
Ensure AI solutions are secure, compliant, and ethically sound.
Implement APIs and MCP connectors to enable seamless system integration.
Experiment, prototype, and continuously bring new ideas into production.
