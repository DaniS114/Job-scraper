Responsibilities
We are looking for a
Senior UX/UI Engineer
who is passionate about building
beautiful, intuitive, and high-performance user interfaces
for modern digital products. In this role, you will combine
design thinking with frontend engineering
to create seamless experiences across desktop, web, and mobile platforms.
You will work closely with
product managers, designers, and engineers
to transform complex requirements into elegant user experiences while helping define the
next generation of interface architecture and design systems
.
Key Responsibilities
Design and develop
modern, high-quality user interfaces
for web, desktop, and mobile applications.
Create
wireframes, prototypes, and interaction models
, then translate them into production-ready front-end code.
Help build and evolve
scalable design systems
to ensure consistency across products.
Collaborate with
engineering, product, and design teams
to deliver user-centered solutions.
Lead efforts to establish
next-generation front-end architecture
enabling faster UI development.
Ensure consistency in
visual language, UI components, and interactions
across the ecosystem.
Conduct
code and design reviews
and mentor team members on UX and frontend best practices.
Stay current with
emerging UX/UI trends, tools, and technologies
and evaluate new approaches.
Requirements
8+ years of experience
in UX/UI design and frontend engineering (with senior or lead experience preferred).
Strong expertise in
user-centered design, interaction design, wireframing, and prototyping
.
Advanced
frontend development skills
(HTML, CSS, JavaScript and modern frameworks).
Experience building and maintaining
design systems
and scalable UI components.
Strong understanding of
responsive and cross-platform design
.
Ability to communicate design decisions and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree
in Human-Computer Interaction, Design, Computer Science, or similar (or equivalent experience).
A
strong portfolio
demonstrating impactful, user-centered digital products.
