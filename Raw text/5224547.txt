Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the role
You will work as part of a trusted and experienced team to drive innovation across on-prem, private cloud, and public cloud environments, with a strong focus on automation, stability, security, and cost efficiency. The team builds and operates self-service database platforms for Swiss Re business units, enabling reliable and scalable database services at enterprise scale.
The Database as a Service (DBaaS) squad is looking for a Database Engineer who can take technical ownership of database platforms and actively shape their long-term evolution. The role combines engineering, operations, and platform responsibility, working on backlog items within an agile setup to deliver predictable and sustainable outcomes.
Operational on-call coverage is limited (approximately once every two months and decreasing as the team grows) and applies only to high-priority incidents. The team follows a “you build it, you run it” philosophy and actively strives to automate operational activities wherever possible.
In this role, you will
•	Take technical ownership of enterprise and mission-critical database platforms
•	Influence architecture, HA/DR strategies, and operational standards
•	Act as a technical point of contact for complex database topics and escalations
•	Support and guide other engineers through technical challenges
•	Work closely with international and geographically distributed teams
•	Share knowledge, ideas, and best practices across the team
•	Maintain a strong customer orientation and understanding of business requirements
•	Proactively identify risks, issues, and opportunities during backlog refinement
•	Coordinate with your team to support hybrid working arrangements
•	Operate with integrity and strong ethical standards

Personality requirements and skills
Nobody meets 100% of the requirements — and that’s perfectly fine. If you meet the core qualifications below and are genuinely interested in enterprise database platforms, multi-cloud environments, and automation, we would be happy to meet you.
You will join a highly skilled, dedicated, and cross-functional database team located between Bratislava and Zürich, within the Global Technology Services department.
Minimum qualifications
•	Experience designing and operating complex database environments in cloud or private cloud settings, preferably on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure
•	Strong understanding of Oracle databases in RAC environments, preferably on Exadata
•	Good understanding of PostgreSQL database internals
•	Deep understanding of SQL performance tuning and optimization in Oracle and PostgreSQL
•	Experience supporting mission-critical database platforms, including high availability, replication, backup and recovery, and operational readiness
•	Experience with scripting and automation (Shell/Bash, PL/SQL, Python)
•	Experience taking technical ownership of database platforms, including design decisions, operational trade-offs, and long-term sustainability
•	Experience developing automation and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) for database platforms

•	Hands-on attitude with a willingness to continuously learn and improve
•	Proficiency in English, both written and spoken
