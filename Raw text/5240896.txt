Job description, responsibilities and duties
Project & Service Manager is responsible for leading projects from initiation through successful delivery, following PMI project management methodologies. This role owns the full project lifecycle, including planning, execution, monitoring, controlling, and closure, while managing scope, resources, timelines, and stakeholders. In addition to project delivery, this position ensures strong post-delivery relationships and supports service transitions and ongoing operations.
The role may also involve managing specific components or sub-projects within larger programs and contributing to the overall success of complex service deliveries.
Key Responsibilities
Act as the primary point of contact between internal teams, customers, vendors, and partners
Lead end-to-end delivery of projects and services in line with PMI standards
Translate customer requirements into clear project objectives and delivery plans
Proactively manage risks, issues, scope changes, and priorities impacting schedule, cost, or quality
Develop, maintain, and adjust detailed project plans and schedules
Track project progress and ensure delivery against agreed milestones and plans
Maintain high standards of formal project documentation and produce executive-level reports as required
Coordinate and oversee activities such as architecture design, engineering, change control, provisioning, installation, and operations acceptance
Ensure deliverables align with customer requirements and Statements of Work (SoW)
Lead enablement activities and related sub-projects within larger programs
Provide regular project and status reporting to customers and stakeholders
Serve as a point of escalation and drive issues through to resolution in line with SLAs
Support break-fix processes when required and ensure timely issue progression
Deliver both proactive and reactive support to customers
Continuously collaborate with stakeholders to align expectations and project plans
Provide feedback to improve tools, processes, and procedures to enhance service quality
Compile reports and assist with ad-hoc program setup activities
