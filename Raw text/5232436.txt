Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you speak both "Client" and "Data Logic"?
We are a specialized Bloomreach
delivery partner
, pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in e-commerce automation. As a high-end
service company
, we don’t just "set up" platforms; we orchestrate complex data ecosystems to build advanced personalized experiences for global brands.
We are looking for a
Technical Project Manager
—a bridge-builder who understands the logic of system integrations, can navigate a technical roadmap, and keeps international projects running like clockwork.
Your Mission
As our PM, you are the strategic lead for Bloomreach integration projects. You don't need to write the code or configure the APIs yourself, but you must be able to
understand the flow
. You will translate client business requirements into technical reality by guiding our expert team through the integration process.
What you’ll do:
Orchestrate Integrations:
Lead the end-to-end delivery of Bloomreach implementations. You’ll oversee how data sources connect and ensure the technical puzzle pieces fit together perfectly.
Scope & Capacity Mastery:
You are the guardian of the project’s boundaries. You manage client expectations, handle "feature creep" with diplomatic firmness, and ensure our technical team's capacity is planned and utilized efficiently.
Technical Translation:
Act as the primary link between the client’s business goals and our technical execution. You need to understand the
why
and
how
of the integration to clearly explain progress and risks.
International Client Management:
Lead meetings and manage relationships with global e-commerce players. Your fluency in English ensures nothing gets lost in translation.
Strategic Oversight:
Monitor project health, identify bottlenecks before they happen, and keep the "big picture" in focus while the team handles the deep-level configuration.

Brief description of the company
Voxwise is a Slovak company with more than 10 years of history in customer data analysis, creating loyalty programs and pricing solutions. Our specialists work primarily with the data of prestigious foreign clients, mainly from the UK and Saudi Arabia, from various business sectors, helping them make better decisions, with the aim of increasing their profits. Our ambition is to continuously expand into foreign markets and build an excellent reputation, especially with foreign clients. We have the international award Loyalty Awards London in data processing in CRM. Our Voxwise team combines humanness, collaboration, and result orientation. Ambition and an emphasis on innovation go hand in hand with being clear-headed. We think big, but we live in reality.
