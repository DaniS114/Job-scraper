Responsibilities
In your role as SAP S/4HANA RAR Consultant, you will manage standard Revenue Accounting & Reporting (RAR) operating procedures, including contract management, cost recognition, contract combination and modification, posting management, reporting, and integration with FI and CO-PA
You will work closely with the SD functional team and support designing processes aligned with RAR revenue postings
Your responsibilities will include advising and supporting customers in the introduction and further development of processes and applications
You will take ownership of projects, managing goals, budgets, and timelines effectively
You will plan IT project resources and track progress using waterfall and agile methods
Together with the enterprise architect and software development team, you will coordinate solution definitions and ensure alignment across stakeholders
Requirements
Qualification
: A degree in business administration, computer science, or a comparable field
Experience
: At least 4 years of professional experience, thereof at least 2 years as a consultant
Know-how and skills
: Strong functional knowledge of SD scenarios (e.g., intercompany processes, maintenance services), ideally experience in RAR implementation; understanding of order-to-cash processes, SD/FICO module integration, IFRS 15, CO-PA, and revenue recognition; extensive knowledge of SAP and Salesforce
Nice to have:
Experience with agile project management and familiarity with enterprise architecture principles
Personality
: You are a proactive team player with excellent stakeholder communication skills and are willing to drive performance and change
Language skills:
Fluent English skills, German or other languages are a plus
