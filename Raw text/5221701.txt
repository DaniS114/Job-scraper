Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you interested in building and running the data integration and reporting platforms that support analytics across Swiss Re? Join our team and help evolve critical Azure Data Factory (Self-Hosted Integration Runtime) and Microsoft BI services so they stay reliable, secure, and easy to operate.
About the role
As a Data Integration & BI Platform Engineer, you’ll be a key technical contributor for SHIR and the MSBI stack (SSIS/SSRS/PBIRS) running on Windows. You’ll help keep the platform stable day-to-day while also improving how we monitor, change, and scale it.
What you’ll do
•	Own and evolve parts of the SHIR/MSBI platform architecture, standards, and roadmap
•	Improve reliability through monitoring, SLO/SLI-style metrics, and continuous platform enhancements
•	Support major incidents when they happen, helping with technical coordination and communication
•	Manage changes and releases with clear validation and rollback practices
•	Maintain practical documentation (runbooks, decisions, support guides) and share knowledge with support teams
•	Prioritize technical work balancing stability, compliance, scale, and cost
