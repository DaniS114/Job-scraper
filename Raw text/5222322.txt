Service DevOps Lead - Workplace Services, Alma Media
Service DevOps Lead - Workplace Services, Alma Media
Alma Media is a digital service business and media company with a strong capacity for renewal. We are building sustainable growth from media to services, providing content and services that benefit users in their everyday lives, work and leisure time. In Finland, our best-known brands include Kauppalehti, Talouselämä, Iltalehti, Etuovi.com, Nettiauto and Nettimoto. Our recruitment services include prace.cz and jobs.cz in the Czech Republic, Profesia.sk in Slovakia and mojposao.net in Croatia.
In Finland, our business operations include leading housing and automotive marketplaces, financial and professional media, national consumer media and content and data services for professionals and businesses. Alma Media’s international business in Eastern Central Europe, Sweden and the Baltic countries consists of recruitment services and an online marketplace for commercial properties. Alma Media operates in 10 countries in Europe and employs approximately 1,700 professionals. Alma Media’s revenue from continuing operations was EUR 305 million in 2023. Alma Media’s share is listed on NASDAQ Helsinki.

About the Role
Would you like to build a digital workplace people actually love using? If that sounds like you, we’re now hiring a Service DevOps Lead to lead and develop our Workplace service. In this role, you will design, deliver and continuously improve our digital workplace environment. You own services like end-user devices, Microsoft 365, meeting rooms, collaboration and communication tools, software distribution, asset management, and overall reliability of workplace IT services. If you get this role right, employees stop thinking about IT. Meetings start on time. Devices just work. Software shows up when needed. Collaboration feels effortless.
Key Responsibilities
Own the end-to-end lifecycle and roadmap for workplace services. From strategy to daily performance
Shape service strategies for devices, collaboration tools, communication platforms, and modern workplace capabilities
Lead device lifecycle from procurement to retirement. Standard configurations and software delivery at scale
Ensure asset and inventory accuracy in cooperation with partners
Govern telephony, unified communications, and meeting room technology environments
Ensure reliable performance and governance of the Microsoft 365 platform
Drive DevOps practices, proactive monitoring, operational excellence and shift-left
Identify and implement automation opportunities in endpoint management and software deployment
Pilot and scale AI-driven solutions that boost productivity, efficiency and employee experience
Own vendor governance. SLAs, KPIs, OLAs, service reviews, performance management and contract discussions
Own the numbers. Budget planning, forecasting, business cases, cost optimization, transparency and accountability
Be the main point of contact for workplace technologies. Communicate releases, changes, and roadmaps clearly
Jump into daily operational topics when it matters
You will succeed in this role if you have:
Deep expertise in endpoint management. Intune, SCCM, JAMF. And the confidence to standardize and scale
Strong knowledge of Microsoft 365, AV and meeting room tech, telephony, and automation
ITSM and service governance know-how. Ideally ITIL aligned. Plus asset management and cybersecurity basics
Proven experience running outsourced or multi-vendor services in an international environment
A real automation and AI mindset. You turn emerging tech into practical, scalable services
Strong communication in English and a data-driven way of working
Typically 10+ years in workplace technology. 5+ years managing outsourced operations
In this role you’ll take ownership of a service that people rely on every single day, making your impact both immediate and highly visible. You’ll have the freedom to modernize in a meaningful way, working with DevOps practices, automation, and AI instead of just producing slide decks. And you’ll do all of this in an international environment with real scale and complexity, supported by talented teams and strong partners.
