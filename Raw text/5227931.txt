Job description, responsibilities and duties
Shape the architecture. Mentor the team. Build systems that matter.
We’re looking for a
Senior Developer / Tech Lead / Architect
who enjoys working hands-on while also guiding others and shaping technical direction. This role is ideal for someone who wants real ownership — over architecture, code quality, and the growth of a small, capable development team.
You’ll play a central role in designing and evolving
custom-built CRM solutions
, working closely with product owners and business stakeholders to turn complex requirements into clean, scalable, and maintainable systems. Beyond building software, you’ll help define best practices, mentor developers, and influence long-term technical decisions.
What you’ll be doing
Lead the design and architecture of custom CRM solutions and related systems
Act as a technical mentor: review code, provide architectural guidance, and support developers’ growth
Collaborate with product owners and business stakeholders to translate real business needs into technical solutions
Take ownership of solution quality, performance, scalability, and security
Establish and promote best practices in coding standards, testing, CI/CD, and documentation
Stay hands-on, especially for complex, high-impact features and technical challenges
Foster a collaborative, respectful team culture with open communication
Support key technical decisions and help shape the long-term technical roadmap
