Who is CAT Digital? What do we do?
Big machines require big thinking. Are you looking for your next big challenge?
Cat® Digital is the digital and technology arm of Caterpillar Inc., leveraging the latest technologies to build industry leading digital solutions for our customers and dealers. With over one million connected assets worldwide, our teams use data, technology, advanced analytics, telematics, and AI capabilities to help our customers build a better world.
Working with a Fortune 100 leader, you can build your career on a global scale and take advantage of development opportunities with emerging technologies. We’ve created an inclusive environment for you to explore your passions, make an impact and do the work that really matters. Join Us.
Job Opportunity:
We are seeking talented individuals to join our team to help us grow our presence, organization, culture, which will drive our digital product development efforts. As a member of our team, you will have the opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies and collaborate with top professionals working on very high impact and visibility products. Don't miss this chance to join a newly forming presence and support digital innovation for a global leader in heavy machinery!
What will you do?
As a Lead Data Analyst you will be an integral part of our team of talented analysts in Cat Digital, working alongside of other data scientists and analysts in the organization. You will gain experience in a vibrant and diverse multinational organization and develop your understanding of business practices and the data driven revolution. You will be as well:
Leading people, monitoring their projects, providing help, mentorship, and guidance.
Independent contributor to multi-person analytics teams.
Assisting in the development, testing, and delivery of services/products.
Demonstrates knowledge and ability to handle issues independently.
Act as a project lead on complex and highly visible projects.
Locate and obtain data from various internal and external sources.
Rapidly iterations of new ideas and debug issues.
This position works directly with Slovakia based engineering leadership and Cat Digital IT in the US.

Personality requirements and skills
What you have:
Accuracy and Attention to Detail: Understanding the necessity and value of accuracy; ability to complete tasks with high levels of precision.
Business Statistics: Knowledge of the statistical tools, processes, and practices to describe business results in measurable scales; ability to use statistical tools and processes to assist in making business decisions.
Strong people management and supervision skills.
Strong experience using SQL to query interconnected databases.
Capacity to adapt documents and presentations for the intended audience (including senior management).
Ability to communicate, simplifying complex data to assist business partners and decision makers.
Ability to collect required business data through various data sources and applies data analysis techniques to establish, modify, and maintain business data structures.
Top Candidates Will Also Have:
Data driven skills with the ability to organize and analyze significant amount of information.
Good logical reasoning skills.
Excellent communication skills.
Exposure to specific organizational tools such as Azure DevOps and GitHub.
Knowledge with programming languages such as Python and R preferred
