Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced AI / Machine Learning Engineer who will help us develop and adapt neural network models for novel hardware architectures, including memristor-based and neuromorphic systems.
The role sits at the intersection of machine learning, hardware-aware AI optimization and emerging computing paradigms.
You will work closely with electronics engineers, microelectronics technologists and researchers developing next-generation computing architectures.
This role focuses on core machine learning and hardware-aware AI research, not on building applications around existing AI APIs or prompt-engineering workflows.
What you will work on
Development, training and evaluation of neural network models for research and applied projects
Adaptation and optimization of AI models for non-standard hardware architectures
Exploration of neuromorphic computing approaches and spiking neural networks
Cooperation with hardware teams working on memristor-based devices and in-memory computing
Implementation and evaluation of ML workloads on GPU/CUDA platforms
Experimentation with new AI paradigms relevant for low-power and hardware-efficient AI
Preparation of technical materials for international research projects (IPCEI, Horizon Europe)
Internal knowledge sharing and mentoring of colleagues
Required skills and experience
Strong background in machine learning and neural networks
Practical experience with training and optimization of ML/DL models
Solid understanding of linear algebra, probability, statistics and optimization
Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
Strong programming skills in Python
Experience with GPU computing (CUDA or similar) and understanding of AI workloads on modern hardware
Ability to analyze model performance and optimize training and inference
Highly desirable
Experience with hardware-aware machine learning
Knowledge of neuromorphic computing
Experience with spiking neural networks
Understanding of memristive devices or in-memory computing concepts
Experience with C++ or other low-level programming languages
Experience collaborating with hardware or semiconductor teams
Research background (PhD or equivalent experience)
