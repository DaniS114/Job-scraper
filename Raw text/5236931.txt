Popis pracovnej pozície
The position is based in Slovak Republic. Applicants must have a place of work in Slovakia.
We are looking for a Business Analyst to support the development of an internal system for underwriters. In this role, you will work closely with business stakeholders across countries to understand processes, needs, and expectations, and translate them into clear, structured documentation.
Your outputs will serve as a reliable foundation for the Product team, enabling them to create and maintain a well‑defined backlog.
What you will do
Engage and collaborate with business stakeholders across countries and teams; conduct interviews, clarify needs, align expectations, and manage requirements‑related discussions.
Elicit, analyse, and refine business requirements for new system stages and functionalities, ensuring they are clearly understood and consistently documented.
Build and maintain end‑to‑end process understanding by mapping business processes and user journeys, identifying dependencies, gaps, and overlaps across topics and markets.
Translate business needs into structured documentation such as functional requirements, process descriptions, business rules, and decision records that can be used by Product Management to create backlog items.
Facilitate workshops (e.g. requirements workshops, clarification and alignment sessions), document outcomes and decisions, and ensure shared understanding among stakeholders.
Identify standardisation and simplification opportunities by recognising patterns, redundancies, and improvement areas across countries and processes.
Maintain high‑quality documentation and “single source of truth” artefacts, including process flows, functional descriptions, stakeholder maps, glossaries, and decision logs.
What you can expect
A key role in a major transformation programme with visibility and impact across multiple countries.
Close collaboration with business stakeholders and Product Management, with clear ownership of requirements clarity and documentation quality.
The opportunity to shape how business requirements, process documentation, and shared understanding are built and maintained within the programme.
A stable internal position with growth opportunities in business analysis and product‑oriented work.
