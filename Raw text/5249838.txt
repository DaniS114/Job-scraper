Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you want to help create the future of healthcare? Our name, Siemens Healthineers, was selected to honor our people who dedicate their energy and passion to this cause. It reflects their pioneering spirit combined with our long history of engineering in the ever-evolving healthcare industry.
We offer you a flexible and dynamic environment with opportunities to go beyond your comfort zone in order to grow personally and professionally. Sounds interesting?
Then come and join our global team as
Information Security Management System (ISMS) Manager (f/m/d) for certifications to ensure market access for Customer Service's digital solutions.
Choose the best place for your work
– Within the scope of this position, it is possible, in consultation with your manager, to work mobile (within Germany) up to an average volume of 60% of the respective working hours.
Even more flexibility?
Mobile working from abroad is possible for up to 30 days a year under certain conditions and in selected countries.
The position can be filled in Erlangen (Germany) or Kosice (Slovakia).
This position is full-time or part-time with a minimum of 30h/week.
Your tasks and responsibilities:
You will act as the contact person and coordinator for the ISMS and certifications. You will support the certification strategy and ensure resource-optimized certification efforts for the digital solutions of Customer Service.
You will be responsible for the design, introduction, expansion, and management of internal control systems for testing.
You will manage all necessary documentation and evidence for the preparation and implementation of ISO27001 audits.
You will conduct audits yourself, as well as prepare and lead internal and external audits of the ISMS.
You will work closely with corporate functions to align corporate standards, best practices, and benchmarks, and to assess regulatory requirements.

https://www.siemens-healthineers.com/products-services
Your qualifications and experience:
You have a degree in information technology, business law, or a comparable field.
You have many years of professional experience in dealing with integrated management systems and their certifications (e.g., ISO 27x; ENS), and ideally attestations (e.g., SOC2, C5).
Do you have extensive, long-standing professional experience and additional qualifications in this field, but no degree? Then we would also be happy to receive your application.
You have proven experience as a project or sub-project manager coordinating the necessary measures in the certification process in large organizations and can ideally demonstrate the various project management methods with relevant certifications. Additional experience with agile methods would be an advantage.
You have in-depth knowledge of the design, development, and operation of group-wide IT solutions, as well as comprehensive knowledge of the processes for the secure management of IT and service organizations with regard to cybersecurity.
Your attributes and skills:
You have business fluent written and spoken English skills; additional German skills are a great advantage.
You have a diverse toolkit for analyzing complex interrelationships and developing solutions together with international teams.
You always keep long-term goals in mind when developing solutions, weigh up options comprehensively, and ensure sustainable results.
You have excellent social skills, negotiating skills, and assertiveness to develop and implement decisions across all technical disciplines and various hierarchical levels.
Your digital skills in researching, analyzing, and processing data and information help you to efficiently identify and solve challenges.
