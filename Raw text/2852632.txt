Responsibilities
Strengthen the completeness and reliability of the Group's Data Catalog (Data Galaxy) by conducting in depth analyses of data systems, governance frameworks, and business usage across the organization
Develop, maintain, and expand KPIs, dashboards, and steering materials to effectively communicate progress and insights to business units, management and data governance committees, enabling informed decision making and actionable follow ups
Co design and structure group wide data policies in collaboration with IT teams, using POCs to validate approaches and ensure sustainable improvements to data quality
Leverage the advanced tools available to drive governance efforts, act as an early tester for new technologies, and provide feedback to enhance adoption and reliability
Gather and synthesize user feedback regarding data accessibility and usability, and translate it into clear, actionable specifications for IT teams to enhance and stabilize the Data Catalog
Partner with DPO, IRM, and IT teams to build a unified approach to data accessibility, accountability, and regulatory compliance, ensuring alignment with Group wide standards
Represent the Data Office in strategic discussions on major data transformation initiatives, such as scaling self service BI and maturing the Group's Data Platform operating model
Become a Champion in data initiatives across the organization by promoting best practices, driving awareness, and acting as a visible representative of the Data Office
Guide business teams toward the right data resources, tools, and governance principles, enabling them to become more autonomous and effective in their daily use of data
Requirements
Master degree in business administration, economics, business informatics or computer science
Strong interest in the financial and banking sector, with relevant experience
Experience in one of the following areas is appreciated: data analytics, data governance, financial analysis, consulting or change management
Strong capacity for autonomy and initiative to lead projects and communicate a clear action plan also to senior audiences.
Ability to convince and simplify technical subjects for business or IT audiences through the production of high-quality PowerPoint presentations
Advanced data processing skills (SQL, Python or Excel VBA).
First hands-on experience with a Data Platform (Snowflake, DataBricks, MS Fabric) is a plus
Previous experience working with a data catalog (e.g., DataGalaxy, Collibra, Purview, or similar) is a strong plus and will help the candidate ramp up more quickly
Fluent in German and English, French is a plus

Company info
ODDO BHF is an independent European financial group active in the areas of Private Wealth Management, Asset Management and Corporates & Investment Banking. The Group benefits from its strong Franco-German roots and its strategic presence in Switzerland and currently manages client assets of 140 billion euros. ODDO BHF has around 3,000 employees and a unique shareholder structure that ensures its independence. 90 percent of the shares are hold by the Oddo family and employees.
