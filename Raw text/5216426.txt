You will be directly involved in the development of browser extensions and privacy protection projects and will cooperate with a variety of stakeholders from different teams, work independently as well as a part of your organization unit.
We are looking for a motivated technology enthusiast ready to learn new things who will help us to achieve our goals and grow as an expert in the entrusted responsibilities.

Development and support of browser extensions for our privacy protection projects
Participation on the development of associated components of our products
Design and implementation of automatization tools for development and operational tasks
Participation on code reviews, technical analysis and research
