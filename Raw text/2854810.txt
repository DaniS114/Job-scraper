Company info
DXC Technology is a global IT services leader founded in 2017 through the merger of CSC and the Enterprise Services business of Hewlett Packard Enterprise. The company provides end-to-end IT services and solutions to clients across various industries worldwide, focusing on digital transformation, cloud, security, and analytics. With a workforce of over 130,000 professionals, DXC Technology operates in more than 70 countries, delivering innovative technology solutions to help clients succeed in a rapidly changing digital landscape.
