Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the role
In this pivotal role, you'll be the driving force behind our team's delivery success during a significant transformation period. You'll implement pragmatic agile practices, create clarity in priorities, and ensure the team can focus on what matters most while balancing operational demands with strategic improvements.
Key Responsibilities:
- Establish effective delivery rhythm by implementing lightweight Scrum practices focused on outcomes rather than ceremony, creating a sustainable and productive cadence for the team
- Drive execution excellence by reducing context switching, removing blockers, and helping the team complete one work package at a time before moving to the next
- Enhance prioritization by supporting the team lead in balancing capacity against demand, confidently saying "no/not now" when necessary to protect team focus
- Create transparency through breaking down complex deliverables into manageable chunks with clear acceptance criteria and providing stakeholders with regular, honest status updates
- Support audit compliance by translating findings into structured action plans with clear ownership, sequencing, and measurable outcomes
- Foster continuous improvement by facilitating retrospectives that lead to meaningful team and process enhancements
About the team
Our Logging & Monitoring squad develops and operates state-of-the-art logging, monitoring and event management platforms to collect application behavior information, detect/limit service disruption and provide the associated reporting capabilities. Our ambition is to help empower developers, application and platform owners identify any growing risks, have a clear understanding of their SLAs, reduce the mean time to resolution and be ahead of the curve with regards to long term trends.
