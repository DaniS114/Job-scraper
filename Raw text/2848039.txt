Our platform includes the software MaxLab Live, a custom-designed integrated circuit, and an FPGA-based data acquisition system that generates vast amounts of data when electrically imaging neu-ronal tissue. These data are processed, analyzed, and visualized in real-time.We are seeking a highly motivated Computational Neuroscientist or Electrophysiology Data Analysis Engineer to join our interdisciplinary team.
