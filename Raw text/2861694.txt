Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Develop, optimise, and maintain backend services using Java (Spring Boot)
Contribute to design decisions for new and existing projects
Work on football-related data pipelines that power FIFA, Serie-A and UEFA operations
Deploy and scale applications in AWS (ECS, Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, etc.)
Enhance system observability, ensuring real-time monitoring and logging using tools like Datadog
Work on event-driven architectures, leveraging message queues (Pulsar, Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS, etc.)
Collaborate with engineers, stakeholders and external partners to meet high-impact project deadlines
Ensure system reliability and performance in high-traffic, real-time environments
Requirements
Strong Java 17 & Spring Boot experience
Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS preferred)
Familiarity with observability tools (e.g., Datadog, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry)
Experience with microservices and distributed systems
Understanding of monitoring, logging, and traceability
Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment
Nice-to-have
Bonus Skills: Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS, etc.), Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automation (GitHub Actions, Terraform), Familiarity with containerisation and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes), Exposure to real-time data processing and sports tech
What we offer
Annual Leave: Standard according to local labour law
Flexible Working: Hybrid model (2 days in the office per week)
Exclusive Perks: Access to sporting events and tickets, Sony Group Company discounts
Additional Benefits: Cafeteria options (SZÉP Card, Nursery & Kindergarten contributions, Annual MOL Bubi Pass), private medical insurance, life & accident insurance, dental clinic discounts, seniority awards, an Employee Assistance Program, and the option to purchase an "All You Can Move" Pass
