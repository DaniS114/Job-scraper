Responsibilities
Guiding the Kotlin backend developers in designing, developing, and maintaining software applications, while setting technical direction and upholding code quality standards
Collaborating with the software architect to define and evolve system architecture for enhanced scalability, performance, and security
Actively engaging in coding, code review, and the enforcement of best practices
Collaborate closely with our Customer Support (L1), Infrastructure Support (L2), and Application Support (L3) teams to efficiently manage escalations- helping identify, troubleshoot, and resolve operational issues that keep our clients running smoothly
Participate in a rotating on-call schedule to ensure reliable 24/7 client support paired with appropriate compensation for your time and expertise
Mentoring and coaching team members to promote continuous learning and growth
Efficiently managing tasks and providing technical insights to ensure on-time and on-scope project delivery
Maintaining consistent communication with the Project Manager to ensure alignment and shared objectives
Staying up-to-date with the latest Java, Kotlin, backend technologies, frameworks, and tools, and introducing relevant technologies to enhance development processes

Company info
Our vision is simple yet powerful: we want to create a place where engineers thrive, taking on exciting projects and using the latest technologies, free from the shackles of legacy solutions.
Thanks to the diverse range of industries we work with, our days are never boring: from world-class apps to AR solutions or even programming robots, we’ve got it all covered. We don’t just write code for our screens; rather, we develop impactful products used by millions every day: let them be large-scale public transport applications, leading fintech platforms, or storage solutions contributing to a greener future.
We are a team of 100+ engineers (Java, JavaScript, Android, iOS, NodeJS and dotnet platforms) and QA engineers with our coding fingers at the ready working on the next big thing. We value openness, modesty, hard work, and simple yet great solutions.
