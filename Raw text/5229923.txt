Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a Senior Angular Developer to join our team on an interesting project for one of our clients operating in the heavy-machinery industry. The project is primarily web-based and requires a strong senior profile who will take technical ownership of the frontend and support other developers on the team.
The role requires participation in meetings with US colleagues, typically scheduled between 17:00 and 19:00 CET , with flexibility to adjust working hours accordingly.
As an Angular Developer with us you will:
Design, develop, and maintain complex web applications using Angular.
Take technical leadership over the frontend part of the project.
Guide, mentor, and support other developers within the team.
Collaborate with backend developers, architects, and product stakeholders.
Ensure high code quality, scalability, and performance of the application.
Participate in architecture discussions and technical decision-making.
Actively contribute to code reviews and frontend best practices.
Participate in agile ceremonies, including planning and stand-ups with international teams.
