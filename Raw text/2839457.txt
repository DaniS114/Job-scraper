About us
Bei bp haben wir nur eins im Kopf: die Energie von morgen. Wir wollen Energie neu denken, damit wir bis spätestens 2050 NetZero erreichen. Wir setzen dabei auf die Kompetenz und Leidenschaft unserer rund 66.000 Mitarbeiter:innen weltweit, davon ca. 4.000 in Deutschland. Sie sichern jeden Tag mit den Produkten und Dienstleistungen unserer Marken bp, Aral und Castrol die Energie und Mobilität für Millionen Menschen – heute und in Zukunft.
In this role You will:
Would you like to discover how our diverse, hardworking people are leading the way in making energy cleaner and better – and how you can play your part in our world-class team?
Join our Team and advance your career as an
EV Data and Reporting Analyst
As Data and Reporting Analyst you will be managing all the business related Data and reporting dashboards. You will be responsible for engaging with Business Stakeholders and track business Key Performance Indicators.
In this role You will:
Create, design and maintain reporting dashboards (Power BI)
Manage, extract and consolidate data from multiple sources
Capture and deliver reporting requirements from several business and finance collaborators
Create standard and ad-hoc reports for management decisions
Work closely with Commercial Lead for Performance Management
Present management reports and business targets to product owners
Skills:
Analysis and modelling, Analytics, Commercial Acumen, Commercial performance management, Communication, Data visualization and interpretation, Financial Analysis, Integrated Planning, Investment appraisal, Performance management
What You will need to be successful:
University degree in Engineering / Management / Data Analytics  (or equivalent).
Several years of experience in Data/Reporting Roles
Written and oral fluency in English. Other European languages would be an advantage
PowerBI knowledge is mandatory
DAX, SQL and Power Query M knowledge is mandatory
Experience with Star schema, Snowflake or other data model designs is mandatory
Phyton knowledge is a plus
Good interpersonal skills being able to approach and discuss with diverse collaborators
Strong Team spirit and "we" oriented thinking
Critical and Analytical thinking
