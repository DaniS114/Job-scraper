MES Business Analyst
What can you expect?
Adient (NYSE: ADNT) is a global leader in automotive seating. With 70,000+ employees in 29 countries, Adient operates more than 200 manufacturing/assembly plants worldwide. We produce and deliver automotive seating for all major OEMs. From complete seating systems to individual foam, trim and metal components, our expertise spans every step of the automotive seat-making process. Our integrated, in-house skills allow us to take our products from research and design to engineering and manufacturing — and into millions of vehicles every year. For more information, visit www.adient.com.
At Adient, we are proud to offer hybrid working to our employees, which involves being able to work remotely for up to 3 days per week based on the agreement within specific department.
What will your responsibilities be?
•	Analyze plant processes and translate business needs into clear functional requirements.
•	Support MES/KAPP logic including workflows, traceability, PLC-based signals, and event sequencing.
•	Coordinate with system integrators, KAPP Central Team, and plant stakeholders to ensure aligned and efficient deliveries.
•	Prepare and execute UAT, validate functional behavior, and support multi-plant deployments.
•	Provide Level-2 functional support and contribute to continuous improvement.
Which qualifications are we looking for in potential employees?
•	Experience with MES systems in automotive or JIT/JIS environments.
•	Understanding of PLC integration, machine/station logic, and shopfloor execution processes.
•	Ability to document requirements, create functional specifications, and manage test scenarios.
•	Strong communication skills and ability to work with business, IT, and external integrators.
•	Preferred: experience with KAPP, multi-plant MES rollouts, or lean/JIT operations.
Preferred MES platform experience

Brief description of the company
Adient is the leading automotive seating supplier.
With around 2500 employees and five locations in Slovakia, the company is one of the key employers in Slovakia’s automotive industry. We are committed to Sustainability and are proud ambassador of Diversity Charter in Slovakia since 2017.
Adient’s only business center worldwide is in Bratislava. Approximately 650 employees in Bratislava support the company’s business around the globe in finance, information technology, purchasing, sales, human resources, transport and logistics and project management.
In Trencin, Adient operates the biggest automotive technology center across Slovakia. More than 400 employees there develop a wide range of highly innovative products for the cars of tomorrow.
In Zilina, the company produces complete seat systems and automotive fabrics, in Lucenec foam components for seats as well as metal components for head restraints.
Adient (NYSE: ADNT) is a global leader in automotive seating. With more than 65,000 employees in 29 countries, Adient operates ~200 manufacturing/assembly plants worldwide. We produce and deliver automotive seating for all major OEMs. From complete seating systems to individual components, our expertise spans every step of the automotive seat-making process. We take our products from research and design to engineering and manufacturing — and into millions of vehicles every year.
