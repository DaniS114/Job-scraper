Responsibilities
Your contribution:
As a Process Owner within the Product Cybersecurity Engineering team, you will be responsible for leading and supporting the development and integration of cybersecurity engineering and related processes in alignment with industry standards and internal quality frameworks. This role will focus on embedding cybersecurity engineering processes, methods, and tools into the product engineering lifecycle, ensuring alignment with ASPICE process models and ISO 26262 Functional Safety activities.
You will work cross-functionally with system architects, software and hardware engineers, and product security and safety experts to ensure robust and compliant processes are integrated into project execution. This role is pivotal in fostering a cybersecurity culture and continuous improvement in the development of embedded control units for the management of automotive low voltage energy storage and power distribution systems.
Key Responsibilities:
Process Development and Integration
Lead the development of our automotive product Cybersecurity Management System aligned with global regulations and industry standards.
Define and maintain cybersecurity engineering processes aligned with ISO/SAE 21434 across the V-model lifecycle.
Integrate cybersecurity activities into ASPICE base practices, ensuring traceability and consistency with system, software, and hardware development processes.
Align cybersecurity lifecycle with Functional Safety (ISO 26262) processes, including hazard analysis, safety goals, and safety requirements.
Methods & Work Product Management
Define and maintain templates, work instructions, checklists and guidelines for cybersecurity work products to ensure compliance and audit readiness.
Establish and maintain standardized methods for:
Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA)
Cybersecurity Goals, Concept and Requirements Engineering
Cybersecurity Verification and Validation
Cybersecurity Case and Assurance Documentation
Tooling & Workflow Integration
Implement and maintain engineering tools that support cybersecurity engineering activities and work product management (e.g., TARA development, requirements management, fuzz testing, etc…).
Define and embed process workflows into engineering tools to ensure traceability and consistency.
Training & Enablement
Develop and deliver training materials to educate engineering teams on cybersecurity engineering processes, methods, standards and tools.
Act as a mentor and coach to engineers, promoting process adherence and continuous improvement.
Establish baseline process implementation in support of development projects.
Cross-Functional Collaboration
Align interfaces between cybersecurity engineering processes and adjacent domains (systems, software, hardware, project management, manufacturing, and quality assurance).
Facilitate cross-functional reviews and technical decision-making processes.
Governance & Compliance
Establish and enforce process governance frameworks.
Conduct and support regular audits and assessments to ensure compliance with internal and external standards.
Support certification and regulatory efforts in collaboration with quality and compliance teams.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives based on lessons learned, assessment findings, and industry best practices.
Stay up to date with industry trends, regulations, and advancements in cyber security and automotive technologies.
Lead process improvement initiatives.
Requirements
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Systems Engineering, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
Minimum 10 years of experience in automotive engineering, with a focus on process development and compliance complemented by proven experience in hands-on engineering development.
English on a fluent level, German is nice to have
Experience in defining and deploying engineering processes and methods in a regulated development environment.
Deep understanding of ISO/SAE 21434, ASPICE, and ISO 26262 standards and their interdependencies
Familiarity with model-based systems engineering (MBSE), requirements management, and safety/security analysis tools.
Experience in engineering and/or process development in the field of battery systems at an automotive OEM or tier 1 is a plus.
Experience with requirements management tools (e.g., DOORS, Jama)
Experience with process documentation and management tools (e.g., UL Stages).
Demonstrated ability to lead process improvement initiatives and manage cross-functional collaboration.
Experience in mentoring and training engineering teams.
Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
Familiarity with configuration management and change control processes.
