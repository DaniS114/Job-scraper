Responsibilities
Join a client service team for 8-10 weeks
Contribute to workstreams to help solve client challenges
Uncover true challenges behind a client’s strategy
Create actionable plans that drive real change
Combine research, analyzing data, interviews, and brainstorming to generate insights and solutions
Collaborate directly with clients to solve problems, refine strategies, and implement transformations
Equip clients with tools and knowledge to continue innovating and improving after engagement
Requirements
Undergraduate degree in progress
Excellent organization capabilities, including ability to initiate tasks independently and see them through to completion
Strong communication skills, both verbal and written, in English and local office language(s)
Ability to adjust communication style to suit different perspectives and seniority levels
Proficient in rational decision making based on data, facts, and logical reasoning
Ability to create work product-focused materials such as PowerPoint decks, Excel models, articles, or other written deliverables
Exceptional time management to meet responsibilities in a complex and largely autonomous work environment
Ability to work or attend meetings outside of traditional business hours or take on projects with limited or no notice
Ability to travel to and work in varying environments that may be challenging and/or not accessible (e.g., factories, hospitals, mines)

Competitive salary based on location, experience, and skills

Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves some of the most influential organizations around, from Fortune 500 giants to innovative startups. The firm operates in over 65 countries with a diverse workforce of over 100 nationalities. McKinsey is committed to making a meaningful difference in the industries and communities it serves, driven by an unwavering commitment to integrity and ethics. The company provides a high performance/high reward culture focused on leadership development, continuous learning, and delivering exceptional results for clients.
