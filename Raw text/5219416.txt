Job description, responsibilities and duties
Why This Job Is Exciting
As a Technical Project Manager, you will lead complex customer deliveries of Innovatrics biometric solutions across global markets. You will operate at high ownership and high ambiguity, coordinating cross-functional teams and proactively shaping scope, timelines, and risks with customers and internal stakeholders.
Within one month, you will…
Build a full delivery map of our solutions, delivery lifecycle, governance, and escalation paths
Join customer and internal ceremonies as the primary delivery owner on at least one active project
Identify top delivery risks across your assigned scope and propose mitigation plans
Align with Professional Services team on how you will run delivery rhythm (status, reporting, decision logs)
Within three months, you will…
Lead at least one complex client delivery end to end, including scope control, milestones, resourcing, and stakeholder management
Run project governance confidently with clients including steering, risk reviews, and change control
Drive cross-team alignment on dependencies and unblock critical path issues with minimal supervision
Contribute improvements to delivery playbooks, templates, and internal tooling based on real project learnings
Within six months, you will…
Lead multiple concurrent projects or a larger program with several workstreams
Handle escalations and course-correct delivery when priorities, scope, or constraints change
Confidently bridge technical and non-technical audiences in customer conversations
Contribute to mentoring and knowledge sharing across Professional Services team
Within one year, you will…
Be a trusted delivery partner for key clients and internal stakeholders
Consistently deliver measurable value by balancing scope, time, cost, and quality tradeoffs
Influence product and solution evolution by turning customer and delivery insights into actionable input for product roadmaps and internal OKRs
Raise the delivery bar across the team by shaping best practices and contributing to PM standards
Who We Are
At Innovatrics, we build biometric technologies for elections, border control, digital ID, and secure onboarding in 80+ countries. Our algorithms have reached over a billion people.
Your work here won’t get lost in a backlog; it’ll shape real-world systems that matter.
We’re a small team, so your ideas ship fast. From day one, you’ll build production features and shape global-scale projects within weeks.
