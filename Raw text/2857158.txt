About Us: Digital Factory @ MOL Group
It’s a place where the agility of a start-up meets the stability of a global powerhouse!
That’s us. We’re Digital Factory, the innovation engine behind MOL Group’s transformation from a traditional fuel retailer into a digital-first, customer-obsessed mobility and consumer services powerhouse.
We build smart systems powered by data and AI – from our Salesforce-based loyalty app (MOL MOVE) to advanced analytics in pricing, forecasting, and workforce optimization. Our Group Data Analytics team is at the heart of this transformation, and now we’re looking for a new teammate to join the journey.
