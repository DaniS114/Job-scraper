Company info
As the world leader in serving science, we solve the world’s toughest problems, like giving cancer patients hope, ensuring water and food are safe, helping law enforcement solve cases through forensics and most recently, serving as the largest provider of COVID-19 solutions. Our Data Science, Digital and IT careers are a unique opportunity to apply your specialized skills and advanced experience in analytics, statistics, data science, programming, information technology and more to drive our Mission of enabling our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. It’s truly a career where your work makes a positive impact in our world. When you join Thermo Fisher Scientific, you’ll make an impact inside and outside the company on work that matters. #TechWithPurpose
Position Summary
As part of the Thermo Fisher Scientific team, you’ll discover meaningful work that makes a positive impact on a global scale. Join our colleagues in bringing our Mission to life every single day to enable our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer. We provide our global teams with the resources needed to achieve individual career goals while helping to take science a step beyond by developing solutions for some of the world’s toughest challenges, like protecting the environment, making sure our food is safe or helping find cures for cancer.
DESCRIPTION:
Join our IT team at Thermo Fisher Scientific and contribute to technological innovation that enables our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner, and safer. As a Systems Analyst II, you'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement, maintain, and optimize critical business systems. You'll leverage your technical expertise and analytical skills to gather requirements, design solutions, and ensure smooth operations of enterprise applications.
Work closely with business partners to understand needs, document requirements, and translate them into effective technical solutions. Lead the configuration, testing, and deployment of system enhancements while ensuring compliance with corporate standards. Provide technical guidance to resolve complex issues and drive continuous improvement initiatives. Your work will directly impact our ability to serve customers and advance scientific discovery.
Skills & Qualifications Required
Advanced Degree with no prior experience, or Bachelor's Degree plus 2 years of experience in systems analysis or similar technical role
Preferred Fields of Study: Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, Engineering or related field
Additional relevant certifications (e.g., ITIL, ServiceNow, SAP, Agile) preferred
Strong understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC) and project management methodologies
Demonstrated ability to gather, analyze and document business requirements
Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
Strong verbal and written communication skills to interact with all levels of stakeholders
Experience with supporting SAP primarily in the SD space
Knowledge of IT service management practices and change management processes
Proficiency in Microsoft Office suite and relevant technical tools
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Experience in regulated industries (e.g., GxP, FDA, SOX) preferred
Experience mentoring team members and providing technical guidance
Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities effectively
Flexibility to support operations outside standard business hours occasionally
Foreign language skills may be beneficial
