About the role:
The Onsite Services Technician Intern will be responsible for providing comprehensive support for Schneider Electric workplace and infrastructure equipment. This role involves hands-on technical tasks, coordination with external vendors, and ensuring high levels of end-user satisfaction. The technician will perform a variety of services including hardware break and fix, warranty management, asset tracking, IMACD (Install, Move, Add, Change, De-Install), eyes and hand support, and escort services.
What you will do:
We need our future intern’s support in the following areas:
•Desk-side Support: Provide direct support to end-users for workplace equipment including desktops, laptops, tablets, smartphones, and other devices.
•Hardware Break & Fix: Assist in diagnosing and repairing hardware issues, manage replacement of defective units, and ensure data restoration.
•Warranty Management: Coordinate repairs with manufacturers and third-party maintenance suppliers, manage warranty claims, and handle logistics for defective units.
•Asset Tracking: Maintain accurate records of equipment status, location, and user assignments in the asset management system.
•IMACD Services: Perform installations, moves, additions, changes, and de-installations of workplace equipment, ensuring proper configuration and user acceptance.
•Eyes and Hand Support: Execute technical tasks under the direction of remote administration teams, including equipment resets, installations, and troubleshooting.
•Stock Management: Manage inventory levels, procure items, and ensure availability of spare accessories and consumables.
•Onboarding: Assist new employees with IT asset delivery, setup, and training on Schneider Electric systems and tools.
•Event Support: Provide technical support for special events, including setup and troubleshooting of audio/visual equipment.

•High level of end-user focus and customer service orientation.
