Job description, responsibilities and duties
Help us grow, improve sales, and make better decisions through data.
At MultiSport, we are looking for someone who can connect data with real business impact, helping us respond faster to market changes, improve sales decision-making, and increase the value of our portfolio while enhancing client satisfaction.
This is not a reporting-only role. We are looking for a true business partner who understands both data and business, and can turn insights into concrete actions that drive results.
What will you do:
Turn      client, portfolio, and sales data into clear, actionable recommendations      for Sales and management.
Identify      growth opportunities (upsell within existing clients, new segments).
Help      increase the value of our client portfolio and improve sales efficiency.
Analyze      client behavior and help prevent churn.
Identify      process gaps (CRM, sales cycle) and propose improvements.
Collaborate      on the development of CRM and data solutions.
Ensure      high data quality for reliable decision-making.
Support      the Sales team with fast, on-demand analyses.
Who are we looking for:
Strong      analytical thinking combined with solid business understanding.
Ability      to translate numbers into simple, actionable insights.
Proactive      mindset – you bring ideas, not just execute tasks.
2      years experience with data tools (Power BI, SQL, CRM/ERP).
Motivation      to create real business impact, not just build reports.
What we offer:
Direct      impact on Sales strategy and company results.
Close      collaboration with the Sales Director and leadership team.
Opportunity      to shape how we work with data locally.
Fast-paced,      dynamic environment with minimal bureaucracy.
Room      to grow from reporting to strategic decision-making.
