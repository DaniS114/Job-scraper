Role Overview
We are seeking a professional to be driving the
deployment of AI Act compliance and digital ethics assessment frameworks
across our organization. This role will bridge legal, technical, and governance domains—ensuring our AI and data-driven systems meet regulatory requirements under the
EU AI Act
, while embedding
digital ethics principles
into our AI use-cases, operations, and governance structures.
The AI Act & Digital Ethics Assessment Deployment Lead will coordinate across compliance, legal, technical, and business teams to establish AI ACT complient governance processes and risk management for
responsible AI adoption at scale
.
Key Responsibilities:
AI Act Readiness & Compliance
Drive

(e.g., Digital Ethical Assessment) to all Ai use-cases.
Identify risks
,
implement mitigation strategies
in dedicated areas, help in providing documentation for regulatory bodies and ensuring the t
rustworthy and ethical development and deployment of AI
in line with the EU AI Act's requirements, preventing potential non-compliance fines.
Provide status

for all AI use-cases in development & already deployed.
Digital Ethics Operationalization
Develop, deploy, and maintain
AI system risk classification frameworks
(Minimal, Limited, High-risk, Prohibited categories).
Create and keep on
methodology
in dedicated areas.
Translate ethical principles
(fairness, transparency, accountability, human oversight, inclusivity, sustainability) into actionable assessment criteria.
Ensure
ethics-by-design practices
are applied throughout AI/ML development lifecycles.
Partner with
product and engineering teams
to embed responsible AI practices into workflows and tooling.
Stakeholder
engagement & reporting
.
Educate executives, regulators, and staff
on AI Act compliance obligations and digital ethics impacts.
