Responsibilities
Designing and developing proof of concepts (PoCs) and demonstrating the solution to internal customers on a tight schedule
Utilize GenAI no code, low code, and SDKs to build robust GenAI agents that automate business processes to build PoC
Take GenAI and Agentic AI solutions from concept to Proof-of-Value (PoV) and work with other team members to scale the PoV to production
Develop documentation for each development, including testing and validation procedures/plans
Embed/integrate GenAI agents (Copilot agents) into business platforms such as Workday, Teams, etc.
Work on small to medium-sized projects with minimal supervision
Apply Agile methodology to ensure efficient project delivery
Support the AI CoE in its user engagement and AI Toolkit adoption mission
Requirements
Bachelor’s or master’s degree in AI, computer science, Engineering, mathematics or a related field
2-4 years of experience in developing and deploying Agentic solutions to production
Hands-on experience developing solutions using no code/low code platforms and SDKs
Must be proficient in Microsoft Azure GenAI platforms (AI Foundry, Copilot Studio), Power Platform
Experience applying advanced AI concept such as MCP servers for connecting AI Agents to tools
Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail
Strong communication and collaboration skills
Nice-to-have
Preferred Skills: Automation of complex processes using GenAI agents, especially using the Azure Foundry, Copilot Studio, Power Automate and other low code/no code platforms, Advanced programming with Python, SQL, Hands-on experience with at least one of the following data storage systems: Snowflake, Azure Fabric (One Lake), and PostgreSQL, Experience building sites with SharePoint building automation workflows with Power Automate platform
