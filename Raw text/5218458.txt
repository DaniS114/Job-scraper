Job description, responsibilities and duties
Senior Pricing Python Developer
Are you a motivated, hands-on professional with a passion for driving innovation and supporting team success?
Are you interested in solving complex business challenges while mentoring others and managing key stakeholder relationships?
Then this job is something for you!
We are looking for a Senior Pricing Python Developer to join the Zurich Integrated Benefits (ZIB) team—an entrepreneurially minded global team managing employee benefit platforms and providing technical expertise for corporate life insurance across multiple markets.
As a Senior Pricing Python Developer, your main responsibilities will involve:
Drive initiatives:
Proactively identify opportunities, take ownership of projects from conception to completion, and make decisions that move the team forward.
Manage stakeholder relationships:
Act as a key technical contact for actuaries and business stakeholders across multiple markets, translating their needs into actionable technical requirements.
Support team coordination:
Help with planning, coordinate workstreams, mentor junior developers, and support task prioritization for smooth project delivery.
Provide technical guidance:
Guide team members through complex challenges, conduct code reviews, and ensure best practices are followed.
Design and architect solutions:
Transform legacy pricing tools into modern pricing APIs built with Python and hosted on Databricks.
Create innovative features:
Build brand new pricing calculation engines addressing emerging business needs.
Optimize and monitor:
Improve existing pricing calculations for performance and set up automated health monitoring checks and reports.

Brief description of the company
Zurich is a leading multi-line insurer that serves its customers in global and local markets. With about 60,000 employees it provides a wide range of property and casualty, life insurance products and services in more than 200 countries and territories. Zurich’s customers include individuals, small businesses, and mid-sized and large companies, as well as multinational corporations.
Zurich Slovakia is a Competence Center based in Bratislava and Kosice providing highly specialized services for Zurich's headquarter in Switzerland and all Zurich Business units around the globe. Our services are focusing on all types of actuarial services – Life and Non-life, pricing and reserving, on data science and engineering, risk management, business intelligence, information technology, software asset management as well as insurance network management and technical accounting. Our Competence Center employs around 400+ staff from 20+ different countries.
