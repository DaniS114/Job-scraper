Céginformáció
Vodafone Shared Services Budapest (VSSB) is the international service center of the Vodafone Group, operating in Hungary since 2007. The company plays a key role in supporting Vodafone's global operations across multiple areas, such as finance, IT, HR, procurement, and business analysis.
The Budapest center is home to a diverse, multinational team, representing more than 50 nations, and offers an innovative, flexible, and development-focused working environment. VSSB's goal is to create value for Vodafone's global business activities while providing excellent career opportunities for talented young professionals.
