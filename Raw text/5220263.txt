Hardware Asset Management Engineer
What can you expect?
As a Hardware Asset Management Engineer, you will be globally responsible for the optimization and technical implementation of our asset processes inside ServiceNow. You will work closely with internal teams and external service providers, drive initiatives forward, implement new functions and ensure that our hardware assets are managed efficiently and in compliance with standards. You will also oversee and manage the IT Hardware lifecycle and coordinate model upgrades.
Adient (NYSE: ADNT) is a global leader in automotive seating. With more than 65,000 employees in 29 countries, Adient operates ~200 manufacturing/assembly plants worldwide. We produce and deliver automotive seating for all major OEMs. From complete seating systems to individual components, our expertise spans every step of the automotive seat-making process. We take our products from research and design to engineering and manufacturing — and into millions of vehicles every year. For more information, please visit www.adient.com
At Adient, we are proud to offer hybrid working to our employees, which involves being able to work remotely for up to 3 days per week based on the agreement within specific department.
What will your responsibilities be?
•	Implement and maintain ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management functionalities.
•	Oversee and manage Hardware Asset lifecycle
•	Perform model normalization and other technical configurations within the HAM module.
•	Support contract management for leasing agreements.
•	Contribute to the hardware refresh process, ensuring timely execution.
•	Coordinate small-scale projects and collaborate with external service providers.
•	Communicate and collaborate effectively with global teams and internal stakeholders.
•	Proactively identify improvement opportunities and implement solutions independently.
Which qualifications are we looking for in potential employees?
•	Strong expertise in ServiceNow Hardware Asset Management.
•	Experience with model normalization and technical HAM functions.
•	Knowledge of contract management for leasing.
•	Familiarity with the hardware refresh process.
•	Certifications in Hardware Asset Management or ServiceNow.
•	Experience working with external service providers.
•	Experience coordinating small projects.
•	Highly motivated and proactive in driving initiatives forward.
•	Ability to work independently with strong problem-solving skills.
•	Excellent communication and collaboration skills in a global environment.

Brief description of the company
Adient is the leading automotive seating supplier.
With around 2500 employees and five locations in Slovakia, the company is one of the key employers in Slovakia’s automotive industry. We are committed to Sustainability and are proud ambassador of Diversity Charter in Slovakia since 2017.
Adient’s only business center worldwide is in Bratislava. Approximately 650 employees in Bratislava support the company’s business around the globe in finance, information technology, purchasing, sales, human resources, transport and logistics and project management.
In Trencin, Adient operates the biggest automotive technology center across Slovakia. More than 400 employees there develop a wide range of highly innovative products for the cars of tomorrow.
In Zilina, the company produces complete seat systems and automotive fabrics, in Lucenec foam components for seats as well as metal components for head restraints.
