About us
At evosoft – a trusted partner of Siemens – we don’t just write code, we create solutions that make life better. Our software powers Smart Industries, System Tests, IT Solutions, Digitalization and Medical Innovations, helping industries move forward and shaping the future of technology.
Our culture is defined by our values: you’ll join a team where innovative thinking, collaboration and continuous learning shape how we develop solutions that improve everyday life across industries.
If you’re looking for a place where your ideas matter and your work makes a real difference, this is the right opportunity for you.
Tasks
Project specific intro:
Our main activity is testing and developing the Siemens S7-1500 PLC system. The S7-1500 product family, being one of Siemens' most innovative developments. Testing from basic functions to system diagnostics, various industrial bus systems (PROFIBUS, PROFINET, AS-i), and even OPC UA, there are numerous interesting and challenging tasks that await our colleagues every day.
You’ll make an impact by:
Take ownership of system‑level testing of Siemens SIMATIC S7 PLC engineering systems, including unreleased products
Plan, execute, and evaluate manual and automated tests for complex industrial automation scenarios
Independently design and optimize hardware and system test configurations
Define, review, and continuously improve test strategies, test plans, and test cases
Perform in‑depth defect analysis, ensure high‑quality documentation, and actively drive resolution
Actively contribute to the development of PLC software, test frameworks, and test tools
Support and improve test automation solutions, CI/CD pipelines, and testing best practices
Share knowledge and mentor junior colleagues within the team
Expectations
University degree in electrical engineering, software engineering, mechatronics, or a related technical field
Strong hands‑on experience in industrial automation and testing complex systems
Solid and proven SIMATIC S7 PLC programming experience (STL / SCL / FBD / LAD)
Good understanding industrial communication standards (OPC UA, TCP/IP, PROFINET)
Experience with Windows‑based test environments and system configurations
4+ years of experience in a similar system test / automation role
Strong analytical, problem‑solving mindset and high level of autonomy and responsibility
Confident English communication skills (German is an advantage) and proactive attitude toward continuous improvement
Advantages
Deep knowledge of TIA Portal and SIMATIC S7‑1200 / 1500 ecosystems
Experience with test automation, CI/CD pipelines (e.g. Jenkins)
Programming skills in C# and/or Python
ISTQB certification or experience with industrial communication analysis (e.g. Wireshark)
