Responsibilities
We are currently seeking a skilled and experienced Dynamics 365 CRM Consultant to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will be tasked with implementing, customizing, and optimizing our Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM solutions to meet the unique needs of our organization. Your expertise will be instrumental in ensuring the seamless integration and functionality of our CRM system within our ecosystem.
This includes:
Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and analyze business requirements for CRM solutions
Design and implement Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM (on-premises) solutions, including customizations and configurations
Provide technical expertise and support during the implementation of CRM projects
Perform system testing, identify and resolve issues, and ensure successful project delivery
Customize and configure forms, workflows, business rules, business process flows, and other CRM components to enhance system functionality
Support Power BI dashboard designs creation and configure SSRS reports
Conduct training sessions for end-users and create documentation as needed
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration with other systems
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
Proven experience as an MS CRM Dynamics 365 Consultant or in a comparable role
Comprehensive proficiency in Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM on-premises, encompassing customization and configuration
Robust comprehension of CRM processes and industry best practices
Expertise in CRM solution design and implementation
Analyse and understand business requirements and provide an optimal solution within the CRM environment
Skilled in system testing and troubleshooting

Company info
As one of Europe's leading specialists for alternative investments, we manage more than €15 billion in assets. Our focus lies on Private Equity, Private Debt, Infrastructure, Secondaries, and Impact Investing.
Our modern corporate culture is defined by openness, dynamism, and international diversity – at Golding, over 200 colleagues from 24 countries work together across our offices in Munich, Luxembourg, Milan, Tokyo, and Zurich.
Our Employer Value Proposition – “Driven by Impact. United as One.” – reflects our firm belief that lasting impact can only be achieved through close collaboration and long-term partnerships.
Diversity is an essential part of our culture. We explicitly welcome applications from people of all genders, backgrounds, and perspectives.
