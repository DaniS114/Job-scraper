Operational Readiness Manager
Job Description
Helps to maintain seamless communication and quote to cash process flows between Order Fulfillment (OF) and other departments such as IT, TS, Sales, Sales Support, Renewals, Products etc.
Project coordinator for Order Fulfillment, working closely with the OF management team
Managing projects such as task automation, coordination of support tickets with IT etc.
Provides regular, structured updates to OF Management on project status, operational issues, risks and opportunities that may impact OF processes.
Prepares reports, presentations and dashboards to keep OF management informed and enable data-drive decision-making
Acts as trusted-advisor to the OF management team to proactively identify potential challenges and proposing solutions
Supports Order Fulfillment internal users to review Order & Fulfillment Plans
Reviews the impact of operational impact on new or changing product introductions and helps to identify risks, required documentation (updates) and communication/training required for the teams in coordination with OF management
Reviews that new products do not break existing workflows, fulfillment or billing requirements
Designs and executes operational testing plans
Runs operational UATs (User Acceptance Tests) including but not exclusive to product launch UAT
Ensures OF cross-functional readiness for systems, training, SOPs
Creates or updates SOPs and training materials for operational teams
Focused entirely on
"Is the org ready?”
Validates that
people, processes, and systems
are aligned before/during/after a product launch
Collaborates with product managers to influence design decisions that affect Operations
Owns post-launch monitoring for operational issues or process gaps
