Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a Senior Automation Tester to join our team. The role focuses on complex test automation with a strong emphasis on API testing in a cloud-native, DevSecOps environment.
You will become part of an established project team and work on a large-scale system integrating multiple external and internal information systems. The position requires a high level of seniority, independence, and responsibility for overall test quality.
As an Automation Tester with us you will:
Design, implement, and maintain automated tests (API, E2E, UI).
Focus heavily on complex API automation testing.
Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines within a DevSecOps environment.
Apply BDD principles using Gherkin-based test scenarios.
Perform integration testing across multiple interconnected information systems.
Test cloud-native applications running in Kubernetes environments.
Collaborate closely with developers, architects, and QA team members.
Ensure high quality, stability, performance, and security of delivered solutions.

Personality requirements and skills
You should have:
5+ years of experience in test automation.
Strong experience with API test automation using tools such as RestAssured and Postman.
Hands-on experience with Java-based automation frameworks and modern UI automation
tools (e.g. Playwright).
Experience with BDD tools and approaches (Cucumber, Gherkin).
Knowledge of performance and load testing tools (e.g. JMeter).
Experience with CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD) in DevSecOps environments.
Hands-on experience with containerized and cloud-native systems (Docker, Kubernetes).
Understanding of Agile methodologies (Scrum), BDD, and TDD principles.
Knowledge of security testing principles, GDPR, and security-related methodologies.
Ability to work independently, take ownership, and contribute at senior level.
Strong analytical thinking and communication skills.
English level B2 or higher (written and spoken).
