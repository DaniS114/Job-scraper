Responsibilities
INNOVATION IS IMAGINING WHAT NO ONE ELSE CAN.
Artificial Intelligence and machine learning are at the cutting edge when it comes to shaping next-gen mobility in areas like automated driving. But it takes real intellectual leadership and expertise to identify and then harness the AI trends and technologies that will transform the way people travel for good.
Requirements
What should you bring along?
University degree (Master's or PhD) in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Mathematics, Physics or comparable field.
At least 5 years of practical experience in software development with Python.
At least 3 years of experience in implementing and industrializing AI solutions, including at least one major open-source framework (e.g. LangChain, Pytorch).
Strong methodological expertise in at least two of the following areas: Generative AI, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, MLOps or platform development.
Hands-on experience in data engineering, including building and managing database systems and data pipelines.
Practical experience in cloud and infrastructure engineering using AWS or Azure, as well as GitOps practices in production environments.
Strong proficiency in software development best practices such as clean code principles, object-oriented programming, DevOps and testing.
Are you motivated to take technical responsibility for cross-functional AI initiatives and drive innovative technologies across various business areas? Apply now!
