Job description, responsibilities and duties
For our client in the finance sector, we’re looking for an experienced Expert Lead who combines strong hands‑on data engineering and analytics skills with proven team leadership. You’ll guide a high‑performing data team, deliver scalable data solutions, and drive continuous improvement across analytics, reporting, and data architecture.
Key Responsibilities
- Lead, coach, and develop a team of 10+ data experts, promoting autonomy, accountability, and professional growth.
- Manage the local delivery roadmap and ensure alignment with strategic priorities.
- Oversee end‑to‑end delivery of data models, pipelines, dashboards, and BI solutions.
- Design scalable, governed data architectures and ensure standards, documentation, and quality.
- Conduct impact assessments and coordinate cross‑functional collaboration for complex initiatives.
- Drive adoption of new data capabilities including advanced analytics and GenAI use cases.
- Promote Agile ways of working (stand‑ups, refinements, planning).
- Manage hiring, development, performance processes, and overall team engagement.
- Understand and support key data flows related to client reporting, analytics, and financial insights.
