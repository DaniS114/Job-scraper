The Knowledgebase Technical Writer III is responsible for creating, editing, and reviewing content for the ESET support knowledgebase (support.eset.com), as well as developing internal and external support materials as needed.
To be successful in this role, the employee must possess a high-level understanding of all core ESET products from a user experience perspective, encompassing the entire lifecycle from acquisition to adoption.
This expertise enables the creation and review of technical documentation tailored to the primary ESET customer personas.
Functional Responsibilities and Duties:
Create and edit knowledgebase content and deliverables from other teams to ensure high-quality English content that adheres to ESET and Knowledgebase standards, and serves also as the main source of knowledge for the AI support chatbot
Ensure adherence to the ESET style guide, manage workload and tasks, and oversee content quality and standards
Test and recommend improvements to internal systems, tools, and processes
Research and promote new ideas for online self-service, technical support services, and product improvements
Work with HQ participants and global partners and serve as the team expert point of contact, proactively revising/editing content, and report bugs, issues, and user experience reports to responsible teams (development, product management, etc.)
Train and mentor less experienced colleagues
Organize relevant internal and external input and information to classify new content correctly within the established
Knowledgebase categorization hierarchy and recommend new or updated information architecture as necessary
Collaborate with colleagues to ensure that technical content is discoverable, usable, and informative for the end-user

O ESETe
Spoločnosť ESET je globálnym lídrom v oblasti digitálnej bezpečnosti a je jedným z najväčších dodávateľov bezpečnostných riešení v Európskej únii. Zároveň je najväčšou a najoceňovanejšou IT firmou na Slovensku.
Už viac ako 30 rokov vyvíjame popredný softvér a služby zamerané na IT bezpečnosť a ochranu podnikov, kritickej infraštruktúry a domácností z celého sveta
Neustále hľadáme nové kolegyne alebo nových kolegov, ktorí chcú byť súčasťou úspešného slovenského príbehu s globálnym dopadom. V ESET neustále dokazujeme, že špičková expertíza a inovácie vytvorené na Slovensku majú silu konkurovať najväčším hráčom v oblasti kyberbezpečnosti po celom svete. TEBA.
