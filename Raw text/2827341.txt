Responsibilities
Lead the end-to-end design process—from early discovery and ideation to high-fidelity visuals, prototyping, and developer handoff
Collaborate closely with fellow designers and cross-functional stakeholders to deliver cohesive and impactful experiences
Partner with Product stakeholders to ensure design work aligns with strategic goals and meets user needs
Advocate for design excellence, accessibility, and ethical AI practices across all touchpoints
Mentor junior designers and contribute to the evolution our team culture
Requirements
5+ years of professional experience in product design, preferably in B2B or enterprise SaaS environments
Strong foundation in UX principles, interaction design, and visual design, with a portfolio showcasing end-to-end product work
Proficiency in Figma
Fluent in English
Nice-to-have
A proactive mindset: you are comfortable making decisions, defending them with clarity, and owning outcomes, A strong belief in user-centered design and empathy for diverse user needs, A collaborative spirit: happy to work as part of a team, A forward-thinking perspective you are excited by the potential of AI to transform user experiences, Familiarity with integrating generative AI into product design

Company info
Diligent is the AI leader in governance, risk and compliance (GRC) SaaS solutions, helping more than 1 million users and 700,000 board members to clarify risk and elevate governance. The Diligent One Platform gives practitioners, the C-Suite and the board a consolidated view of their entire GRC practice so they can more effectively manage risk, build greater resilience and make better decisions, faster.
