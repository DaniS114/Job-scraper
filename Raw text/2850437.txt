Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, develop, and maintain scalable fullstack applications using .NET (preferably .NET 9) and Angular.
Integrate and manage messaging systems such as Kafka and RabbitMQ.
Deploy and manage applications using Docker and Kubernetes in AWS environments.
Work with various databases including PostgreSQL, MSSQL, and Couchbase.
Implement search capabilities using OpenSearch or Elasticsearch.
Write and maintain frontend code using TypeScript and ensure quality with Playwright or Cypress.
Collaborate closely with team members and product owners in an agile environment ensuring that the organization’s business goals are met.
Utilize AI tools and services to enhance development workflows and productivity.
Requirements
Extensive experience with .NET technologies, preferably .NET 9.
Strong proficiency in Angular and TypeScript.
Hands-on experience with Kafka, RabbitMQ, Docker, and Kubernetes.
Proficient in working with AWS cloud services.
Deep understanding of relational and NoSQL databases including PostgreSQL, MSSQL, and Couchbase.
Experience with search technologies like OpenSearch or Elasticsearch.
Familiarity with frontend testing frameworks such as Playwright or Cypress.
Proven experience working in agile development environments.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
Nice-to-have
Experience designing and negotiating technical solutions across teams., Ability to design and implement end-to-end solutions across the full technology stack., Exposure to and use of AI tools and services to support software development.
