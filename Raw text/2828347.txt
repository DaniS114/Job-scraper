Company info
KOSTAL Group is a global, independent, family-owned company, headquartered in Germany. We develop and manufacture technologically complex electronic and mechatronic products for automotive and other industries. KOSTAL employs more than 20.000 people at 46 locations, offering flexible, knowledgeable and customer-focused service. Corporate HR functions based in Budapest, such as Compensation & Benefits, Corporate Learning, HR Master Data and Talent & Learning Technology, define group-wide standards, establish global HR processes, and own the HRIS technology.
