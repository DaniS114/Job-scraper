Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced Senior Data Analyst with a minimum of 5 years of professional experience to join our team. This is a client-facing role focused primarily on delivering advanced data analytics solutions for customers, operating in the Unified Communications and Collaboration (UCC) domain, with a strong emphasis on Microsoft Teams environments.
The ideal candidate combines strong analytical thinking with hands-on technical expertise across the Microsoft data ecosystem, including Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, and Azure-based data services. Beyond building reports, this role requires ownership of the full data lifecycle — from ingestion and transformation to automation, monitoring, and optimization.
You will design new analytical solutions, enhance and maintain existing ones, and actively contribute to shaping innovative, automated data platforms that deliver actionable insights to clients. Eventually internal reporting will as a secondary priority.
Primary Responsibilities
Design,      develop, and maintain advanced analytics solutions using Power BI Desktop      and Power BI Service (cloud).
Work      with Microsoft Fabric to build scalable data architectures and analytics      platforms.
​Implement end-to-end data solutions including data ingestion, data cleansing and transformation, data modeling, scheduled refresh and automation, alerting and monitoring.
Utilize      SQL, Microsoft Fabric, Azure Data Factory, Logic Apps and/or Power      Automate for data orchestration and automation.
Develop      new reporting and analytics solutions while providing support and      optimization for existing ones.
Act as      a client-facing consultant — gathering requirements, presenting insights,      and explaining complex data in a clear and business-friendly manner.
Interpret      complex datasets and translate them into actionable insights.
Drive      automation-first thinking — aiming for fully automated and scalable data      pipelines
Stay      up to date with the latest developments in Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, and      AI-driven analytics.
Develop      an understanding of underlying UCC technologies (especially Microsoft      Teams environments) to ensure meaningful data interpretation.
Collaborate      with operational support and professional services teams to deliver      integrated solutions.
