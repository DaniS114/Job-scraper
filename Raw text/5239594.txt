Job description, responsibilities and duties
Join a globally respected technology organization delivering mission-critical digital banking platforms used by leading financial institutions across Europe, Africa, and Asia.
This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the next generation of cloud-native, AI-powered core banking systems while working in a stable, long-term environment with exceptionally low employee turnover. If you are a Java developer who wants to combine modern microservices architecture with real-world, business-critical impact, this role offers both challenge and long-term growth. Become part of a dynamic, globally connected team operating across Europe and Asia.
Role Overview:
We are seeking an experienced
Java Developer
to contribute to the evolution of a next-generation core banking platform. The role initially combines development (50%) and production support (50%), gradually shifting toward full development within a modern cloud-native architecture.
You will collaborate with distributed teams across Europe and Asia, reporting directly to an international manager. This position offers exposure to both legacy enterprise systems and modern microservices-based architecture, including AI-driven banking solutions.

Contribute to system modernization and architectural improvements
Ensure high code quality through adherence to development standards
Participate in onboarding and structured training for next-generation platform development
Support database-related development tasks (Oracle and MySQL)
Assist in technical documentation and knowledge sharing
