Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about developing integration solutions and driving innovation in the IT landscape? Join our dynamic Integration Competence Center team and play a pivotal role in the development of cutting-edge integration solutions that enable seamless customer onboarding and process optimization.
About the Role:
As a member of our Integration Competence Center, you will work on creating and maintaining robust integration solutions for our company. You'll be responsible for the planning, development, and support of B2B system interfaces, ensuring smooth data exchange across our IT systems. From standardized interfaces to custom-tailored solutions, you’ll contribute to the end-to-end process—from requirements analysis to technical implementation.
Key Responsibilities:
Integration Solution Development:
Analyze, design, and implement system interfaces for B2B business solutions, supporting protocols like HTTP, FTP, and AS2, and formats such as CSV, XML, JSON, EDIFACT, and VDA.
Support & Maintenance:
Provide ongoing maintenance and support for existing EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) implementations, ensuring they meet the company’s evolving needs.
Requirements Analysis:
Collaborate closely with business analysts and sales teams to analyze project requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
EDI Workflow Design:
Develop technical concepts for EDI workflows that align with business processes and enhance operational efficiency.
Collaboration with IT:
Work alongside the IT department to ensure the technical feasibility of integration solutions and establish transport protocols and data formats.
