Our Water Line Integrity Solutions business unit focuses on developing advanced technologies for checking and ensuring the integrity of water pipe networks. In doing so, we contribute to the better use of limited water resources, supporting sustainability and environmental protection. Our state-of-the-art inspection devices autonomously move through water pipelines, gathering extensive data, including video and ultrasound data. To evaluate this data, we develop cloud-based software solutions that utilize classic signal processing algorithms and artificial intelligence (AI). In our interdisciplinary, agile, and autonomous teams, we foster innovations that create both business and ecological value.
