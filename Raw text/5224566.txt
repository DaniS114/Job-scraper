Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Your tasks:
• Creating new test scenarios based on technical and business requirements for manual testing and executing them
• Documenting the testing status of individual test cases
• Creating test data
• Defining/reporting identified defects
• Supporting the client/developers in resolving identified defects
• Supporting clients in using the testing environment
• Getting familiar with existing automated tests (including running/monitoring them if needed)
• Cooperation with colleagues from Slovakia and Germany
Requirements:
• German at a communicative level (the interview will be conducted in German)
• Attention to detail
• Results-oriented mindset (proactive problem-solving)
• Good communication skills/experience
• Teamwork – interpreting team results and collaborating on task completion
• Knowledge of software testing, test analysis, and test design is an advantage (school, courses, practical experience)
• Basic overview of programming languages
• Knowledge of the software development lifecycle
• Willingness to learn and gradually gain skills in automated testing
• Knowledge of tools for tracking and executing test scenarios is an advantage
• Knowledge of agile development (SCRUM, LEAN, KANBAN) is an advantage
