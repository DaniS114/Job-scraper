Company info
At montamo, we have a clear mission:
to revolutionize the skilled trades and permanently overcome the shortage of qualified professionals — all while driving one of the most important transformations of our time: the transition to 100% renewable energy.
We don't just participate in the energy transition — we actively drive it forward. Through innovative processes, bold ideas, and a strong commitment to setting the standards of tomorrow.
We believe that craftsmanship and high-tech are a perfect match — and that real change only happens when people take ownership, dare to try new things, and stand together as a team.
