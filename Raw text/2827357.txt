Responsibilities
Assist in developing and implementing a comprehensive learning and development strategy that aligns with BlackRock's business goals and objectives
Support learning needs for various BlackRock Academies
Support the intake, design, development, and delivery of global and regional training courses, ensuring they meet the needs of the business and our employees
Shaping the curriculum of globally required training courses, including Compliance, Information Security and HR courses
Collaborate with key stakeholders to identify learning needs and ensure the effective implementation of learning initiatives
Partner with HR Technology Platform team to drive the adoption of innovative learning technologies and methodologies to enhance the learning experience and improve outcomes
Provide support on Learning Experience Platform for learning content/pathways management
Partner with various team members to establish metrics and evaluation processes to measure the effectiveness of learning programs and continuously improve their impact
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Education, Business, Human Resources, or a related field
Graduate degree preferred
Minimum of 3 years of experience in learning and development, with proven track record of program/project management with a high degree of detail orientation
Learning design and program management skills
Strong familiarity with learning content authoring tools and LMS/LXP with a good understanding of AI capabilities in this market
Strong strategic thinking and planning abilities
Strong stakeholder management skills
Strong project and time management skills
Ability to drive innovation and implement new learning technologies
Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
Ability to work collaboratively across teams and regions
Superior skills in Microsoft suite including proficiency knowledge in Excel and PowerPoint
Desire to leverage AI in day-to-day practice
Relevant certifications in learning and development, such as CPLP, PMP, or similar, are welcome

Company info
At BlackRock, we are all connected by one mission: to help more and more people experience financial well-being. Our clients, and the people they serve, are saving for retirement, paying for their children’s educations, buying homes and starting businesses. Their investments also help to strengthen the global economy: support businesses small and large; finance infrastructure projects that connect and power cities; and facilitate innovations that drive progress.
This mission would not be possible without our smartest investment – the one we make in our employees. It’s why we’re dedicated to creating an environment where our colleagues feel welcomed, valued and supported with networks, benefits and development opportunities to help them thrive.
