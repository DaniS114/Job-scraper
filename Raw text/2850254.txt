Responsibilities
In this role you will own and shape our accounting and payments-related products, ensuring they solve the right business problems and deliver measurable value. You will work within the Product organisation, with a strong focus on product strategy, prioritisation and cross-functional alignment, supported by business analysis rather than deep technical system design.
Own the product vision and roadmap for accounting and payments-related products, aligning it with company strategy, regulatory needs and operational priorities.
Lead product discovery to understand customer and business problems, payment and accounting flows, and opportunities to simplify, automate and scale across products and operations.
Work closely with stakeholders across Finance, Operations, Risk, Compliance and Engineering to align on priorities, scope and expected outcomes.
Define clear product goals and success metrics, and ensure initiatives are driven by measurable business impact.
Translate business and customer needs into clear product direction, including well-defined epics, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Continuously prioritise the product backlog, balancing value, risk, dependencies and delivery capacity.
Partner with engineering and design to break down complex initiatives into MVPs and iterative releases that deliver value early.
Drive alignment and clarity across teams by explaining the business context, product intent and trade-offs behind decisions.
Assess impact and feasibility of proposed changes, particularly those affecting payments, accounting and ledgers, in collaboration with engineering and domain experts.
Support delivery by leading planning and backlog refinement, ensuring teams are focused on the most valuable outcomes.
Validate delivered functionality against intended business behaviour and product goals, and use feedback and data to inform next steps.
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics or a related field.
4+ years of experience as a Product Manager, Product Owner or in a closely related product role within a software or financial services environment.
Proven experience owning and evolving products that involve complex business processes, ideally in fintech, payments or accounting domains.
Strong ability to define product strategy, prioritise effectively and communicate a clear product vision to diverse stakeholders.
Experience working in Agile environments and collaborating closely with engineers, designers and analysts throughout the product lifecycle.
Ability to translate complex business and regulatory needs into clear, actionable product requirements.
Strong analytical skills, comfortable using data, reports and KPIs to inform decisions and measure success.
Experience using product and delivery tools such as Jira or Azure DevOps.
Experience defining, tracking and iterating on product KPIs in a product-led organisation.
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, able to align different perspectives and drive decisions.
Fluency in English. German language skills are a strong plus, as many stakeholders are German-speaking.
Nice to have
Fluency in one of the following languages: German, Dutch, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish.
Experience in payments, finance, accounting or BNPL, for example working with reconciliations, settlements, ledgers or booking logic.
