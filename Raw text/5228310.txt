Job description, responsibilities and duties
The IT Business Analyst is a member of the Center of Excellence for HR at Siemens Healthineers IT. The CoE’s prime goal is to support the global HR organization with their IT related demands by providing IT solutions and managing & continuously improving global IT services.
Your tasks and responsibilities:
As an IT Business Analyst you are responsible for the analysis of business process demands and requirement engineering
You partner with business process stakeholders as subject matter expert to implement our label printing strategy in the discrete manufacturing process globally
Translate business demands into IT service and requirement specifications, ensuring strong and smooth collaboration between business and service management teams
Oversee implementation of IT solutions that follow IT service management and compliance frameworks.
Take a bigger picture perspective of end-to-end processes, identifying cross-functional dependencies.
Collaborate closely with internal stakeholders and external service providers, driving changes from start to finish.
Required Knowledge/Skills, Education, and Experience:
Bachelor or Master degree in Computer Science/Engineering and/or Business Management
2-5 years of experiences in Business Process Analysis
Passion for digital technology, cloud solutions, and emerging trends like AI
Ability to translate business challenges into innovative, value-driven IT solutions
Strong analytical skills, Critical thinking, attention to detail, and commitment to quality under pressure
Proactive, collaborative team player who takes ownership and drives tasks to completion
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with a strong service mindset.
Structured, proactive, and self-directed work style
Fluent in English (C-Level) and German (at least B-Level)
Preferred Knowledge/Skills, Education, and Experience:
Comfortable working across cultures and organizational levels where international experience a plus
Experience in Healthcare industry business processes
Experience in the SCM process area
Experience in an agile process environment and Microsoft Azure DevOps
Availability for occasional international business travel
Project Management Professional certification is a plus
