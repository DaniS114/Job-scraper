Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the Role
As a Full Stack Developer in our IT Tax Team, you'll collaborate closely with our Technical Lead to maintain business-critical applications while designing and developing new components. You'll play an instrumental role in our quarterly closing process, combining architectural design, business software development, and third-level operational support to ensure our tax reporting systems operate flawlessly.
Key Responsibilities
•	Design, develop and maintain code in our Group Tax environment
•	Actively contribute to project delivery activities, focusing on engineering excellence to meet project timelines and objectives
•	Provide essential technical support during financial closing activities, ensuring all technical requirements are met efficiently
•	Conduct comprehensive code reviews to uphold code quality, consistency with coding standards, and maintainability across the project
•	Utilize your extensive experience and technical expertise to identify and resolve critical issues promptly, preventing recurrence and ensuring system stability
•	Continuously identify and implement process improvements in engineering support to enhance efficiency and effectiveness
About the Team
You'll be joining our international Digital and Technology team, Tax Solutions that partners closely with our Group Tax colleagues based in various countries globally. Our team plays a crucial role in supporting tax professionals by developing and maintaining the solutions they need to fulfil their obligations with regard to Group IFRS reporting, Local Statutory reporting and many other indirect tax reporting requirements. We bridge the gap between complex tax requirements and efficient technical implementations, while supporting strategic projects and crucially, ensuring stability during our critical financial closing activities.
We value a curious and continuous improvement mindset, appreciate pro-activity and applicants that also provide a critical eye on existing solutions and challenge the status quo.
