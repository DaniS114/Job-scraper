Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a skilled
UiPath Cloud Engineer
to join our sister company, Millennium. In this role, you will be a key technical contributor to our transition from on-prem Orchestrator to
UiPath Automation Cloud
and help build the next generation of AI-powered automations.
You will focus on the technical setup, maintenance, and optimization of a secure and efficient cloud platform while supporting our development team with cloud-first best practices. This is a
hands-on role
perfect for an engineer who enjoys technical problem-solving and high-quality delivery.
Your mission:
Execute the Migration:
Support the technical transition from on-prem Orchestrator to Automation Cloud – including environment preparation, testing, and production cutover.
Platform Configuration:
Set up and manage the tenant and access models (folders, accounts, RBAC, SSO) ensuring strong security and least-privilege principles.
Observability & Health:
Build and maintain monitoring dashboards using UiPath Insights and Orchestrator to track SLOs, alerts, and performance reporting.
DevOps Integration:
Implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated package deployment and configuration management (Git-based).
AI Implementation:
Develop and deploy AI Agents using UiPath Agent Builder, ensuring alignment with AI Trust Layer governance and traceability.
Connectivity:
Integrate diverse systems using UiPath Integration Service and manage secure connection credentials.
Technical Mentoring:
Act as a go-to expert for developers on cloud-native patterns, such as queue-centric design, long-running workflows, and human-in-the-loop processes.
Employee perks, benefits
Participation in high-impact automation and AI projects with enterprise clients.
Opportunity to shape the UiPath Cloud & AI automation landscape within Soitron Group.
Work in a modern environment that combines the stability of a large IT integrator with the agility of a family-oriented culture.
Motivating financial package based on experience and expertise.
