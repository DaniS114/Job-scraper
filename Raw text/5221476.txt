Job description, responsibilities and duties
As the SAP Basis Delivery Lead, you will be at the forefront of managing and enhancing our client's SAP environment, overseeing critical functions such as Service Level Management, Capacity Management, and SAP projects. Your expertise will drive excellence and reliability in our SAP operations.
You will be responsible for:
• Service Level Management, Capacity Management (perform capacity checks and provide trend analysis as required)
• Availability Management (Regular checks, availability reports and investigation of reported outages on regular basis)
• Problem Management, Incident Management and Escalation (act as an escalation engineer, own the Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and the technical solution)
• Perform manual and automated monitoring, maintenance with or without 3rd party tools
• Troubleshooting and resolution of incidents using preferred ticketing tool of the client's Change Management (act as Change Supervisor)
• Release Management (perform a proper handover into production of new applications/services)
• Configuration Management
• DB configuration and patching, backup and disaster recovery management
• Provide technical support and leadership in a complex SAP environment
• Work with other domains to establish implementation of plans for SAP changes, projects, implementations, backup and recovery
• Lead and participate in SAP projects and complex changes, such as new implementations, transitions, migrations, upgrades, conversions etc.
• Manage/consult on the architecture and design of HW and SW within the client's environment
• Develop, implement and ensure compliance of technology standards and solutions
