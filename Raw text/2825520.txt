Responsibilities
NEW SOLUTIONS EMERGE WHEN THINKING ISN'T HARD-WIRED.
More than 90% of automotive innovations are based on electronics and software. That's why creative freedom and lateral thinking are so important in the pursuit of truly new solutions. Naturally, this includes flexible working models, so that our IT specialists are free to go in unconventional directions.
Requirements
What should you bring along?
University degree in (business) computer science, mathematics, or a comparable qualification with a strong IT focus.
At least 5 years of experience in software or platform engineering, ideally in a cloud environment (AWS or Azure).
Solid knowledge of CI/CD, automation, and infrastructure as code, ideally with Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, or similar tools.
Experience in building and operating enterprise-wide AI or data platforms with a focus on scalability, performance, and user experience.
Practical experience in machine learning or generative AI (e.g., Jupyter, PyTorch, TensorFlow, SageMaker, LangChain, Haystack) together with knowledge in modern programming languages (Python, Java, or Go) and API technologies.
Deep understanding of IT security, data protection, and compliance requirements.
Very good English skills; German skills are an advantage.
