Informácie o pracovnom mieste
Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
As a Business Analyst in the software department, you will play a crucial role in bridging the gap between our business needs and technology solutions throughout the implementation of our software
You are responsible for the collection and documentation of the requirements and their acceptance criteria
Thanks to your professional abilities, you will team-up with product owners, UX and stakeholders to prioritize the best possible added value for the customer in the form of features
Together with product owners and stakeholders, you continuously ensure that the backlog is adequately detailed
You will be in direct contact with colleagues from development and sales and clarify questions regarding requirements for the scrum teams
You will moderate and actively participate in specific meetings, such as concept meetings
