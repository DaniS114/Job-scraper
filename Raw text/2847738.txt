Responsibilities
Learn the existing system architecture, codebase, tools, and development practices
Validate existing technical documentation and flag any gaps or outdated content
Proactively highlight blockers to knowledge transfer (e.g., missing info, unclear logic, inaccessible systems)
Maintain coding standards, version control practices, and agile routines to ensure smooth delivery
Detect missing logic, undocumented features, or technical debt that could impact future development
Share acquired knowledge with peers and help ramp up the rest of the team
Actively participate in stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives to stay aligned
Clarify requirements, validate assumptions, and ensure technical feasibility of user stories
Implement the functionalities required to support new process and workflow for counterparty management
Enhance applications and infrastructures to align with business needs
Define and review technical requirements for integration and performance
Maintain applications according to service level agreements
Track associated key performance indicators with cloud operations teams
Manage capacity effectively to ensure optimal performance
