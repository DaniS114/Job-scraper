Looking for more than just another routine role? Ready to make a real difference?
We’re not just hiring a
Scrum Master

As
Scrum Master,
you will provide day-to-day support in relation to product implementation, customization, questions, and problems. You also will:
Facilitate key Scrum at scale ceremonies
Manage efficiency and productivity of Scrum teams, ensure Scrum artifacts are of proper quality and value and integrated amongst other teams
Coordinate with product development engineers to track and communicate design, development, support, and maintenance problems and solutions
Gather and consolidate feedback from included project members
Coordinate relevant individuals to address and resolve problems and expand product usage
Answer project related questions; provide guidance and support for product integration
