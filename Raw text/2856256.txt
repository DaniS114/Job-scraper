Responsibilities
Key Responsibilities
Platform transformation & optimisation
Lead the strategic evolution and rationalisation of a large, complex Dynamics 365 platform, ensuring it becomes more efficient, maintainable, and accessible for integration with other systems.
Identify opportunities to retire or simplify processes, while modernising architecture and functionality.
Team leadership & empowerment
Lead, mentor, and inspire a team of ~8 senior Dynamics engineers — fostering autonomy, creativity, and proactive solution finding.
Encourage the team to think beyond stakeholder requests, initiating proposals that enhance business processes and deliver measurable impact.
Technical strategy & delivery
Own technical strategy and roadmaps for Dynamics 365 (Sales & Marketing) and Power Platform solutions, balancing new features, modernisation, and operational stability.
Oversee architecture, configuration, data model governance, and integrations with other business systems and Azure services.
Ensure system performance, scalability, and reliable change/release management.
Innovation & AI enablement
Drive adoption of Power Platform capabilities (Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Power BI).
Embed AI / Copilot functionality into production workflows to improve efficiency and user experience.
Maintain close collaboration with Microsoft to adopt new features early and follow best practices.
Stakeholder partnership
Act as the primary technical sparring partner for Sales, Marketing, and Operations stakeholders.
Translate business needs into robust, scalable CRM solutions — with a focus on proactive engagement, not just reactive delivery.
Ensure transparency on progress, risks, and priorities through clear communication and reporting.
Engineering excellence
Implement and enforce development, testing, CI/CD, and DevOps standards to improve delivery speed and quality.
Promote best practices across development, deployment, and documentation.
Requirements
Required Skills & Experience
7+ years working with Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM, with hands‑on experience in Sales & Marketing modules.
Proven expertise in Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Power BI).
Strong understanding of system architecture, API integrations and Azure deployment patterns.
Practical experience integrating AI or Copilot features in Dynamics/Power Platform environments.
Track record of leading engineering teams or large CRM projects, with excellent stakeholder management skills.
Agile mindset, familiar with DevOps and CI/CD practices.
Microsoft Dynamics / Power Platform certifications are a plus.
Fluent English; German is a plus.
Willingness to travel within Germany and internationally.
Self‑starter with a high drive to deliver in a fast‑moving, international environment.
