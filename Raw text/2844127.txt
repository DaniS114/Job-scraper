Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Implement, configure, and optimize SAP eWM solutions in S/4HANA Public Cloud (and Private Cloud where applicable)
Drive global rollout activities, including fit-to-standard workshops, template localization, testing, and deployment
Design warehouse processes such as: Inbound/Outbound processing, Picking strategies, Stock placement/removal, Physical inventory, Handling Units & packaging specifications
Support labor management, RF framework, and integration with automation equipment (MFS experience is a plus)
Collaborate closely with MM, SD, TM, and PP teams on end-to-end logistics flows
Translate business requirements into scalable, cloud-ready eWM configurations
Participate in cutovers, go-lives, hypercare, and continuous improvement cycles
Requirements
5+ years of hands-on experience with SAP eWM (embedded or decentralized)
Strong experience with S/4HANA Public Cloud (Private Cloud knowledge is also valuable)
Proven participation in S/4 rollout or global template projects
Deep understanding of key eWM components (Warehouse structure & master data, Inbound/Outbound processes, Picking/packing strategies, Warehouse Order creation & optimization, RF integration)
Ability to design stable, high-performance warehouse processes that follow SAP fit-to-standard principles
Strong analytical mindset, troubleshooting ability, and communication skills
Advanced English (German is an advantage)
What we offer
Participation in full-cycle and diverse international projects
Opportunity to work as a specialist or manager/team lead
Constant career development with internal mentorship
Chance to leading-edge functional and technical SAP trainings
Access to a whole set of learning platforms, paid certifications
Flexible working conditions, home working is allowed
Attractive base salary & Wide range of benefits included cafeteria, bonuses, private health insurance package, life insurance, AYCM sport card, referral bonus, family-oriented benefits, company shares on a discount price
