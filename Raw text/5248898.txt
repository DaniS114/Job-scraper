Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a motivated Azure Cloud Engineer to join our IT Infrastructure team.
This role is a great fit for someone with a solid foundation in Azure and Microsoft infrastructure who wants to keep growing in a modern cloud environment. Your main focus will be on the Azure side, while also supporting and gradually learning the Microsoft 365 ecosystem alongside experienced colleagues.
We are looking for someone with the right mindset, strong technical fundamentals, and a genuine willingness to learn and take ownership over time.
What will you work on?
Manage and maintain Azure services including Storage Accounts, Key Vault, Backups, Update Management, Azure Policies, Conditional Access, Application Gateways, and governance-related configurations
Work with Entra ID (Azure AD), user and group provisioning, app registrations, and authentication configurations
Help maintain Group Policy and on-premises Active Directory
Assist with Microsoft 365 services such as Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, and Teams
Contribute to automation and operational improvements using PowerShell and other scripting tools
Support endpoint and device management in collaboration with Intune and Microsoft security solutions
Troubleshoot infrastructure and cloud-related issues together with internal teams
Support integrations with third-party tools and platforms
Participate in security initiatives, including Microsoft Defender configuration and monitoring
Create and maintain clear technical documentation to support the team
Work closely with colleagues across infrastructure, networking, security, and other IT teams
