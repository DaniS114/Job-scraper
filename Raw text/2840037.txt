Responsibilities
You enjoy further developing business-critical applications and technically navigating complex system landscapes? You like working analytically, taking responsibility for quality, and finding sustainable solutions for demanding requirements?
Then this role in the RPG environment is exactly right for you.
You will take on a central role in the further development and stable operation of our core applications, combining technical expertise with business understanding and actively contributing to shaping our system landscape in a reliable, efficient, and future-oriented way.
How you'll make an impact
Development, maintenance, and operation of business-critical insurance applications on the IBM i platform using RPG (RPG III, RPG/ILE, RPG/Free)
Ensuring stable, efficient, and secure operation of core systems
2nd-level support for IBM i applications
Analyzing errors, identifying development needs, implementing fixes and enhancements, and presenting results and solutions to business departments
Translating business requirements into technical concepts and ensuring smooth implementation
How you'll make an impact
Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a comparable qualification, along with several years of experience in RPG development and operation of business applications
Strong knowledge of RPG (RPG III, RPG/ILE, RPG/Free), IBM Db2 for i, SQL, and web services
Experience with database design, system integration, and service-oriented architectures
Knowledge of traditional development models (Waterfall) and ITIL is an advantage
Strong analytical skills and high problem-solving competence
Ability to communicate technical topics clearly to non-technical stakeholders
Strong commitment to delivering high-quality work
Excellent communication skills in English and/or German

Company info
At AND-E, we're redefining the future of mobility insurance. As part of the global MS&AD Group and in close partnership with Toyota, we aim to make tomorrow's journeys simpler, safer, and smarter. Our expertise spans over 30 markets in Europe and South Africa, providing tailored insurance solutions for Toyota and Lexus customers and various white-label partners. In Germany, our offerings include motor insurance, commercial fleet coverage, payment protection insurance, and support for Japanese businesses operating abroad.
Our commitment extends beyond business: at AND-E we support our people to grow, make a difference for customers, and help shape the future of connected mobility protection.
