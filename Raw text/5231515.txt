Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a
DevOps Engineer
to join a dynamic team focused on delivering and maintaining highly reliable, secure, and automated cloud-native systems. In this role, you will take ownership of tooling and processes that enable continuous delivery, infrastructure automation, and operational excellence. You’ll collaborate with development teams to enhance delivery speed, system stability, monitoring, and automation across the software lifecycle.
