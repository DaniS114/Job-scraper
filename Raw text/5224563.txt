Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
What awaits you with us:
• Work either in a team or independently
• Responsibility for all aspects of analysis, test design, and creation of test scenarios for automated tests
• Execution and monitoring of automated test results (script integration and continuous integration support)
• Creation/maintenance/error handling related to automated testing
• Preparation of test data required for automated testing
• Documentation and reporting of automated testing results
• Defining and reporting identified defects
• Close collaboration with the internal development team
• Supporting the client/developers in resolving identified defects
What we require:
• Practical experience with automated application testing (approximately 2 years)
• Practical experience with application testing
• Ability to work in a fast-changing environment and meet deadlines
• Knowledge and hands-on experience with automation tools used in testing (Selenium, Tosca, etc.)
• Knowledge of at least one programming language is an advantage (Java, JavaScript, Python, …)
• Understanding of the software development lifecycle
• Knowledge of various testing methods, techniques, and processes
• Experience with tools for managing and executing test scenarios/tickets (e.g., Jira)
• Knowledge of agile development (SCRUM, LEAN, KANBAN) is an advantage
• Knowledge and use of AI/ChatGPT in testing is an advantage
• Basic knowledge of SQL/API testing is an advantage
• Strong attention to detail
• Result-oriented mindset (proactive problem solving)
• Good communication skills/experience
• Teamwork – ability to interpret team outputs and collaborate effectively
• Good command of the German language, as the interview will be conducted in German
