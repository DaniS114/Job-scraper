Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It With Pride.
The PM SAP key user will be responsible to overseeing Bratislava plant providing support and training for SAP end users in all different departments such as Operations, Shipping, Receiving, Scheduling etc. This position will be leading projects that facilitate simplification of SAP process following Integrated Lean Six Sigma principles having as a main goal to synchronize the supply chain end to end.
How you will contribute - Main Responsibilities:
•	Responsible to provide training activities related with the SAP based process. Responsible to analyze main errors and losses related to SAP transactions and perform Root Cause Analysis to eliminate GAP using training as main tool
•	Lead day to day activities that includes - monitoring, corrective actions & reporting system to insure integrity of data, transparent maintenance process, SAP data & functional location are up to date
•	Responsible as a local coordinator to participate and contribute in new SAP projects implementation that are being led by Global resources
•	Lead and execute the support and operations, bringing value through the planning and scheduling and contributing to the plant performance through the monitoring of the execution and breakdown analysis
•	Owns SAP based maintenance process, Notification and PM Order management , SAP reporting systems , SAP task list management system, SAP Documentation management systems, have deep technical mastery of the process and equipment
•	Be part of the PM Pillar, methodologically lead the Technical leads, Partner with the Asset and Reliability team for system improvement and execution of special maintenance tasks
•	Execute internal and external benchmarking and manage improvement process for the plant

At Mondelēz International, our purpose is to empower people to snack right through offering the right snack, for the right moment, made the right way. That means delivering a broader range of delicious, high-quality snacks that nourish life's moments, made with sustainable ingredients and packaging that consumers can feel good about.
