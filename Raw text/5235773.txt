Job description, responsibilities and duties
Our client is seeking a motivated and experienced
Asset Valuation Analyst
to join their international team based in
Bratislava
. This role is ideal for someone who is detail-oriented, data-driven, and eager to contribute to business process improvements through accurate reporting and analysis.
Key Responsibilities:
Consolidate and analyze financial, operational, and market data to identify key business drivers
Develop and maintain reports, dashboards, and pricing models (vehicles & operating leasing components)
Perform variance analysis and support budgeting and forecasting processes
Track and evaluate KPIs, identifying risks and opportunities for improvement
Ensure high levels of data accuracy, consistency, and timely reporting to Headquarters
Monitor market developments and benchmark company performance against industry standards
Prepare performance overviews and present insights to senior management
Collaborate cross-functionally (Sales, Operations, Strategy, Controlling) and actively support process improvements
