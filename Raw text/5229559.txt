Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
As a Senior Software Developer .NET , you will develop and support Global Blue’s self-service kiosk client and backend applications with the highest quality and commitment throughout the software development lifecycle.
You will work in a scrum team, contributing to business and technical goals aligned with our development standards and quality criteria.
A glimpse into your role. What you will do:
Design, develop, test, support, and deploy self-service clients, web, and backend applications in .NET
Build kiosk client software integrating hardware peripherals (document and barcode scanners, printers, cash dispensers, touch screens, payment terminals)
Research and evaluate new technologies and products
Implement best practices, standards, and procedures to ensure quality and compliance throughout the software lifecycle
Work in an Agile team setup
Collaborate with development teams, product owners/managers, and business analysts
Provide support for Level 1 and Level 2 application support teams (ITSM)
Take an active role in technical improvement initiatives.
