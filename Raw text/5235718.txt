Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Start your career where finance operations meet digital transformation. In this junior role, you will help improve Cash & Cost processes, learn how modern tools—including AI—can support smarter operations, and grow your skills in process analysis, problem solving, and change delivery.
If you’re a recent graduate or early-career talent with a technical or analytical background and a passion for learning, this is a great opportunity to develop within a dynamic, transformation focused environment.
About the Role
As a Junior Transformation Specialist, you will support process improvement initiatives across Cash & Cost Operations. You’ll analyze how things work today, help identify where efficiency gains can be made, and assist in introducing new tools, including AI-supported features, to make everyday work smoother and more effective.
This is an ideal role to grow into transformation work—you don’t need to be an IT expert, but you should be curious about how technology and data can support operational excellence.
Key Responsibilities
Assist with analyzing existing Cash & Cost processes and identifying improvement opportunities.
Support senior colleagues in preparing process documentation, business requirements, and validation/testing materials.
Participate in small transformation initiatives, helping evaluate automation options and AI-driven enhancements.
Help coordinate with operational teams and technical experts to ensure solutions address real business needs.
Contribute to user training, communication, and adoption activities during rollouts.
Learn continuously about financial processes, process improvement methodologies, and modern digital/AI tools.
About the Team
Cash & Cost Operations ensures the smooth and accurate handling of internal cost processes, payments, reconciliations, and financial support activities.
The team works in an evolving environment where digital solutions and AI-supported tools play an increasing role, making it an excellent place to accelerate your learning and professional growth.
