Job description, responsibilities and duties
As an AI research institute, KInIT develops and maintains multiple software solutions for its research activities and administration (e.g., data scraping infrastructure, computing infrastructure, deployed ML models, experimental user applications).
We are looking for a research intern to support our team with running our experiments, internal tools, and analyzing data from systems, helping both engineers and researchers move faster.
Responsibilities
Assist engineers with operating internal tools and infrastructure (running pipelines, basic troubleshooting, documentation)
Support researchers with analyzing data collected from internal systems (Python analyses, summarizing the results)
Contribute to improvements that strengthen our capability to audit social media platform algorithms
Support development and maintenance of data scraping and processing infrastructure
Help with execution of research studies.

Brief description of the company
KInIT is an independent non-profit institute connecting industry to excellent science. We bring together and enable growth of experts in artificial intelligence and other areas of computer science, with connections to other disciplines:
- Web and user data processing including empowering humans with the ability to evaluate credibility of information and its manipulation
- Processing and comprehension of natural language
- Green and secure environment
- Ethics and human values in intelligent technologies
KInIT combines the excellent competencies of academic researchers and educators with innovative companies, their needs and experience. By involving the private sector, our team of top notch Slovak AI research experts seek to create a healthy research ecosystem that bridges academia and industry.
