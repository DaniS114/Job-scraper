Contract type

Product Analyst contributes to ESET’s product portfolio development strategy by performing technical and functional benchmarking against industry-leading solutions to ensure market superiority.
Who are we looking for
Product Analyst is ideal for a candidate who enjoys working in a technical and ever-evolving industry and is ambitious to self-develop his / her specialization. Job is also suitable for fresh graduates.
We are looking for talent able to dive deeper, solve specific issues, filter out key points and understand context and relations from a higher perspective.
The ideal candidate should have a sense of deliberation, professionality, and integrity.
Job Description
The Competitive Intelligence team of experts is an integral part of ESET Product Management department that helps Product Managers and ESET internal stakeholders make critical strategic decisions and improve ESET products.
Product Analysts actively participate in following the evolution of ESET products.
The Competitive Intelligence team has great team spirit, an open atmosphere and high integrity. We love to apply technical gadgets, which help us in our work. We work in the ESET HQ in Bratislava in a private office.
Product Analyst, as a member of the Competitive Intelligence team, is an expert in the competitive landscape of the IT Security industry. His / Her daily routine is analyzing competitive security products. He / She also monitors market trends and competition in the IT Security industry from a global perspective and collects and evaluates Early Warnings.
Responsibilities
Product Analyst drives or participates in strategic projects by performing operations which include:
Deploy and maintain security solutions based on specific project requirements.
Perform technical, functional and business analysis, and use case testing.
Identify product strengths and weaknesses.
Transform observations into required outputs.
Advisory and consultancy - delivering and presenting conclusions and recommendations
Requirements
Higher technical education and advanced experience in IT are a must.
Fluent in written and spoken English
Interest in technology trends and education in the field of security, telecommunications and information networks
Expertise in the Windows platform is an advantage.
Experience in security software & services is an advantage.
Experience in software & IT service analysis is an advantage.

O ESETe
Spoločnosť ESET je globálnym lídrom v oblasti digitálnej bezpečnosti a je jedným z najväčších dodávateľov bezpečnostných riešení v Európskej únii. Zároveň je najväčšou a najoceňovanejšou IT firmou na Slovensku.
Už viac ako 30 rokov vyvíjame popredný softvér a služby zamerané na IT bezpečnosť a ochranu podnikov, kritickej infraštruktúry a domácností z celého sveta
Neustále hľadáme nové kolegyne alebo nových kolegov, ktorí chcú byť súčasťou úspešného slovenského príbehu s globálnym dopadom.
