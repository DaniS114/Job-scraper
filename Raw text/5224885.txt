Purpose

Project description
Software Engineer (Agile)
for the internal Telekom application
IPLS-ROT (International Production Line Service – Roll Out Tool)
.
The role focuses on the continuous development and enhancement of an existing, highly integrated OSS system used for technical provisioning of VPN customer connections in an international telecom environment.
IPLS-ROT translates commercial orders from BSS systems into technical components and workflows, orchestrates provisioning activities across multiple downstream systems, manages resources and inventories, and processes order feedback. The application operates within a complex system landscape with numerous interfaces and dependencies.
What will you do?
Continuous development and enhancement of the IPLS-ROT application
Implementation of changes across multiple small but diverse functional topics
Backend development within an existing system architecture
Solution design at component and feature level
Technical consulting and advisory support for business stakeholders
Collaboration with cross-functional teams in an agile environment
Contribution to documentation and system understanding
