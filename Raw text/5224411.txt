Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are seeking a Senior IT Operations Lead to act as the architect of our digital backbone. You will not just "manage" from a distance; you will be the hands-on operational core of our IT, coordinating operations across Slovakia, USA, Brazil, and the UK. You will work side-by-side with our current IT team and engineers to ensure they have the secure, robust tools they need to move at the speed of this emerging industry.

Move AI beyond "hype" by selecting, piloting, and rolling out AI productivity tools that actually improve efficiency and knowledge sharing.
IT Operations & Processes:
Define and implement core IT processes (incident, change, asset management, backups) and run the day-to-day service desk and monitoring.

Collaboration & Vendor Coordination:
Partner closely with our IT specialist (providing mentorship and guidance) and coordinate external vendors to ensure reliable, 24/7 support for the company.
