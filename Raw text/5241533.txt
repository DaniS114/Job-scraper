Job description, responsibilities and duties
- Define and continuously evolve the Digital vendor strategy in alignment with target architectures, platform roadmaps, and global Digital priorities.
-Translate technology strategy into clear vendor positioning: strategic partners, preferred vendors, and exit candidates.
- Prevent vendor‑driven architecture by enforcing technology standards and long‑term design principles.
Technical Evaluation & Decision Support
- Lead technical evaluation of vendors and solutions across cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), enterprise systems (SAP, Salesforce, PLM), cybersecurity, and AI‑enabled solutions.
- Challenge architectural, scalability, security, and operability assumptions proposed by vendors.
- Assess technical trade‑offs between cost optimization and long‑term platform sustainability.
Architecture & Platform Alignment
- Act as a strong partner to Digital Architects and Platform Owners, ensuring vendor choices reinforce target architectures.
- Identify opportunities for technology rationalization, simplification, and platform consolidation.
- Support FinOps‑driven optimization of cloud and SaaS consumption models.
Execution & Governance
- Contribute technical leadership to RFQs, vendor evaluations, and selection processes.
- Support Procurement during negotiations by providing technical insights, risk assessments, and cost drivers.
- Define and monitor vendor performance from a technical, operational, and risk perspective.
Cross‑Functional Leadership
- Influence stakeholders across regions and Digital domains without formal authority.
- Act as a technical counterbalance between Digital delivery teams, vendors, and commercial stakeholders.
- Drive consistent vendor and technology decisions across Marelli’s global organization.
Employee perks, benefits
- A high‑impact role shaping the future Digital landscape of a global automotive leader.
- Strong executive visibility and influence on technology decisions.
- A remote working model supporting focus, flexibility, and collaboration.
- Continuous learning and development in a fast‑evolving Digital environment.
