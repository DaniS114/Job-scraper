About the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you know how important is Identity & Access Management (IAM) within the company?
Our work matters a lot because we keep the company safe from the inside. We are the ones who make sure that the right people have the right access at the right time.
IAM team at ING Hubs Slovakia supports several countries worldwide and plays an important role in delivering services to our ING clients.
What we can offer for sure:
We are currently looking for our new teammate and we would like to offer you opportunity to boost you career in IT field or specifically in IT Security area.
We are very happy to give you the opportunity to gain a lot of new knowledge from IT Security and later on you will be able to solve various problems that we encounter.
Don't be afraid that you will solve the same problem over and over again - your future colleagues can confirm that their work is diverse!
If you join to us, you will definitely work in an intercultural dynamic environment in touch with foreign language on daily bases.
Your main daily responsibilities will be here:
Verification and review of applied access rights within ING assets
Processing access related requests based on tickets in line with SLA
Validation of access requests
Processing of system exports to clear and understandable form
Communication with stakeholders and user access teams
Participating in innovation and improvement process
Working with wide range of IT technologies
