Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you want to help create the future of healthcare?
Our name, Siemens Healthineers, was selected to honor our people who dedicate their energy and passion to this cause. It reflects their pioneering spirit combined with our long history of engineering in the ever-evolving healthcare industry.
We offer you a flexible and dynamic environment with opportunities to go beyond your comfort zone in order to grow personally and professionally. Sounds interesting?
Then come and join our global team as
UI Designer (f/m/d)
and use your visual expertise to design products which add real value for our company alongside other professionals in the UX department.
We kindly ask you to also upload your portfolio with the application.
Your tasks and responsibilities:
As a UI Designer in our team, you will take a key role in shaping the visual language of a product portfolio used by thousands of healthcare professionals worldwide. You work at the intersection of aesthetics, usability and clinical reality - creating interfaces that are not only beautiful but genuinely help people in critical moments.
Create high‑quality visual concepts and screen designs based on our advanced design system and evolve it further where needed.
Design for a broad spectrum of touchpoints with complex workflows and diverse user groups: from radiology departments to radiation therapy
Work closely with UX Research, Interaction Design, Industrial Design and Development, forming cross‑functional teams that challenge and inspire each other.
Experience real clinical environments firsthand, joining shadowing sessions in hospitals and practices to deeply understand users’ needs.
Shape the visual design language end‑to‑end from foundational layout decisions to polished pixel-perfect delivery.
Become part of a growing, international UX organization that values open dialogue, collaborative critique, craftsmanship and continuous improvement.
Required education, skills and personality requirements
Required education
University education (Bachelor's degree)
Educational Specialization
in User Interface Design, Communication Design or a related field
Language skills
English - Upper intermediate (B2)
Personality requirements and skills
You are confident working in English in an international environment.
You have an excellent design-oriented mindset and way of working, with a focus on users
You are particularly passionate about working on creative concepts, set high quality standards for your work and are tenacious with strong attention to detail
As a team player you encourage collaboration and dialog within and outside the central UX department at Siemens Healthineers
You enjoy lifting others up, work proactively and with ownership, and appreciate a workplace where professionalism and humor can coexist.
Your qualifications and experience:
Several years of professional experience in an agency or in‑house UX environment, with a strong digital portfolio showing depth, craft and systems thinking.
Exceptional visual design skills: typography, spacing, hierarchy, color, visual storytelling.
High proficiency in Figma and an understanding of design systems at scale.
Experience in medical technology is a plus, curiosity and willingness to dive deep is even more important.
A collaborative mindset: you enjoy working closely with others, giving and receiving feedback, and pushing quality together.
