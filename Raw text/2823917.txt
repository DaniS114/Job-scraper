Responsibilities
Join a client service team and take ownership of a workstream to solve client challenges
Deliver solutions that solve immediate problems and drive long-term change
Uncover true challenges behind a client’s strategy and create actionable plans
Collaborate directly with clients to solve problems, refine strategies, and implement transformations
Combine research, data analysis, interviews, and brainstorming to generate insights and solutions
Requirements
Typically, an advanced graduate degree (e.g., MBA, PhD, etc.) or equivalent work experience
Excellent organization capabilities, including the ability to initiate tasks independently and see them through to completion
Strong communication skills, both verbal and written, in English and local office language(s)
Proficient in rational decision making based on data, facts, and logical reasoning
Ability to create work product-focused materials such as PowerPoint decks, Excel models, articles, or other written deliverables
Exceptional time management to meet responsibilities in a complex and largely autonomous work environment
Ability to work or attend meetings outside of traditional business hours or take on projects with limited or no notice
Ability to travel to and work in varying environments that may be challenging and/or not accessible
What we offer
Resources, mentorship, and opportunities to become a stronger leader
Continuous learning culture with structured programs and clear, actionable feedback
A voice that matters from day one, valuing ideas and contributions
Global community with colleagues across 65+ countries and over 100 nationalities
Competitive salary based on location, experience, and skills
Comprehensive benefits package including medical, mental health, dental and vision coverage, telemedicine services, life, accident and disability insurance, parental leave and family planning benefits, caregiving resources, retirement contributions program, financial guidance, and paid time off
Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves some of the most influential organizations around the world, including Fortune 500 giants and innovative startups. The firm operates in over 65 countries with a diverse workforce of over 100 nationalities. McKinsey is committed to making a meaningful difference in the industries and communities it serves through delivering lasting impact and building long-term capabilities with its clients.
