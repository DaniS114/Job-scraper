Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are now hiring a
Lead Data Engineer (Product)
, responsible for architecting and delivering the foundational data products.
This is not a back-office BI job.
This is a
product-building role
, focused on creating market-facing insight services, automated reporting, and AI-ready datasets that enable customers to “chat with their buildings,” generate QBR-ready output at the click of a button, and access persona-driven dashboards tailored to the real needs of property managers, building owners, leaseholders, on-site teams, and contractors.
As a founding member of our data capability, you will shape the standards, define the architecture, and establish the ways of working that underpin our long-term data strategy. You will also collaborate closely with our AI Innovation and Data Architecture functions as they evolve, ensuring the platform is future-proofed for predictive analytics and machine learning.
What You’ll Be Responsible For
Designing & Building the Unified Customer Data Platform
Developing Persona-Based Dashboards & Insight Products
Building Our Automated Reporting Engine
Enabling Conversational Analytics (“Chat With My Buildings”)
Collaborating With AI Innovation & Data Architecture
About You
You are a hands-on technologist with a product mindset. You care about how customers use data — not just how it is transformed. You know that great insight design starts with understanding the user and their workflow. You enjoy bringing structure to ambiguous environments and thrive when creating systems, standards, and experiences from scratch.
You will be a great fit if you:
Combine deep data engineering capability with a passion for customer-facing analytics
Are excited by applied AI and the future of data-driven user experiences
Are comfortable working with ambiguity and building from a blank slate
Are energised by creating new capabilities that will scale across multiple products
Enjoy the influence and ownership of being a founding data hire
Skills & Experience
Essential
Extensive experience in data engineering, analytics engineering, or hybrid roles within SaaS or product-led companies
Strong applied experience with
Microsoft Fabric
or equivalent Azure technologies
Excellent SQL and solid Python capability
Proven experience delivering
customer-facing dashboards, reporting suites, or insight products
Strong understanding of dimensional modelling, semantic layers, and data product design
Experience building datasets suitable for AI/LLM-based natural language querying
Strong knowledge of data contracts, metadata, quality, and governance
Desirable
Experience with property, asset, compliance, maintenance, or operational domains
Exposure to predictive modelling or ML-enabled product features
Familiarity with embedded analytics, multi-tenant data permissions, and RLS
Experience in early-stage or scaling data teams
Acting as a Data Product Architect & Founding Engineer
