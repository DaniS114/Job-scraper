Responsibilities
Lead technical discussions and mentor junior engineers
Work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver on ambitious goals
Develop and optimise solutions for AR/VR and backend graphics
Integrate with in-house art assets
Address critical bugs
Architect and implement core functionalities
Drive high standards in testing, peer reviews, and debugging
Contribute to daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and design reviews
Requirements
Strong proficiency in Unreal Engine (ideally Unreal 5) with expertise in both C++ and Blueprints
Experience with other game engines is a plus
Deep understanding of modern C++11/14/17
Ability to write clean, maintainable, and high-performance code
Hands-on experience with backend graphics engines (DirectX/OpenGL/Vulkan)
Proficiency in HLSL/GLSL and shader development for real-time graphics
Strong grasp of 3D mathematics relevant to graphics programming
Experience working with AR/VR technologies and pipelines
Familiarity with Scrum, Kanban, or similar Agile methodologies
Proficiency with Git or equivalent version control systems
Experience mentoring team members and leading by example
Nice-to-have
Leadership & Collaboration, Technical Excellence, Code Quality, Team Engagement, We foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and technical excellence, Autonomy to steer development efforts while being part of a supportive team, Pivotal role in delivering pioneering graphics solutions that redefine sports technology

Company info
Hawk-Eye Innovations, a Sony company, is a world leader in sports technology. We deliver sophisticated officiating, broadcast, and player tracking solutions across more than 25 international sports. Each year, our technology supports over 30,000 events in 500+ stadiums across 90 countries.
