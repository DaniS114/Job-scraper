Purpose
We are looking for a
MariaDB Expert
who will serve as a technical specialist for designing, building, monitoring, optimizing, and maintaining MariaDB database environments.
The role also includes analyzing datasets, identifying optimization opportunities, improving performance, and validating technical approaches through data-driven decision-making.
Team description
Join the team at operational services GmbH within Deutsche Telekom IT Solutions Slovakia, offering opportunities for German- and English-speaking talents. OS is one of the leading ICT service providers in the German market, delivering modern and secure IT solutions, long-term operational support, and ensuring availability of critical business processes for medium-sized and enterprise customers.
What will you do?
• Administration, operation, and maintenance of
MariaDB
database environments
Design, implementation, and optimization of
MariaDB architectures
Planning and executing installations, upgrades, migrations, and patching of MariaDB instances
Monitoring database performance and tuning queries, indexes, and configurations
Implementing and maintaining
backup & recovery strategies
, including PITR (Point-in-Time Recovery)
Troubleshooting database issues, analyzing root causes, and implementing long-term fixes
Ensuring high availability, reliability, and optimal performance of MariaDB systems
Managing
replication setups
(master-slave, GTID, semi-sync, etc.)
Supporting high availability and disaster recovery (HA/DR) environments (e.g., Galera Cluster, MaxScale)
Creating and maintaining technical documentation, runbooks, and operational procedures
Collaborating closely with development, infrastructure, and operations teams
Ensuring compliance with security policies, audit standards, and general governance requirements
Contributing to automation initiatives and continuous service improvements
You will succeed if you:
• Have minimum
3 years of hands-on experience with MariaDB or MySQL administration
Strong understanding of MariaDB architecture, components, and internal processes
Practical experience with:
Installation, configuration, maintenance
Query tuning, execution plan analysis, indexing strategies
Backup & recovery concepts and tools
Monitoring and troubleshooting complex issues
Solid knowledge of SQL and ability to optimize complex queries
Familiarity with
Linux server environments
(administration fundamentals required)
Experience with replication (ROW/STATEMENT, GTID, async, semi-sync)
Experience with
HA/cluster setups
(Galera, MaxScale – advantage)
Analytical, systematic, and structured way of working
Strong teamwork, communication skills, and customer-oriented approach
Ability and willingness to perform on-call duties
Nice-to-Have Technical Knowledge
• Experience with additional RDBMS platforms (Oracle, PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server)
Understanding of cloud-based database services (Azure, AWS RDS, Google Cloud SQL)
Knowledge of shell scripting (Bash) or automation tools
Experience with monitoring tools and dashboards (e.g., Grafana, Prometheus, Zabbix)
Familiarity with containerized database deployments (Docker, Kubernetes)
Certifications (Preferred)
• MariaDB, MySQL, or Linux administration certifications are an advantage
