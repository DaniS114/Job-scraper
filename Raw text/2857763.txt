Responsibilities
Product Engineer (f/m/d) for Software as a Service (SaaS)
We are looking for a Product Engineer to support us in continuous improvements of our GNSS networks software solution. You will be working in an international environment using the latest cutting-edge technologies that are the backbone of Hexagon's 24/7 HxGN SmartNet correction service. Our software seamlessly manages users and subscriptions, integrates with resilient IT infrastructure, enables deployment at a global scale, and ensures operational excellence through comprehensive monitoring.
If this sounds like the perfect job for you, join our team and make your visions come true!
About Us
Hexagon is a leading provider of digital reality solutions and employs more than 24,000 people in 50 countries. You will be part of a strong, experienced, inspiring and motivated team of experts driving the future of Hexagon. You will use and develop your skills in our highly innovative and diverse environment.
Flexible working models allow you to ideally combine work and private interests.
