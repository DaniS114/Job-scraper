Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner is bringing their impressive global success story to Hungary, building a brand-new Global Business Center in Budapest. They are looking for an Application Support Engineer to maintain and enhance core enterprise platforms, with a primary focus on the HR (Workday) ecosystem. They offer a unique environment that prioritizes an inclusive culture and service excellence from day one. In this role, you will help ensure smooth day-to-day operations and resolve issues efficiently within a collaborative, supportive, and social international team. It is a rare chance to join a community that values teamwork, celebrates success, and genuinely enjoys working together to build a fresh operation.
Platform Support: Provide Level 1, 2, and 3 support for Workday and other SaaS applications, troubleshooting configuration and integration issues.
Incident Resolution: Manage incidents, service requests, and system alerts within SLAs, performing root-cause analysis on recurring issues.
User Management: Support user onboarding, maintain access and permission structures, and perform regular audits of security settings.
Upgrades & Maintenance: Assist with Workday patching, application upgrades, and coordinate UAT cycles with business teams.
Data & Compliance: Support data quality initiatives, assist with integration monitoring, and ensure application changes follow IT governance and compliance frameworks.
Elvárások / Requirements
You hold a Salesforce Platform Developer I or II certification.
Proven experience as a Salesforce Developer with strong knowledge of Salesforce CRM, Apex, Lightning, Visualforce, and integrations.
Experience with other programming languages (e.g., Java, Python, JavaScript, .NET) and application development frameworks.
Solid understanding of the software development lifecycle, version control systems, and agile methodologies.
Strong problem-solving skills, business analysis capabilities (requirements gathering), and the ability to work cross-functionally.
Excellent communication skills to interact with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Good to have: Advanced Salesforce certifications or a Bachelor’s degree in IT/Computer Science.
