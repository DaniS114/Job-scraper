Job description, responsibilities and duties
What You Will Do
Design and maintain automated test suites for APIs, web applications, and backend systems in complex, configurable environments.
Prepare and manage scenario-based tests for digital banking user journeys and AI/LLM-driven workflows.
Validate multi-agent orchestration, individual agent behavior, tool integrations, prompts, and conversation quality.
Monitor AI-related risks and ensure safety and data privacy controls function correctly.
Verify compliance of AI systems with banking regulations and safety standards.
Create and maintain realistic test data.
Contribute to test strategies and automation frameworks to ensure scalability and reliability.
