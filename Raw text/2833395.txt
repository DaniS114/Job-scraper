Company info:
We are looking for a disciplined Project Manager to lead business improvement and application development initiatives within our financial services framework. In this role you will act as the strategic interface between business units (Front/Middle Office) and IT delivery teams, ensuring that all technical solutions meet the rigorous security, stability and regulatory standards of the financial industry.
What you will do:
Financial project governance
: you will drive the end-to-end lifecycle of application development and process optimization projects using industry-standard methodologies
Strategic stakeholder management
: you will mediate between business product owners, internal IT infrastructure teams, and third-party financial software providers to align technical roadmaps with commercial goals
Engineering requirements and compliance
: you will elicit complex business demands, provide expert review of functional specifications to ensure that they meet both user needs and internal audit/risk requirements
Documentation and audit readiness
: you will prepare meticulous project documentation, including specifications and risk registers, ensuring all artifacts are compliant with financial sector reporting standards
Incident and release coordination
: you will manage the escalation and resolution of critical system errors (tickets) originating from business fields, ensuring minimal impact on financial operations and SLAs
Executive decision support
: you will synthesize complex project data into high-level presentations and decision sheets for Steering Committees and senior leadership
What we will need from you:
Studies and experience
: degree in IT or finance is a must together with minimum of 3 years’ experience in IT Project Management, preferably within banking, insurance or fintech environments
Background knowledge
: familiarity with financial workflows (e.g. consumer lending, car leasing, transaction processing, regulatory reporting, etc.) is highly desirable
Communication skills
: advanced and active English proficiency with "self-assertion" skills necessary to manage expectations of high-pressure business stakeholders
Negotiation skills
: ability to hold external IT vendors accountable to strict SLAs
