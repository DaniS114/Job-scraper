Job description, responsibilities and duties
We're seeking a senior-level architect with strong expertise in MLOps, DevSecOps, deep learning pipelines, and high-performance networking to design and maintain our GPUaaS platform's backbone. This role emphasizes low-latency, high-bandwidth networks for GPU clusters (e.g., RDMA over InfiniBand/Ethernet), cloud integrations, security, and automation. Ideal for someone with experience architecting ML systems in enterprise environments.
Architect and deploy high-performance networking for GPU      clusters, including RDMA fabrics, InfiniBand/Ethernet switches, VPC      peering, low-latency interconnects, and traffic optimization for      distributed AI workloads.
Design and implement MLOps pipelines for deep learning: data      ingestion, model training/distribution on GPUs, deployment (e.g.,      Kubernetes-based), monitoring, and CI/CD integration.
Apply DevSecOps principles: embed security in infrastructure      code, automate compliance, vulnerability scanning, and secure      cloud/on-prem environments.
Optimize AWS (or multi-cloud) setups for GPUaaS scalability,      cost control, high availability, and fault tolerance in AI-centric      systems.
Diagnose and resolve network/performance issues in      high-throughput GPU environments, including latency reduction and      bandwidth maximization.
Automate infrastructure using tools like Puppet for      configuration management, Terraform for IaC, and orchestration platforms.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams on integrations with      data platforms, BI tools, and monitoring solutions (e.g.,      Prometheus/Grafana).
