We are currently looking for a skilled & passionate
Senior Technical Project Manager (m/f/d)
to become part of our Supply Tech Team at ABOUT YOU.
Our Supply Tech team focuses on the technology and systems that power our supply chain, from initial procurement and product data management to inventory and shipping. They seamlessly integrate merchants who sell at ABOUT YOU across a wide range of business models in over 20 countries.
As a key member of our team, you will lead and own critical supply chain projects from end to end – from initial business analysis to post-launch supervision. You will also play a crucial role in ensuring the integrity of our systems through proactive monitoring and investigating technical bugs.

You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
