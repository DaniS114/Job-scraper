Állás leírása
Responsibilities
You take functional and technical ownership of our M365 Power Platform, covering governance, continuous development, and operations.
Designing and implementing modern solutions using Power Apps, Power Automate, and other tools from the Microsoft ecosystem is at the core of your role.
You work closely with international business units, including requirements gathering, consulting, and implementation.
As part of 2nd level support, you support the Collaboration Team with general M365 topics and handle a wide range of tickets, always aiming to find suitable business solutions.
You carry out routine maintenance tasks and troubleshoot system-related issues to ensure stable operations.
Requirements
Several years of hands-on experience with the Microsoft Power Platform (Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse), ideally in global organizations.
Solid knowledge of the general M365 environment, such as SharePoint and Teams, combined with a good understanding of the Microsoft ecosystem.
Experience with Azure, Entra ID (formerly Azure Active Directory), and hybrid cloud architectures supports your daily work.
Basic knowledge of Power BI and Dynamics 365 is an advantage.
Strong analytical skills, a solution-oriented mindset, and a high level of ownership characterize the way you work.
Very good English skills for communicating with stakeholders worldwide; German skills are an advantage.
What we offer
Flexible working hours – with flextime and time off in lieu
Holiday and Christmas bonus – special payment at Christmas and a €1,000 vacation bonus
Corporate benefits and employee discounts – 15% discount at Otto and additional perks via our benefits portal
Hybrid working – work partly remotely
Workation – work up to 30 days from another European country
Subsidy for the Deutschlandticket – discounted mobility for you
Company info
You feel at home in the Microsoft universe and love creating well-thought-out solutions that deliver real value? At EOS, you shape the digital workplace of tomorrow in an international environment and with state-of-the-art tools. As part of our Collaboration Team, you ensure that M365 is used efficiently, securely, and innovatively across the globe.

You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
