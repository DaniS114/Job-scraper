Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
We are seeking an experienced Senior B2B Integration Developer & Architect (L3) responsible for maintaining, troubleshooting, and enhancing integrations built on IBM Sterling B2B Integrator and IBM Sterling File Gateway.
The role requires deep technical expertise in Sterling Integrator business process development using BPML language, production support, and integration troubleshooting in a high-availability B2B environment.
You will act as highest technical escalation level (L3), resolving complex integration issues and implementing enhancements to business processes and trading partner integrations.
Key Responsibilities:
Production Support (L3):
·         Provide Level 3 technical support for applications running on IBM Sterling B2B Integrator and IBM Sterling File Gateway.
·         Investigate and resolve complex incidents, failures, and performance issues.
·         Perform root cause analysis (RCA) and implement permanent fixes.
·         Support 24/7 critical integration platform operations (on-call if required).
·         Analyze logs, process data, and transaction flows.
Development & Enhancements
·         Develop and modify Business Processes using BPML language.
·         Installation and configuration of multi-node architecture environments, including upgrades and platform migration
·         Create and maintain:
o   Business Processes
o   Services and adapters configuration
o   Maps and workflows
·         Implement new file-based integrations and partner onboarding.
·         Optimize existing business processes for performance and reliability.
Monitoring & Troubleshooting
·         Monitor Sterling platform health and transaction processing.
·         Troubleshoot:
o   Failed business processes
o   File transfer issues
o   Mapping errors
o   Connectivity problems
·         Work with infrastructure teams on:
o   JVM issues
o   Database connectivity
o   Cluster nodes
o   Virtualization platform the solution is running on
Documentation & Governance
·         Maintain documentation for:
o   Business processes
o   Integration flows
o   Troubleshooting guides
·         Follow change management and release procedures.
