Are you an experienced IT Integration Solution Designer ready to help with transformation of legacy IT Integration domain at Slovak Telekom to modern micro service architecture? We are looking for an expert who will help design and implement our comprehensive IT Integration transformation, replacing legacy integration processes with a new architecture based on the TM Forum ODA (Open Digital Architecture) and Open API concepts. This role will define new rules in the IT Integration domain and will force compliance with these rules across the organization.
About the Role:
We are looking for an experienced IT Integration Senior Solution Designer for IT Integration squad in Enterprise IT Tribe. Main role of this position will be close cooperation with business analytics, architects and developers with focus on analysis of business requirements, technical specifications and functional design in IT Integration area.
Key Responsibilities:
Lead the design and implementation
of a
new IT integration layer
fully compliant with TM Forum Open APIs and Open Digital Architecture (ODA) principles across our IT ecosystem.
Define and implement integration and orchestration patterns
to ensure scalability, resilience and decoupled system communication.
Design and develop
modern
cloud-native solutions
for all IT Integration domain, utilizing microservices, containerization, and event-driven architectures as the foundation for this new IT Integration.
Mentor technical teams
and establish
best practices
to build organizational capability in the new IT integration solution.
Collaborate closely with DevSecOps teams
to design and maintain robust CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and deployment strategies supporting continuous delivery of integration components.
Ensure architectural alignment
between design, development, and operations through API-first and cloud-native design practices.
