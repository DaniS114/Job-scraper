Join Our Energy Team – Shape the Future of Smart Grids!
Are you passionate about power systems and innovative energy solutions? Do you want to work on projects that keep the lights on for millions of people? We are looking for a Junior AGC/EMS Consultant to join our dynamic team and help configure cutting-edge control systems for transmission and distribution networks. If you enjoy solving complex challenges and working with advanced technologies, this is the role for you!
EXPECTED STARTING DATE:
Immediately
WORK LOCATION (CITY, COUNTRY):

EDUCATION - DIPLOMAS REQUIRED:
University degree in Electrical Power Engineering, Informatics or relevant experience
LANGUAGES:
English knowledge in spoken and written B2
JOB DESCRIPTION (RESPONSIBILITIES, TASKS AND OTHER SPECIFIC FEATURES OF THE POSITION):
Configure AGC and EMS applications (including State Estimation, Contingency Analysis)
Build and maintain the distribution network model (CIM-based)
Perform parameter tuning, topology validation, and data integrations
Conduct FAT/SAT testing, prepare documentation, and provide operational support
Deliver AGC/EMS system service and maintenance
Communicate effectively with customers and suppliers
Prepare technical documentation and training sessions
​
PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE:
Minimum 2 years of experience in the energy sector, specifically in power generation and distribution
Hands-on experience with mathematical modeling and network analysis of electrical grids, including optimization tools for grid operation
Background in development, implementation, or operation of automated control systems for electrical networks (distribution and transmission level)
Knowledge of:
Energy Management Systems (EMS) and AGC for Transmission System Operators
Electrical network analysis and energy market business systems
Real-time IT systems implementation
