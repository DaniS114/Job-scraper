About this position
We are looking for a candidate passionate about solving complex challenges at the intersection of 3D computer vision and robotics. We are seeking a Robotics Software Developer to be a pivotal part of our team, taking our state-of-the-art Vision Guided Robotics (VGR) solutions to the next level. This is a unique opportunity for a creative and analytical mind to dive deep into research and development, shaping the future of vision guided robotics.
You will take ownership of our powerful robotic intelligence software, a core component of our technology that is used to set up and provision robotic applications in the bin picking domain.
Location
Bratislava, Slovakia

Key Responsibilities:
Contribute to and shape all stages of our robotic intelligence software’s development, spanning research, design, and implementation.
Design and implement advanced algorithms for robot guidance, collision avoidance, path planning.
Collaborate with our world-class R&D team to integrate your solutions into our product portfolio.
Develop and maintain high-quality, robust, and efficient code in C++ and Python.
Contribute to our knowledge base by documenting your research, experiments, and results.
Requirements:
Strong background in mathematics, computer science, physics, robotics, cybernetics or a related STEM field (M.S. or Ph.D. preferred).
Proven programming experience in C++ and Python.
Solid understanding of the robotic control domain.
Hands-on experience with ROS and libraries providing robot motion planning (e.g. MoveIt)
Familiarity with topics like collision objects, virtual robot representation, joints, coordinate transformations.
Ability to work in a Linux environment with Docker containers.
A curious and analytical mindset, with a passion for solving challenging problems.
Fluency in English.
