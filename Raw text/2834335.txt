Who we are
VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.
As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth.
About this role
We are seeking a Creative Designer who can deliver visually compelling digital and print materials that reflect VOIS’s brand identity and business objectives. The individual will independently manage multiple projects, collaborate with cross‑functional teams, and support colleagues through constructive peer guidance. This role is ideal for someone who excels in producing high‑quality, design‑led solutions, enjoys working with modern design tools and CMS platforms, and values continuous improvement within a fast‑moving environment.
What you will do
Manage and deliver a wide range of design projects, ensuring all outputs meet brand standards and business expectations.
Translate creative briefs into engaging design concepts across digital, print, and multimedia channels.
Produce design assets for marketing, digital commerce, internal communications, sales enablement, and campaign activity.
Collaborate with internal partners to maintain brand consistency across all materials.
Maintain a well‑structured design asset library, ensuring efficient reuse and continuous improvement of content.
Support colleagues by providing constructive feedback and participating in peer‑review processes.
Contribute to ideation sessions, presentations, and cycles of creative feedback.
Keep up to date with digital design trends, new technologies, and emerging AI‑based tools.
Who you are
You have 5+ years’ experience in digital or creative design within an agency or corporate setting.
You are fluent in English and confident in your written and verbal communication.
You are proficient in Adobe Creative Suite and have at least two years’ experience working with Figma
You bring strong knowledge of digital design principles, CMS workflows, and AI‑enhanced design practices.
You are highly organised, detail‑oriented, collaborative, and proactive.
You have experience supporting colleagues or mentoring junior designers.
You can present a portfolio and demonstrate proficiency working on Mac platforms and in Agile environments.
What’s in it for you
The opportunity to work within a global organisation that values creativity, innovation, and inclusive collaboration.
Exposure to a diverse portfolio of projects across multiple business areas, enabling professional growth and creative range.
Access to modern design tools, digital platforms, and emerging AI technologies that support continuous learning.
A workplace culture that encourages experimentation, constructive feedback, and the freedom to bring new ideas forward.
The chance to work alongside international colleagues, broadening cross‑cultural collaboration skills and industry knowledge.
What skills you will learn
Advanced digital design techniques:
Deepen your expertise in creating high‑impact digital assets, from web components to multimedia visuals.
AI‑enhanced design workflows:
Gain hands‑on experience integrating generative design tools, automation, and AI‑powered creative processes.
Enhanced collaboration and stakeholder engagement:
Strengthen your ability to interpret briefs, present creative concepts, and align deliverables with business needs.
Design system and CMS optimisation:
Learn how to structure, maintain, and scale design libraries, templates, and content management workflows.
Creative leadership fundamentals:
Develop skills in peer review, mentoring, and supporting others through structured, constructive feedback.
