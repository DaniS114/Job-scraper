Responsibilities
We are expanding our Budapest engineering hub and seeking a
Senior Java Developer with strong AWS experience
to help integrate three internally built products, each with its own tech stack, into a scalable, modern, cloud-native platform. This role is key to enabling X-Chem to fully leverage our data assets and advanced AI tools in support of next-generation scientific workflows.
Hybrid role with flexible working hours, based in a modern and inspiring workspace at Graphisoft Park in Budapest’s 3rd district.
Develop and enhance cloud-native backend services using modern frameworks to support scalable scientific and AI workflows.
Integrate three existing in-house products built on different technology stacks into a unified, cohesive platform that improves interoperability and data flow.
Modernize targeted legacy components where appropriate, refactoring or rebuilding selected parts to align with current best practices and cloud standards.
Design and implement microservices and APIs that connect new and existing systems across chemistry, analytics, production, and logistics workflows.
Work within event-driven and AWS-native architectures (EKS, S3, Lambda, containers, etc.) to support robust and efficient system interactions.
Integrate machine learning models and data pipelines into production environments.
Bridge older and newer technology stacks (Java, Python, C# , JSF, HTML, React), ensuring smooth transitions and consistent user experiences.
Lead architectural planning and platform strategy discussions, helping determine when to extend, integrate, or modernize components.
Improve performance, reliability, and maintainability of both existing systems and newly developed services.
Work closely with cross-functional scientific teams to translate domain requirements into scalable cloud-based solutions.

Enjoy a mission-driven environment where technology directly impacts human health.
