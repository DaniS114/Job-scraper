Job description, responsibilities and duties
Inalogy
provides managed IAM services, and you will be part of our Infrastructure Team. Your main responsibilities will include managing, deploying, monitoring, and evaluating our solutions in a cloud-based infrastructure.
We are looking for an experienced System Administrator with a proactive mindset, who is eager to continuously improve our systems and motivated to learn and gain new skills.
What would be your competences:
Monitor system performance, troubleshoot issues, and implement solutions to prevent recurrence (Grafana)​
​Diagnose and resolve technical issues related to software, hardware, or network problems​
Escalate complex issues to appropriate engineering teams with detailed information
Monitor system health and proactively identify potential issues (Wazuh)
Controll and automate deployment processes and configuration management with tools Jenkins, FreeIPA, Terraform, Ansible
​Ensure security best practices across all infrastructure and deployment processes
Collaborate with development teams to improve application reliability and deployment speed​
Document all customer interactions, solutions, and known issues in the knowledge base
​Automate deployment processes and configuration management with tools like Terraform
Participate in 24/7 on-call rotation, providing incident response and customer support
Provide first-line technical support via email, chat, phone, and ticketing system
