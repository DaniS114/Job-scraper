Key Responsibilities & Tasks
Maintain comprehensive knowledge of the Finance solution throughout all project stages to ensure consistency and operational excellence.
Oversee the day to day functioning of Oracle Fusion Finance modules, including General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and Fixed Assets, and ensure stable financial operations.
Design and implement monitoring and alerting mechanisms. Resolve financial system issues promptly and coordinate error handling and resubmissions during testing, cutover, hypercare, and ongoing operational cycles.
Validate and manage integrations between Oracle Fusion Finance and external platforms, ensuring reliable connectivity and accurate financial data flow across all connected systems.
Lead the transition from Oracle E Business Suite Finance to Oracle Fusion Finance, ensuring smooth operational handover and minimal disruption.
Conduct internal knowledge transfer sessions within the Finance team to build capability and ensure alignment.
Perform operational acceptance testing, including financial performance validation, to confirm system readiness prior to go live.
