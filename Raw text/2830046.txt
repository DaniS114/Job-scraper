Company info
Jiangsu Changshu Automotive Trim Group Co., LTD (CAIP) was established in 1996, and was listed in Shanghai Stock Exchange in 2017 (stock code 603035). It has established joint ventures with Peguform (Germany), Antolin (Spain), Greiner (Austria), Magna (Canada), and the WAY Group of Germany, endeavoring to become a core supplier of the more valuable products for whole automobile enterprises, to provide clients with integrated services "from project engineering development and design to mold design and manufacturing, equipment automatic solutions, product testing and verification as well as cost optimization solutions".
