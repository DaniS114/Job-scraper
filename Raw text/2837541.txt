Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Your Responsibilities
Design, implement, and enhance SAP HCM functionalities with a primary focus on Payroll (DE/CH/AT), Time Evaluation, and Pension-related processes.
Analyze, troubleshoot, and optimize payroll results, time evaluation logic, and cluster data.
Develop enhancements, user exits, BADIs, and various integration interfaces (RFC, IDoc, proxies, APIs).
Provide support for Payroll Year-End activities, operational follow-up tasks, and issue resolution.
Work on integrations with SuccessFactors Employee Central and external systems (experience with CPI is an advantage).
Handle incidents and change requests using standard ITSM and agile tools (e.g., ServiceNow, JIRA), including effort estimation and implementation.
Collaborate with technical teams on system maintenance, upgrades, patches, and testing cycles.
Offer expert guidance to business stakeholders to ensure process reliability, compliance, and continuous improvement.
Participate in agile ceremonies and contribute to development best practices.
Requirements
Your Profile
At least 5 years of hands-on experience in ABAP/ABAP OO, HR-Forms, and complex SAP HCM development.
Strong knowledge of Payroll schemas, PCRs, Time Evaluation, and cluster analysis.
Familiarity with S/4HANA-based HCM solutions (H4S4), HANA DB, Fiori, or modernization initiatives is a plus.
Solid understanding of integration technologies: CPI, RFC, IDoc, API-based communication.
Experience with payroll specifics for German-speaking countries (DE/CH/AT) is beneficial.
Ability to mentor junior colleagues and contribute to coding standards and quality assurance.
Excellent communication skills, proactive mindset, and solution-oriented approach.
Fluency in English (German is a huge plus)
