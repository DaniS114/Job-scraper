Responsibilities
CTC Analytics AG () is an internationally active Swiss technology company in the field of laboratory automation with around 140 employees. For 40 years, the company has been a leader in automated sample preparation for gas and liquid chromatography (). Our products are used worldwide in analytical laboratories across the life sciences, food industry, environmental analysis, chemical industry, and research sectors. We are a highly successful employer within this environment, offering a family‑like atmosphere, short decision-making paths, and an uncomplicated, supportive way of working together.
Our team now has a new position where you can take on responsibility and contribute directly to innovative solutions in laboratory automation.
Product Owner – Driver Integration (80–100%)
Your Responsibilities
Own the product strategy and roadmap for instrument driver integrations across multiple CDS platforms
Coordinate product planning and execution with distributed development teams in Europe and North America
Define clear requirements and acceptance criteria for internal driver implementations
Support instrument vendors and customers in developing and integrating drivers
Ensure interoperability, validation, and regulatory compliance of internal and external drivers
Collaborate closely with development, product management, regulatory teams, and external partners
Your Profile
Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Chemistry/Analytical Sciences, or comparable experience
Experience as a Product Owner, Technical Product Manager, or in a similar role in a laboratory or analytical environment
Good knowledge of chromatography systems (HPLC, UHPLC, GC) and analytical instrumentation
Ability to understand and discuss driver?level software code
Experience with CDS platforms (e.g. Empower, Chromeleon, OpenLab, LabSolutions) is a strong plus
Familiarity with regulated environments (ISO 13485, EU Cyber Resilience Act, EU Data Act)
Strong communication skills and confidence working with customers and external vendors
Fluent in English; German is a plus
Willingness to collaborate internationally and travel occasionally
Your Environment:
Technically challenging and meaningful projects at the intersection of software, laboratory instrumentation, and regulated environments
Close collaboration with international teams and leading instrument vendors
Short decision-making paths and a collaborative, supportive working culture
High degree of autonomy, ownership, and room for professional growth
Stable and future-oriented company with a strong focus on innovation and long-term development
