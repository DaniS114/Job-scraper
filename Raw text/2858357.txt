Responsibilities
Define and improve metrics reflecting product performance, service quality, and operational efficiency
Analyze client behavior and service processes to identify trends, bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and drivers of performance
Work on problems related to attrition / churn, customer behavior, operational effectiveness, and service improvement
Collect data from multiple sources and ensure its quality, accuracy, and completeness
Clean, structure, and transform raw data into datasets suitable for advanced analysis
Conduct deep-dive analysis and research to uncover patterns and generate actionable insights
Design and interpret A/B tests and other analytical frameworks for measuring impact
Apply causal inference methods to understand what truly drives observed business outcomes
Assess solutions through backtesting, experimentation, and modeling
Build analytical models to support decision-making and evaluate opportunities for improvement
Create visualizations, reports, and presentations for stakeholders
Support advanced analytical initiatives, including work related to LLM-enabled internal solutions, such as insight extraction, text analysis, or knowledge support use cases, where relevant
Partner with Data Engineers and cross-functional teams to improve the data landscape and analytical infrastructure
Requirements
4+ years of hands-on experience in Data Analytics / Data Science / Analytics Research
Strong command of SQL and Python
Solid understanding of mathematical statistics and probability theory
Strong experience with A/B testing, experiment design, and interpretation of results
Good practical understanding of causal inference
Experience with machine learning and/or mathematical modeling in applied business problems
Ability to move from raw data to structured insights and business recommendations
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a high level of intellectual curiosity
Team player with strong communication and stakeholder management skills
Structured and analytical thinker, detail-oriented, results-driven
Fast learner, able to work independently in a dynamic environment
Creative problem-solver with adaptability to changing requirements
