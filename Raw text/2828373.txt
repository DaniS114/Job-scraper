Company info
DXC Technology is a global IT services leader that helps clients harness the power of innovation to thrive on change. With a presence in more than 70 countries, DXC Technology provides end-to-end IT services and solutions to businesses across various industries. Founded in 2017 through the merger of CSC and the Enterprise Services business of Hewlett Packard Enterprise, DXC Technology combines deep industry expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver digital transformation and operational excellence for its clients.
