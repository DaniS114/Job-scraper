Responsibilities
Within the Information Systems department of the ODDO BHF Group, you will be responsible for designing client lifecycle workflows in a digital environment for all of the group's business units (private wealth management in France and Germany, independent financial advisors, ODDO services etc).
Designing Client Lifecycle workflows for various Group business lines
Conducting functional and technical design, acceptance tests, and projects reporting
Deploying projects internally and coordinating teams
Regular monitoring and staying attentive to digital opportunities and impacts
Having advanced knowledge of new technology culture and digital maturity
Encouraging innovation and maintaining links with different group departments
To fulfill these tasks, you will be in regular contact with all business units in France, Germany, and the subsidiaries, with the COOs of the group's business units, with the compliance and legal departments, with the group's DPO, and the IT Security and Innovation department.
Requirements
In this responsible position, which combines operations and project management, you can further develop your skills in an entrepreneurial and international context. As part of the department's regular activities, several trips to France and Tunisia are to be expected throughout the year.
A degree in engineering or business (or equivalent)
Significant experience in IT and advanced knowledge of new technology culture and digital maturity
Sound knowledge of digital project management
Knowledge of the customer lifecycle, KYC, and finance
You are proactive and enjoy working independently
You are known for your organizational skills, ambition, and precision Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Excellent English and French skills, German would be a plus

Company info
ODDO BHF is an independent European financial group active in the areas of Private Wealth Management, Asset Management and Corporates & Investment Banking. The Group benefits from its strong Franco-German roots and its strategic presence in Switzerland and currently manages client assets of 140 billion euros. ODDO BHF has around 3,000 employees and a unique shareholder structure that ensures its independence. 90 percent of the shares are hold by the Oddo family and employees.
