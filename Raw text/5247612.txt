About
the position
As a Solution Designer, you perform requirement analyses while taking specific business needs and implications into account and develop business and process models within the scope of Agentic AI and AI solutions, including alignment with relevant stakeholders.
Your main responsibilities include:
Contributing to Agentic AI and AI solution concepts for the further development of virtual or physical products and services, considering specific business requirements
Assessing requirements, integration into technology solutions, and technological implementation options (bridging business and technology), as well as clarifying current operational prerequisites and the use of existing interfaces with stakeholders
Creating technical design documents (e.g., functional specifications) as well as cost benefit analyses, primarily for Agentic AI and AI solutions • Supporting the technical implementation, testing, and approval processes (including necessary project management tasks and documentation) in collaboration with relevant departments and/or external service providers
Leading projects
We work in a HYBRID STYLE of working with the possibility of working from home , but twice a week we meet in the office in Košice or Bratislava for meetings.
The published salary is the minimum possible offer.
