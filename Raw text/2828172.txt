Állás leírása
Responsibilities
You will be closely working with our finance and controlling departments, locally and internationally, to understand their requirements and to provide a best practice approach for their business needs
You will support the decision and implementation process by developing and providing a risk-benefit analysis
You will lead (sub-)projects as a technical leader and in the meaning of a trusted advisor
The configuration and further development of the SAP modules FI and CO has focus in your job
You are part of various projects, such as the introduction of SAP S4HANA, System-Rollouts and Go-Directs, as well as the support for value flows from SAP for PCA/CO-PA via BW to SAC
You are passionate about what you do and you are willing to share your knowledge with the business and the SAP Key Users by providing regular trainings
Providing process documentation is a must for you and enables continuity and system integrity
Support of key users in day-to-day operations is part of your job, as well as 1st and 2nd Level support
Requirements
You have a degree in economics, business informatics, business administration or a comparable degree
You are very experienced in end-to-end value flows and you have a very deep knowledge in process design
You have a professional working experience of 3-5 years in a consulting company or an inhouse position
You have in-depth knowledge of SAP modules CO-OM, CO-PC, CO-PA, PCA and Production Controlling including customizing
Your excellent knowledge of interfaces to the SAP modules SD, MM, PS, PM and PP are mandatory
Knowledge of other SAP modules and the ABAP programming language are desirable
SAP S/4HANA or practical knowledge in the area of SAP BW or planning and reporting solutions in general are an advantage
Working in interdisciplinary and intercultural teams is our daily business
A proactive personality paired with strong communication skills, as well as a confident, convincing demeanor and assertiveness complete your profile
Very good knowledge of German and English is part of your assets
What we offer
Broad variety of tasks and freedom to act
Plenty of opportunities to challenge and drive things forward
Enjoying taking time off together with motivated and professional colleagues
The chance to shape a loved brand that makes people smile all around the world
An established and growing company with a strong reputation
A fair compensation and many cool benefits
