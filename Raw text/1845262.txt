Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner is bringing their impressive global success story to Hungary, building a brand-new Global Business Center in Budapest. They are looking for a Senior Information Security Manager to lead the governance and strategy of their security function.
They offer a unique environment that prioritizes an inclusive culture and service excellence from day one. In this role, you will shape the security strategy within a collaborative, supportive, and social international team. It is a rare chance to join a community that values teamwork, celebrates success, and genuinely enjoys working together to build a fresh operation.
Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)
Own and maintain the ISO 27001 ISMS framework, ensuring continuous improvement and readiness for external audits.
Manage the corporate risk register, conducting regular risk assessments and reporting to senior leadership.
Develop and enforce global information security policies and standards.
Strategic Leadership & Culture
Design and deliver security awareness training programs to foster a security-conscious culture across the organization.
Act as the primary advisor to the business on security matters, bridging the gap between technical risks and business impact.
Manage client expectations regarding security assurance and oversee third-party vendor risk management.
Operational Oversight
Provide high-level oversight for the incident management process, acting as an escalation point for critical security events.
Collaborate with Legal and IT teams to ensure compliance with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR).
Elvárások / Requirements
You have extensive experience in Information Security Management, ideally in a regulated industry (Financial Services is a plus).
You hold advanced certifications such as CISSP, CISM, or CISA.
Proven track record as an ISO 27001 Lead Implementer or Auditor.
Strong experience in Risk Management frameworks and Business Continuity Planning.
Business language fluency in English with exceptional communication skills to present to C-level executives.
