Responsibilities
Work with clients and the global Orchestro team.
Join a cross-functional squad owning an end-to-end slice of Orchestro platform.
Anchor high-impact initiatives bridging product vision with technical execution.
Prototype solution options, articulate trade-offs, and guide team toward architectures.
Own critical services, model testing, observability, and security practices.
Drive complex refactors to keep platform scalable and maintainable.
Lead post-incident reviews and automate guardrails for continuous delivery.
Mentor engineers of every level and share learnings across McKinsey’s global tech community.
Shape product roadmap and technical strategy of Orchestro platform.
Collaborate with domain-driven-design and platform-engineering experts.
Requirements
5+ years of experience delivering and operating production systems.
Strong TypeScript/JavaScript skills with proficiency in Node.js & React.
Experience designing scalable solutions around bounded contexts and event-driven or API-based integrations.
Deep familiarity with automated testing strategies, CI/CD, and Kubernetes deployments.
Demonstrated record of mentoring engineers and driving cross-team technical initiatives.
Fluent English for day-to-day collaboration and stakeholder discussions.
What we offer
Continuous learning culture with structured programs and clear, actionable feedback.
A voice that matters from day one with opportunities to make tangible impact.
Global community with colleagues across 65+ countries and over 100 nationalities.
World-class benefits including competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries with a diverse workforce of over 30,000 professionals. The firm is committed to helping clients make distinctive, lasting, and substantial improvements in their performance and building a great firm that attracts, develops, excites, and retains exceptional people.
