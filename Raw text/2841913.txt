Responsibilities
Mission & main responsibilities:
Provide both strategic and tactical direction and leadership excellence for all finance and controlling processes as well as reporting within our SAP template
Lead workshops to design future finance and controlling processes and educate the users
Interface with business key users to gather and document business requirements
Obtain sign-off and validation of the future finance and controlling processes from business key users
Liaise with the SAP functional consultants in mapping the requirements into the S4 system
Define and document the detailed future process flows and business requirements
Be responsible for business process alignment and improvement, across Dynapac entities including the standardization of reporting in S4 as well as the reporting tools
Take responsibility for maintaining the integrity of the global process throughout the project lifecycle, and establishing a robust life cycle management governance
Act as key liaison between the operational business key users and the Center of Excellence team
Drive change management to implement new business practices
Prepare and guide key users in the new ways of working in the new and improved ERP's and applications implemented
Secure the deployment of the finance and controlling process supported by the S4 solution from a time, innovation, cost and quality perspective
Create a “willingness to change“ atmosphere with the key users

Company info
Dynapac is a leading supplier of high-tech soil and asphalt rollers, light equipment and pavers, committed to strengthen customer performance by being a partner on the road ahead. Dynapac is represented worldwide via its own regional sales- and service offices, and cooperates with an extensive and professional distribution network. Headquartered in Wardenburg, Germany, Dynapac has production facilities in Europe, South America, and Asia. Dynapac is part of the FAYAT Group.
We are looking for a successor of our
GLOBAL BUSINESS PROCESS MANAGER – FINANCE & CONTROLLING (m/f/d)
Dynapac Business Process Manager (BPM) is a functional/business position focused on the main stream business processes (Finance & Controlling) of SAP S/4HANA and the relevant applications used within the Dynapac Product Companies (PC´s), Customer Centers (CC´s) and Distribution Centers (DDC´s).
The BPM will have a full understanding and responsibility of a specific domain and will be able to create common, harmonized solutions that are shared by all entities while eliminating irrelevant applications and other solutions currently in use, processes from end-to-end.
You are a key member within the Dynapac IT team with global responsibility and report directly to the Head of Enterprise Applications.
