Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced Project Manager to lead and coordinate ERP implementation projects, primarily focused on Odoo solutions. You will act as the central point of coordination between clients, consultants, developers, and business stakeholders, ensuring smooth delivery and high-quality outcomes.
This role requires strong organizational skills, proactive communication, and the ability to keep projects on track while maintaining visibility over timelines, budgets, and deliverables.
Manage end-to-end ERP implementation projects (planning, execution, monitoring, delivery)
Communicate regularly with clients, including handling expectations, change requests
Schedule and coordinate meetings with customers
Monitor project progress, timelines, and resource allocation
Maintain a clear overview of all ongoing projects and upcoming steps
Ensure timely delivery of inputs from both internal teams and clients (follow-ups, escalation when needed)
Actively coordinate across teams (consultants, developers, sales, and other stakeholders)
Track project budgets and hours spent (e.g., analysis vs. allocated time)
Identify overconsumption of hours and address it with the team
Provide regular reporting on project status, costs, and performance to management
Manage SLA-based projects and transition to helpdesk support where applicable
Ensure transparency on project profitability (under/over budget) and support invoicing decisions
