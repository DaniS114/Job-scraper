Job description, responsibilities and duties
BUSINESS KOŠICE, n.o.
is expanding its team and is looking for a Data Analyst to
take part in the implementation of the “Innovative Košice 2030” project, funded by EU funds (ERDF).
Your responsibilities:
Management, analysis, and visualization of public and economic data within the implementation of the project’s main activities (mapping the business environment, Open Data Platform, development of the city development action plan).
Preparation of data analyses and outputs for the creation of strategic documents and for supporting the innovation ecosystem in the Košice region.
Collection, processing, and analysis of economic and socio-economic data for the Annual Economic Profile of the City of Košice (“Košice in Numbers”) as a key project output.
Creation and development of the city’s economic data bank within the Košice Open Data Platform - technical preparation, processing, and publishing of data.
Professional cooperation with partners from the public, private, academic, and civic sectors in data collection, validation, and processing within the main project activities.
Development of interactive dashboards, analytical reports, and visualizations for both technical and non-technical audiences as project outputs.
Technical support in preparing publications and presentations using data analyses and visualizations.
