Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a
Senior QA Engineer
to join a dynamic, product-focused team. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring the quality, reliability, and performance of software solutions through both manual and automated testing. You’ll work closely with cross-functional teams and a US-based Quality Lead to design effective testing strategies and continuously improve QA processes.
Key Responsibilities
Create detailed, comprehensive, and well-structured test plans and test cases
Review system requirements, specifications, and technical design documents, providing timely and meaningful feedback
Estimate, prioritize, plan, and coordinate testing activities across projects
Design, develop, and execute automated test scripts
Identify, document, track, and verify software defects throughout the lifecycle
Perform thorough regression testing after defect resolution
Collaborate with a US-based Quality Lead to develop and apply testing processes for new and existing products
Coordinate with stakeholders to clarify and validate system requirements
Monitor debugging results and investigate the root causes of defects
Support users in implementing solutions for non-conforming software
Track and analyze QA metrics such as defect density and open defect counts
Stay current with modern testing tools, frameworks, and QA best practices
