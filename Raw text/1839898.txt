Pozíció leírása / Job description
The Company is a leading supplier of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), spun out from Sanofi on May 6, 2022 and listed on EURONEXT, with the aim of becoming a global champion in API solutions and CDMO. The company has a diversified portfolio of approximately 200 APIs for its API Solutions business and its CDMO (Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization) activities. With more than 150 years of experience in the API market, around 3,350 employees and a consolidated turnover of approximately €1 billion in 2023, The company has six production sites and development centers equipped with state-of-the-art technology, all located in Europe (France, Germany, Hungary, Italy and the UK).
This role is a
senior IT & OT Network Engineer
position responsible for designing, deploying, and operating secure network infrastructure (LAN/WAN/WLAN, firewalls, SD-WAN) across global pharma manufacturing sites, in line with corporate standards. Strong experience in
regulated production environments
, network validation, and collaboration with IT, OT, and business stakeholders is essential.
The Network engineer activities include WAN, WLAN, LAN, FW & Automation projects and support for all its entities in Europe, US & Japan.
The Network engineer will be integrated into a small team to cover network projects (SD-WAN, LAN Refresh, etc.) and Day-to-day operations.
Define sourcing model aligned with company and ensure knowledge is kept internally.
Ensure ROI of the sourcing model meets technologies requirements & budget efficiency
Ensure network solutions are easy to deploy in the event of onboarding new companies
Participate in ensuring all Pharma compliances are met.
Participate in ensuring innovation automation processes – continuous improvement
Collaborate with Group IT expert to the definition of Network Standards and SOPs
Configuring and maintaining network devices such as routers, switches, and firewalls, according to Corporate Standards and SOPs,
Planning and executing network upgrades, expansions, and migrations in close collaboration with Group IT
Ensuring network infrastructure adheres to relevant GxP regulations and guidelines
Participating in network validation activities and maintaining validation documentation
Collaborating with cross-functional teams
Providing technical support and guidance to users and other team members
Possibilities of On-call duty
