We are home to people from all over the world, from different backgrounds, with diverse talents and specialist areas. What binds us together is our passion for excellence, our dedication to our mission of improving patient care, and our understanding that we are stronger together: we are one AO.For more information, visit: /Senior Manager IT Service Delivery & Operations (ID1879)Short DescriptionThe IT Service Delivery Manager is responsible for ensuring the reliable, efficient, and high-quality delivery of IT services and operations across the organization, managing a work force of currently 17 persons in 3 teams, for an organization with about 500 employees world-wide. This role combines strategic oversight of service delivery with hands-on management of IT infrastructure, systems, network and support functions. The manager ensures that IT services meet business needs, comply with SLAs, and continuously improve in performance and value.IT Infrastructure & Operations at the AO Foundation contains the operation of around 100 applications, majority running on MS Azure, network services, data archiving and storage.
