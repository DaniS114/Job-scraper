Responsibilities
Conduct research in automated software engineering, including LLM-based code representation, automatic program repair, and generative AI for test generation.
Design next-generation user interfaces and architectures for AI coding agents.
Provide technical leadership and collaborate with global R&D teams to develop novel software development technologies.
Publish research results in top-tier academic venues and contribute to applied innovations for commercial use.
Mentor junior researchers and interns, fostering a culture of experimentation and innovation.
Requirements
Ph.D. (or equivalent) in Computer Science, AI, Machine Learning, or a related field.
Proven research track record, with publications in leading AI or software engineering conferences/journals.
Strong programming and system design skills, including Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Docker, Linux, and GitHub/GitLab.
Hands-on experience with LLM/RAG technologies, deep learning, and AI model development.
Knowledge of knowledge graphs or graph databases (e.g., Neo4j) is highly desirable.
Excellent communication and presentation skills in English.
Nice-to-have
Understanding of LLM agents, explainable AI, and graph ML., Familiarity with GPU-based training, cloud environments, and scalable ML infrastructures., Experience bridging research innovation and commercial product development.
What we offer
Competitive salary and benefits aligned with experience.
Opportunity to work on high-impact global AI research projects.
Collaborative and inclusive international environment.
Professional growth and support for continued research publication.
