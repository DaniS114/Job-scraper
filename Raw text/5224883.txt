Purpose
The Senior Power Platform & Analytics Enablement Engineer is responsible for the engineering, governance, and enablement of Microsoft Power Platform solutions within a Cloud Data & Analytics ecosystem based on Azure Lakehouse architecture. This role acts as a bridge between Self-Service BI teams and the governed Azure backend, ensuring analytics solutions are reliable, secure, scalable, and compliant while supporting enterprise data-driven decision-making.
Team / Project Description
The Cloud Data & Analytics team is responsible for delivering a scalable Azure Lakehouse platform and enabling enterprise-wide self-service analytics through the Microsoft Power Platform. The team collaborates closely with business, governance, and engineering stakeholders to ensure secure, compliant, and high-quality data products across international teams.
What will you do?
Design, implement, and operate Microsoft Power Platform solutions including Power BI, Power Apps, Power Automate, and SharePoint Online in alignment with enterprise standards.
Act as the technical bridge between Self-Service BI teams and governed Azure Lakehouse backends, enabling secure and reliable analytics solutions.
Configure and manage Power BI Service and Desktop environments, including workspaces, datasets, gateways, refresh schedules, and capacity planning.
Establish and enforce governance, security, and lifecycle management standards across Power Platform solutions, covering access control, privacy, and compliance requirements.
Integrate Power Platform solutions with Cloud Data & Analytics services such as Azure Data Factory, Databricks, dbt, and CDC frameworks.
Manage identity and access using Azure AD / Entra ID, RBAC, service principals, and secure data connections.
Monitor platform usage, performance, cost efficiency, and data reliability, proactively identifying optimization opportunities.
Diagnose and resolve platform incidents in collaboration with engineering, governance, and operations teams.
Manage and optimize data refresh cycles to ensure trusted and consistent analytics outputs.
Develop and maintain platform documentation, operational playbooks, and governance guidelines.
Coach and enable self-service BI teams, translating complex technical concepts into clear, actionable guidance for non-technical stakeholders.
Collaborate in agile, international environments (SAFe, Scrum) with cross-functional teams.
Support audits, governance reviews, and enterprise BI compliance initiatives.
Drive continuous improvement, automation, and operational excellence across the analytics platform.
