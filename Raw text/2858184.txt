Responsibilities
Trend Scout & Strategist:
You will monitor global shifts in tech, sustainability, and digital trends within the sanitary sector. You'll distill these findings into sharp problem statements and strategic opportunity briefs.
AI Knowledge Architect:
You will help build and structure a cutting-edge, AI-supported innovation knowledge base using tools like Gemini and NotebookLM to keep the team ahead of the curve.
Innovation Storyteller:
You will translate abstract insights into early-stage concept documentation. You'll create user journeys and narratives that visualize "future-state" experiences and clearly communicate the innovation value.
Technical Tinkerer:
You will get hands-on with experimental setups to validate technical hypotheses.
Early-stage Prototyper:
You will support the birth of new mechanical, electronic, and fluid technology concepts through 3D printing, building and testing low-fidelity prototypes, functional demonstrators, and workshop activities.
User-Centric Tester:
You will support scenario testing with real users and conduct rigorous lab tests, documenting the results to ensure our innovations are both functional and human-centered.
Requirements
Enrolled student:
You have a technical, creative, or business background, e.g., in Engineering, Innovation Management, Industrial Design, Data Science, UX Management, or related fields.
Deeply Empathetic:
You have a genuine passion for understanding user needs, pain points, and behaviors through active listening and keen observation.
Analytical Problem-Solver:
You can synthesize complex user feedback and data into clear, unbiased, and actionable insights.
Curious & Creative:
You naturally question the status quo and bring "out-of-the-box" ideas to the table.
Resilient & Adaptable:
You thrive in a fast-paced, agile environment, viewing rapid iterations and setbacks as learning opportunities.
Proactive "Doer":
You bring a hands-on mentality, taking ownership of your tasks and independently exploring new methods or tools.
Strong Communicator:
You can convincingly translate your research findings into compelling stories for diverse stakeholders.
What we offer

Health-Conscious:
Access our comprehensive health management system, including an on-site fitness room, digital health platforms, and "Health Cashback" incentives.
Inclusive & Supportive:
Join a culture of Diversity & Inclusion where everyone receives equal opportunities and tailored individual support to grow.
Perk-Savy:
Enjoy a wide range of exclusive employee discounts on travel, fashion, media, mobile plans, and more.
Part of a Global Team:
Work within a highly motivated, appreciative, and international team designed to inspire you and accelerate your learning.
Fuelled for Success:
Our modern company restaurant provides fresh, high-quality meals daily to ensure your well-being throughout the workday.
Company info
Are you keen on identifying and solving real user-related problems? We are an international team that drives transformative and radical innovation within our global footprint for future sanitary systems. We will provide benefits in enhanced wellbeing, user experience, sustainability and quality appearance. We are looking for a motivated working student, with a generalist perspective on identifying and solving real problems to support the activities within our Global Research Lab, located in Hemer (NRW). Your role will involve both general assistance and working independently on specified tasks and themes.
You would fit best if you're interested in the following fields plus having a special focus in some of them.
