Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a Platform Compliance Manager with a
strong focus on audits
. In this role, you drive the planning, execution, and successful closure of platform, security, and regulatory audits
across multiple markets
. You’ll interpret compliance requirements, translate them into actionable technical solutions, and ensure correct implementation together with development teams.
A technical foundation is highly advantageous, as you will be discussing proposed solutions, validating implementations, and maintaining clear documentation throughout the audit lifecycle.
Your tasks:
Lead external and internal audits, including preparation, documentation, interviews, and explanation of technical implementations
Review audit reports, address findings, and coordinate corrective actions with development teams
Interpret regulatory requirements and communicate them clearly to technical teams for implementation
Work with legal and compliance stakeholders to clarify regulatory questions and ensure alignment
Guide the technical implementation of approved solutions and verify compliance before sign-off
Prepare and maintain audit-relevant documentation (e.g., evidence, technical descriptions, license submissions)
Support investigations and problem-solving on compliance issues, offering clear and concise explanations
Keep stakeholders up to date on status, risks, and progress throughout audit and implementation processes
