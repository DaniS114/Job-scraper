Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, configure, and enhance SAP PP (Production Planning) processes in S/4HANA Public Cloud
Participate in S/4 rollouts, ensuring consistent global template adoption and local fit-to-standard workshops
Support clients in implementing best-practice production processes (MTS, MTO, discrete/serial production, planning strategies)
Provide functional expertise for PP-related integrations (MM, QM, PM, SD)
Document configurations, perform testing activities, and support go-lives and hypercare
Identify improvement opportunities and contribute to digital transformation initiatives within the manufacturing domain
Work closely with global cross-functional teams, business stakeholders, and technical experts
Requirements
5+ years of experience as an SAP PP Consultant
Strong hands-on experience in SAP S/4HANA (Public Cloud preferred; Private Cloud also valuable)
Proven experience with fit-to-standard processes and global rollout/template projects
Solid understanding of core PP processes: Production planning & control, Master data (BOMs, routings, work centers), MRP & planning strategies
Experience with PP integrations (MM, QM, SD, PM)
Ability to translate business needs into scalable, cloud-ready PP solutions
Proactive problem-solving and strong communication skills
Advanced English (German or another EU language is an advantage)
What we offer
Participation in full-cycle and diverse international projects
Opportunity to work as a specialist or manager/team lead
Constant career development with internal mentorship
Chance to leading-edge functional and technical SAP trainings
Access to a whole set of learning platforms, paid certifications
Flexible working conditions, home working is allowed
Attractive base salary & Wide range of benefits included cafeteria, bonuses, private health insurance package, life insurance, AYCM sport card, referral bonus, family-oriented benefits, company shares on a discount price
Company info
Accenture is a global professional services company that excels in digital, cloud, and security solutions. We have a longstanding relationship with SAP that extends more than 40 years. As a leading business partner, we work with SAP end-to-end from product innovation to development and delivery.
