Job description, responsibilities and duties
​Are you passionate about troubleshooting complex systems and ensuring seamless operations? Do you enjoy working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment where most work is driven by Jira tickets and ownership really matters? Join our Technical Operations team and play a key role in maintaining and improving our online gaming platform, used across multiple countries.
Your Responsibilities will be:
Jira Issue, Ticket & Incident Ownership:
Own day-to-day Jira issues and operational tickets — investigate, communicate, and drive them to full resolution and closure. Most work is ticket-driven; as experience grows, progressively participate in incident troubleshooting and resolution with the same ownership mindset.
Platform Operations & Reliability:
Contribute to the stability, availability, and operability of the platform by identifying systemic issues, recurring patterns, and cross-service risks.
Stakeholder & Brand Ownership:
Be the technical operations partner for assigned brand(s): build relationships with brand stakeholders, learn brand-specific needs, and propose improvements both technically and in process. More broadly, collaborate effectively with internal teams (engineering, product, platform) to ensure smooth operations, clear communication, and effective issue resolution.
Operational Excellence & Improvement:
Proactively identify gaps in monitoring, alerting, documentation, and processes, and work with the wider Nova organization to improve operational efficiency and platform resilience.
Brand Compliance & Policy Adherence:
Ensure that operational activities for assigned brand(s)comply with regulatory requirements and company policies, while collaborating with internal teams (e.g. compliance, IT, developers, etc.) tomaintainaudit-ready processes across the wider platform.
Support and collaborate with development teams during deployments and operational changes, ensuring smooth execution and rapid issue resolution.
Documentation & Knowledge Sharing:
Maintain and improve operational documentation and procedures, and actively share knowledge across the team.
SLA & Operational Commitments:
Work towards SLA targets for incidents and operational requests, balancing timely response with durable fixes and platform stability.
Continuously Enhance Skills and Knowledge:
Engage in ongoing learning and development activities
