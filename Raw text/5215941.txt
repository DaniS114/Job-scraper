Are you ready to elevate your software engineering career with cutting-edge Microsoft technologies? Join us and shape the future of cloud-based business applications!
We are seeking a talented Developer to join our team and work on Microsoft Dynamics 365 solutions with a focus on Azure Cloud integration, where you’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams on an exciting long-term projects.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
• Design, develop, test, and deploy scalable solutions on the Dynamics 365 platform using C#/.NET
• Extend Dynamics 365 CE functionality using Azure Functions
• Integrate Dynamics D365 CE solutions into other client’s legacy systems
• Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into cloud-first solutions
• Troubleshoot and resolve performance bottlenecks in custom plugins and optimize SQL/database queries
WHAT WE
EXPECT
• 2–5 years hands-on experience with MS Dynamics 365 development and Azure Functions
• Solid experience in C#, .NET Framework, and enterprise application architecture
• Proficient in web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
• Familiar with CI/CD pipelines, TFS, Git, and ALM practices
• Experience with unit testing, data migrations (SSIS, Scribe), and transformations
• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in IT or a related field
• Strong communication skills in English B2+ (German is a plus)
