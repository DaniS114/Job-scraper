Responsibilities
What you'll build:
As Data Architect Global Program Lead (f/m/d), you will manage the implementation of global Data Architect initiatives, ensuring strong alignment between global strategy and regional execution. Reporting to the Global Head of Data Architect Advisory, you will drive structured program governance across Strategy, Enablement, and Value Realization while enabling two-way communication with regional teams in the Global Delivery Hub to continuously adapt and fine-tune execution based on feedback.
This is a global program leadership role focused on operating model design, stakeholder alignment, communication, and performance steering at scale.
Key Responsibilities:
You will lead the Governance of the global Data Architect program by defining and continuously refining the operating framework that enables consistent execution worldwide. This includes establishing role definitions and RACI models, clarifying deliverables and effort expectations, aligning resource and hiring plans, and supporting funding acquisition. You will define customer qualification criteria and engagement standards to ensure high-value impact, while owning the global tools strategy and support model to enable scalable and efficient program delivery across regions.
You will oversee Performance Steering by defining KPIs, reporting structures, and measurable success criteria for the program. Through regular regional steering cadences, you will ensure alignment on priorities, targets, and execution quality. You will track performance and business impact, proactively address gaps, and establish structured product and field feedback loops that support continuous improvement and help influence SAP's broader Data and AI evolution.
You will support Enablement and Communication to ensure capability building and alignment at scale. This includes leading global certification paths and structured learning journeys, strengthening university alliances and early talent pipelines, and enabling the broader SAP ecosystem across Sales, Enterprise Architecture, Services, and partners. You will ensure consistent field communication through clear messaging, playbooks, and updates, while governing the Data Architect onboarding journey and amplifying global success stories to reinforce best practices and measurable outcomes.
This role provides a unique opportunity to operationalize and scale SAP's global Data Architect program. You will:
Drive global alignment between strategy and execution across regions
Influence how SAP enables, measures, and scales its Data Architect capabilities worldwide
Work closely with senior leaders across SAP's ecosystem
Shape a high-impact global program with measurable and lasting business outcomes
Requirements
What you bring:
Global Program Leadership Expertise: Strong experience aligning senior leadership teams and driving execution in complex, matrix environments.
Strategic and Governance Excellence: Proven experience designing operating models, governance structures, and decision frameworks at global scale. Ability to translate strategic vision into structured execution frameworks, clear standards, and measurable outcomes.
Stakeholder and Change Leadership: Strong communication and influencing skills, with storytelling skills to simplify complex topics and drive alignment.
Data and AI Business Acumen: Understanding of SAP's Data and AI strategy and enterprise data transformation concepts, with the ability to guide program direction at a strategic level
Performance Management Mindset: Experience defining KPIs, managing performance cadences, and driving accountability across distributed teams
Must-Have Requirements:
Several years of experience in global program management, transformation leadership, or strategy execution within SAP or enterprise software environments
Strong understanding of SAP's Data and AI portfolio and enterprise data transformation landscapes
Experience working in cross-functional environments spanning Development, Sales, Enterprise Architecture, and Services
Demonstrated ability to drive structured governance, funding alignment, and resource planning at global scale
Exceptional executive communication and stakeholder management skills
Excellent German and English language skills required
Nice-to-Have Requirements:
Experience building global enablement or certification frameworks
Background in performance steering, KPI governance, or value realization programs
Familiarity with hyperscaler ecosystems and partner enablement models
Experience in large-scale organizational change initiatives
