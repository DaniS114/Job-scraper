Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about data governance and improving data quality? Join our dedicated teamand help strengthen the foundation of data excellence across Swiss Re. In our data-drivenenvironment, this role plays a key part in supporting high-quality data assets through structuredgovernance, effective issue management, and consistent data quality controls.
About the Role
As a Data Issue & Data Quality Specialist, you will support the implementation and operation of Swiss Re’s data governance and data quality frameworks. Working at the intersection of business and technology, you will help identify, track, and resolve data quality issues, and contribute to the development and monitoring of meaningful data quality indicators across the organization. This role offers hands-on exposure to enterprise-wide data governance practices and is well suited foran early-career professional looking to grow in the data management and data quality domain.
Key Responsibilities:
•	Support framework implementation - Assist in maintaining the central data issuemanagement framework and solutions to meet stakeholder requirements
•	Perform quality checks - Execute data quality control processes and help monitor dataquality across systems
•	Track KPIs - Collect and analyze data for our Data Quality KPI framework to help measureand improve data quality standards
•	Influence tooling direction - Help define and influence the direction of SwissRe group datagovernance tools to track data issues, analyze patterns, and document solutions
•	Collaborate across teams - Work with Tech and Business stakeholders to implement dataquality standards and resolve data issues
•	Promote best practices - Help foster a culture of data quality through documentation,training materials, and day-to-day support
About the Team
Our department, part of Group Data Services, is committed to strengthening data and AIgovernance practices across Swiss Re. We are the global focus point of data governance,operating from Zurich, Bratislava, and Bangalore, focusing on meeting regulatory requirementsand enhancing data issue management and quality control.
