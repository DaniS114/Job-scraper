Cyber Security Specialist (Full‑Remote)

As a Cyber Security Specialist, you will:
Design, develop, and maintain the organisation’s cybersecurity architecture.
Review IT systems and solutions from a cybersecurity perspective in collaboration with enterprise architecture teams.
Lead the implementation of robust, scalable security solutions in a global environment, ensuring industry‑standard compliance.
Conduct and drive security assessments, architecture reviews, audits, and contribute to incident and vulnerability management.
Support IT and OT projects, identifying cybersecurity risks and defining effective mitigation plans together with key stakeholders.
What you bring:
10+ years of experience in IT and/or cyber security.
Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
Strong understanding of cybersecurity principles, architecture design, and configuration.
Experience working in complex environments and leading technical initiatives.
Excellent communication, documentation, and stakeholder‑management skills.
Strong analytical and problem‑solving abilities.
Fluent English.
