Purpose
You ensure operational excellence and technical stability across our learning platform ecosystem. You connect the dots between projects, recognize patterns that work, and replicate scalable solutions across initiatives. You continuously improve the platform to enhance customer experience and business impact.
This role is execution-driven and operationally focused:
~60% Platform Administration & Operational Excellence
~20% Technical Product & Governance Topics
~20% Stakeholder Enablement & Coordination
What will you do?
Platform Administration & Operational Excellence (~60%)
Own and manage day-to-day administration of our LMS/LXP environment
Ensure system stability, performance, and high user experience
Configure structures, permissions, reporting, and content setups
Identify improvement opportunities and continuously enhance platform usability
Recognize best practices from one project and apply them efficiently to others
Act as expert contact for troubleshooting and solution implementation
Technical Product & Governance (~20%)
Translate business and HR requirements into pragmatic technical solutions
Support integrations (e.g., Teams, APIs, external providers)
Manage governance and compliance processes (e.g., PSA/PSI, works council topics)
Contribute operational expertise to roadmap and development discussions
Support and execute platform migration activities if required
Stakeholder Enablement & Coordination (~20%)
Act as trusted partner for HR, IT, and business stakeholders
Drive topics forward with ownership and follow-through
Coordinate with internal IT units and external vendors
Ensure alignment and smooth execution across initiatives
You will succeed if you:
have strong technical affinity and ability to understand system architecture and integrations
have experience with LMS and/or LXP platforms (Super/admin experience preferred)
have ability to connect concepts across projects and scale proven solutions
clear ownership mindset and willingness to actively drive improvements
have structured, reliable, and solution-oriented working style
are motivated to continuously optimize user experience and platform performance
are confident in cross-functional collaboration (HR, IT, Business)
speak fluent in English and German (minimum C1 level)
