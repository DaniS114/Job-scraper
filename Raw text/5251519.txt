Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about Artificial Intelligence (AI) and eager to make a real-world impact in a global organization? AT&T Business International is seeking a driven and creative Technical Intern to join our team. This internship offers a unique opportunity to explore the transformative potential of AI, identify innovative use cases, and develop strategies to boost AI adoption within our organization.
Our goal is to build a flexible AI application platform that supports diverse AI and Machine Learning (ML) projects while remaining open to innovation. We work across a broad spectrum of activities and recognize the opportunity for your skills to be pivotal in shaping our future AI development.
If you want to expand your expertise in backend development, data science, data engineering, or deep learning, this role offers hands-on opportunities to experiment with Proof of Value concepts and explore long-term AI/ML and Deep Learning applications.
What You’ll Do
AI Application Development & Integration:
Develop and integrate AI applications, including large language model (LLM) chat systems combined with advanced approaches such as Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agent-based workflows, and toolset development.
Experiment with MCP servers and develop machine learning components for internal applications, including API endpoint creation.
Build preventive systems focused on sensitive data protection (e.g., SPI, RPI).
Automated Metadata Extraction for Text-to-SQL:
Explore and implement profiling, query log analysis, and SQL-to-text generation using LLMs.
Collaborate with AT&T researchers and use benchmarks like BIRD to improve extraction techniques.
AI Awareness & Adoption:
Create engaging content leveraging LLM tools such as AT&T’s internal NotebookLM.
Actively promote AI adoption and support users in adapting to new AI technologies across the organization.
Use Case Identification & Strategy:
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to analyze needs and identify high-impact AI use cases.
Design and implement strategies to increase awareness and adoption of AI tools.
Research & Data Analysis:
Stay updated on emerging AI/ML trends and technologies to recommend innovative solutions.
Support data-driven decision-making by gathering and analyzing relevant data for AI initiatives.
Collaboration & Independent Work:
Work independently on technical tasks including LLM implementation and API integration into new or existing application environments.
Partner with both technical and non-technical stakeholders to align AI technologies with business goals.
