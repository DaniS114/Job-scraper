Responsibilities
Collaborate with business counterparts to conduct requirements engineering and design effective solutions
Prepare programming requests for internal development team or external developers
Coordinate and oversee the work of external consultants and developers
Test, implement, and manage the rollout of new developments and release upgrades
Provide support for quality management processes across various tools
Focus primarily on manufacturing-related QM processes, such as Control Planning and Statistical Process Control (SPC)
Deliver user support, handle incidents, and ensure standard service fulfillment
Requirements
University degree in IT or a related field
Minimum of 2 years’ experience in IT, ideally with exposure to CAQ systems
Experience in the automotive industry, with knowledge of automotive quality standards such as VDA and AIAG
Initial experience with AI is considered an asset
Ability to understand code in various programming languages
Experience with database technologies such as Microsoft SQL Server, as well as supporting data analytics activities
Basic knowledge of SAP QM
Fluency in English
Proven experience working in international, virtual teams
Strong skills in project management, change management, and rollout management
Excellent communication skills, including confidence in moderating meetings, workshops, and trainings in English

Supportive, diverse and highly skilled community

Company info
KOSTAL Group is a global, family-owned German company that develops market-leading solutions for the solar and automotive industries. The company was established in 1912 and employs about 20 000 people at 47 locations in 22 countries. In Hungary, KOSTAL has built a flagship business service center to provide business support in IT, finance, procurement, master data, sales and HR fields. This is now a global center with high value-added and complex processes which you can help to build.
