Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries and employs over 30,000 professionals. The firm is committed to helping organizations achieve lasting impact through strategic advice, digital transformation, and operational improvements.
