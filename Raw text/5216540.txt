Job description, responsibilities and duties
Product Designer will be part of Digital unit team working on following global products in Fintech area, for users from Europe, South America and Asia.
Responsibilities:
· Strengthening the UX by visual means appropriate to user needs and cognitive abilities
· Taking care of usability and visual coherence of our digital products
· Designing final UI views and assets for web and mobile solutions
· Researching, developing and executing design solutions for acquiring portals, e-commerce solutions and mobile applications
· Creating and presenting interaction design concepts (wireframes), prototypes and final designs
· Engaging directly with consumers through user research activities, designing concepts and prototypes to be tested, uncovering key unmet needs and rapid-prototype solutions to big problems. Translating those solutions to meet the needs of our merchant and financial institutions customers. Analyzing the results of usability testing and research and recommend winning design solutions
· Monitoring UX/UI trends and technology
· Ambition to design applications that are able to compete with Revolut, Stripe or Klarna
