About Graphisoft
Graphisoft® empowers teams to design great buildings through award-winning software solutions, learning programs, and professional services for the AEC industry. Our award-winning products and solutions support OPEN BIM for workflow transparency, longevity, and data accessibility for built assets.  Archicad®, the architects’ BIM software of choice, offers a complete end-to-end design and documentation workflow for architectural and integrated architectural and engineering practices of any size. BIMx®, the most popular mobile and web BIM app, extends the BIM experience to include all stakeholders in the building design, delivery, and operations lifecycle. BIMcloud®, the AEC industry’s first and most advanced cloud-based team collaboration solution, makes real-time collaboration possible across the globe regardless of the size of the project and the speed or quality of the team members’ network connection. DDScad solutions support users with intelligent Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) design tools, integrated calculations, and comprehensive documentation of all building system disciplines. As a Business Intelligence Trainee, you will have the opportunity to develop your skills in data analysis, reporting, and business intelligence. This trainee position is ideal for individuals who have a strong interest in data-driven decision-making and are eager to learn and grow in the field of business intelligence.
You will be working closely together with the members of the Business Intelligence (BI) Team, who are the data engineers and data analysts.
The BI Team is responsible for ensuring that the business data are consistently processed from source to taget according to business requirements. The team also produces reports upon business requests.
Responsibilities:
•Assist in gathering and analyzing data from various sources.
•Support the development and maintenance of reports and dashboards.
•Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business requirements.
•Learn and utilize BI tools and technologies to extract insights from data.
•Contribute to the improvement of data quality and accuracy.
•Ongoing university studies for at least two semesters, in computer science, information systems, business economics, mathematics / statistics, data science or related fields.
•Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
•Basic understanding of databases, relational databases and SQL.
•Good communication and teamwork skills.
•Eagerness to learn and adapt to new technologies and processes.
•Intermediate English language skills (written and spoken).
•Hungarian language skills at native speaker level.
•Ability to work at least 20 hours per week.
•Exposure to at least one business intelligence tool such as Power BI, Tableau, Qlik or similar or strong willingness to learn them.
•Basic programming / scripting experience (e.g., Python, R) is a great advantage.
We Offer:
•A dynamic and collaborative team.
•Professional development opportunities.
•A creative and innovative work environment.
•The flexibility of a hybrid workplace, with at least one day of in-office presence per week (our location: Budapest, Graphisoft Park).
