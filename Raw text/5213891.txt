Job description, responsibilities and duties
- Strengthening the portal administration team and supporting the development team with programming, design, and testing
- Setting up new portal servers/portal applications
- Familiarizing yourself with existing systems, including documentation (standard portal, application management, user access control, internal portal)

- Debugging and analyzing customer issues related to portal requests (supporting application operators in identifying connection and Protocol issues, performance tuning, etc.)
- Integration of portal systems/portal applications into logging, monitoring, and backup
- Proof of concept for new products within the portal and developer platform
- Support in setting up the developer platform (Git, Nexus, CI/CD, GitLab Runner pipelines, etc.)
- Further development of the portal software and portal administration tools (support of the portal development system by creating feature requests and bug reports, participation in the development and design of new portal systems)
