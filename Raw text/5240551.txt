Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about creating elegant, high-performance user interfaces that transform complex financial data into intuitive experiences? Join our dynamic team at Swiss Re where your expertise in Angular development will directly impact how our financial professionals interact with critical systems and make informed decisions.
About the Role
As a Senior Frontend Software Developer, you'll be at the forefront of designing and implementing sophisticated web applications that power our financial operations. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business requirements into seamless user experiences while maintaining the highest standards of code quality and performance.
Key Responsibilities
•	Architect and develop responsive, user-friendly web applications using Angular and related frontend technologies
•	Lead technical implementation of new features and enhancements for financial applications
•	Collaborate closely with backend developers, UX designers, and business stakeholders to deliver cohesive solutions
•	Implement and maintain robust testing strategies including unit tests and integration tests (Cypress)
•	Mentor junior developers and provide technical guidance on frontend best practices
•	Participate actively in agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives
•	Stay current with emerging frontend technologies and evaluate their potential application
About the Team
The EFR Services team is dedicated to building APIs and GUI applications for Finance IT within Swiss Re. We develop and maintain critical systems that enable financial operations, reporting, and analysis across the organization. Our solutions help transform complex financial processes into streamlined, user-friendly experiences that drive business efficiency.

Personality requirements and skills
You're a problem-solver at heart who thrives in collaborative environments. You have a keen eye for detail and a passion for creating exceptional user experiences. You're comfortable navigating complex requirements and translating them into elegant technical solutions. You value clean, maintainable code and enjoy sharing knowledge with your colleagues.
We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements:
•	Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience
•	Minimum 5 years of professional experience with Angular development (versions 2+)
•	Strong proficiency in TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3/SCSS
•	Experience with responsive design and cross-browser compatibility
•	Proven track record of building complex enterprise-level applications
These are additional nice to haves:
•	Knowledge of UI component libraries (Angular Material, PrimeNG)
•	Familiarity with testing frameworks (Jasmine, Cypress)
•	Experience with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
•	Knowledge of financial domain or experience in fintech/finance industry
•	Experience with RxJS and reactive programming patterns
•	Additional frontend frameworks (React, Vue.js)
