Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Develop and customize Microsoft Dynamics 365 applications using C#/.NET and Power Platform tools
Build integrations between Dynamics 365 and Azure services (Logic Apps, Functions) as part of a modern cloud architecture
Support the migration from Salesforce to Dynamics 365 — helping to unify global customer data and business processes
Work closely with senior developers and solution architects to design clean, scalable solutions aligned with best practices
Participate in code reviews, testing, and CI/CD pipeline activities to ensure high-quality deliverables
Troubleshoot and optimize Dynamics plugins and SQL queries to improve system performance
Requirements
2–5 years of experience in Microsoft Dynamics 365 development or similar CRM platforms
Confident with C# /.NET, web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and understand object-oriented principles
Hands-on experience or strong interest in Azure Functions, Logic Apps, and CI/CD pipelines
Comfortable communicating in English and working in a multicultural team environment
Knowledge of data migration tools (SSIS, KingswaySoft, Scribe) is a bonus but not mandatory
What we offer
Be part of a global digital transformation — delivering modern Microsoft 365 and Dynamics solutions for international enterprise clients
Gain real-world exposure to Azure Cloud, Power Platform, and modern integration tech
Hybrid work setup – join us in the Budapest office and work remotely when you need focus
Learn directly from senior Dynamics developers and architects through mentoring and certification support
Long-term project stability combined with the variety of consulting work — each day brings new technical challenges
Work with friendly, international colleagues who value collaboration and continuous learning
