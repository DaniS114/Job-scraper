Job description, responsibilities and duties
We seek you – an experienced full stack developer with focus on combination of C# and Angular!
What do 2D/3D cameras, tablets, and today’s most advanced Computed Tomography scanners have in common?
They all rely on intelligent automation to transform medical imaging—and that’s exactly where you come in.
At Siemens Healthineers, we’re not just building software. We’re creating innovative solutions that help clinicians diagnose faster, work smarter, and ultimately save lives. Every line of code you write directly supports medical professionals and improves patient outcomes across the globe.
If you’re excited by the idea of tackling real-world healthcare challenges and want your work to make a meaningful impact, join us in shaping the future of CT scanner software.
Key Responsibilities
Design, develop and maintain .NET Framework / .NET Windows desktop application
Implement and refine Angular frontends running on tablets
Write clean, testable, well-documented code and participate in peer reviews
What you bring:
Proven experience in C#/.NET programming and Angular frontend development
A proactive mindset to solve complex problems
Experience with CI/CD principles and test automation
