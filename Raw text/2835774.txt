Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Define and evolve user research strategy in close collaboration with Product Design and Product Management, aligned with business outcomes
Design and conduct qualitative and quantitative research tailored to product goals and user flows
Embed research into shaping and discovery rituals, ensuring insights inform decisions early in the product lifecycle
Lead and manage en-to-end research operations (e.g. interviews, usability testing, workshops, surveys), including participant recruitment, ensuring compliance, consent, and representative user samples
Synthesize findings into clear deliverables (personas, journey maps, opportunity briefs), engage stakeholders, and drive a user-centered product culture
Create rigorous, repeatable research protocols, build a scalable user knowledge repository, and enable research at scale across squads through templates, guidelines, and lightweight frameworks
Act as a strategic partner for Product and Design, not only as a research executor
Requirements
At least 3 years of professional experience in user research, ideally in B2B, tech-driven, or complex product environments, with a track record of delivering high-impact research; experience in the mobility or EV charging sector is a plus
Expertise in designing and conducting qualitative and quantitative studies and translating insights into actionable product and business decisions
Strong skills in participant recruitment, research operations, and user research tools to drive scalable, efficient research campaigns
Ability to synthesize complex data into clear, eviden-ce-based recommendations and communicate findings to diverse stakeholders
Collaborative, proactive, and hands-on leader with exceptional communication, storytelling, and problem-solving skills, thriving in fast-paced, Agile environments
Fluent in English and German with French or Italian as a plus
