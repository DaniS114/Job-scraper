Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a highly experienced
Senior Windows Infrastructure Engineer (Tier 3)
to join our team and work in complex enterprise environments for demanding customers.
This role is for a true expert who can
design, install, migrate, troubleshoot, and operate critical Microsoft infrastructure technologies
in large-scale environments. You will be part of the
last escalation level
, meaning you will handle the most complex technical challenges without further escalation paths.
Key responsibilities:
Design, install, configure, and maintain Microsoft Windows infrastructure services in complex enterprise environments
Perform migrations and transformations of Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, and File Services
Troubleshoot and resolve high-complexity incidents and problems as Tier 3 support
Work with multi-domain forests, multi-site architectures, and tiered security models
Ensure high availability, security, and performance of core infrastructure services
Collaborate with internal teams and customers on architecture and implementation projects
Create technical documentation and contribute to best practices and standards
