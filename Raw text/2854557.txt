Állás leírása
Responsibilities
You take overall responsibility for planning, managing, and executing our SAP RISE migration, leading internal and external project teams from SAP, hyperscalers, and integration partners
Your tasks include developing and steering our technical migration strategy — from brownfield to selective data transition to system consolidations
You ensure adherence to timelines, budget, quality, and security standards, and you take ownership of escalation and interface management between SAP, hyperscalers, and internal stakeholders
By technically assessing our existing SAP landscape, you develop a future-proof target architecture, including network, security, identity, monitoring, and integration
Together with our SAP Basis teams, you secure stable and sustainable operations after go-live and define a viable cloud operating model
Your responsibilities also include managing SAP RISE service offerings, coordinating availability strategies with hyperscalers, and leveraging relevant SAP BTP services
Requirements
Qualification
: You have a degree in business administration, computer science, or a comparable qualification
Experience
: You bring several years of experience in the SAP Basis environment and have successfully led large international IT transformation projects
Know-how and skills:
Your expertise includes SAP architectures, HANA, Linux, security, and interfaces, and you possess deep technical understanding of SAP migrations — ideally SAP RISE
Nice to have:
You have knowledge of SAP BTP services, hybrid SAP architectures, Cloud ALM, or SAP RISE operating and contract models
Personality
: You impress us with a structured working style, strong analytical skills, and reliable, solution-oriented communication with stakeholders at all levels
Language skills
: You speak fluent English and feel comfortable in an international environment; German language skills are an advantage
What we offer
Hybrid work
: We offer our employees the opportunity to work on a hybrid basis. There is also the option to temporarily work from a country within the European Union
Salary
: We offer an attractive remuneration package in accordance with the NRW IG Metall collective wage agreement, including vacation pay and Christmas bonus
Onboarding
: Our clearly structured onboarding process, including an onboarding app, enables us to integrate new employees into Vaillant Group quickly and in a targeted manner
Health management
