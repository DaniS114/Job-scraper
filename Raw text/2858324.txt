Company info
SEON is a company founded in 2017 with over $187 million in funding and more than 290 team members. SEON helps online businesses reduce the cost, time and challenges faced due to fraud by simplifying fraud management so companies can focus on growth and scaling. SEON operates globally with offices in the Americas, EMEA and Asia-Pacific, serving thousands of companies worldwide across multiple time zones. The company is committed to transparency, open communication, and innovation, fostering a customer-obsessed, resilient and humble culture. SEON offers a range of products and solutions in fraud prevention, KYC, AML compliance, and risk management, serving industries such as financial services, fintech, payments, iGaming, and retail.
