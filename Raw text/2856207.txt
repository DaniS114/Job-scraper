Responsibilities
Co-lead the global OT Security domain
within the Digitalization Team, ensuring close alignment with Corporate IT, Maintenance, Engineering, and Production functions
Coordinate and support NIS2 implementation
, including risk assessments, control validation, and audit readiness across all plants; act as key interface between Corporate IT Security, site operations, and external auditors
Define, harmonize, and maintain global OT security standards
, including OT network architecture, firewall segmentation concepts, secure remote access, VPN policies, and identity/access procedures for OT assets
Evaluate and mitigate cybersecurity risks
in OT environments, in close collaboration with Corporate IT Security
Support the secure integration of machines and systems
into the company's Industrial Data Platform, including connectivity middleware and visualization environments (e.g., Kepware, ThingWorx)
Provide guidance for global and plant-level rollouts
, ensuring compliance with corporate OT security requirements and alignment with connectivity standards
Prepare, maintain, and manage OT cybersecurity documentation
, including network diagrams, asset inventories, access concepts, and audit evidence
Conduct training and awareness sessions for automation, engineering, and maintenance teams
on secure OT operation, incident response, and best practices
Act as OT–data enablement interface
, ensuring that OT data provided for analytics/AI use cases meets security, reliability, and quality requirements.
Requirements
Educational background in automation technology, industrial IT, or electrical engineering, or comparable qualification
Minimum 3 years of experience in OT/IT integration, industrial networking, or cybersecurity
Strong knowledge of network architectures (Layer 2/3, VLANs, VPNs, firewalls) and industrial communication protocols (OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, Ethernet/IP).
Familiarity with NIS2, ISO 27001, IEC 62443 or related cybersecurity frameworks.
Experience with IoT gateways, SCADA/MES systems, and digital shopfloor solutions
High analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate technical issues into actionable measures
Excellent communication and collaboration skills across IT, production, and external partners
Independent, well-structured, and results-driven working style
Excellent command of English; German is an advantage
