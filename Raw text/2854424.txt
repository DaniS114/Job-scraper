Responsibilities
What if you could shape the future of our ServiceNow platform and drive service excellence across our organisation? As a ServiceNow Platform Consultant / Expert (f/m/x), you will play a key role in designing, developing, and continuously improving our ServiceNow environment, with a focus on IT Service Management (ITSM). You will combine technical expertise, solution architecture, and business consulting to ensure the platform delivers sustainable value for our organisation. Your key responsibilities include:
Partnering with IT and business stakeholders to translate operational needs into scalable ServiceNow solutions, including service catalogs, workflows, and reporting
Facilitating workshops and aligning ITIL-based processes with ServiceNow capabilities to drive adoption and process excellence
Designing and maintaining the overall ServiceNow platform architecture, including CMDB/CSDM alignment, integrations, security, and governance
Defining scalable platform standards and reviewing major changes, third-party apps, and custom solutions for architectural fit
Configuring and developing solutions on the ServiceNow platform (e.g., Flow Designer, Business Rules, Client Scripts, IntegrationHub, REST/SOAP integrations)
Collaborating with external partners and vendors to ensure high-quality platform delivery and governance
Providing advanced platform support (Level 2/3), troubleshooting issues, and ensuring system stability, data integrity, and SLA compliance
Driving continuous improvement through upgrades, release management, documentation, and optimisation initiatives
Managing the platform roadmap and backlog for ITSM capabilities, prioritising features, and coordinating releases
Measuring platform performance through KPIs and identifying opportunities to increase automation, efficiency, and service quality
Requirements
This role is ideal for a ServiceNow professional who enjoys combining technical expertise with strategic thinking. If you like translating business needs into scalable platform solutions and continuously improving processes, you could be a great fit for our team. Ideally, you bring the following qualifications and strengths:
University degree in IT, business informatics
,
or a comparable qualification
5+ years of experience working with the ServiceNow platform, ideally across development, architecture, consulting, or product management
Strong hands-on experience with ServiceNow configuration and development (JavaScript, Glide API, Flow Designer, IntegrationHub, catalog items, REST/SOAP integrations)
Solid understanding of ITSM processes aligned with ITIL and experience designing scalable platform architectures (e.g., CMDB/CSDM, integrations, security models)
Experience translating business requirements into technical solutions and facilitating stakeholder workshops
Experience working with external ServiceNow development teams or vendors
Product mindset with experience managing backlogs, defining roadmaps, and measuring performance through KPIs
ServiceNow certifications (e.g., CSA, CIS-ITSM, CAD) are an advantage
Experience with ITOM capabilities such as Discovery, Service Mapping, or Event Management is a plus
Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain technical topics to diverse stakeholders
Fluent in English; additional languages are an advantage in our international environment
