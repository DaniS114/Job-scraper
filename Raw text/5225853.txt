Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
Position Description Overview:
	Works as Enterprise Business Analyst based in Bratislava.
	Lead requirement discussion with client and responsible for gathering business requirements from Fiserv client banks or Financial Institutes.
	Primarily responsible for delivery of the requirement documents such as BRDs/SSDs/Solution Design Documents/ RQMs/Configuration Documents etc.
	Should have an excellent communication skill and should be good in relationship building with internal stakeholders and third parties (Schemes, Acquiring banks and Financial Institutions)
	Works as SME for Merchant Acquiring solution (end-to-end)
Job Responsibilities
•	Responsible for drafting an end-to-end merchant acquiring solution for banks or financial institutions.
•	Assures requirement gathering, identifying appropriate solution to the requirement and ensure successful implementation of merchant solution.
•	Identifies, analyses, research, and resolves functional queries from internal teams and third parties.
•	Ensures complete and prompt resolution for moderate to complex queries from internal teams and third parties directly.
•	Effectively interact and communicate with varying internal departments.
•	Sound Knowledge on acquiring solution, platforms, systems, and technical knowledge as a plus.
•	Keen learner and acts as consultant, expected to understand system parameters and options.
•	Identify systemic and business-impacting trends and then escalate through proper channels of leadership.
•	Should be flexible to travel and work at client location in Vienna, Austria
Location: 5 days a week in Bratislava office is required.

Osobnostné predpoklady a zručnosti
Candidate Requirements:
•	Bachelor or Master degree for a renowned university or college
•	At least 5-6 years of experience in payment card industry
•	Extensive SME Functional knowledge in Merchant Acquiring Solutions covering payment gateway, Authorization switches, Scheme clearing & settlements, merchant pricing (blended, IC+/++ models), Interchange and scheme fees, financial reconciliations.
•	Good organizational, planning and problem-solving skills, and excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
•	Self-motivated and able to work independently with minimal supervision towards the achievement of personal and team goals.
•	Analytical skills, understanding industry metrics and trends.
•	Has got previous experience working on payment systems, understands end to end merchant acquiring and payment solution
•	Additional Preferred skills - German speaker, Understands ECR integrations (ZVT, OPI), Experience of working in Austrian payment market
Inzerujúca spoločnosť
Stručná charakteristika spoločnosti
CGI je popredná spoločnosť poskytujúca poradenské a technologické služby zamerané na pomoc klientom pri dosahovaní úspechu v ich podnikaní. Od založenia v roku 1976 zdieľame s našimi zákazníkmi ich problémy a poskytujeme kvalitné služby k ich riešeniu. 90 000 odborníkov v 40 krajinách sveta znamená pre našich klientov blízkosť, odbornosť a kompletné IT služby, ktoré naplnia ich obchodné potreby kdekoľvek a kedykoľvek. Ponúkame tak prístup a zodpovednosť lokálneho partnera, ako aj služby a schopnosti potrebné pre splnenie rozvíjajúcich sa potrieb v globálnom meradle. Naše pobočky v strednej a východnej Európe sa nachádzajú v Českej republike, Slovensku a Maďarsku. Zároveň pracujeme na projektoch významných organizácií v iných štátoch, ako je Bulharsko, Chorvátsko, Macedónsko, Čierna Hora, Rumunsko a Srbsko.
