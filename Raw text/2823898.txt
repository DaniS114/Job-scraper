Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm serving leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey helps clients make lasting improvements to their performance and realize their most important goals. With offices in more than 65 countries, McKinsey combines deep industry knowledge with specialized expertise in strategy, operations, technology, organization, and digital transformation to deliver sustainable results.
