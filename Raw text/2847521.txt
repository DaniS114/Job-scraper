Responsibilities
You develop and maintain the digital transformation roadmap within your area of responsibility, leveraging your expertise to identify digital initiatives that create measurable business value.
You lead digital initiatives from concept to implementation and ensure structured, sustainable execution.
You define a clear product vision and translate it into concrete priorities and deliverables for the agile project team.
You own and prioritize the product backlog, balancing business needs, technical feasibility, and user impact.
You work closely with business stakeholders and senior leaders to align expectations and ensure transparency.
You collaborate with the Scrum Master and development team to ensure efficient and effective agile delivery.
You identify risks early and take appropriate measures to keep projects on track in complex environments.
You define relevant KPIs and regularly review delivery progress, product performance, and resulting business impact.
You ensure clear communication across teams and promote continuous improvement.
You monitor market and technology developments to incorporate relevant innovations into your product strategy.
Requirements
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Management, Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemistry, or a related discipline within the chemicals value chain
Minimum of 8 years of professional experience, including experience as a Product Owner in digital transformation environments
Proven track record in delivering complex digital initiatives with measurable business impact
Strong experience in managing and aligning diverse stakeholder groups, including senior leadership
Solid understanding of Agile principles and practical experience working in cross-functional agile teams
Agile certification (e.g., CSPO, PSPO, or equivalent) is considered an advantage
Strong analytical and structured problem-solving capabilities
Good understanding of digital technologies and their practical application in a business context
Clear and effective communication skills in English, both written and spoken
What we offer
A collaborative team environment and a diverse range of responsibilities with significant scope for initiative and early ownership.
Flexible working hours and a hybrid work model, supporting a healthy work-life balance within a full-time schedule averaging 38.5 hours per week.
Competitive compensation complemented by modern social benefits, including a company pension scheme.
Individual development opportunities through tailored training programs at specialist, project, or management level, enabling diverse career paths.
Comprehensive health and well-being support, including access to work-life services, personal coaching, and webinars.
Company info
Sasol is a global integrated chemicals and energy company with a 75-year heritage. Through our talented people, we use our expertise and selected technologies to safely and sustainably source, manufacture and market chemical and energy products globally. When you join Team Sasol, you are joining a company that puts people at the center of everything we do. Sasol invests in its employees along every stage of the career path and offers development opportunities to help you cultivate your career in a culture that embraces diversity and inclusion.
Shape the digital future with us – as a strategic Product Owner
Are you passionate about driving digital transformation in a complex industrial environment? Do you combine strong business understanding with structured product thinking? Then we are looking for you.
As Senior Product Owner - Digital Transformation, you will drive key digital initiatives within our Chemicals business. You will work at the interface between business functions and development teams, translating strategic objectives into clear product priorities and actionable roadmaps. In close collaboration with stakeholders across the organization, you will ensure that digital solutions are implemented in a structured, agile, and value-driven way - creating measurable impact for our colleagues and customers.
