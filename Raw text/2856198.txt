Responsibilities
Responsibility for the entire design process from initial discovery to final delivery
Identification of research needs and transformation of insights into intuitive, accessible, and inclusive user experiences
Creation of wireframes, mockups, user flows, and prototypes using the most appropriate tools and methods
Facilitation of collaborative ideation and problem-solving with teammates and stakeholders
Ensuring of consistency and efficiency through the use of the design system, including the proposal of improvements or new components when needed
Validation and refinement of designs through feedback gathering, usability testing, and data analysis
Delivery of clear communication and comprehensive handover documentation for seamless developer implementation
Close collaboration with Product Managers, Engineers, and other stakeholders to shape user-centered solutions and drive product outcomes
Contribution to raising UX maturity and improving design processes across the organization
Continuous monitoring of emerging trends, tools, and best practices within the UX field
Requirements
You have 5+ years of professional experience in UX design (medior/senior capacity).
You have a bachelor's degree or vocational qualification in design, HCI, or a related field.
You are skilled at creating wireframes, prototypes, and design documentation using tools such as Figma.
You have experience working with or contributing to design systems.
You can validate designs through research, usability testing, and feedback.
You bring strong collaboration and communication skills, helping you work effectively with engineers, product managers, and other designers.
You are proficient in English, ensuring clear communication across teams.
You are passionate about accessibility, inclusivity, and solving complex design challenges.
You have a proactive mindset and a drive for continuous improvement.

Company info
TOPdesk is an international software company specializing in service management with 900 employees in 8 countries and 5,000 customers worldwide. For our Kaiserslautern office, founded in 2004, we are looking for a UX Designer (m/f/d).
Your environment
At TOPdesk's headquarters in Kaiserslautern! As a UX Designer, you'll join a multidisciplinary product development team, collaborating closely with Product Managers, Engineers, and other Designers. You'll work in an environment that values creativity, user-centric thinking, and teamwork, with colleagues based in the Netherlands, Germany, and Hungary. You will report to a UX Manager and regularly engage with the Design System team and other stakeholders.
How to apply
You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
