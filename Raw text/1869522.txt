IT System engineer - microsoft/Office 365 (F/M/X)
Blum manufactures lift, hinge, and pull-out systems for furniture, which are delivered to over 120 markets. Did you know that around 300 IT specialists take care of our modern IT environment? The IT application managers provide basic services for our approximately 8800 employees.
YOU
are responsible for the ongoing operation of IT systems in the Microsoft/Office 365 environment (e.g., Exchange, Teams, SharePoint, Azure AD)
evaluate and implement new functions or services and provide 2nd level support
work from the conception through development to the implementation of new solutions in the area of collaboration and communication
act as a technical contact and IT interface for internal and external partners
WE
value your experience with Office 365 services and your knowledge of PowerShell
emphasize precise and conscientious work – handling confidential information with care is a given for you
embrace innovation – continuous development motivates you, and you are open to new ideas
support and assist our worldwide subsidiaries from our headquarters in Vorarlberg – good English skills are essential for this
