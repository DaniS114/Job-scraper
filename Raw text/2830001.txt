About ATLANTICA
ATLANTICA is a next-generation digital logistics platform designed to connect carriers, brokers, warehouses, clients, and investors into one scalable ecosystem.
We are building a B2B platform with multimodal logistics, AI-driven routing, ERP/CRM integrations, and marketplace functionality.
We are looking for a strong, system-oriented Business Analyst who can bridge strategy, business architecture, and technical execution.
