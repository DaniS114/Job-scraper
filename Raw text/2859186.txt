Responsibilities
You'll work alongside a Senior Engineering Manager, experiencing what it takes to lead software engineering teams at a global scale. You'll contribute to real decisions, build AI and data tools that improve how we manage our teams, and connect technical skills to the people side of software development.
Experience engineering management firsthand: from team rituals to strategic planning
Build and evaluate AI tools that support leadership and operational tasks
Improve data-driven approaches to organizational decisions
Contribute your ideas in team meetings where they'll have a real impact
Develop both technical and leadership skills with dedicated mentorship
Requirements
Studying Computer Science or a related field, with at least one year remaining
Strong communication skills in English; German is a plus
Hands-on programming experience and familiarity with AI tools (e.g., Cursor, Claude Code, MCP)
Curious about how great software gets built, not just the code, but the process and the people
Available to work from our Hamburg office
What we offer
What We Offer You!
A role that blends engineering and management
20 hours/week, flexibly arranged around your studies
Mentorship from engineering managers, product managers, and engineers
Exposure to high-scale cloud systems serving millions of users
Team events and connections across Adobe
Workplace accommodations so everyone can do their best work
Potential pathways to thesis projects or full-time roles after graduation
