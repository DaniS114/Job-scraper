Responsibilities
Overseeing and managing a dedicated segment of Supercharge Group’s project portfolio
Acting as the line manager for selected Project Managers, driving their personal development plans, supporting team KPIs, and ensuring continuous professional growth
Serving as a mentor, supporter, and escalation point for Project Managers, actively guiding and enabling them to achieve successful project outcomes
Tracking portfolio-level project performance metrics and reporting directly to the Head of Operations
Collaborating closely with the Delivery Management Team to address staffing needs and resource planning
Supporting and collaborating with the Account/Sales team in decision-making, opportunity assessment, and engagement management
Working with the PMO and fellow Project Portfolio Managers to improve processes, share knowledge, and promote best practices across the organization
Stepping in to lead delivery on the most challenging engagements where complexity, novelty, or strategic importance calls for senior leadership- bringing experienced judgment from discovery through design, development, and transition
Understanding technology-related issues to ensure optimal solution delivery for clients
Managing both in-house and remote project teams effectively
Requirements
At least 5 years of experience in the software development industry
Proven track record of delivering multiple software projects, including both short-term engagements and long-term initiatives of 9-12 months or more
Strong experience with agile and other project management methodologies-we are practitioners, not theorists
Excellent English communication skills- written and spoken- as we operate within an international environment
Strong client and account management and communication capabilities-we value long-term, positive client relationships
High sense of ownership: our PMO is responsible for delivery excellence, so taking full responsibility for tasks and outcomes is essential
People-first mindset: caring for team morale, providing clarity, and protecting teams from uncertainty is core to how we work
Understanding of business requirements and their technical implications, ensuring alignment between strategy, delivery, and technology
Previous experience mentoring and line managing Project Managers or holding similar people-management responsibilities

Company info
Supercharge is a company with local offices in several countries, headquartered in Budapest, Hungary. The Budapest office is the beating heart of the company, a buzzing, vibrant space filled with talented individuals dreaming up and delivering amazing products for clients. This Office of the Year award-winning space is home to legendary after-work events and is located in the heart of the city, close to some of the best restaurants, cafes, and bars around.
