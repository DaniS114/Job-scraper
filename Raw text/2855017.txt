Responsibilities
Senior IS Project Manager
Established in 1927 and headquartered in Switzerland, SICPA is best known for safeguarding the majority of the world's banknotes. With operations across five continents, today our platform for sovereignty delivers integrated solutions safeguarding revenue mobilisation, natural resources, health, and brand protection as well as digital sovereignty and secure public services.
Senior IS Project Manager
SENIOR IS PROJECT MANAGER
MISSION
As a Senior IS Project Manager, you will be responsible for leading and delivering complex IT programmes and projects across the organization. You will oversee the full project lifecycle - from initiation to closure - ensuring alignment with business strategy, organizational commitments, and operational objectives.
In this role, you will drive the successful implementation of solutions while coordinating cross-functional teams and managing stakeholders across the organization.
ROLE
Lead large and/or multiple projects simultaneously, adapting plans to prioritize initiatives and ensuring appropriate resources and governance structures are in place
Gather and clarify requirements for complex, multi-dimensional projects, working closely with Business Sponsors to align on objectives, scope, and deliverables
Build and maintain strong relationships with customers and stakeholders, balancing expectations and facilitating effective decision-making
Provide leadership to project teams by guiding, motivating, and coordinating internal and external contributors, including cross-functional and remote teams
Navigate ambiguity and evolving priorities, adjusting project plans when necessary to ensure successful delivery
Contribute to the continuous improvement of project management methodologies, tools, and best practices within the IT department
Provide expertise, mentoring, and coaching to other project managers
Ensure compliance with IT project management standards, including proper documentation and governance processes
Ensure proposed solutions are aligned with the enterprise architecture framework
Define and monitor project budgets throughout all phases, ensuring alignment with the overall IT budget
Identify project risks and potential issues, develop mitigation strategies, and manage escalation when required
Ensure proper project closure, including transition to operational support where applicable
PROFILE
10+ years of experience in IT project management, with a proven track record of delivering large and complex projects
Demonstrated leadership and influencing skills, with the ability to mobilize teams and drive results in a matrix organization
Strong ability to understand business performance drivers and translate business needs into effective IT solutions
Solid understanding of business processes and modern IT technologies (e.g., Microsoft 365 ecosystem, AI-enabled tools)
Experience managing projects related to SAP and ServiceNow environments
Ability to work both independently and collaboratively within cross-functional teams
Strong knowledge of project management methodologies such as PMI, Agile, or SAP ASAP
Proficiency with project management tools and reporting frameworks
PMP certification is a strong asset
English fluent (C1 minimum); French is an asset. Additional languages are a plus in the context of a global organization
