Customer Experience & Solutions Senior Analyst, Tax Engine
Job ID: 3190462 | Amazon /Slovakia/ s.r.o.
DESCRIPTION
Amazon is seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented experienced analyst to join the Tax Engine Operations team in Bratislava, as part of the Global Tax Engine team. This role supports the customer and seller experience across Amazon’s global marketplaces, with a specialized focus on indirect tax calculation.
The ideal candidate demonstrates strong analytical skills, sound business judgment, and a passion for solving complex problems. You will collaborate with global stakeholders across Tax, Product, Tech, Retail and Amazon Business teams by advocating on behalf of customers and sellers, resolving contacts related to tax calculation and strengthening Amazon’s customer-centric culture.
This challenging role is for the team, where we embrace ambiguity and conquer change. We are looking for a dynamic individual who can be a driving force in our ever-evolving environment.
A day in the life
A Day in the Life Your day will be a blend of creative problem-solving and strategic communication. You'll dive into tax calculation challenges, translate technical complexities into user-friendly content, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to streamline information delivery.

Key job responsibilities
Translate customer pain points into actionable requirements for technical teams.

Define success metrics and track performance improvements across content quality, process efficiency, and strategic outcomes; establish baselines and demonstrate continuous improvement.
Navigate ambiguous problem spaces by translating vague requirements into clear execution plans, making data-driven decisions with incomplete information, and adapting strategies as circumstances evolve.

Basic Qualifications
3-5 years of experience in finance, tax operations, compliance, or related customer support roles.
Proven ability to manage high-volume customer inquiries across multiple channels (email, ticketing systems, escalations).
Strong problem-solving skills with ability to diagnose root causes and implement sustainable solutions.
Experience working with AI tools.
Basic understanding of indirect tax, especially VAT.
Must have excellent written and verbal communication skills with experience in cross-team collaboration.
Excellent attention to detail and ability to perform research.
Ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment.
Preferred Qualifications
Understanding of EU Tax legislation framework.
Basic experience with Machine Learning and Machine Learning Libraries (MML), and/or gen AI concepts.
Experience in client support, e-commerce, operations, or a multinational corporate environment.
