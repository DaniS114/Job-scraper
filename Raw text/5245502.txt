As the Login & Access Hub, we provide a central authentication service for millions of external customers, enabling access to essential services such as TV, email, Customer Center, and OneApp. With over 70 million logins and 15 million unique active users daily, this functionality is critical to the customer experience. The login process is far more complex than it appears, relying on a combination of existing and new applications. Our team works every day to enhance and simplify the login experience while integrating innovative solutions like OneApp and OneShop. We are looking for talented professionals to join us in shaping the future of secure and seamless access.
Purpose
The Senior Java Developer is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining complex software applications while independently managing all phases of the software development lifecycle. This includes analyzing requirements, creating technical solutions, implementing features, testing, and optimizing performance. The role requires strong technical expertise, ownership of delivered solutions, and the ability to ensure high code quality and architectural consistency.
The Senior Java Developer collaborates closely with Product Owners, Managers, and other technical team members to align development activities with business objectives and contributes to technical discussions and decision‑making processes. The position offers the opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies in an agile environment that fosters innovation, scalability, and continuous improvement.
What will you do?
Design, develop, and maintain software applications for strategic products.
Analyze requirements, prepare solution designs, and implement features.
Automate infrastructure and application deployments.
Monitor and optimize system performance to guarantee high availability and reliability.
Contribute to system testing and support fault analyses.
Develop and execute unit tests to ensure code quality and functionality.
Works in SCRUM team on developing and building complex application solutions for customer DTAG
Implement customer’s requirements based on specification in selected object-oriented programming language into functional and user-friendly application
Provide testing of programmed functionality before deployment to avoid bugs and ensure quality of code
Listen to user requests, consult with internal /external customers, project managers.
Mentoring
Take part on internal evaluation processes
You will succeed if you:
Hold a Master’s degree in Information Technologies
Have at least 5 years of experience in ICT Systems Engineering
Are an expert in programming with Java and have strong knowledge of Spring Boot and Hibernate
Possess advanced knowledge of RESTful APIs, web services, containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and CI/CD pipelines
Have advanced experience with database development (OracleDB, PostgreSQL)
Are skilled in working with Linux-based operating systems
Demonstrate strong general IT skills
Excel in communication, analytical thinking, team cooperation, mentoring, and product management
Speak English at an upper-intermediate level (B2)
Bring experience from a senior application development position
You’re driven by a passion for AI, committed to continuous learning, and excited to make AI a core part of your daily work—constantly exploring innovative ways to implement it and unlock its full potential
