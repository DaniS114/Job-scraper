Responsibilities
We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer with deep experience building cloud‑native systems to help evolve this globally distributed foundation. In this role, you'll work on backend services operating at massive scale, with high expectations for reliability, security, and performance.
You'll join a collaborative team based in Hamburg, working closely with colleagues in the US and India, and contribute to capabilities used across Adobe products like Photoshop, Acrobat, Illustrator, Firefly, and Express.
Design, build, and operate Java‑based cloud services, owning them from design through production
Evolve scalable, secure, and reliable capabilities using modern cloud and distributed systems practices
Improve system performance, resilience, and operability across multiple cloud regions
Strengthen engineering quality through clear design, collaborative code reviews, and effective testing
Work closely with partner teams that build on our services
Requirements
8+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a strong backend or platform focus
Strong Java programming skills and a solid understanding of distributed systems
Hands‑on experience with cloud‑native services (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
Experience diagnosing and resolving issues in large‑scale, production systems
Strong communication skills in English (German is a plus)
Curiosity to learn and apply new technologies, including AI‑assisted development
What we offer
Why you'll enjoy working with us
Build cloud platform software that empowers everyone to create, trusted by customers around the world and designed to work reliably at global scale
Hybrid work model – 2-3 on‑site team days per week at our Hamburg office near the Fish Market, with flexibility to work from home
Inclusive, collaborative culture – We learn together, treat mistakes as opportunities to grow, and focus on outcomes, not heroes: success is a team effort
If this role excites you, but you don't meet every requirement, we still encourage you to apply. We value the perspective you bring and how you approach problems – not checking every box.
