Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for Vulnerability Management Engineer. If you have a strong IT background, are tech-savvy, and enjoy working analytically and process-oriented, you’ll become part of a dedicated Technology & Operations team. Together, we create a secure and future-proof IT environment.
You can work in a combination of : Nitra+Homeoffice/Presov+Homeoffice/remote by agreement
Key Responsibilities:
• Own the end to end vulnerability lifecycle — from scanning and analysis to prioritization and remediation
• Configure and operate enterprise grade scanning tools (e.g., Tenable One)
• Create impactful reports and KPIs for management, technical teams, and customers using modern reporting tools, especially PowerBI
• Contribute to the development of security policies, standards and workflows
• Support internal and external audits as well as compliance assessments
Personality requirements and skills
Required Qualifications:
• Completed IT education (school, university degree or similar) with a focus on cybersecurity or computer science
• Minimum 3 years of experience in IT security, ideally in vulnerability management and risk assessment
• Solid understanding of security standards (DORA, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, CIS Controls)
• Comfortable working with IT infrastructures (servers, networks, cloud) and risk evaluation frameworks (CVSS)
• Strong communication skills and the ability to act as a bridge between Security and Operations
• Hands-on experience with PowerBI
• Bonus: Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), container security and CI/CD environments
• Nice to have: Certifications such as CISSP, CISM, GIAC, OSCP, CRISC or similar
Language:
• English (German is nice to have)
Personal Attributes:
• Team-oriented with a strong ability to work cross-functionally with other IT teams
