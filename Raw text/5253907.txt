Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
As a Salesforce Administrator, you are the vital link between business and technology. You will ensure that Salesforce is optimized, scalable, and aligned with business needs - driving efficiency, adoption, and innovation across the organization.
You will act as a trusted advisor, challenging the status quo and continuously improving how Salesforce supports our business.
Your Key Responsibilities would be the following:
System Configuration & Automation (95%)
Customize Salesforce (fields, page layouts, record types) to align with business processes
Design and implement automation (Flows, approval processes) to streamline operations
Continuously optimize system performance and usability
Platform Management & Support (5%)
Manage users, roles, and access to ensure data security and compliance
Maintain data quality through validation rules and cleansing processes
Create insightful reports and dashboards for business stakeholders
Troubleshoot issues and ensure system stability
Support integrations with external systems
Conduct system health checks and manage upgrades
Stakeholder Collaboration
Gather and translate business requirements into scalable Salesforce solutions
Train and support users to drive adoption and best practices
Work closely with IT, security, and business teams
