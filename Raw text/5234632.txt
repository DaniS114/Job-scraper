POSITION
DESCRIPTION
We are looking for a skilled SAP Security & Authorizations Consultant with hands-on experience in S/4HANA to join our growing team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, implementing, maintaining, and optimizing SAP security frameworks and authorization concepts across complex landscapes. You will collaborate closely with functional, technical, and project teams to ensure compliant, efficient, and secure SAP system operations.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
•Design, build, and maintain SAP security and authorization concepts for S/4HANA and connected systems
•Perform role design, role testing, troubleshooting, and adjustments based on business requirements and in line with legal requirements, security standards etc.
•Collaborate with SAP functional and technical teams during implementations, rollout projects, and upgrades
•Analyze security incidents, propose mitigations, and continuously improve SAP security frameworks
•Support project documentation, security guidelines, and end‑user guidance
WHAT WE
EXPECT
•Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in IT or a related field
•3–5 years of experience in SAP Security & Authorizations
•Strong knowledge and hands-on experience with SAP S/4HANA security architecture
•Experience with Fiori authorization concepts, catalogs, and tiles
•Ability to design and implement role concepts from blueprint to go‑live
•English language proficiency at upper intermediate (B2) or advanced (C1) level
•German language proficiency at upper intermediate (B2) or advanced (C1) level
Nice-to-Have:
•Experience in multi-system landscapes (ECC, BW/4HANA, CRM, SRM, Solution Manager)
•Knowledge of cloud platform security (SAP BTP, IAS/IPS)
•Certification in SAP Security/GRC
