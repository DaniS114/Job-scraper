We are hiring a Senior QA Automation Engineer, who will act as a technical lead within QA and test teams and oversee all aspects of software and product testing as well as overall development quality across the company. The role includes developing test strategies, preparing test documentation, identifying defects, and reviewing QA reports. The Senior QA Automation Engineer leads the evaluation of test automation tools and technologies, choosing those most appropriate for each project, and drives the transition from manual to automated testing to minimize manual effort.
The role also includes leading the team, building test automation frameworks, and supporting QA teams with expert QA knowledge. The Senior QA Automation Engineer is responsible for defining and updating company quality standards to ensure high customer satisfaction, implementing test frameworks and test architecture, and setting up continuous integration within CI/CD pipelines. The role leads the entire QA process across one or multiple projects—from pre‑testing analysis, through software testing and test automation, to post‑testing evaluation. The Senior QA Automation Engineer ensures that all released products meet both company and industry quality standards, stays up to date with modern processes, established tools, techniques, and technologies, and ensures a consistently high degree of product quality. The role requires a good overview of DevOps methodology and frameworks such as the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) or similar.
About the project
This role focuses on automation testing within the Dedicated Connectivity & Infrastructure (DCI) Hub, specifically on regression testing of highly complex BSS releases prior to customer rollout. It supports enterprise‑grade network products and applications, ensuring quality, stability, and legal compliance across large‑scale, business‑critical systems.
