Responsibilities
Develop and maintain the front-end of our internal admin site, ensuring a user-friendly experience
Implement and improve user interfaces for new features and functionalities, particularly those related to our geofencing initiatives
Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to define, design, and ship new features
Contribute to the full development lifecycle, from concept and design to testing and deployment
Troubleshoot, debug, and optimize front-end and back-end components
Stay informed about emerging technologies and industry trends, advocating for adoption where beneficial
Monitor changes in browser behavior and advancements in device fingerprinting techniques
Requirements
Strong proficiency in TypeScript or JavaScript
Experience with front-end frameworks and libraries
Solid understanding of web development concepts and best practices
Familiarity with cloud platforms, specifically AWS
Experience with container orchestration, particularly Kubernetes
Proficiency with relational databases (PostgreSQL) and NoSQL databases (DynamoDB, Redis)
Experience in API design and development, with an understanding of RESTful principles
Fluent in spoken and written English

Company info
SEON is the command center for fraud prevention and AML compliance, helping thousands of companies worldwide stop fraud, reduce risk and protect revenue. Powered by 900+ real-time, first-party data signals, SEON enriches customer profiles, flags suspicious behavior and streamlines compliance workflows - all from one place. SEON provides richer data, more flexible and transparent analysis, and faster time to value than any other provider on the market. We’ve helped companies reduce fraud by 95% and achieve 32x ROI, and we’re growing fast, thanks to our partnerships with some of the world’s most ambitious digital brands like Revolut, Wise, and Bilt.
