Responsibilities
Identify, Drive the design of the national Open Banking framework, including its regulatory, market, and governance models.
Conduct comprehensive regulatory and legal gap analysis to define actionable requirements for the framework.
Develop the market operating model, covering participant onboarding, compliance monitoring, and dispute resolution processes.
Define high-level standards and rules of participation for all ecosystem members, including API principles and security baselines.
Design and structure a pilot program by identifying and prioritizing key use cases, setting success metrics, and defining its scope.
Lead and facilitate high-stakes workshops to manage alignment with senior stakeholders, including regulators, banks, and fintech leaders.
Produce and present high-quality, consulting-grade deliverables such as decision papers, strategic roadmaps, and steering committee presentations.
Requirements
Proven experience in strategy consulting (Big3/Big4/Tier-2) or an equivalent role within a regulator, bank, or fintech.
Proven ability to facilitate workshops and manage complex relationships with senior stakeholders, including C-level executives and government officials.
Strong background in the consulting, banking, fintech, or regulatory sectors.
Experience with large-scale digital finance initiatives involving multi-stakeholder governance.
Familiarity with regulatory gap analysis, API economy principles, and digital identity frameworks (e.g., OAuth, OIDC) is advantageous.
