Responsibilities
With us, you have the opportunity to perfectly combine your studies with practical experience while actively contributing to exciting projects. This allows you to gain valuable skills, expand your network, and grow both professionally and personally.
Your role
We are seeking a passionate and talented student who wants to make an impact by shaping next-generation products at ZEISS. Together with a team of students, scientists and research engineers, you will design, implement, and evaluate innovative miniaturized scanning solutions for use at Carl Zeiss Corporate Research and Technology.
What We Offer
An opportunity to join a growing company with many career options
Working hands-on with cutting-edge technology
Interdisciplinary mentorship by experienced PhD-level experts
A modern working environment, partial remote work is possible
Requirements
You are enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree in physics, engineering, computer science or related fields
You have very good programming experience, preferably scientific and hardware-oriented programming and Python
Experience with control systems and/or embedded systems is a plus
You are interested in technology and motivated to cooperate on demanding tasks
You are enthusiastic to learn and explore with a high degree of initiative and creativity
You are committed to collaborating in cross-functional teams
Good communication skills in English and/or German
Sounds exciting? Then become part of #teamZEISS and help us shape the future! Please provide your complete application documents (CV, transcript of records, etc.).
