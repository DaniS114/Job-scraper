Responsibilities
Do you dream of becoming Product Owner one day?
Mercedes-Benz Insurance Services is the insurance service provider of Mercedes-Benz Group. Our primary focus is the worldwide development and expansion of automotive insurance products for Mercedes-Benz customers. We also provide private insurance products to our employees in Germany.
If you're eager to be part of a team that's reshaping the insurance landscape, consider this your invitation to join IPOS, the backbone of Mercedes-Benz Insurance Services. IPOS is a global platform designed to simplify and integrate insurance solutions within the Mercedes-Benz ecosystem, supporting multiple markets (currently 9) and offering a range of products, including motor insurance, payment protection, and bundled solutions. We drive sales, enhance customer experiences, and maximize value through streamlined processes and data-driven insights.
We're offering a role where you'll gain practical experience and professional development, mentored by our Product Owners. For those interested in SAFe methodology, eager to develop an agile mindset, and keen to see how strategies unfold into real-world execution, this position is tailored for you. Engage with cutting-edge software development practices in a team that proudly holds a Mercedes-Benz Insurance (MBI) award for agile empowerment.
While Stuttgart is our preferred onsite location, we offer the flexibility to work remotely from anywhere in Germany to a certain extent. Teams will come together at the office on scheduled days for collaborative work and events.
Your role will involve:
Support the Product Owner and Requirements Engineers in gathering and structuring business requirements, facilitating discussions with the development team to ensure a shared understanding and contributing to the product's foundation
Testing Insights: learn from our testing experts about documenting and conducting test plans, ensuring our products meet high standards of quality and performance
Support the development and execution of automated tests, gaining practical experience in ensuring software quality while learning industry-standard automation tools and techniques
Agile Support: help our agile teams with planning processes, playing a supportive role in our product management efforts
Product Insights: Collect and analyze product data and user feedback to provide actionable insights that drive product improvements, backlog prioritization, and data-driven decisions
Management Presentations: summarize findings and present them to management, providing clear and actionable insights
Support the refinement of the product communication plan and creation of internal updates for the company portal, contributing to broader awareness of the product's success throughout the organization
Join us and contribute to a team that values innovation and excellence. Your work will help shape the future of digital insurance.
The activity can begin from May 2026.
Requirements
Studies in the field of computer science, business informatics, or a related field, with a minimum of three semesters of relevant experience -
prior experience as a working student or intern is highly desirable
Business fluent in English - proficiency in German is advantageous
Proactive individuals who are natural team players - candidates should be able to operate independently, demonstrating a high degree of self-motivation and initiative
A strong digital affinity is essential, with a keen interest in staying abreast of technological advancements
Analytical mindset, with the ability to approach problems systematically and logically
Attention to Detail: a meticulous approach to work, with a focus on precision and accuracy in all tasks undertaken
