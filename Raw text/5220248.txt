What will you do?
Purpose
As a Lead Cloud Architect, you are responsible for designing, governing, and evolving the enterprise-grade cloud architecture of the One Data Ecosystem (ODE) on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). You ensure platform stability, scalability, security, and compliance while enabling advanced data, analytics, and AI workloads.
You act as a technical authority and strategic leader, working closely with Data & Analytics teams, business stakeholders, and international engineering teams to deliver a future-proof, secure, and highly available cloud platform.
This role is critical to maintaining the stability and availability of the ODE_S environment and driving the successful migration from legacy architectures to GCP.
About team and project
You will be part of the Finance & B2B domain within the DATA TRIBE, contributing to the transformation of the enterprise data and cloud landscape within the One Data Ecosystem (ODE).
Our mission is to:
Migrate and modernize existing data and platform architectures to GCP
Establish standardized, secure, and scalable cloud landing zones
Enable data engineering, analytics, and AI teams with robust cloud foundations
You will work in a future-oriented, agile, and international environment, collaborating across domains and geographies.
WHAT WILL YOU DO?
Cloud Architecture & Strategy
Analyze the current (“As-Is”) cloud and data platform architecture, identifying risks, gaps, and optimization opportunities
Design enterprise-scale GCP architectures that are scalable, resilient, secure, and cost-efficient
Define cloud strategy, target architecture, and migration roadmaps for ODE workloads
Establish cloud landing zones, VPC design, networking, and identity architecture
Platform & Infrastructure Design
Architect and govern GCP infrastructure using services such as:
o    Compute: GKE, Cloud Run, Cloud Functions
o    Data & Storage: BigQuery, Dataplex, Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL
o    Messaging & Streaming: Pub/Sub
o    AI/ML: Vertex AI
Design and manage data platforms, data lakes, and analytics architectures
Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform for compliant, repeatable deployments
Define standards for CI/CD pipelines and GitOps practices
Security, Governance & Compliance
Design and enforce cloud security architecture, including:
o    IAM, VPCs, firewalls, VPNs, Cloud KMS
Ensure compliance with GDPR and enterprise security standards
Apply data protection concepts such as data classification, pseudonymization, and anonymization
Define governance models for cloud resources, cost management, and access control
Operations & Reliability
Ensure platform stability, availability, and performance of the ODE_S environment
Architect monitoring, logging, and observability solutions (Cloud Monitoring, Logging, Prometheus)
Identify and resolve complex platform and infrastructure issues
Prevent availability risks and operational bottlenecks
Leadership & Collaboration
Lead international and cross-functional teams of architects, engineers, and data experts
Translate business and technical requirements into clear architectural designs and specifications
Support presales, solution design, and strategic decision-making
Mentor junior architects and engineers; establish architecture best practices and standards
Document architecture decisions, patterns, and technical solutions thoroughly
