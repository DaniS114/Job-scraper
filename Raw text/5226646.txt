About the Role
As IT Business Analyst, you'll drive the requirement engineering of our cutting-edge tools and platforms. We're seeking someone with an entrepreneurial spirit, pragmatic mindset, and intellectual curiosity who is focused on achieving meaningful progress.
Key Responsibilities
•	Gather and analyse business requirements in close collaboration with the Product Manager
•	Translate complex business terminology into technical language and vice versa
•	Create clear, concise requirements and acceptance criteria that are unambiguous, complete, consistent, and verifiable
•	Design and optimize user journeys to enhance overall experience and efficiency
•	Facilitate communication between technical teams and business stakeholders
•	Create tangible metrics for tracking user adoption and business impact
•	Present technical progress to a broader business audience
About the Team
If tackling complex challenges in a wide range of industries excites you, then join our innovative team, where you are at the forefront of creating new ingenious solutions in a collaborative way.
Our team is an empowered tech counterpart for business units, responsible for the design and implementation of inventive data projects. We develop new analytics capabilities and improve Swiss Re's risk assessment capabilities.
Joining us, you will learn not only how insurance premiums are modelled and how data analytics can enrich business but also improve in stakeholders' management, agile development practices and tools used to drive impactful projects.
