Responsibilities
Your Future:
* Within the Data & Processes team of Dr. August Oetker Nahrungsmittel KG, we provide data & processes management standards and guidelines for our 40 international subsidiaries. We work together with our colleagues from around the world. We ensure that data is accessible, consistent, and structured in a way that supports decision-making and business objectives. This is how we accelerate the digitalization at Dr. Oetker.
* In this function within the Data & Processes department you are part of the Foundation team, which is responsible for establishing the strategy, governance, processes, and architecture necessary to manage and leverage data effectively within our organization.
* As an intern in the International Data & Processes department at Dr. Oetker, you will gain valuable insights into the company and the work of the Data & Processes team.
Your Responsibilities:
Develop a comprehensive understanding
of the International Data & Processes department, and the
Foundation team
, including
their roles and responsibilities.
Support the
further development of our process management maturity
by conducting
process modelling, mapping, and analysis
using our process modelling tool.
Collaborate closely with
International Process Experts
and
Business Architect Teams (BATs)
to model
end-to-end processes
across different departments, sites, and countries.
Answer questions from various stakeholders regarding
process modelling conventions (BPMN 2.0)
and the
process modelling tool
.
Actively contribute to
Robotic Process Automation (RPA)
initiatives by preparing and modelling
automation use cases
in close cooperation with the
IT Business Process Automation team
.
