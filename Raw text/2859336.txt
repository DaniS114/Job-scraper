Responsibilities
Head of Software Development, Microsoft 365 Apps (80%-100%)
Join a fast-growing innovator in Microsoft 365-based compliance software
Born in the heart of Switzerland, BPA Solutions delivers modern electronic Quality Management Systems built on Microsoft 365 and SharePoint. We help organizations move beyond scattered spreadsheets and manual follow-ups by introducing structured processes, smart automation, and real operational visibility.
Our platform blends Swiss precision with intuitive design and AI-driven capabilities. Organizations in highly regulated industries rely on BPA to meet compliance requirements while strengthening efficiency, collaboration, and operational excellence.
BPA Apps run directly within clients’ Microsoft 365 environments using the SharePoint Framework (SPFx), complemented by optional Azure-hosted services and integrations with Dynamics 365. The BPA ecosystem includes preconfigured AI tools, Power Automate workflows, Power Apps, and Power BI dashboards, creating a seamless and powerful user experience.
To shape our technology strategy and lead our development teams into the next stage of innovation we are seeking a visionary :
Head of Software Development, Microsoft 365 Apps (80%-100%)
Your Mission
As Head Software Development, you will:
Lead and grow our core development team at HQ and coordinate remote teams.
Drive product innovation aligned with Microsoft’s evolving ecosystem—SharePoint, Power Platform, Dataverse, Dynamics 365, and AI.
Develop BPA components and help other developers on analysis and development with BPA technologies (C#, .NET Core, SPFx, React, SharePoint REST, CSOM and PnP).
Shape the technical roadmap and ensure scalability, security, and performance across our product suite.
Ensure our applications are transactable on Microsoft AppSource platform.
Spearhead our transition to a fully integrated Dynamics 365 QMS solution.
Maintain best coding practices, performance, maintainability, and security in compliance with ISO 27001.
Design and maintain automated build, test, and deployment workflows to ensure fast, reliable releases (Azure DevOps).
Manage Scrum sprint content and calendar.
Your Profile
We’re looking for someone who:
Has proven experience as a successful Head of Development in Microsoft-centric environments, ideally with SharePoint or Dynamics 365.
Is passionate about Microsoft technologies, AI, and driving innovation.
Has a clear strategic understanding of Microsoft’s direction for SharePoint, Dataverse, and Dynamics 365.
Demonstrates strong leadership and coordination skills across remote teams.
Thrives in a collaborative, agile environment with a strong team spirit.
Is fluent in spoken and written English - minimum C1 (spoken French is a plus).
What BPA Solutions Offers
Full-time position based at our HQ in Yverdon-les-Bains.
Hybrid work model: up to 2 days/week remote.
International and dynamic work environment.
A monthly in-office massage therapist.
Regular team-building events and free coffee.
Ready to take the next step? Send us your resume and a motivation letter that projects you into the role — tell us what energizes you about our mission, our technology, and the apps we create.
