Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, implement, and manage Azure cloud infrastructure to support Databricks and data platform workloads
Build and maintain Azure resources including Resource Groups, VNets, Key Vaults, Storage Accounts, and Azure Active Directory (AAD) integrations
Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform, Bicep, or ARM templates
Apply DevOps best practices for automation, deployment, monitoring, and platform reliability
Ensure cloud environments are secure, scalable, and cost-effective
Collaborate with data engineers, architects, and security teams to support enterprise-grade data platforms
Troubleshoot and optimize infrastructure and platform performance
Contribute to standards, documentation, and continuous improvement initiatives
Requirements
Strong experience with Azure Cloud Infrastructure and DevOps
Minimum 6 months of Databricks experience
Hands-on expertise with Azure Resource Groups, Virtual Networks (VNets), Azure Key Vault, Azure Storage Accounts, Azure Active Directory (AAD)
Proven experience implementing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform, Bicep, or ARM templates
Experience operating and supporting Azure Databricks platforms
Solid understanding of cloud security, networking, and identity management
Ability to work independently and take ownership in a senior-level role
Nice-to-have
CI/CD pipeline experience (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, or similar), Experience with monitoring and logging tools in Azure, Knowledge of data engineering or analytics platforms, Azure certifications (e.g., Azure Solutions Architect, DevOps Engineer)
What we offer
Participation in full-cycle and diverse international projects
Opportunity to work as a specialist or manager/team lead
Constant career development with internal mentorship
Access to a whole set of learning platforms, paid certifications
Flexible working conditions, home working is allowed
Attractive base salary & wide range of benefits including cafeteria, bonuses, private health insurance package, life insurance, AYCM sport card, referral bonus, family-oriented benefits, company shares on a discount price
Company info
Accenture is a leading global provider of a broad range of professional services in strategy and consulting, interactive marketing, information technology and business operations services, with digital capabilities in all of these areas. With more than 800,000 employees worldwide, we serve clients in more than 120 countries. Accenture globally has ranked 4th on the Great Place to Work® World’s Best Workplaces™.
