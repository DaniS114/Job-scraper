Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a Citrix / VDI Engineer who will be responsible for the design, implementation, operation, and continuous improvement of virtual desktop and application environments. The role focuses on providing stable, secure, and high-performing VDI solutions while ensuring a great user experience.
Responsibilities
Design, implement, and maintain
Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops (VDI)
environments
Operate and support Citrix infrastructure, including troubleshooting performance and availability issues
Manage VDI components such as
Delivery Controllers, StoreFront, Citrix Cloud,
Collaborate with infrastructure, networking, security, and workplace teams
Optimize VDI performance, scalability, and user experience
Support Windows-based virtual desktops and applications
Participate in upgrades, migrations, and lifecycle management
Create and maintain technical documentation and operational procedures
Ensure compliance with security and internal IT standards
