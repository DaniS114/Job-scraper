Job description, responsibilities and duties
Overview
The role is twofold and requires both the complex maintenance of the new Market Data Hub application and the development and management of its IT‑infrastructure integrations.
As an
IT Application Manager
, you will support the new Treasury Market Data Hub application across the full application lifecycle, ensuring adherence to ITIL processes, compliance standards, and quality requirements. This includes activities such as deployment support, operations, incident management, testing, and other related tasks.
As an
Integration Developer
, you will be part of the development team responsible for building integration pipelines to various Treasury interfaces. Your tasks will include developing and maintaining pipelines that distribute market data and other data types. These interfaces will be implemented using our strategic integration platform, SnapLogic.
This role is part of the Treasury IT team operating from the Global Delivery Centre (GDC) in Bratislava and supports operational processes for the global Corporate Treasury function.
Task and Responsibilities
Support ITIL based application management, including Incident, Change, and related processes
Provide 1st and 2ndlevel support for the SaaS platform (Market Data Hub)
Ensure compliance and quality assurance for the ICFR relevant application, including coordination of internal and external audits
Create, configure, and maintain the functional setup of the Market Data Hub
Manage access rights, support UAT activities (test suite creation, test execution), and handle release management (validation of new versions and fixes)
Perform functional monitoring, maintain Treasury reporting, and create operational dashboards
Conduct requirements engineering for integration needs and interfaces to and from Treasury Management applications
Prepare technical specifications and integration designs
Align with source and target application owners regarding integration start and end points
Develop and maintain interfaces in SnapLogic
