Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a colleague to strengthen our team. Location of work : in Prešov or Nitra
Strengthening our team with main focus as operator for systems and technical applications to the technical support of the first level of treasury and risk applications.
Your tasks:
• First level technical support in a 5x24-hour, five-shift operation
Begins Sunday at 10:00 PM and runs continuously until Saturday at 6:00 AM.
Weekly Rotating Shifts:
Night Shift: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM
Early Shift: 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Evening Shift: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM
Day Shift: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Weekend On-Call Duty:
From Saturday 6:00 AM to Sunday 10:00 PM, an on-call service is in place.
• Perform operational activities such as software updates, system checks, provision of test environments in cooperation with the second level and the service owner
• Resolving technical problems in application systems, including maintaining regular operations
• Active monitoring and support of application components
• Active participation in the continuous internal improvement process
• Documentation and implementation of operational processes in a modern ticketing system
• Weekend assignments and on-call duty

What are we expecting:
• Knowledge of UNIX operating systems (Linux, AIX and Solaris)
• Knowledge of scripting languages (especially Unix shell scripts, SQL)
• Knowledge of infrastructure components in large IT environments
• Analytical thinking and customer-orientation
• Proficiency in written and spoken English
As advantage is:
• Knowledge of database technologies (ORACLE)
• Knowledge of the operation of enterprise job scheduling systems (Automic - UC4)
• Experience with Windows Server
