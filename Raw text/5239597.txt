We are seeking an experienced and highly skilled .NET engineer to join our technology team. The ideal candidate combines strong backend development expertise with deep knowledge of database architecture, data processing, and system performance optimization.

AI tools and services
(ChatGPT, Copilot, Azure OpenAI)
Collaborating on data and AI-driven projects across industries
