We are looking for an

This role focuses primarily on delivering multi‑site infrastructure initiatives across networking, endpoint environments, data centers, cloud services, resilience, and production‑site connectivity. Security aspects are included, but mainly as a natural extension of good infrastructure design—not as the core function of the role.
