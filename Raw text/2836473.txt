Company info
dentalPrices is a fast-growing healthtech startup building the global infrastructure for dental price transparency, booking, and payments. We help patients discover the right clinic, book appointments seamlessly, and access installment payment options — making dentistry more accessible worldwide.
Operating across Europe and expanding globally, we combine marketplace dynamics, fintech (BNPL & payment solutions), and AI-driven workflows to transform how patients and clinics connect.
We are building a scalable, borderless platform designed for rapid international growth — and we’re just getting started.
