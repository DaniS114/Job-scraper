Responsibilities
Implement new or maintain existing solutions specialized in one or more SAP modules
Provide functional expertise, guidance, and instruction on SAP processes
Propose, configure and test business solutions according to requirements
Keep close communication with clients and development teams
Requirements
At least 4-5 years of experience as an SAP consultant
Experience in one or more of the following areas: SAP FI, CO, MM, PP, PM, EWM
Experience in SAP implementation, customization, and/or maintenance projects
Advanced English skills (German knowledge is an advantage)
Problem-solving skills, creative mindset and a can-do attitude
Proven knowledge or experience with S/4HANA is an advantage
What we offer
Participation in full-cycle and diverse international projects
Flexible working conditions, home working is allowed
Attractive base salary & wide range of benefits included cafeteria, bonuses, private health insurance package, AYCM sport card, family-oriented benefits, company shares on a discount price
Opportunity to work as a specialist or manager/team lead
Chance to leading-edge functional and technical SAP trainings
Access to a whole set of learning platforms, paid SAP certifications
Constant career development with personalized internal mentorship
Company info
Accenture is a global professional services company that excels in digital, cloud, and security solutions. We have a longstanding relationship with SAP that extends more than 40 years. As a leading business partner, we work with SAP end-to-end from product innovation to development and delivery.
