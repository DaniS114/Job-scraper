Responsibilities
This will be your future job
Development and maintenance of embedded software components in C/C++
Porting and rehosting existing software solutions to new target architectures
Parallelisation of software processes on multi-core processors
Integration and use of tools such as Tiger Lab for simulation and analysis
Development and maintenance of software modules in ADA
Working on x86-based platforms and other embedded architectures
Close collaboration with system architects, hardware developers and test engineers
Your benefits at FERCHAU
Fascinating and pioneering development of technological solutions for defense and security in the aerospace industry
The opportunity to further develop yourself in a talented and international team
Excellent capacity to balance your personal, family and professional commitments with flexible working time models and up to 2 mobile working days each week
Plentiful career opportunities within our team: individual professional development in various soft and hard skills at several institutes, as well as ready-made technical career paths for long-term development prospects
Numerous additional benefits through our GVP collective agreement including corporate benefits, as well as regular team and sports events to support a healthy work-life balance
Requirements
Your profile
Degree in computer science, electrical engineering or comparable qualification
Several years of experience in embedded software development in C or C++
In-depth knowledge of rehosting and parallelisation of processor architectures
Knowledge of programming with ADA and VxWorks is an advantage
Analytical thinking, structured working style and ability to work in a team
