Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Define and own the performance architecture for Sportsbook applications, focusing on frontend performance and customer-perceived latency
Establish and maintain performance-related non-functional requirements (NFRs) across web, mobile, and platform layers
Work with Product, Engineering, and Architecture to align performance goals with business objectives and customer experience
Drive best practices for frontend performance including rendering and hydration strategies, asset loading, caching, and CDN usage, and web performance metrics
Collaborate with frontend teams to review designs, implementations, and technical decisions from a performance perspective
Partner with Solution Architects, Platform Architects, and DevOps to identify performance bottlenecks across infrastructure, networking, cloud and Kubernetes environments, APIs and backend dependencies
Contribute to architectural decisions and roadmaps to ensure long-term scalability and performance sustainability
Define and promote performance measurement standards, tooling, and dashboards
Lead performance analysis, load testing, and capacity planning activities
Participate in incident reviews and root cause analysis, ensuring performance learnings are fed back into architecture and design
Act as a performance ambassador across the organization
Communicate complex performance topics clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
Mentor teams and help raise the overall performance maturity of the Sportsbook organization
Requirements
Strong experience with frontend performance engineering in large-scale, high-traffic systems
Solid understanding of web architectures and modern frontend frameworks
Solid understanding of HTTP, networking fundamentals, caching, and CDNs
Solid understanding of cloud infrastructure (preferably AWS) and containerized environments (e.g. Kubernetes)
Experience designing and reviewing systems with performance, scalability, and resilience in mind
Proven experience working in architectural or cross-team technical roles
Ability to influence architectural decisions without direct authority
Comfortable collaborating with multiple teams and architectural disciplines
Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal
Ability to explain performance trade-offs and recommendations clearly to engineering, product, and non-technical stakeholders
Experience working closely with multiple departments in a complex organization
What we offer
Work on a mission-critical, real-time Sportsbook platform operating at significant scale
Shape how performance is designed and delivered across both current systems and future initiatives
Collaborate with experienced architects and engineering leaders across multiple domains
Make a measurable impact on customer experience and platform quality
