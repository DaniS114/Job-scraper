Állás leírása
Who we are
VOIS (Vodafone Intelligent Solutions) is a strategic arm of Vodafone Group Plc, creating value for customers by delivering intelligent solutions through Talent, Technology & Transformation.
As the largest shared services organisation in the global telco industry with 30,000 FTE, our portfolio of next-generation solutions and services are designed in partnership with customers across Vodafone Group, local markets, and partner markets to simplify and drive growth.
About this role
We are seeking an experienced UX/UI Designer who can create user‑centred, accessible, visually coherent digital experiences. The individual will lead UX and UI activities from research to high‑fidelity design, apply design‑system principles, and support agile squads with intuitive solutions that contribute to product and customer‑experience improvements. This role suits someone who enjoys solving design challenges, working collaboratively across functions, and translating insights into meaningful digital journeys.
What you will do
Produce responsive and multi‑platform UX and UI assets including wireframes, flows, prototypes, and high‑fidelity compositions.
Maintain structured, systematic design files and supporting documentation.
Apply and evolve design‑system components and style guides.
Incorporate research insights into design decisions and advocate for user needs.
Communicate design rationale effectively to peers, partners, and stakeholders.
Contribute to measurable improvements in product performance and user experience across digital journeys.
Who you are
You have 2–5 years of UX, UI, or Product Design experience, ideally in a product or service‑led environment.
You work confidently within agile, cross‑functional, distributed teams.
You are proficient in Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud, producing high‑quality wireframes, user journeys, and prototypes.
You understand modern responsive design, usability principles, and mobile/web standards.
You have a relevant qualification or equivalent experience and a strong portfolio showing UX/UI design outcomes.
You present ideas clearly and explain design decisions with confidence and clarity
What’s in it for you
As part of VOIS, you will join a community that prioritises learning, collaboration, and purposeful innovation. You will benefit from opportunities to develop your craft in UX and UI design, participate in cross‑functional projects, and contribute to products used across multiple markets.
You will work in an inclusive, people‑centred environment that encourages curiosity, continuous learning, and creative exploration. You will also gain exposure to agile methodologies, design‑system practices, and user‑research techniques that support long‑term professional growth.
What skills you will learn
You will build a deeper understanding of user‑centred design by learning how to translate research insights into scalable digital experiences. You will strengthen your skills in responsive interface design, prototyping, and interaction design using industry‑standard tools. You will also enhance your ability to work within agile frameworks, collaborate across diverse product teams, and contribute to design‑system development. Additionally, you will grow your communication and storytelling capabilities by presenting design solutions and engaging with stakeholders.
