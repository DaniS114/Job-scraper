Job description, responsibilities and duties
Be part of a well-established international project with significant global impact.
We are looking for s
Senior Software Test Engineer
to strengthen our quality assurance capability within a large-scale enterprise platform used across multiple countries.
This is a senior-level role with strong ownership, technical depth, and the opportunity to actively mentor and influence the broader engineering team.
As a Senior Software Test Engineer, you will contribute to the quality assurance of complex software systems and applications, ensuring delivery aligned with client and business requirements.
You will work in a cross-functional Agile team alongside developers, product owners, designers, and data specialists, directly contributing to product reliability, scalability, and performance.
Key Responsibilities
Perform manual and automated testing of new and existing software products
Design, develop, and maintain robust test automation frameworks
Execute system, regression, integration, performance, and user acceptance testing
Analyze business requirements and translate them into effective test scenarios and test cases
Identify, document, and track defects using bug tracking tools
Prepare comprehensive test plans (scope, objectives, risks, deliverables)
Monitor and report qualitative and quantitative test metrics
Ensure testing is timely, repeatable, and efficient
Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and automation strategy
Communicate with internal stakeholders and end users to ensure solution accuracy and completeness
Independently resolve complex technical and application-level issues
Mentor junior team members and provide technical guidance
