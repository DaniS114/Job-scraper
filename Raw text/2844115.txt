Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, develop, and maintain cloud infrastructure solutions for SaaS/PaaS products using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles
Implement and optimize CI/CD pipelines to enable efficient software delivery
Create and maintain APIs and automation scripts to streamline infrastructure management
Deploy and orchestrate containerized applications in production environments
Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions for high-availability services
Collaborate with development teams to troubleshoot infrastructure issues
Participate in on-call rotations to ensure system reliability and uptime
Contribute to architectural decisions and infrastructure planning discussions
Stay up to date with cloud technology trends and implement best practices
Requirements
2-3 years of hands-on experience working with AWS
Proficiency with at least one Infrastructure as Code(IaC) tool: Terraform, AWS CloudFormation, or Pulumi
Strong experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
Solid understanding of high-availability patterns and disaster recovery strategies
Linux system administration skills (shell scripting, package management, networking)
Experience with CI/CD tools and pipelines (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions etc.)
Understanding of security best practices for cloud environments
Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail
Excellent communication skills in English (written and verbal)
Nice-to-have
Active AWS certifications (Solutions Architect, DevOps Engineer, SysOps Administrator), Expertise with configuration management tools (Ansible, Chef, Puppet), Programming experience in Python, Go, or similar languages, Experience operating production services in a DevOps capacity, Contributions to relevant open-source projects, Experience with automated testing methodologies, Familiarity with observability tools (CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, ELK stack)
What we offer
Participation in interesting, long-term projects
Opportunities for continuous professional growth and valuable training (including AWS certifications)
Access to cutting-edge cloud technologies, AI tools, and automation platforms
Company English lessons with a native teacher
Flexible working hours, home office options, and a healthy work-life balance
Dog-friendly downtown office in Budapest
Opportunities for career advancement and leadership roles
Company info
Cheppers was founded in 2012 and today we are the market leaders among the Hungarian Drupal companies, with a strong international reputation. We currently have 40+ colleagues who work on cloud services and consulting (AWS) and Drupal based web solutions.
We believe in continuous learning and we like to share knowledge with each other through in-house training.
We are transparent and aim to be as open as possible with each other, and we strongly believe in growing together.
Most of our colleagues have participated in open source projects.
We are regular attendees/presenters/organizers/code sprinters/sponsors of professional conferences in Hungary and abroad.
More than half of our team members have visited our clients in Western Europe and the USA to work on-site or to attend team building events.
We have an office in Chicago and a constantly growing team in the USA.
From 2019 we are a member of the AWS Partner Network.
Lots of our colleagues have acquired AWS and Acquia certifications.
In 2025, we became the first official GitLab partner in Hungary.
