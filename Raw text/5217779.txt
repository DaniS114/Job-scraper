Security Specialist
Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
The Security Specialist connects application design, AI solutions, and cybersecurity. This hands-on role ensures that applications are built and operated securely while meeting business, regulatory, and organizational requirements.,
Key Responsibilities:
Business Partnership
Embed security requirements into applications and AI solutions without limiting agility.
Translate technical security concepts into clear, practical documentation.
Support teams in understanding and applying security best practices.
Project Delivery
Document security controls, configurations, and risk assessments for new and evolving solutions.
Coordinate investigations with specialized security teams.
Contribute to threat modeling and secure design reviews.
Operational Stability
Act as the primary contact for security topics in live systems.
Maintain documentation for monitoring and incident response.
Support compliance by aligning documentation with policies and audit needs.
Track and report the effectiveness of implemented controls.
Team Contribution
Promote continuous improvement and proactively identify issues.
Support and strengthen company culture through collaboration and best practices.
Professional Conduct
Complete administrative tasks accurately and on time.
Follow internal policies, procedures, and code of conduct.
Encourage effective communication and constructive feedback.
Support organizational values and assist with additional duties as needed.
