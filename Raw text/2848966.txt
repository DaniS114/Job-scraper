Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries with over 30,000 employees worldwide. The firm is committed to helping organizations achieve lasting improvements in performance and build capabilities to sustain growth. McKinsey's mission is to deliver transformative solutions through technology, innovation, and deep industry expertise, fostering an entrepreneurial culture that values creativity, collaboration, and impact.
