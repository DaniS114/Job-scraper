Data Manager – Procurement & SAP MM
We are looking for a skilled Data Manager to join our team and play a key role in the development and management of procurement-related master data in SAP ECC and S/4HANA.
In this role, you will focus primarily on contracts, tenders, and MM objects, supporting a strategic transformation project aimed at digitalization, improved data quality, and the implementation of robust contract management processes. Acting as a subject matter expert in procurement data, you will actively contribute to shaping and evolving procurement data governance across the organization.

About
the position
Key Responsibilities:
Manage procurement master data objects in SAP MM, especially contracts, tenders, and purchasing info records.
Participate in the implementation of contract and tendering processes in SAP.
Analyze business requirements and propose solutions within the SAP data model (ECC/S/4HANA).
Collaborate with procurement, IT, and data governance teams on solution design, testing, and deployment.
Define and maintain mapping and transformation rules for data distribution between systems.
Ensure data quality, consistency, and compliance across procurement-related data domains.
Identify and implement process improvements and automation opportunities.
Prepare and execute test scenarios, support defect resolution and root cause analysis.
Create documentation and deliver training for procurement data processes.
Work in an agile environment (SAFe framework) and contribute to cross-functional teams.
Support internal audits and compliance checks related to procurement data.
Strongly focused on Data Governance

Personality requirements and skills
Proven experience with SAP MM, especially in areas related to contracts, tenders, and procurement master data.
Strong understanding of end-to-end procurement processes and their data dependencies.
Experience with data management and governance in large-scale SAP environments.
Hands-on experience with SAP ECC and/or S/4HANA.
Experience with at least one Master Data Management (MDM) tool, ideally SAP MDG-M (Master Data Governance for Materials).
Familiarity with customizing settings in SAP MM related to procurement data.
Experience with digital transformation and automation initiatives.
Agile mindset and experience working in SAFe, Scrum, or Kanban teams.
Strong analytical, documentation, and communication skills.
English – C1 level minimum; German is an advantage
