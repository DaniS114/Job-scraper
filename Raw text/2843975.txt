Responsibilities
Your tasks
Taking responsibility:
Responsibility for the successful planning, execution, and completion of internal IT infrastructure projects; work closely with other teams to deliver projects on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards; lead IT projects by developing and managing project plans, including defining project scope, goals, documentation and deliverables
Living collaboration:
Coordinate and manage project resources, including personnel, equipment and third-party vendors; lead follow-up projects to reflect process compliance and Leadec standards; act as a point of contact for internal departments as well as collaborate with our global IT teams and external partners
Tackling tasks:
Track project progress, identify potential risks and develop mitigation plans; effectively communicate project status and updates to stakeholders; ensure adherence to project timelines, budgets, and quality standards
Being passionate about technology:
Understand the technical requirements and manage partners and vendors; provide technical support to troubleshoot and resolve integration issues; assist with handover to operations and service desk support.
Driving Progress:
Implement and manage project change control processes; foster a collaborative and productive project environment; implement the strategic governance of Leadec Global IT
Requirements
Your profile
Education:
Completed studies in computer science, business informatics or comparable education (i.e. IT specialist (m/f/d) with several years of practical experience); professional certification (PRINCE2, PMP etc.) is an advantage
Experience:
At least 3 years of professional experience in enterprise level company or IT consulting
Know-How:
Strong project management and organizational skills; experience in analyzing problems and finding and elaborating solutions
Expertise:
Understanding of general IT topics (security, hardware, software, network etc.); excellent understanding of organization's processes, policies, goals and objectives; in-depth knowledge regarding project management frameworks; risk management; process management (e.g. BPMN)
Personality:
Assertiveness and excellent communication skills; systematic and goal-oriented way of working as part of a team; willingness to constantly deal with new technologies
Languages:
Conversationally fluent English and German language skills required (at least B1-B2 level)
Travel:
Willingness to occasionally travel nationally and internationally

Company info
Every day, our services ensure that everything runs smoothly in our customers' production facilities around the world. We think in terms of solutions and optimise along the entire process - from planning to maintenance, modernisation and digitalisation. As a service specialist in the manufacturing industry, we know that people make the difference. Respect, team spirit and variety define working at Leadec. We are proud of our work - over 22,500 different personalities at more than 350 locations on four continents for over 60 years.
Join our global IT operations team, which manages and continuously develops our state-of-the-art IT infrastructure from the network to the cloud.
