AI SERVICES - Automation Engineer – n8n Workflows , BA
Join our team of AI Team at Deutsche Telekom Services Europe in Slovakia, where we design and deliver cutting-edge automation and integration solutions. Our work connects systems, transforms data, and automates manual processes, making a real impact across the company.
We are looking for a technically skilled AI Automation Engineer to design and implement advanced n8n workflows, helping scale automation across our internal systems.

About
the position
What you will do:
Design, implement, and maintain automation workflows in n8
Build end-to-end integrations between internal and external system
Work with REST APIs, webhooks, and event-driven integration
Process, transform, and validate data in multiple formats (JSON, CSV, XML, etc.)
Implement workflow logic: filtering, mapping, aggregation, branching, and error handling
Connect workflows to Large Language Models and design effective prompts
Work with databases using SQL, Spark, or GraphQL queries
Monitor, debug, and optimize workflow executions for performance and reliability
Collaborate with business stakeholders and analysts to translate requirements into technical automation solutions
Document workflows and integrations
Identify opportunities for automation and continuous process improvement
We work in a HYBRID STYLE of working with the possibility of working from home , but twice a week we meet in the office in Košice or Bratislava for meetings.
The published salary is the minimum possible offer.
The starting salary may be higher depending on the extent of fulfillment of the employee's requirements (education, language skills, required practice, personality assumptions and skills).
