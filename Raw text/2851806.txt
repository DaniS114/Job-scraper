Responsibilities
Driving business development with US insurance carriers, brokers, and InsurTechs by identifying prospects, building pipelines, and leveraging insights on trends, pain points, and technology adoption
Developing expertise in insurance regulatory and compliance requirements to guide strategy, solution positioning, and client engagement
Collaborating with product, delivery, and technical teams to tailor custom software, data and AI solutions with value propositions for underwriting, claims, risk, and customer experience
Leading client presentations, demos, and proposals, managing complex sales cycles across multiple stakeholders, including IT, operations, and C-level executives
Executing targeted outbound campaigns, attending industry events, and building referral networks to generate leads and strengthen relationships
Assessing market SaaS solutions, identifying gaps, and creating industry-specific case studies, ROI models, competitive positioning, and marketing material
Managing the full sales process from initial contact to contract closure while maintaining accurate pipeline forecasting and CRM data
Providing actionable market intelligence on competitors, pricing, client needs, and technology trends to inform leadership and commercial strategy
Collaborating with marketing, delivery, legal, and compliance teams to develop campaigns, ensure smooth project handovers, and enhance client satisfaction
Advising on pricing strategies and service offerings tailored to the insurance market
Requirements
Solid understanding of US insurance operations, including underwriting, claims processing, policy administration, and regulatory compliance requirements
3-5 years of B2B sales experience selling technology solutions to insurance companies, ideally in data analytics, AI/ML, or digital transformation initiatives
Strong awareness of the insurance technology landscape, including core systems, emerging InsurTech solutions, and digital modernization challenges
Proven ability to manage complex sales cycles with multiple stakeholders and navigate formal procurement processes typical in the insurance industry
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Insurance, Finance, or a related field; relevant insurance certifications (CPCU, ARM, etc.) are advantageous
Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to articulate technical concepts to both business and IT audiences
Established network within the US insurance industry, including relationships with carriers, brokers, and technology decision-makers
Self-motivated and results-oriented professional, capable of working independently while collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams
