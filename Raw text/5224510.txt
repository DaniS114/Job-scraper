The Opportunity
Takeda’s Agile coaches support the implementation of Takeda’s Agile Framework (TAF) and oversee the development of Agile teams to ensure effective outcomes for the organization.
Responsibilities
•	Guide Agile teams as they use Takeda’s Agile Framework to adopt new ways of working through multiple Agile methodologies, including Kanban, Agile Scrum and SAFe.
•	Advise technical leads on staffing, mobilizing, activating, and sustaining Agile teams.
•	Coach and Mentor Product Managers/Owners, Scrum Masters, and team members as they work to improve team dynamics, velocity, psychological safety, and delivery of value.
•	Aid Scrum Masters and Agile team members in the use of Jira, Confluence, and other internally supported tools to manage Agile projects.
•	Assess best practices and determine necessary adjustments to improve team agility.
•	Support Product Managers and Functional/Chapter leads to maximize team effectiveness through structures that incorporate best Agile/Lean practices.
