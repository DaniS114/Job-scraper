Job description, responsibilities and duties
Would you like to work for a well-established Swiss Management Consulting company with 29+ years of experience in a new, exciting, and fast-growing Bratislava office? Send us your CV and work on various exciting projects within the banking or insurance industry and learn innovative project management techniques combined with IT skills!
We are looking for self-driven enthusiasts with an entrepreneurship spirit backed by a banking and team leader background. If you have project management skills or previous experience as a business consultant, even better. Join us and be part of our successful financial business expansion!

Lead the dedicated banking consulting team

Drive end-to-end delivery of dedicated projects
, ensuring professional development of all team members by utilizing Synpulse’s wide range of development opportunities
Act as a trusted advisor to banking clients
, understanding their strategic vision and translating business needs into implementable, value-driven solutions
Ensure excellence in project execution
, actively contributing to delivery while managing scope, timelines, budgets, risks and clients/stakeholder expectations within all project phases
Analyze clients’ business models, processes and organization
, identifying improvement opportunities and developing recommendations for improvement
Lead the preparation, planning, and governance of business projects
, including proposal development, project setup, and steering committee communication
Foster a high-performance team culture
, motivating team members and supporting continuous skill development through mentoring and structured feedback
