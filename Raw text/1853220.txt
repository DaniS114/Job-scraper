Pozíció leírása / Job description
At The Company we empower people to make a real impact. As a Finance Analytics Engineer, you’ll drive process improvements and automation within the Finance Reporting & Business Process Development Team. This Team Leader–equivalent role has no direct people‑management duties, but you’ll hold strong technical influence and act as a trusted advisor in key transformation initiatives.
●
Design and Build: Develop robust Power BI dashboards and data models (using DAX and Power Query) to replace legacy manual reporting.
●
​Automate Workflows: Eliminate repetitive manual tasks and excel-based processes using the Power Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps), VBA, or Python.
●
​Drive Innovation: Act as the hub for Low-Code and AI adoption within Finance, helping us deploy intelligent agents and automated workflows.
●
Translate & Bridge: Partner with Finance stakeholders to translate complex business requirements into precise technical specifications for data engineering.
●
​Data Stewardship: Act as the guardian of the "Single Source of Truth" for Working Capital and Financial data, ensuring backend integrity and accuracy.
