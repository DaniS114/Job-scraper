Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Develop, optimise, and maintain backend services using Java (Spring Boot)
Contribute to design decisions for new and existing projects
Work on football-related data pipelines that power FIFA, Serie-A and UEFA operations
Deploy and scale applications in AWS (ECS, Lambda, S3, DynamoDB, etc.)
Enhance system observability, ensuring real-time monitoring and logging using tools like Datadog
Work on event-driven architectures, leveraging message queues (Pulsar, Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS, etc.)
Collaborate with engineers, stakeholders and external partners to meet high-impact project deadlines
Ensure system reliability and performance in high-traffic, real-time environments
Requirements
Expert-level Java 17+ & Spring Boot
Proven ability to lead technical initiatives and mentor senior engineers
Strong experience with AWS (or other major cloud providers)
Familiarity with observability tools (e.g., Datadog, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry)
Experience with designing and evolving microservices and complex distributed systems at scale
Thorough understanding of monitoring, logging, and traceability
Experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka, RabbitMQ, SQS, etc.)
Ability to bridge the gap between technical details and business requirements in a fast-paced environment

Company info
Hawk-Eye Innovations is a company that provides technology solutions for football and sports events, working with major organizations such as FIFA and UEFA. The company focuses on building scalable backend systems and data-intensive services to deliver real-time insights to global football leagues and partners. Hawk-Eye Innovations operates in a fast-moving environment emphasizing collaboration, ownership, and innovation, aiming to create an inclusive and diverse workplace where engineers can contribute meaningfully and solve challenging problems.
