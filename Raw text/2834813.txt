Responsibilities
Drive AI and Platform Adoption:
You drive the adoption of AI, Micro­soft 365, Copilot, Power Platform and GenAI tools across MB Energy so that employees under­stand, trust, and effectively use these technologies.
Own AI Products and Governance:
You define and own the pro­duct vision and roadmap for MB Chat, Copilot Studio and GenBI, and you run AI pro­cesses inclu­ding use case intake, validation, risk checks and alignment with Legal, Compliance and Data Gover­nance.
Enable Learning and Communities:
You provide trai­ning formats, office hours and build commu­nities of prac­tice to develop AI and analytics capabi­lities and foster respon­sible AI usage across the organization.
Deliver Analytics for Enabling Functions:
You support HR and Finance with data inte­gration, reporting, automation and analytics solutions.
Automate Processes and Ensure Compliance:
You drive opera­tional excel­lence by automating manual processes and ensuring compliant hand­ling of sensitive data such as PII, payroll and organizational data.
Lead and Develop the Team:
You lead a cross-functional team of AI engi­neers, data and specia­lists, and adoption managers, fostering a culture of conti­nuous improve­ment, inno­vation, and responsible adoption of emerging techno­logies.
Requirements
Professional Background:
You bring several years of experience in AI, analytics, digital transformation or data / automation leadership – example as Head of Data/AI, Analy­tics Lead or as a senior pro­ject lead in data-driven trans­formations.
Enabling Functions Expertise:
You have expe­rience in finance analytics, people analy­tics or financial reporting automation, and are familiar with the needs of HR and Finance in a trans­forming, complex corporate environment.
Technical Environment:
You have a solid under­standing of Microsoft techno­logies, espe­cially Power Platform, Copilot, Power BI and the broader M365 ecosystem, and you know how to use them to build scalable solutions.
Stakeholder and Governance Skills:
You are skilled in stake­holder management and cross-functional align­ment and under­stand data models, repor­ting archi­tecture, and gover­nance require­ments for sensitive data.
Leadership Experience:
You have proven experience in leading teams or strong func­tional / project leadership, guiding cross-functional teams and exter­nal partners, and helping your team stay focused across many use cases.
Language:
You are fluent in both German and English.
With these characteristics, you fit perfectly with us:
Strategic and Hands-on:
You combine stra­tegic thinking with a pragmatic, hands-on approach and can move from vision and roadmaps to concrete implemen­tation together with your team.
Communicative and Influential:
You are an excel­lent communicator, build a strong standing with senior stakeholders, and can explain complex AI and analytics topics in an accessible way.
Structured and Focused:
You keep priorities clear for your­self and your team, even with many parallel use cases and demands, and you focus on scalable solu­tions instead of one-off projects.
Responsible and Ethical:
You act with inte­grity, are mindful of risks and compliance aspects, and advocate for respon­sible, trans­parent, and secure use of AI and data.
Curious and Learning-Oriented:
You stay up to date with develop­ments in AI, analytics, and auto­mation and actively bring new ideas and improve­ments into the organization.
Even if you do not fulfill all of the above require­ments, we encourage you to apply if you believe your expe­rience will add value to our team.

Company info
MB Energy,
founded in 1947 and head­quartered in Hamburg, is an inde­pendent and integrated energy company with operations in Europe, the United States, and Singapore. The company is active in the import, storage, distri­bution, and marketing of petroleum products, liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), chemicals, and biofuels. With decades of industry experience and an inno­vative approach, MB Energy plays an active role in the global energy landscape, supporting its customers in the transition to lower-emission energy sources by deve­loping and pro­viding forward-looking alter­natives.
