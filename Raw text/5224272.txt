Job description, responsibilities and duties
YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:
You will be responsible for the development, testing and support of iOS and/or Android applications with a high degree of innovation
You will monitor the development of individual components throughout the entire development cycle and present your solutions and results in reviews
You will review the technical feasibility of business requirements and support the design of mobile apps based on agile software development models
You will drive the technical roadmap and ensure that all initiatives are aligned with the strategic roadmap
You will design and implement technical solutions according to best practices and internal coding and design standards together with other developers in a team
You will contribute to the creation and updating of technical documentation
YOUR PROFILE:
Practical experience in developing apps using Flutter or Objective-C
Practical experience in software development in the mobile environment (iOS, Android)
Experience with the app publishing process in stores and corresponding tools (AppStore, Connect, TestFlight, Google Play, Keystore, ... )
You are fluent in English language
