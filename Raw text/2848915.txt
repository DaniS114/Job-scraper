Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries with over 30,000 employees worldwide. The firm is committed to helping organizations improve performance and achieve lasting impact through deep industry knowledge, advanced analytics, and innovative solutions.
