Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a
Senior Salesforce CPQ & Billing Specialist
to join our team and support the design, configuration, and optimization of our Salesforce platform. In this role, you will work closely with business and IT stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable Salesforce solutions, focusing on CPQ, Billing, and integrations. You will play a key role in improving quoting, pricing, and billing processes while ensuring system reliability, performance, and alignment with business goals.
​
Join our team in Bratislava! This is a full-time, permanent hybrid position, offering the perfect balance of three days in the office and two days working from home.
​
What you'll do
Partner with business stakeholders to gather and translate requirements related to quoting, order processing, products, pricing, and contract management.
Design and configure
Salesforce CPQ and Billing solutions
, including product setup, pricing strategies, discounting rules, and quote templates.
Collaborate with IT teams to develop and maintain
Salesforce flows, custom objects, validation rules, Apex, and integrations
(including AvaTax).
Identify process improvements and recommend scalable solutions aligned with
Salesforce best practices and architecture
.
Troubleshoot complex CPQ and Billing issues and manage enhancement requests within the Salesforce platform.
Lead or contribute to
CPQ and Billing projects
, ensuring proper testing, documentation, and smooth deployment.
Work closely with cross-functional teams and provide technical guidance to ensure successful implementation and adoption of Salesforce solutions.​
