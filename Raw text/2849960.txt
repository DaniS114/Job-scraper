Responsibilities
Project Delivery
:
Owns end-to-end delivery of medium to large transformation projects, ensuring alignment with business strategy and measurable benefits realisation
Business Implementation and Handover:
Works closely with the business area to coordinate effective business readiness, implementation, and handover activities
Governance and Reporting:
Provides regular status updates to Accountable Executives, platform and lab teams, and other stakeholders
Planning and Financial Control:
Owns project plans and budgets, monitors performance, and re-forecasts as needed.
Risk & Issue Management:
Identifies and mitigates risks early, including regulatory and operational risks
Agile Ways of Working:
Applies agile practices within projects and supports teams in using agile principles
Supplier & Partner Management:
Oversees sourcing, contract negotiation, and performance management of suppliers.
Group Values & Culture:
Positively influences others by demonstrating Lloyds Banking Group values and behaviour
Requirements
Bachelor or Master's degree (e.g. strategic Project management, business informatics, computer science, engineering, business administration or related field)
Several years of relevant professional experience in classic and agile project environments based on recognised standards (e.g. SCRUM, KANBAN PMI, IPMA, Prince2)
Track record in leading cross-functional projects, including supplier and partner management, preferably in multinational banking, financial service industry or IT implementation projects
Strong knowledge of agile frameworks and tools (Jira, Confluence)
Experience in requirements, test, and change management; IT governance; and regulatory compliance
Excellent English communication skills (German highly desirable)
Strong presentation, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills
Financial and resource management expertise
