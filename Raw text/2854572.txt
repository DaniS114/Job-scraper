Responsibilities
What you'll do:
The Role:
As a (Senior) Technical Product Manager, you will ...
Shape the Roadmap & Drive Delivery
Own the project roadmap and backlog — from high-level initiatives down to individual work items — making sure the team always has a clear, compelling picture of where we're headed and why.
Translate strategic goals into well-scoped milestones and actionable items that developers can pick up and run with.
Keep the backlog healthy, current, and honest — a living document that reflects both what we've achieved and what's next.
Enable the Team to Do Their Best Work
Plan sprints and iterations collaboratively with developers, setting shared expectations on scope, timelines, and what 'done' looks like.
Be the person who spots blockers early, clears the path, and keeps momentum high.
Make sure the tracking tools tell an accurate story of where we are — so the team can celebrate real progress and course-correct quickly when needed.
Steward the Open-Source Ecosystem
Navigate the rich landscape of upstream dependencies (e.g., kcp, kube-bind, Flux, Argo) and help the team make smart decisions about where to invest effort.
Champion upstream contributions that advance our project goals, while keeping a healthy balance so the team doesn't drift into work that doesn't move the needle.
Build and maintain transparent connections between what we contribute upstream and the value it creates for our roadmap.
Grow the Community & Project Visibility
Represent the project in the broader cloud-native and open-source community — at meetups, in forums, and across CNCF channels.
Foster an inclusive, energizing contributor culture where both internal team members and external contributors feel welcome and valued.
Transport the project vision, progress, and impact to stakeholders, leadership, and the wider ecosystem in a way that builds trust and excitement.
Contribute Technically — When It Energizes You
Roll up your sleeves and contribute code when you have the bandwidth and interest — it's encouraged, not expected as a daily obligation.
Use your technical depth to enrich discussions, challenge assumptions constructively, and help the team make better architectural decisions.
Act as a bridge between the Kubernetes API ecosystem and the project's goals, spotting opportunities in adjacent open-source projects that can amplify our impact.
Requirements
Strong problem-solving and analytical capabilities, with a collaborative, proactive mindset and a commitment to continuous improvement.
Experience in community engagement and open-source contributions, ability to clearly communicate and present complex technical concepts
A distinguished university degree in Computer Science or a closely related field; or equivalent work experience.
Strong hands-on technical skills with 3+ years of experience in full-time software engineering related roles and a strong focus on interacting and working with code
Solid understanding of container technologies, and the broader cloud-native ecosystem, with familiarity with the CNCF landscape
Confidence navigating the Kubernetes ecosystem and elaborating its extension concepts including CRDs, the operator pattern or extension API Servers
High proficiency in English, both written and spoken. German language skill is beneficial.

By donating both projects to the neutral NeoNephos Foundation, we've committed to open governance, shared ownership, and a durable ecosystem. Make your mark, invite others, build what's next together, and grow with us.
The Cloud-Native Lifecycle Management team is building Platform Mesh, applying cloud-native paradigms to enable enterprises to discover, order, and orchestrate services across any providers. Build entirely on Kubernetes Resource Model (KRM), Platform Mesh eliminates vendor lock-in and API fragmentation, creating a unified interface for a decentralized, interoperable service mesh for the cloud-edge continuum - all managed through with Kubernetes APIs and familiar tooling such as kubectl.
