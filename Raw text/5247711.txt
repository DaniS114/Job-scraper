Job description, responsibilities and duties
The role
We are looking for a curious, hands-on
full-stack software developer with junior to mid-level experience
who wants to grow deeper into AI, computer vision, and modern software systems.
You will work on R&D and product-facing initiatives such as:
• AI and computer vision experiments and prototypes
• API-based services and service orchestration layers
• Internal tools and proof-of-concepts that often evolve into real products
• Integration of AI services into existing enterprise systems
• Development of simple dashboards or web interfaces for AI-driven tools
We value engineers who enjoy experimenting, building prototypes, and exploring new technologies.
The role involves
both backend and frontend development
, with backend services, APIs, and integrations forming a core part of the work.
What you will do
• Build and extend API services in a modern backend stack
• Develop web interfaces or dashboards for internal tools and AI services
• Work with AI and computer vision pipelines (model inference, data flow, results processing)
• Prototype, test, and iterate on new technical ideas
• Take ownership of small to medium tasks and deliver them independently
• Collaborate remotely with senior engineers and consultants
• Document findings and communicate clearly in English
What we are looking for
Required
• Professional software development experience (typically
1–5 years
)
• Experience building backend services or APIs
• Basic experience with frontend development (JavaScript frameworks or modern web apps)
• Some exposure to AI, machine learning, or computer vision through projects, coursework, or professional work
• Solid programming fundamentals and problem-solving skills
• Ability to work independently with light supervision
• Good spoken and written English
• Availability to work in Western or Central European time zones
Nice to have
• Computer vision experience (OpenCV, YOLO, image pipelines, inference services)
• Python and/or Node.js backend experience
• Frontend frameworks such as React
• Cloud experience (GCP, AWS, containers)
• Experience with experimentation, PoCs, or R&D-style work
Education
A university degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field is
preferred
. It is not a hard requirement, but it is a strong signal of your ability to work through complex problems independently.

• Practical R&D that leads to real-world deployments
• A small, experienced team with minimal bureaucracy
• Room to grow into a senior or specialist role over time
