Company info
You’ll be based in our office in Warsaw, Budapest or Prague as part of Orphoz Central Europe, where our mission is to carry out large-scale transformation projects for our clients.
With only 1/3 of transformations being successful and sustainable, you will help to change this. At Orphoz, we aspire to help our clients to achieve one of their biggest challenges: making their transformation plans really happen. Our day-to-day work is to support large companies who need to adapt their operating models to the accelerated changes of their environment, by combining agility, sustainable efficiency, and employee engagement.
You can expect a uniquely varied and exciting working environment, as well as the opportunity to work with a talented and growing team. Our work will give you access to multiple levels of our clients' organizations, from the field to the management committees, while supporting them on the projects that are at the heart of their strategic agenda.
From day 1, you will enter an accelerated development path thanks to real support and coaching from your peers and directors, who care about frequent interactions and true proximity. Moreover, coaching, mentoring and regular feedback are key in our culture, and motivate personalized support in the development of our teams.
You will have an active role in the firm's growth. Orphoz combines the advantages of a "start-up" - light hierarchy, autonomy, freedom of expression, and initiative - and those of a large group - available resources to develop one's projects, logistical support. We plan and deliver change projects together with our clients, helping them capture their full potential. We know what determines the success of large projects and what can ensure lasting implementation success. We combine strategic and operational project management, with strong execution skills and an understanding of the specific demands of each industry.
