Company info
Would you like to work with modern data center and cloud-based systems (AWS, Azure) as part of an international team?
Is continuous professional development just as important to you as long-term stability?
Our partner is an international banking group present in five European countries, where IT is a true business enabler. You will work closely with Hungarian and Belgian colleagues in a modern data center and cloud-based (AWS, Azure) environment, ensuring the secure operation of millions of daily transactions. If you are looking to join a stable, international team where real career growth is available, this could be the right place for you.
They are expanding their team and are looking for a colleague for the following position:
BACKUP ADMIN – SYSTEM ENGINEER
Tasks
Daily operation of data backup and data protection systems supporting corporate IT applications
Configuration, monitoring, and maintenance of backup infrastructure in an enterprise environment, including:
Deduplication backup target systems (e.g. Data Domain)
Media servers
Modern software-defined backup solutions (Cohesity)
Archiving and long-term data retention solutions (e.g. tape infrastructure)
Participation in the implementation of the Cohesity environment and migration to the new platform
End-to-end lifecycle management of backup and restore infrastructure (data protection)
Active involvement in preventing operational incidents
Communication with internal customers and IT vendors
Continuous maintenance and updating of system operation documentation
Supporting reporting obligations
Expectations
At least 3–5 years of experience operating storage-side and backup systems
Confident English language skills
Hands-on experience with server-side operations (Windows/Linux servers, virtual environments)
Solid knowledge of databases
Practical experience working with ITIL methodology
Willingness to participate in on-call duty, including occasional evening and weekend work
Fast learner with a team-oriented mindset
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
Benefits
Competitive salary and attractive benefits package, including:
Flexible cafeteria benefit
Annual performance-based bonus
Company mobile phone with mobile internet for private use
Employee banking benefits
Private healthcare services (Medicare)
Daily use of English in a professional environment
Flexible working hours, no strict clock-in/clock-out system
Strong emphasis on teamwork: office presence is encouraged, with the option of 3 days of home office per week
Open and receptive to your ideas, valuing your insights and contributions
International environment and friendly workplace culture
Green office located in Millenniumi Park, offering:
Bicycle storage and shower facilities
Panoramic restaurant with views of the Danube and the National Theatre
Free coffee in the community area, darts, and numerous dining options nearby
Stable, long-term career opportunities within the company
Lifelong learning and diverse professional challenges
Working hours: Flexible schedule with up to 3 days of home office per week
Location: Budapest, Lechner Ödön fasor 9. (There is also the option to work from the Baracska or Törökbálint offices.)
Advantages
Experience with Cohesity or NetBackup systems
PowerShell or Python scripting experience
Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and process automation
