Állás leírása
Responsibilities
You will play a crucial role in digital transformation Projects
Ensure project deliverables meet quality standards
Support the development and implementation of technical solutions to monitor and improve data quality across the enterprise, with a strong focus on SAP R/3 and S/4HANA
You will help our business units to establish Master Data standards and increase the quality level of Master Data in ERP, MDG, CRM and consuming systems
Work closely with cross-functional teams, including business users, data owners and IT teams, to ensure technical solutions consider end-2-end processes
Requirements
A successfully completed studies preferably business informatics, information technology or a technical education in informatics, paired with a passion for master data and data quality
At least 5 years relevant professional experience, experience in a consultant role is a plus
Experience in SAP Material Master Data, Business Partner and the corresponding processes
Strong understanding of SAP data and system architecture
The ability to structure and develop your own frameworks and communicate them in a structured and easy-to-grasp way
A natural interest in state-of-the-art technological developments and a good understanding of technical feasibility of data-driven products and services
A proven track record of agile project experience and agile project management success stories
Been part of a data project end-to-end, from data cleaning to data integration, modelling, and productizing
Excellent English and German skills
