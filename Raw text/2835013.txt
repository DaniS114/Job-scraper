IT PMO Intern at TAKKT – Join Our Transformation Journey
TAKKT is a leading B2B specialist for business equipment across Europe and North America. We’re on an exciting transformation journey—and we’ve just launched the TAKKT TCC (Transformation & Collaboration Center), our new hub for innovation, agility, and digital excellence.
As we grow dynamically, we’re looking for a passionate IT Project Management Office (PMO) Intern to join our dynamic team and contribute to the success of our agile project management initiatives. This is an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in project portfolio administration and reporting within an international environment.
Feladatok
What You'll Do
•Participating in project meetings and taking notes
•Reviewing and validating project reports
•Assisting with project portfolio administration and documentation
•Preparing data and reports for management review
•Supporting communication between project teams
•Support the Product Owner in managing and prioritizing the Salesforce product backlog
•Assist in gathering and documenting business requirements from stakeholders
•Support testing activities (UAT preparation, defect tracking)
•Participate in sprint planning, backlog refinement, and review meetings
Elvárások
What You Bring
•Ongoing university studies
•Strong English skills (written and spoken)
•Good communication skills and organizational abilities
•Proactive and confident personality
•Interest in agile project management
•Proficiency in MS Office applications
Nice to Have
•Specialization in Business Informatics or related field
•Previous work experience
•Knowledge of Azure DevOps, JIRA, or Confluence
• Salesforce, ERP, CRM experience
Amit kínálunk
What We Offer
•Practical experience in project portfolio management
•Exposure to international projects and collaboration with experienced professionals
•Flexible working conditions and a supportive team environment
