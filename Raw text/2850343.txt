Responsibilities
Make yourself available to the whole area and support all teams when architectural guidance is needed
Work closely with Software Engineers and Product Managers to prioritize short- and long-term initiatives
Support Team Leads, Development Teams and Product Managers on technical routes to take
Support the Product Manager to ensure correct priorities in backlog and roadmap
Participate in decision making with key stakeholders
Participate in workshops, brainstorming, presentations, and knowledge-transfer sessions
Participate in pre-solution work for new initiatives to outline high level solutions
Participate in design reviews to ensure solutions adhere to guidelines
Identify refactoring activities to address technical debt, performance, scalability, availability, reliability, and security
Support development teams and product owners to define relevant NFRs (KPIs)
Cooperate with Enterprise Architects to align area solutions with company guidelines
Support development teams with architectural decisions, designs and high incidents
Benchmark systems, analyze bottlenecks and propose solutions
Mentor, guide, and be a technology leader to junior team members
Requirements
Good understanding of new technology and how it can improve solutions and drive innovation
Good communicator and collaborator
Experience with microservices and associated tools such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, Docker and Kubernetes
Previous experience building RESTful services and familiarity with best practices
Demonstrated expertise in building applications at scale
Strong knowledge of datastores (SQL, NoSQL) and ability to choose technology based on requirements
Experience with architectural patterns for high availability, performance, scale out architecture, disaster recovery, security architecture
Knowledge of cloud-based architectures, deployment and operational aspects of AWS is a plus
Technical mindset with great attention to detail
Product mindset in feature design, considering future impact
