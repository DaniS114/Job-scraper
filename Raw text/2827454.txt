Responsibilities
What You’ll Do
Lead and maintain release and validation testing for legacy products, ensuring accuracy, reliability, and compliance.
Develop and enhance test methods to keep our established product portfolio performing at its best.
Drive site-to-site test method transfer and support the transfer of new test methods from R&D to operational sites, ensuring smooth adoption and consistent execution.
Troubleshoot test methods and test equipment on-site or remotely, helping teams resolve issues efficiently and sustainably.
Collaborate closely with international teams, aligning processes, sharing best practices, and strengthening global testing capability.
Grow your career in a diverse, international environment with significant opportunities to expand your expertise and influence.
Requirements
What You Need to Succeed
Fluent English skills (written and spoken) — essential for daily collaboration and communication across regions.
Relevant academic background (MSc in Engineering) — ensures you’ve got the fundamentals to thrive.
Solid statistical knowledge — you’ll rely on data analysis to evaluate methods, interpret results, and guide improvements.
A background in test methods, test equipment, or test systems — a strong advantage that will help you contribute from day one.
A proactive, solution-oriented mindset with the ability to work across technical disciplines and cultures.
Motivation to work with global stakeholders and navigate multi-time-zone collaboration.
