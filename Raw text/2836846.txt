Responsibilities
This role sits at the intersection of branding, UI, and go-to-market execution. You will not just “do marketing” but actively help shape how the company looks, feels, and launches, working closely together with the founders.
You will support UI testing and refinement, write and manage tickets, and coordinate closely with developers to ensure high-quality product execution. In addition, you will contribute to defining and sharpening our brand strategy.
More concrete:
Support the development of our brand identity and visual direction
Improve and polish UI flows (e.g. with Lovable, Figma or similar tools)
Build and iterate landing pages (Webflow, Framer or comparable tools)
Create marketing and launch assets (one-pagers, LinkedIn visuals, pitch materials)
Use AI tools to accelerate content and creative production
Support early go-to-market experiments, testing and execution
Requirements
Strong interest in branding, product, and startups
Good design intuition and an eye for clean, modern UI
Basic understanding of SEO and how to optimize landing pages and content for search performance
Hands-on mentality and execution speed
Comfortable using tools like Figma, Webflow/Framer, Canva and AI tools
You know how to use AI effectively through smart prompting to solve problems efficiently and accelerate your work
Structured, reliable and proactive, and integer

Company info
We are building the next generation of regulated payment infrastructure.
Our stablecoin-native platform is designed for compliant cross-border value transfer, embedding FX, settlement, and compliance directly on-chain to reduce intermediaries, lower complexity, and enable programmable financial workflows.
We combine regulatory capability with on-chain technology — creating infrastructure traditional institutions cannot build and crypto-native players cannot operate compliantly.
Beyond payments, we are pioneering infrastructure for AI-driven commerce, including compliant Know-Your-Agent (KYA) frameworks that enable autonomous agents to transact securely within regulated financial systems.
We are looking for someone to build our brand from scratch: shaping how we present a new generation of global payments to the world. This role goes beyond marketing: you will define our positioning, narrative, and overall group strategy from day one, working closely with the founders to build a category-defining payments company.
