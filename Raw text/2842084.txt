Responsibilities
Shape engineering strategy with broad organizational impact
Drive platform evolution by identifying cross-cutting pain points and leading the design of secure, scalable, reusable solutions built on AWS and modern serverless and microservice patterns
Lead architectural discussions and design reviews making trade-offs around performance, security, reliability, and maintainability
Own AI architectural decisions end-to-end including governance, monitoring, and regulatory readiness
Act as the organization's AI thought leader educating engineering teams on model behavior, agentic systems, and responsible AI practices
Mentor and stretch senior and staff engineers, building technical leadership depth and holding a high bar for engineering standards
Requirements
Principal-level track record at scale with 10+ years of software engineering experience including at least 3 years at Staff or Principal Engineer level in an organization of 100+ engineers
Deep, opinionated AWS platform expertise including multi-account AWS architectures and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or CDK preferred)
Production AI systems experience with shipped AI-enabled systems such as RAG pipelines, agentic frameworks, LLM orchestration
Experience defining and implementing AI governance in production environments including bias and privacy checks, audit-ready monitoring, and AI usage policies
Proven track record designing and shipping RESTful APIs and backend platform components using Node.js/TypeScript
Strong org-level communication and influence skills to align stakeholders, run architecture forums, and mentor senior technical leaders
Nice-to-have
Experience building platform components or SDKs that multiple product teams depend on, Involvement in architecture forums, tech councils, or similar bodies that define org-wide standards, Experience optimising cost and performance for data and AI-heavy workloads at scale, Leadership of major modernisation efforts such as legacy migration to serverless, multi-region architecture, or platform consolidation, A public technical presence demonstrating thought leadership beyond current organisation

Company info
Diligent is the AI leader in governance, risk and compliance (GRC) SaaS solutions, helping more than 1 million users and 700,000 board members to clarify risk and elevate governance. The Diligent One Platform gives practitioners, the C-Suite and the board a consolidated view of their entire GRC practice so they can more effectively manage risk, build greater resilience and make better decisions, faster.
