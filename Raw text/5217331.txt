We are looking for a new colleague to join our team for the position of C#/.NET Developer. We develop solutions which are part of a broader security ecosystem where our focus is on identity protection, which for example includes, but is not limited to multi-factor authentication.
We are an agile, technically well-backed-up, and well-played team. The product is mainly Windows and cloud based but has also on-premises parts and mobile apps as well. However, nearly all the code is in C#. By developing the product, you will come across several technologies, thus we are looking for an open-minded candidate who is willing to learn.
The main task of our work is the design and implementation of functionality and its integration into existing products. The work covers also the maintenance of existing product instances.
