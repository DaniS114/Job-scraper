Responsibilities
As a Senior AI Engineer in our Technology & Innovation department, you will play a key role in developing Riverty's AI platform to accelerate software engineering productivity. You will design and implement AI-driven solutions based on Large Language Models, agentic architectures, and knowledge graphs – building the foundation for automation and intelligent developer workflows across our tech organization.
AI in Coding:
Build AI solutions that support developers in refactoring, documentation, and legacy transformation
AI-driven Innovation:
Integrate external AI services to make Riverty's products and services better, faster, and more human-centric
Advance our AI platform capabilities:
Enhance the platform with LLMs, knowledge graphs, APIs, and agent-based workflows
Build & Experiment:
Create prototypes and proof-of-concepts to quickly demonstrate value and drive adoption
Develop & Deploy:
Deliver production-grade AI services on Azure, ensuring performance, scalability, and compliance
Lead & Partner:
Tackle challenges hands-on and collaborate with external partners to accelerate innovation
Requirements
Degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or related field; business/innovation background is a plus
5–7 years of professional experience in AI/ML engineering or solution architecture
Proven expertise in Python and AI/LLM frameworks (e.g., LangChain, PyTorch, TensorFlow)
Strong knowledge of external AI models and services (OpenAI, Vertex, Claude, etc.)
Experience with cloud-native architectures (preferably Azure), microservices, APIs, and containerized environments
Familiarity with knowledge graph technologies and data architectures
Strong problem-solving and communication skills, able to bridge technical and business needs
Fluent in English; German is a plus
