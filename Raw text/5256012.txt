AI SERVICES - AI Automation Engineer (LLM & System Integrations)- KE
Join our team of AI Team at Deutsche Telekom Services Europe in Slovakia!
We are a team of AI experts who design and deliver automation and integration solutions for internal customers across Deutsche Telekom. Our solutions help connect systems, move and transform data, and automate manual processes.
Due to the growing demand for automation, we are looking to expand our team with a technically skilled AI Automation Engineer for implementing agentic workflows.
Our team works in an international setup, so collaboration via video conferencing and clear communication are part of our daily routine.

About
the position
What you will do:
Design, implement, and maintain automation workflows
Build end-to-end integrations between internal and external systems
Work with REST APIs, including request/response handling and authentication
Process, transform, and validate data in various formats
Implement logic for filtering, mapping, aggregation, branching, and error handling within workflows
Configure and consume webhooks and event-driven integrations
Work with databases to read, write, and update data using SQL, Spark or GraphQL queries
Connect workflows to Large Language Models and design suitable prompts
Monitor, debug, and optimize workflow executions to ensure reliability and performance
Collaborate with business stakeholders and analysts to translate requirements into technical automation solutions
Create and maintain technical documentation for workflows and integrations
Identify opportunities for automation and continuous process improvement
We work in a HYBRID STYLE of working with the possibility of working from home , but twice a week we meet in the office in Košice or Bratislava for meetings.
The published salary is the minimum possible offer.
The starting salary may be higher depending on the extent of fulfillment of the employee's requirements (education, language skills, required practice, personality assumptions and skills).
