Data Science & AI Lead (m/f)
Job description, responsibilities and duties
PwC Slovakia is looking for a dynamic and experienced Data Science & AI Lead to
join our growing team of data professionals
. This role is
ideal for a leader who combines technical excellence with strong communication skills and a proven ability to lead and develop teams
.
As a Data Science & AI Lead, you will play a key role in shaping data-driven strategies, delivering impactful solutions, and mentoring a team of talented individuals.
You will
work closely with clients and internal stakeholders
to
shape data-driven strategies
, oversee
complex delivery engagements
, and ensure high-quality outcomes across projects that blend
machine learning, software engineering, and modern AI techniques
.
This role is
well-suited for a leader who enjoys both hands-on problem-solving and building high-performing teams in an innovative, collaborative environment
.
What You’ll Do:
Lead and manage a team of data scientists, analysts, and AI engineers to deliver high-quality solutions across industries.
Design and implement advanced data science and AI solutions, including predictive modeling, machine learning, and data engineering, fine-tuning LLM models and complex RAG-based systems.
Act as a strategic advisor to clients, clearly communicating technical concepts and recommendations in a business-relevant way.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand client needs and translate them into effective data-driven strategies.
Ensure project delivery excellence – managing timelines, resources, and quality standards.
Mentor and develop team members, fostering a culture of continuous learning, collaboration, and technical excellence.
Contribute to PwC’s thought leadership in data science and AI through publications, presentations, and internal knowledge sharing.
Requirements:
Proven experience in data science, AI, analytics, or data engineering, ideally within a consulting or client-facing environment.
Strong technical skills in tools such as Python, SQL, Spark, cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP), and modern AI architectures (e.g., RAG, model fine-tuning).
Excellent communication and presentation skills – able to engage with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Demonstrated success in leading teams and delivering complex data projects.
Strategic mindset with the ability to align technical solutions with business goals.
Degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field (Master’s or PhD preferred).
Why PwC?
At PwC, we combine deep technical expertise with strategic insight to deliver real impact through data. You’ll have access to continuous learning, leadership development, and a collaborative environment that encourages innovation. Join us to shape the future of data and AI in Slovakia and beyond.
