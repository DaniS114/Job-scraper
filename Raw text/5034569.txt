Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about building great products and leading agile teams to success?
Join our team as a
Product Owner
and play a key role in shaping innovative digital solutions!
If you have experience in product ownership, agile methodologies, and backlog management, along with a strong ability to prioritize tasks and make strategic decisions, we want to hear from you!
As a
Product Owner
, you’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive product vision, prioritize development, and deliver high-impact solutions.
What You’ll Do:
Own the product vision and ensure alignment with business goals
Manage and prioritize the product backlog, maximizing value for both users and stakeholders
Work closely with the Scrum Master and development team to drive agile delivery
Transform business needs into user stories that bring value with each sprint
Make strategic decisions on features, releases, and development priorities
Represent the customer’s voice, ensuring a user-centric approach
Engage with stakeholders across the organization to align expectations and drive collaboration
Participate in Daily Scrum as needed
Monitor progress, accept deliverables, and communicate updates externally
Foster astrong agile culture, driving efficiency and continuous improvement
