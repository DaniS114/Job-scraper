Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Taking end-to-end responsibility for all project stages (including discovery, design, development and handover to support)
Understanding technology-related issues to be able to deliver the best possible solution for our clients
Having high-level discussions on technical details with the team and the external client
Managing in-house and remote development teams (mobile, backend, web-frontend)
Business level understanding of architecture, APIs, CI/CD
Usage of your amazing change management skills when it comes to unforeseen edge cases
Handling situations where you have to describe how a function works for non-technical people and also being able to translate business requirements to the technical team
Creating functional specifications, detailed timeline and delivery plans with appropriate risk and dependency assessment and mitigation plans
Facilitating sprint planning and development processes to ensure efficient delivery
Project tracking including budget, scope and schedule tracking
Requirements
Leading end-to-end projects in mobile, backend, or web frontend for 5+ years, with any of these areas adding value to our team
Delivering a software project successfully over a 9-12 month timeframe
Practicing agile methodologies, including facilitating daily stand-ups, retrospectives, and other Scrum ceremonies
Applying best practices from a range of project management methodologies in your daily work
Keeping projects on track using tools like Jira, Slack, GitHub, and GitLab
Communicating fluently in English within an international team (bonus if you speak German or other languages)
Demonstrating outstanding interpersonal, verbal, and written communication skills, including negotiation expertise
Building and nurturing strong client relationships through effective client and account management
Taking full ownership of products from initial idea to polished, well-engineered digital experiences
Prioritizing team morale and shielding your team from uncertainty with a people-first mindset
Understanding business requirements and translating them into meaningful technical outcomes
What we offer
To unleash your full potential, we’re here to help you grow every step of the way with exciting international projects, access to inspiring conferences, in-house soft skill trainings, and online courses
We’ll even support your English classes, and with our skill matrix, you’ll always have a clear path to learn

To have a blast, you’ll join a friendly team of 200+ people, all passionate about building amazing digital products

And with our buddy system, you’ll feel part of the team from day one
To brighten your day, our dog-friendly office in the heart of Budapest is filled with good vibes, great coffee, tasty snacks (yes, including Túró Rudi!), and cozy corners to relax, connect, or have a little fun when you need a break
You’ll also have a yearly personal budget and a company library to keep your curiosity growing
Company info
Supercharge is a digital product consultancy headquartered in Budapest, Hungary. Founded in 2010, we specialize in delivering innovative software solutions across various industries including energy, healthcare, finance, insurance, mobility, and the public sector. With a team of over 200 professionals, we combine product strategy, design, and software engineering to help clients bring cutting-edge digital products to market. Our mission is to empower organizations through technology and foster a collaborative, growth-oriented work environment.
