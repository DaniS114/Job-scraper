What will you do
The Internal Audit Data Analyst role plays a pivotal part in strengthening Lenovo’s global Internal Audit function by developing advanced analytics that enhance risk assessment, audit testing, and continuous monitoring across the organization. This position requires a blend of technical expertise, analytical depth, and the ability to translate complex data into actionable insights for diverse business partners. Working within a highly collaborative and international environment, the analyst will support audits spanning multiple regions, processes, and systems, contributing to data driven decision making and improved control effectiveness. The role includes regular collaboration with global stakeholders and may require up to 20% travel.
Key Responsibilities
- Design and develop data analytics routines to support all phases of Internal Audit activities.
- Build and maintain analytics models that identify patterns, anomalies, and trends in large data sets.
- Manage data extraction, transformation, and processing to generate outputs for audit testing and visualization.
- Develop reusable analytics solutions enabling auditor self service and continuous monitoring programs.
- Assess data sources for completeness and accuracy, applying professional skepticism throughout the process.
- Collaborate with business partners and management to identify relevant data elements and insights.
- Transfer data related knowledge and best practices to Data Champions and the broader Internal Audit team.
- Execute special projects and contribute to departmental initiatives as assigned by management.
Position Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in business, mathematics, computer science, or management information systems.
- 3+ years of recent experience in data analysis within audit, finance, risk management, or technology functions.
- Strong proficiency with data extraction, analytics, and visualization tools (Power BI preferred; SQL, Python, R, process mining, Alteryx/KNIME).
- Experience with continuous audit monitoring and familiarity with internal controls and auditing techniques.
- Knowledge of data sources and complex data structures related to manufacturing or high tech environments (preferred).
- Fluency in English (spoken and written), Chinese is advantage
- Strong analytical reasoning, problem solving abilities, and data intuition skills.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to build effective working relationships across teams.
