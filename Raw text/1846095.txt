Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner is a leading independent provider of IT infrastructure services. They work at the heart of digitisation, advising organisations on IT strategy, implementing the most appropriate technology and managing their customers’ infrastructures. We are looking for their expanding team a German Speaking Senior Technical Specialist with hands on presales and vendor management experience.
You will work as part of a large team raising quotes for security vendors for our Client's German customer base
Your workload will be aligned for vendors such as Checkpoint, Fortinet, Zscaler
You build and maintain excellent relationships with partners and stakeholders
You will work as part of an extended team to support tenders and projects from the bidding phase through to contract award
You create and maintain standardized offers for framework agreements and develop clear representations of individual offers
You will be able explain configurations in a clear and convincing way
You sponsor and mentor junior employees.

You have at least 3 years of professional experience in similar role preferably in the IT industry
You have several years of sales experience managing large or medium-sized customers, working with manufacturers or system integrators
You speak fluent English and German
You possess very good technical knowledge of individual manufacturers in the IT environment
You have in-depth knowledge of sales and service processes
You are highly motivated and always looking for new business opportunities
You have strong presentation and communication skills
You demonstrate solid commercial capabilities
You show assertiveness and think in a structured and analytical way
You are able to work independently and are self-driven
Experience of using Salesforce highly desireable.
