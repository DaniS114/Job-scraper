WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR
Create and maintain offline robot programs for
automated welding systems (e.g., MIG/MAG).
Develop and validate robot paths, weld sequences, tool
motions, and cycle times using offline programming and
simulation tools.
Perform collision detection, reachability analysis, and
workspace optimization.
Generate robot programs compliant with OEM
standards (ABB, IGM, Yaskawa, etc.).
Define welding sequences and robot logic in
coordination with product design, fixtures, and process
planning.
Integrate robots, welding equipment, fixtures, sensors,
and peripherals into complete virtual cells.
Support commissioning and ramp-up activities by
transferring validated programs to on-line systems.
Troubleshoot and optimize robot programs during
virtual try-out and on-site start-up.
Ensure welding programs meet quality requirements,
weld standards, and customer specifications.
Apply safety standards and interlocks within robotic cell
simulations.
Support PFMEA, process validation, and documentation
activities
Support production as needed to resolve technical and
manufacturing issues.
Use QC7 tools to investigate and analyze the root cause
of the problem and ensure that effective
countermeasures are implemented.
Implement continuous improvements to achieve key
performance indicators.
Introduces activities to reduce cycle times and
downtime.
Responsible management of the entire life cycle of new
projects.
WHAT QUALIFICATIONS YOU SHOULD HAVE
Completed studies in the field of mechanical
engineering, mechatronical engineering, vehicle
engineering or professional experience on equal level (2
years), gathered in vehicle production area
Knowledge of welding technology and standards
Material knowledge (weldability, raw material groups,
heat input, and material behavior)
Offline programming tools (e.g., Process Simulate,
RoboDK, Delmia, RobotStudio, KUKA.Sim)
Industrial robot programming and logic fundamentals.
Ability to read and interpret CAD models and technical
drawings.
Practical attitude, good technical knowledge
Good knowledge in welding and in assembly and
maintenance processes
User level knowledge of MS Office. Teamcenter
knowledge is an advantage
Analytical mindset and ability to work independently
and responsibly Confident written and spoken English
Adaptability to new processes and components
Works in an organized and systematic manner, keeping
process quality in mind, and is willing to work closely
with manufacturing, design, and quality assurance
