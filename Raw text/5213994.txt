POSITION
DESCRIPTION
We’re looking for a Mobile Developer who will build secure, user-friendly wallet applications for Verifiable Credentials. You’ll collaborate closely with UX/UI designers, a Mobile Architect, and the back-end team, and you’ll work in an Agile setup (Scrum) with regular ceremonies.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
•	Implement mobile applications—specifically wallets for Verifiable Credentials.
•	Work with UX/UI designers to bring high quality designs into production.
•	Collaborate with the back end team to integrate and leverage backend services and APIs.
•	Partner with the Mobile Architect to correctly implement and/or re use the Wallet SDK built in KMM.
•	Participate in Sprint Planning and provide reliable estimates; attend daily stand ups and cooperate with the Scrum Master.
•	Support QA throughout the testing cycle and fix defects promptly.
WHAT WE
EXPECT
•	5+ years of experience in Mobile applications development
•	Kotlin Multiplatform (KMM)—strong hands on experience; ability to share business logic across platforms and structure modules for reusability
•	Android (Kotlin) development expertise (architecture patterns, coroutines/Flow, Jetpack libraries).
•	Experience integrating SDKs and working with REST/GraphQL APIs; strong attention to reliability and security.
•	Solid understanding of Agile practices (Scrum), estimation, and iterative delivery.
•	Flutter experience (optional) for rapid prototyping or cross platform UI when appropriate.
•	Knowledge of identity/wallet standards (DID, VC), cryptography basics, secure storage, and app hardening techniques.
•	Experience collaborating with UX research and design systems.
•	University degree in a Technology or Information Systems field
•	Advanced English language (B2+)
