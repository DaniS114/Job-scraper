Job description, responsibilities and duties
As a Senior Database Administrator, you play a
crucial role
in ensuring that our business-critical data is always available, secure, and reliable. From designing resilient architectures to troubleshooting performance issues, you’ll help keep our systems running smoothly, enabling the business to thrive with confidence in its data foundation.
Your tasks
Ensure database system availability in a 24×7 environment, including out-of-hours on-call support
Design, implement, and maintain sound backup, recovery, and disaster recovery (DR) procedures
Monitor and troubleshoot performance and concurrency issues to optimize database efficiency
Install, configure, upgrade, and maintain multiple SQL environments following company standards and best practices
Implement and maintain database security in line with corporate IT security guidelines
Design and enhance database architecture and maintenance tasks, ensuring scalability and resilience
Provide guidance on SQL Server internals to development teams and support application code releases
Proactively identify potential issues and raise business awareness before they impact operations
