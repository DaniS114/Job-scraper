Responsibilities
Use AI-assisted development as a core engineering approach to accelerate delivery, improve code quality, and reduce operational toil—while ensuring strong human review, test coverage, and controlled releases
Own delivery of cloud-scale infrastructure and automation capabilities from design through implementation, deployment, and operational support
Design and deliver scalable provisioning and deployment workflows using Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and tools such as Chef, Ansible, Terraform, and OpenSearch
Build and evolve complex network automation and data collection workflows using Ansible and related tooling
Provision, maintain, and monitor mission-critical SaaS infrastructure across multiple platforms, operating systems, and technology stacks
Design, build, and maintain AI tooling that exposes automation capabilities with strong authentication, authorization, and auditing
Integrate agentic AI with internal systems and operational workflows, enabling secure, governed tool access for provisioning, troubleshooting, compliance checks, and reporting
Advance IaC/Config-as-Code standards through reusable modules, patterns, and guardrails; mentor others on best practices and operational excellence
Implement and promote test-driven development (TDD) and automated validation to improve release confidence and reduce regressions
Create and enhance multi-stage configuration management workflows and CI/CD pipelines with an emphasis on reliability, observability, and auditability
Develop automated configuration and compliance audit tooling aligned with frameworks such as HIPAA, PCI-DSS, and FedRAMP
Implement data aggregation and reporting processes to deliver actionable operational and business intelligence metrics to leadership
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
Extensive experience managing cloud infrastructure lifecycles using Infrastructure-as-Code
Extensive experience in OS, network device, or application automation at meaningful scale
Experience building and operating within CI/CD pipelines and release processes
Experience with orchestration/automation platforms such as Jenkins
Expertise in AI-assisted development, applying it to implementation, refactoring, test creation, and troubleshooting with rigorous validation
Strong grounding in Agile and DevOps best practices
Advanced programming in Python and at least one of Ruby, Java, or Go, with experience building maintainable, testable, production-grade code
Proficiency configuring and operating Linux and Windows systems
Proficiency with observability and analytics tooling (e.g., OpenSearch, Grafana)
Familiarity with network device configuration management and monitoring
Familiarity with containers and associated cloud-native patterns
Experienced with GitOps practices, including designing and scaling GitOps workflows across multiple teams and environments
Experience with operations and support processes and tools (incident/change management, ticketing systems such as JIRA/Service Desk, SLAs, escalation processes)
Strong written and verbal communication in English
Ability to write clear design docs, runbooks, and operational procedures
Collaborative and effective across teams and time zones
Skilled with influencing and mentoring other team members
Strong analytical, planning, and organizational skills with the ability to juggle conflicting priorities
Skilled at creating troubleshooting guides and instructions that can be handed off and scaled
Independent and accountable; providing transparency to management and stakeholders
Curiosity, continuous learning mindset, and a passion for improving customer experience
Nice-to-have
You hate sub-optimal process and always push for improvements, You’re the Perfect Candidate If…, You are skilled with AI-assisted development and generative coding tools and techniques and using them today in your engineering workflows, You have production experience using MCP services, You have production experience operating AI-enabled services: deployments, on-call operations, reliability improvements, and release strategies, You’re proficient deploying and managing infrastructure using Terraform, You’re proficient in Chef, including cookbook authoring and infrastructure maintenance, You’re proficient in Ansible, including playbook authoring and Tower/AWX configuration, You’re proficient in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, including full-stack lifecycle management, You have experience aggregating data and building visualizations in OpenSearch or Grafana, You have experience creating and maintaining CI/CD pipelines for testing and delivery using Jenkins or similar platforms
