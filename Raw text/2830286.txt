Responsibilities
Drive the test activities within an interdisciplinary agile software development team in the context of a web portal
Ensure the desired product and process quality with suitable test methodologies
Design, implement, maintain and implement automatic and manual test cases
Report bugs, re-test related software changes and help with troubleshooting bugs
Act as sparring partner for the Product Owner, the Developers and the Test Management
Teach test methodologies to other testers
Work with international colleagues in a scaled agile software development program using SAFe methodology and Scrum
Requirements
Closed academical studies, preferably B.Sc. or M.Sc. degree in Computer Sciences/Business Informatics or similar education
5+ years of professional experience as Quality Assurance Engineer OR Software Engineer
3+ years of professional experience as Test Automation Engineer
Experience with automating functional, integration and regression tests of micro services, web applications or web services
Ideally, experience with a test automation framework like Cypress, Selenium or Playwright, or willingness to build-up this knowledge in a short term
Knowledge of programming languages (JavaScript and/or TypeScript) and database querying (SQL and/or non-SQL)
Having ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) and ideally, with the Agile Tester (CTFL-AT) extension
Fluent English
High level of quality awareness and standards
Attention to detail and passion for error-free software
Love to work in a team, like to learn new things every day and like to communicate
Experience with agile methodologies like Scrum or Kanban and ideally, SAFe
Ideally, experience in the EV charging business

Company info
vaylens GmbH is a leading provider of hardware-agnostic, scalable software solutions for Charge Point Operators (CPOs) across Europe and the UK. vaylens focuses on the management and monetization of EV (electric vehicle) charging infrastructure, offering tools for station management, access control, billing, and monetization. In 2024 alone, an amount of energy of 140 GWh was charged via the backend. This corresponds to 700 million kilometers traveled or around 17,500 times around the world. (A consumption of 20 kWh/100km was used as a basis for the calculation.)
vaylens is based in Dortmund and part of the KOSTAL Group. For more information, visit: vaylens.com
