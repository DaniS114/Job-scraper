Responsibilities
Work with clients and the global Orchestro team based in Budapest office
Join a cross-functional squad owning an end-to-end slice of Orchestro SaaS platform
Translate user problems into technical solutions alongside Product and Design teams
Pair-program with teammates, coding, testing, and releasing Node.js- and React-based features
Add unit, integration, and mutation tests to ensure reliability, security, and performance
Refine CI/CD workflows, improve Kubernetes deployments, and extend observability
Propose product or technical improvements from day one
Participate in McKinsey-wide engineering guilds, bootcamps, and global expert networks
Requirements
2+ years building production web software (preferably Node.js & modern React)
Hands-on experience with automated testing and CI/CD workflows
Familiarity with Docker/Kubernetes or another cloud-native deployment stack
Fluent English for day-to-day collaboration and stakeholder communication
What we offer
Continuous learning culture with structured programs and clear feedback
A voice that matters with opportunity to impact and contribute ideas
Global community with colleagues across 65+ countries and 100+ nationalities
World-class benefits including competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package
Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries with a diverse workforce committed to delivering lasting impact and building long-term capabilities for clients.
