Responsibilities
Develop and Maintain Test Plans: Create comprehensive test plans and test cases to ensure the delivery of high-quality software
Collaborate Across Teams: Work closely with cross-functional teams to embed quality practices throughout the development lifecycle and contribute to quality-focused initiatives
Manual Testing: Conduct detailed manual testing to validate software functionality, performance, and user experience
Monitor Quality Standards: Track and ensure adherence to quality requirements during development and support the team in identifying and resolving defects early
Documentation and Reporting: Document test results, maintain issue tracking, and communicate findings effectively to stakeholders
Support: Assist in ensuring quality gates are met and support the team in maintaining high-quality release standards
Requirements
2+ years of experience in Quality Assurance or a related role, with a focus on manual testing
Solid understanding of quality methodologies, best practices, and techniques for effective test coverage
Familiarity with test management tools like TestRail for organizing and tracking test cases
Experience using issue tracking tools like JIRA and documentation tools like Confluence
A self-starter mindset, strong analytical skills, and a positive “can-do” attitude
Nice-to-have
A strong passion for testing and improving product quality, Exposure to Behavior-Driven Development (BDD) practices, ISTQB Certification or equivalent qualification in software testing, Familiarity with online gambling platforms, particularly sports betting applications, Experience in Frontend Test automation framework, Experience with frontend frameworks like Angular is a plus
