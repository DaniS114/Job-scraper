Informácie o pracovnom mieste
Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
The objective is to analyze business requirements for the new project, based on existing company processes. The result of the analysis will be documentation of the existing processes and workflows and then design of the new system which will automate those processes. Design of the new system, creating corresponding features and use cases, in terms of business logic, is included in the scope for this role. In summary, it is a full-stack role in terms of analysis, documentation and testing of the new system in cooperation with a software developer.
In the late stage of the project, an integration with Microsoft Dynamics CRM and other systems will be started, where CRM and other systems will consume data from the newly created system.
The Roles & Responsibilities:
Analyze business requirements
Create business workflow documentation, system documentation, user guidelines using UML
Create Features and corresponding Use Cases for development
Performance & Quality:
Cooperate with business owners on analysis, system design and documentation.
Identify and report issues and risks.
Cooperate with business owners to achieve good test coverage and acceptance test quality
Skills & Experience:
5+ years of experience as software analyst or business analyst or full stack developer
Good knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics
Good knowledge of UML, especially use case, activity or BPMN diagrams
Ability to create business process documentation and user guidelines
Additional Desired Skills:
Advanced communication level of the written and verbal English language.
Proficient in creating documentation, including UML process models
