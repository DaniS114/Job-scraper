Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Integrating delivered components into working embedded systems
Debugging firmware, drivers, and application-level issues
Adapting and extending example code and reference designs
Building internal demos and proof-of-concept applications
Giving structured, technical feedback based on real usage
Requirements
Currently enrolled and studying towards a technical Degree (Bachelors, Masters or PHD students welcome)
Have a real passion for computing that goes way beyond your formal studies
Take a creative and yet structured approach to problem-solving
Have a solid knowledge of C and/or C++, and an interest in maintaining developer-supporting infrastructures
Are comfortable communicating within a small team in English
Have a strong desire for learning and self-improvement
Nice-to-have
Experience with or contributions to the Zephyr project, Hands-on experience with hardware and software debuggers, Knowledge of additional languages such as shell scripting or Python, Exposure to testing and/or verification, Experience with any automation, DevOps and or CI/CD tools and frameworks, Development of user applications using neural network components, Experience in training neural network models, CLI / TUI / GUI development experience
What we offer
Fixed-term internship (usually 3-6 months, full time or part time, starting in 2026)
Regular feedback and development opportunities
Social activities with fellow interns
End-of-internship celebration
Opportunity to be considered for future graduate positions (subject to performance)
Salary and rewards package
On-the-job learning and mentoring/buddy schemes
Hybrid working environment supporting high performance and personal wellbeing
Company info
At Arm, you'll influence 100% of the world's connected devices and all their applications. From healthcare and infrastructure to virtual reality, you'll affect nearly all industries worldwide, racing toward new milestones and achievements together.

You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
