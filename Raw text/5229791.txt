Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Monitoring and maintaining the computer systems and networks of our organization is one of your daily task. You could be remotely installing and configuring computer systems, diagnosing hardware and software faults and solve technical and applications problems, either over the phone or in person.
You are mainly responsible for the smooth running of computer systems and ensuring users get maximum benefits from them.
Individual tasks vary depending on the growing size of our organization and the need of our business, but you'll generally need to:
install and configure computer hardware operating systems and applications
monitor and maintain computer systems and networks
talk staff or clients through a series of actions, either face-to-face or over the phone, to help set up systems or resolve issues
troubleshoot system and network problems, diagnosing and solving hardware or software faults
replace parts as required
provide support, including procedural documentation and relevant reports
support the roll-out of new applications
set up new users' accounts and profiles
work continuously on a task until completion (or referral to third parties, if appropriate)
prioritize and manage many open cases at one time
follow diagrams and written instructions to repair a fault or set up a system
overnight absence from home is only occasionally required due travel to our HQ, located in Herisau, Switzerland
to fulfil all the needs you will work in shifts between 06:00 to 20:00
