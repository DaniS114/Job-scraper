At OMICRON, you’ll work in an environment shaped by trust and openness. We believe that our teams perform at their best when people feel valued, heard and empowered to contribute their ideas. We actively welcome different perspectives and embrace diversity, because it strengthens how we think, collaborate and innovate.
Since 1984, we have been developing testing, diagnostic, monitoring and cybersecurity solutions that help keep energy systems safe, secure and reliable – today used in more than 170 countries worldwide. What connects our teams globally is a shared sense of responsibility: for our work, our customers and each other.
In the OMICRON Academy, we offer high-quality training for technical professionals in the electrical power industry to support them with deepening their knowledge of our products and applications. As an integral element we want to further develop our e-learning offer and are looking for a Learning Systems Administrator (f/m/x) based in Klaus, Austria or Erlangen, Germany.
What's it all about?
You manage, configure, and maintain our Learning Management System (LMS), ensuring consistent branding, smooth navigation, and platform enhancements including customized adjustments
You take care of the LMS platform (docebo) as well as its integrations with other systems (CRM), collaborate with internal and external experts, and ensure its further development
You troubleshoot and resolve LMS-related technical issues, coordinate bug fixes, and support users with system questions and training
You upload, structure, and maintain our e-learning content to create engaging learning journeys
You administer users, manage permissions and maintain notifications for timely information
You generate reports for tracking and analyzing platform usage and create documentation and guidelines for stakeholders on Confluence
What are we looking for?
You have experience in LMS administration as well as system integration with databases and technologies
You have strong technical troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
You bring project management skills and the ability to work collaboratively with stakeholders
You enjoy working in a structured and precise way with attention to detail
You have strong written and verbal communication skills in English and German
