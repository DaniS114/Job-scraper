Responsibilities
Assisting the CG Supervisor/Creative Director and Art Director to fulfill their creative vision by producing quality character, set, and prop rigs and models, as well as scripting solutions
Working closely with the Director/Art Director to understand and technically drive forward his vision
Partnering with the Production Coordinator to schedule the project based on bid days provided by the Producer
Choosing the most efficient technologies, tools, taking into account budget, deadline and quality benchmark
Constantly working with the CG supervisor in optimizing workflow and pipeline, tools, software etc.
Technically checking everything before it is promoted to the next department to ensure technical fit in the pipeline
Resolving technical disagreements between artists and choosing the best course of action for the project
Mentoring and boosting morale of artists on the project
Creating and implementing new tools and techniques while working closely with the CG supervisor
Establishing and continuously developing the Asset Inventory/Library (as an owner)
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent
Good knowledge of programming languages and software engineering practices
Proven track record of technical knowledge of the software required
Experience with asset tracking/management system
Traditional artistic knowledge and background/experience with great aesthetic eye for details
Fluent English
Strong team player and leadership skills
Self-motivated, task-driven, and a great team player
Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
Excellent organizational and communication skills
Proactive (self learner, continuous tracking of tech developments)
Open to direction and able to embrace change
Nice-to-have
Proficient in Autodesk Maya and its application to animation workflows and pipeline, Extensive experience with Solid Angle Arnold
