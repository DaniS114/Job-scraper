Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced IT Support Specialist to provide on-site operational IT support in a manufacturing environment in Uherský Brod. The role covers end-user and production IT support, as well as collaboration at the IT/OT interface and support for digitalization initiatives.
Your Responsibilities
Experience in a manufacturing or industrial environment is preferred
Strong knowledge of IT infrastructure, networking, and Windows/Linux environments
Familiarity with OT environments (SCADA/MES) is a plus
Understanding of ITIL / service management frameworks (e.g. ServiceNow)
Knowledge of the local language (Czech) is an advantage
Degree in IT or equivalent professional experience (preferred)
Our Requirements
Operational IT Support
On-site IT support including hardware swaps, network patching, and non-standard configurations
Support of production IT systems (MES clients, scanners, printers, edge devices)
Ensuring compliance with corporate IT security standards
IT/OT Integration & Digitalization Support
Support system connectivity (SCADA, MES, OPC Router)
Line-side IT/OT integration for production systems
Configuration and backup of industrial PCs, HMIs, and gateways
Lifecycle Management & Procurement
Hardware lifecycle management (installation, replacement, disposal)
Coordination of local IT procurement activities
CMDB and IT asset updates
Support audit readiness and compliance activities
