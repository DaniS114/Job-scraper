Responsibilities
As a Fullstack Engineer, you'll work closely with experienced backend, data, and AI engineers to build and improve features across our platform. You will:
Build Fullstack Features:
Implement user-facing features using modern frameworks and connect them to backend APIs.
Backend Development:
Contribute to Python-based backend services (Django REST or FastAPI), including API endpoints and business logic.
Frontend Development:
Work on responsive, clean UI components and dashboards that surface complex financial data in an intuitive way.
Data & API Integration:
Help integrate third-party systems (ERPs, CRMs) and internal data pipelines into our application.
Testing & Code Quality:
Write unit and integration tests, fix bugs, and participate in code reviews to maintain high-quality standards.
Collaborate Across Teams:
Work closely with product managers, designers, and senior engineers to translate requirements into working software.
Learn by Doing:
Gain hands-on exposure to AI-powered features, LLM-enabled workflows, and production SaaS systems.

Company info
ARC Intelligence is pioneering financial intelligence for today's CFOs and finance teams. Our AIpowered platform integrates and analyzes data from ERPs, spreadsheets, and more— transforming raw financial data into clear, actionable insights. By combining a scalable semantic layer with advanced AI agents, we enable companies to make data-driven decisions without the overhead of a large data or finance team.
We're a fast-growing startup building at the intersection of finance, data, and AI—and we're looking for a Fullstack Engineer who's excited to learn, grow, and help shape our product from the ground up.
