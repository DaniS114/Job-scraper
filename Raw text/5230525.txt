Job description, responsibilities and duties
​We are looking for a Head of Engineering to lead CulturePulse’s engineering organisation across multiple products and high-trust, mission-critical platforms operating in regulated and security-sensitive environments.
This role is responsible for technical leadership, delivery execution, platform reliability, and people management across frontend, backend, data, ML/LLM systems, and cloud infrastructure. The Head of Engineering owns the engineering system as a whole - ensuring that teams are aligned, delivery is predictable, platforms are reliable, and technical decisions support long-term business and mission goals.
This is a senior leadership role working closely with the VP, CEO, Product, Sales, Data Science, and external stakeholders (including public-sector and international partners). While technical credibility is essential, the role is not about being the primary implementer, but about enabling teams to deliver high-quality systems with confidence, clarity, and accountability.
What will you do?
Provide strategic technical leadership
Define and own the technical direction and architecture across CulturePulse platforms.
Ensure engineering strategy aligns with company priorities, including B2G and B2B products.
Make high-impact technical decisions around architecture, scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency.
Evaluate trade-offs, risks, and long-term implications of technical choices.
Guide build vs buy vs partner decisions across infrastructure, AI tooling, and platforms.
Own platform reliability, operations, and risk
Be accountable for system reliability, availability, and operational resilience.
Ensure monitoring, alerting, incident response, and post-incident learning are in place and effective.
Oversee maintenance cycles, technical debt management, and system upgrades.
Ensure platforms meet expectations for stability and predictability in real-world operational use.
Proactively identify technical and operational risks and ensure mitigation plans exist.
Security, privacy, and compliance leadership
Own engineering-level security practices across systems and teams.
Ensure platforms meet security, privacy, and compliance expectations for regulated and high-trust environments.
Oversee secure architecture, access control, and data-sensitivity considerations.
Partner with leadership on audits, governance, and client-facing security or compliance requirements.
Ensure engineering teams understand and apply security and privacy principles in day-to-day work.
Delivery, prioritisation, and execution
Own engineering delivery planning, prioritisation, and execution across teams.
Balance feature development, platform maintenance, innovation, and operational work.
Establish predictable delivery cycles and realistic commitments.
Surface delivery risks early and communicate clearly with stakeholders.
Ensure engineering work is transparent, trackable, and aligned with product and business goals.
Lead and grow the engineering organisation
Lead a multi-disciplinary engineering team (frontend, backend, data, ML/AI, infrastructure).
Own hiring, onboarding, mentoring, and performance development.
Establish and uphold engineering standards, best practices, and healthy team processes.
Foster a culture of ownership, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
Ensure knowledge sharing, documentation, and continuity across the organisation.
Collaborate with product, data and leadership
Work closely with Product leadership to translate goals into executable technical plans.
Provide clear technical input on feasibility, trade-offs, timelines, and constraints.
Act as the primary technical counterpart for executive leadership and key external stakeholders.
Support cross-functional alignment between engineering, data science, design, and operations.

Why this role matters?
The Head of Engineering plays a critical role in ensuring that CulturePulse’s platforms are trusted, reliable, and operationally sound - especially in high-stakes environments where decisions rely on system outputs.
This role ensures that engineering is not only building powerful technology, but doing so in a way that is predictable, secure, and aligned with real-world operational needs. The Head of Engineering shapes how teams work, how risks are managed, and how technical decisions support both immediate delivery and long-term mission success.
Engineering delivery is predictable, transparent, and aligned with business priorities.
Platforms are stable, secure, and trusted by government and enterprise partners.
Technical debt and operational risk are actively managed, not deferred.
The engineering team is motivated, supported, and continuously improving.
Leadership has clear visibility into technical risks, trade-offs, and opportunities.
