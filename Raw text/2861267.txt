Állás leírása
Responsibilities
You will build 3D geometry models representing real-world Automotive Radar & ADAS scenarios
You will run electromagnetic simulations using the Asymptotic Solver in CST Studio Suite and analyze KPIs
You will generate synthetic training datasets from simulation results to support machine learning models
You will develop and optimize ML-based workflows to enhance radar functionality and performance
You will collaborate with senior engineers and researchers on high-priority R&D projects
You will support integration of simulation and ML workflows into research pipelines for automotive radar applications
Requirements
You are currently enrolled as a Master's student in Electrical Engineering or a related field
You have solid Python programming skills for simulation data processing and workflow automation
You are familiar with radar fundamentals and signal processing concepts
What makes you stand out
You have hands-on experience with electromagnetic simulation tools such as CST Studio Suite (optional but a plus)
You have knowledge of machine learning techniques and frameworks
You are experienced in C/C++ programming and are curious, initiative-driven, and motivated to tackle complex technical problems
What we offer
Flexible working hours with the possibility to work partially outside the office
Learning from and working with highly qualified, dedicated and diverse teams
Possibility for professional development in many different domains within our 3DEXPERIENCE University and LinkedIn Learning programs
Additional benefits & perks such as Urban Sports Club membership (because who doesn't love staying fit while having fun?) and of course, unlimited coffee, fruit, and drinks at the office, we've got you covered ;)
Company info
Dassault Systèmes is the second largest software manufacturer in Europe and has been a dynamically growing, innovative company since 1981.
Do you want your code to actually matter? As a working student (m/f/d) in the SIMULIA EMAG R&D team at Dassault Systèmes, you'll contribute to real AI and simulation research for automotive radar and ADAS applications - supporting projects that matter to some of the biggest names in the automotive industry.
