Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Our client is a globally focused fintech company based in Bratislava that aims to transform the way people understand and manage their finances.
We are looking for curious UX/UI designer who is interested in how people interact with digital products and enjoys turning complex ideas into simple, elegant interfaces. If you are comfortable experimenting with modern tools and using AI to speed up design workflows while maintaining high design quality, keep reading.
Your Role
As a UX / UI Designer, you will be responsible for designing user interfaces and improving user experience across our digital products. You will work closely with developers, product manager, and stakeholders to transform concepts into well-structured, functional, and visually appealing designs.
A key part of the role is working efficiently in
Figma
and leveraging
AI-powered tools
to accelerate design, prototyping, and iteration.

Design modern and intuitive user interfaces for web and digital products
