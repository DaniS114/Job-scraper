Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about driving operational excellence and enabling teams to perform at their best? Join us as a Group Data Services Enablement Specialist and become the backbone of our data services organization, orchestrating processes, communications, and continuous improvement initiatives that keep our department running smoothly and efficiently.
About the Role
As a Group Data Services Enablement Specialist, you'll be the operational cornerstone that enables consistent, high-quality service delivery across our organization. You'll coordinate critical processes, facilitate knowledge sharing, and drive strategic initiatives that enhance our department's effectiveness and impact.
Key Responsibilities:
Operational Excellence
- Coordinate key routines and events that keep GDS functioning efficiently
- Track objectives, facilitate quarterly business reviews, and manage strategic initiatives to align with organizational goals
- Identify process improvements and enhance documentation and workflows
- Provide professional administrative support by managing schedules, coordinating meetings and travel arrangements, creating presentations, and organizing events to ensure smooth operations
Communications & Engagement
- Prepare internal communications and coordinate cross-departmental information flows
- Ensure high-quality presentations and materials that represent the department professionally
Learning & Development
- Support capability-building initiatives and training programs
- Collaborate with SMEs to develop effective learning content
- Maintain visibility of learning opportunities across the organization
Performance & Governance
- Reinforce operational discipline and best practices
- Identify inefficiencies and propose improvements to enhance departmental performance
About the Team
You'll be joining the Data Strategy & Operations team, which shapes Group Data Services' strategic direction and transformation while ensuring smooth operations with meticulous risk management. We bridge strategy and delivery - an energetic, solution-oriented team that thrives on collaboration, creativity, and making things happen. Our GDS Enablement team supports operations, awareness, and performance across all GDS departments, making the organization more client-focused, efficient, technically stronger, and profitable through process improvements and risk mitigation.
