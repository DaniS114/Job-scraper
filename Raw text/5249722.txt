Job description, responsibilities and duties
Responsible for the software design and implementation of business requirements. This role ensures that internal and external software components are seamlessly integrated and that the high-level application interacts correctly with the underlying hardware.
Main tasks:
Working in a software development team
Software Architecture: Design and develop C++ applications with a focus on modularity.
Requirement Analysis: Analyze business requirements to design, implement, and test software modules.
Leadership: Lead and coach new co-workers on technology use and Kistler methodologies.
Integration: Ensure continuous systems integration and manage configuration tools.
Automation: Focus on software delivery automation and CI/CD methods.
