Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Senior Database Administrator with at least 8 years of experience andexpertise in managing and optimizing various database technologies along with a strong background in database migra-tion and conversion. The ideal candidate should possess deep expertise in database administration, performance tuning, troubleshooting, automation, and the ability to seamlessly migrate data between various database in On-Premise and tothe Cloud.
Your tasks will include:
Essential skills:
Administering and optimizing database systems on Windows and Linux using Oracle, PostgreSQL, and MSSQL
Installing, configuring, migrating, upgrading, and patching databases
Deploying and troubleshooting High Availability and Disaster Recovery solutions
Familiar with different Backups & Recovery solutions and third-party database backup utilities
Diagnosing and resolving database-related issues, ensuring minimal downtime
Desired skills:
Implement maintenance improvement and automation focus
Perform regular database performance tuning and optimization activities
Working with cloud-based database solutions (e.g., Oracle, AWS, Azure), Performing design evaluations/ recommen-dations for database migration strategies to the cloud
Programming/scripting with PowerShell, Bash, or other scripting languages
Writing complex SQL queries on customer data to provide reports needed
Conceiving and implementing professional/technical training courses for internal employees
