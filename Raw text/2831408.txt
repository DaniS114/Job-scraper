IT Manager (m/w) 100 %Are you an experienced IT leader who thrives in regulated manufacturing environments and wants to make a real impact on compliance, efficiency, and digital transformation? Join us as IT Manager, where you will be responsible for the full IT landscape of our pharmaceutical manufacturing site and play a key role in ensuring reliable, secure, and compliant IT operations that support our strategic and operational goals.Ophtapharm AG was founded in 2023 from a facility with over 40 years’ experience in the manufacturing of sterile products and is an FDA/EU-GMP certified CMO of pharmaceutical sterile products. On our plant in Hettlingen, high quality products such as sterile eye drops, eye ointments and eye gels are aseptically compounded, filled, tested and packed for worldwide markets.You will play a pivotal role in safeguarding and shaping the IT backbone of a pharmaceutical manufacturing site, with real influence on compliance, efficiency, and future digitization.
