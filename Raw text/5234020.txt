Pricing Analyst
We are Clarios, battery producer and we are looking for a Pricing Analyst for our OTC department (Order to Cash). This department`s responsibility covers all processes related to receiving, processing, and fulfilling customers’ requests for goods or services. The department is continuously working on improvements and process enhancements to fulfill our customers’ requirements in highest quality to ensure customer satisfaction.
Key Responsibilities:
Customer price maintenance on monthly, quarterly base
Pricing discrepancy analyses
Core price & Lead surcharge calculations
Follow up on the topics related to pricing process
Material master set-up and issue resolution
Pricing related projects participation
Be a part of discussion with internal customers and business units to ensure smooth process
