Brief description of the company
CIIT GmbH - https://ciit-software.com - is a Vienna-based software development company specializing in the design and implementation of custom digital solutions for organizations across various industries. Founded in 2004, CIIT has over 20 years of experience delivering scalable web, mobile, and enterprise applications tailored to real-world business processes.
Our team works with modern technologies to build secure and maintainable software systems — from backend services and web platforms to cross-platform mobile applications. CIIT supports clients throughout the entire software lifecycle, from concept and architecture to deployment, training, and ongoing support.
We collaborate with well-known organizations such as Robert Bosch AG, Raiffeisen Bank Niederösterreich-Wien, and the Austrian Red Cross, helping them drive digital transformation through innovative and reliable software solutions.
