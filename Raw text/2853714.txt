Responsibilities
Work with C-suite executives to problem-solve key issues and provide actionable recommendations leveraging technology know-how and business sense
Play an active role in all aspects of client projects including gathering and analyzing information, formulating and testing hypotheses, developing and communicating recommendations for client presentations
Present results to client management and advise on implementation in collaboration with client team members
Work in teams of 3 to 5 consultants alongside experts and partners including developers, designers, data engineers, agile coaches and others
Work on projects across all industries and functions, integrated with the global firm
Work on advanced analytics, agile, cloud, cybersecurity, digital marketing and organization transformation
Requirements
Degree (Bachelor or Master) in technical subjects (e.g., computer science, engineering, IT, hard sciences)
Up to 2 years of experience (internships or full-time) in technology/IT strategy roles
Knowledge in areas like digital transformation, IT architecture, IoT, e-commerce, AI, robotics, cloud, big data, analytics, or cybersecurity is advantageous
Outstanding track record of academic and professional achievements
Combination of strong strategic and analytical abilities with a passion for technology
Proven track record of leadership through extracurricular activities
Fluency in both English and Polish or Hungarian (depending on location)
Willingness to travel

Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that brings together the best of McKinsey’s Tech & AI capabilities to help clients use technology to transform their businesses. The firm operates globally with colleagues across 65+ countries and over 100 different nationalities, fostering a diverse and inclusive environment that fuels creativity and innovation. McKinsey is committed to integrity, ethics, and delivering exceptional results for clients while investing deeply in the development and growth of its people.
