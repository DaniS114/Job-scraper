Join the team at Operational Services GmbH, within Deutsche Telekom Systems Solutions Slovakia, providing opportunities for talented IT professionals in Slovakia. OS is one of the leading ICT service providers in the German market and is considered the backbone of digitization for medium-sized companies. With almost 1,000 highly qualified employees, OS develops and operates state-of-the-art IT systems for our customers, ensures long-term system support, and maintains the availability of critical business processes.
If you are excited to join an English and German-speaking team that values innovation, structure, and collaboration, this role could be the perfect fit for you.
What will you do?
Microsoft 365 & Collaboration
Design, implementation and optimization of Microsoft 365 environments including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online and OneDrive
Planning and execution of iCloud replacements or integrations using App Protection Policies (MAM) and structured OneDrive migrations
Consulting on information architecture, collaboration concepts and user adoption
Modern Workplace & Endpoint Management
Design and operation of modern workplace solutions with Microsoft Intune (MDM/MAM)
Implementation of Windows 11 rollouts, device strategies and security baselines
Creation and maintenance of smartphone and device policies (iOS, Android, Windows)
Design and implementation of Azure Virtual Desktop (AVD) environments
Identity, Security & Governance
Design and implementation of modern identity solutions with Microsoft Entra ID
Implementation of Conditional Access, MFA and Zero Trust principles
Migration and decommissioning of on‑premises Active Directory environments
Establishment of governance policies and compliance controls as a sustainable foundation for secure cloud environments
Optional: Azure & Cloud Foundations
Planning and implementation of Azure Landing Zones (identity, management, security)
Support for cloud transformation and hybrid scenarios
You will succeed if you:
Have several years of professional experience as a Microsoft Consultant, Cloud Architect, or in a comparable role
Have in‑depth knowledge of Microsoft 365, Intune, Entra ID and Azure
Possess proven hands-on experience with:
Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive
MDM/MAM concepts and mobile security (especially Intune)
Azure Landing Zones and cloud governance
Identity and security architectures (Zero Trust)
Are experienced in migrating on‑premises infrastructures to the cloud
Strongly understand to security, compliance and governance requirements
Have structured, solution-oriented working style and strong customer orientation
Have experience with technical leadership in projects and workshops in close collaboration with customers, architects and internal teams
