Job description, responsibilities and duties
You will write and illustrate operating instructions and training documents (SMART products, systems, solutions)
Definition of the documentation requirements for subcontractors in form, language, number, etc.
Review of the incoming sub-supplier documentation
Coordination with our customers regarding the documentation requirements in English
Reading and understanding technical drawings and diagrams
Researching technical content from contacts in various global departments
Documentation should follow ISO 20607, IEC 82079-1 and other applicable standards
Structured collection of technical information in our systems
Extract and summarization of detailed information for the customer's maintenance planning
Detailed description of own products with regard to installation, operation and maintenance in the form of modular writing in the XML-based editorial system
Arranging necessary translations in the required end customer language
Compilation of technical drawings and bills of materials for system assembly
Compilation of the entire system documentation into a comprehensive assembly, operating and maintenance manual, consisting of our own products, internal and external supplies and overview documents, taking into account customer-specific requirements
Provision of all documentation in the agreed form (paper, data carrier, etc.)
