Responsibilities
As our (Senior) Product Designer UX/UI (m/f/d), you'll be at the heart of understanding our users and translating insights into impactful design solutions. You will collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and fellow designers to create intuitive, effective, and beautiful experiences.
What you can expect
Plan and conduct user interviews, surveys, and usability tests to gather actionable insights
Lead end-to-end product design
Design user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity mockups that solve real user problems
Lead and participate in weekly design critiques to continuously improve design quality
Support implementation by collaborating closely with engineering teams
Use AI-assisted tools to rapidly prototype and visualize early design ideas
Requirements
5+ years experience in B2C product design; experience in travel or related industries is a plus
Proven ability to run user interviews, surveys, and usability tests, and translate findings into design improvements
Skilled in Figma and prototyping tools, familiar with AI-assisted prototyping tools is a plus
Understanding of accessibility standards and responsive design best practices
Strong collaboration and communication skills and experience working closely with product and engineering teams
German or Dutch language skills are a plus, but not required
What we offer
Regular feedback as well as internal and external training, e.g. at our PiNCAMP Academy
A steep learning curve with a lot of responsibility from day one, flat hierarchies, quick decisions, room for creative ideas and new impulses
A good mix of agile start-up and grown professional structures
Health promotion, e.g. through subsidies for Urban Sports Club or ergonomic workstations, free fruit, vegetables and muesli
Flexible working hours, home office, work abroad, hybrid working
30 vacation days
A camper for your short trip or vacation
An appreciative work culture and atmosphere in an international team
Regular company parties and events
Best hardware of your choice, modern tools
Modern office in the heart of Berlin with excellent public transport connections and a rooftop terrace for fresh air and sun-bathing
Snacks and drinks
Various employee discounts, e.g. Travel Industry Card
Company info
PiNCAMP is the camping startup of ADAC, ANWB and TCS - in Germany, the Netherlands and Switzerland, as well as throughout Europe. An international team of more than 80 digital experts digitises the camping industry by combining the experience and reputation of our shareholders with the speed of an agile start-up. Our goal is to be Europe's leading camping platform. For our further growth we are looking for passionate hands-on people who want to grow with us and are eager to build an excellent product for campers and campsites alike.
