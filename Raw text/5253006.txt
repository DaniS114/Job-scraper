Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you a detail-oriented student with analytical skills and a passion for technology? Join our Wording Insights team as a Junior Business Analyst where you'll bridge business needs and technical solutions while gaining valuable real-world experience. This student position offers an exceptional opportunity to develop professional skills in business analysis while contributing to the evolution of our contracts & wording application.
About the Role
As a Junior Business Analyst, you'll work at the intersection of business and technology, helping shape our contract wording tool. You'll learn how to gather requirements, collaborate with diverse stakeholders, and contribute to Al-enabled features that enhance contract analysis and user productivity.
Key Responsibilities
•	Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and analyze business requirements for our contract wording software
•	Support the liaison between business users and development teams, helping translate requirements into actionable user stories
•	Create documentation for technical solutions, data models, and implementations
•	Work with QA teams to help define acceptance criteria for new features
•	Support UI/UX efforts to ensure a smooth user experience across the tool
•	Assist in identifying Al use cases and automation opportunities that align with product strategy
•	Help manage the product backlog and participate in the prioritization of features
•	Engage in Agile development processes, including sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
About the Team
The Wording Insigh s team is an international team of 40 professionals dedicated to developing and enhancing software specializing in contracts and wording. We bridge business needs and technical solutions, continuously improving our tool to deliver meaningful value to users. Our current focus includes evolving Al-enabled capabilities to enhance contract analysis, user productivity, and decision-making.
