Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you an experienced Data Engineer? Do you want to work with cutting-edge technologies and be part of an established team within an international project? If you want to grow your career further, search no more and let us know you have the abilities to become a moving force among other professionals.
As a
Data Engineer
you will have the opportunity to help build applications that will provide actionable insights across various businesses. You will be a part of fast moving and engineering driven digital organization with skilled engineers what will help you tackle challenges and problems that are critical to our business outcomes.
Key responsibilities:
• Design, develop, and maintain scalable applications.
• Help us identify, design, and implement process improvements such as automating manual processes, optimizing data delivery, re-designing infrastructure for greater scalability.
• Perform debugging, troubleshooting, modifications, and unit testing.
• Create databases, infrastructure, solutions, and methods to monitor our systems.
• Automate code testing and pipelines.
• Engage directly with business partners to participate in design and development of data solutions.
• Work in a scaled Agile environment.
• Engage and actively participate in hackathons, peer groups, etc.
