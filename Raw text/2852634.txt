Responsibilities
You will lead IT projects and develop on‑prem as well as cloud infrastructures aligned with enterprise architecture standards.
You will design and optimize IT system architectures (application, data, infrastructure, security) and ensure continuous improvement of the IT landscape.
You will consult internally on state‑of‑the‑art technologies and evaluate infrastructure performance, scalability, cost efficiency and IT security.
You will manage integration and migration activities, including external business partners and virtual environment implementations.
You will create architectural documentation and IT architecture blueprints aligned with enterprise architecture.
Requirements
You have strong IT expertise in cloud technologies (AWS, Azure, hybrid and multi cloud) and IT security architectures as well as DevOps and automation practices (CI/CD, IaC).
You bring solid project management experience and are confident working with both agile and traditional delivery approaches.
You are versed in business and process analysis and can translate business requirements into clear, feasible technical solutions.
You possess deep knowledge of software and infrastructure technologies, operating systems and modern network environments.
You communicate fluently in English and are willing to travel internationally for project‑related purposes.
