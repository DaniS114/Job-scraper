ITC Engineer
What do you need to succeed in this opportunity
- Having a solid understanding of the process of how operating systems are laid out and loaded and know how to configure operating systems.  Knowledge of Unattend.XML usage, registry and other locations for image customization is required..
- Ability to work closely with various internal teams, such as sales, project management.
- Ability to prioritize tasks in a fast paced environment while keeping quality of the work high as you will be working on several projects simultaneously.
- Clear written and verbal communication skills
Required education
Follow-up/Higher Professional Education
What will you do
- Develop and test custom Windows images for ThinkPad laptops, ThinkCentre desktops, and ThinkStation workstations, with occasional support for non-Lenovo hardware based on client needs.
- Build and deploy custom images for global Fortune 500 companies using tools such as Microsoft DISM, Windows ADK, Sysprep (for custom OOBE), and proprietary in-house manufacturing tools.
- Create and modify automation scripts for image customization using scripting languages like PowerShell, batch files (*.CMD, *.BAT), AutoIt, and VBScript.
- Collaborate directly with clients to gather imaging and BIOS/CMOS requirements, define project scope, and develop test plans for approval.
- Document and deliver technical solutions, including test results and final image packages, ensuring all requirements are met before release to manufacturing.
