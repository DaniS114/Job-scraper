Feladatok
Join our team as the new Head of Data Team and play a key strategic role in shaping our data landscape across multiple countries. We are looking for an experienced leader with strong analytical capabilities, a passion for data driven decision making, and the ability to develop and guide a distributed Data IT team. In this role you will oversee the development and operation of our enterprise data warehouse, ensure high quality reporting standards across the region, and collaborate closely with business and IT stakeholders to drive automation, efficiency, and reporting excellence.
Your responsibilities:
Managing and Expanding Data IT team located in different countries of the Region;
Overseeing the integration of data into the enterprise data warehouse, collaboration with department heads to ensure accurate and relevant reporting data;
Acting as the main point of contact for communication with EA branches and external suppliers regarding the development and maintenance of the data warehouse;
Participation in the development of unified reporting tools, statistics and dashboards across whole EE Unit;
Working closely with the IT department to continuously improve automation, efficiency, and the quality of reported information;
Overseeing the administration of the MSSQL database, including the design, creation and maintenance of data structures necessary for reporting;
Ensuring accurate and timely generation of daily, weekly, and monthly statistics and analyses for all EA departments, as well as reports based on Helpdesk requests;
Taking direct responsibility for meeting deadlines for regular statistical reports sent to business partners;
Ensuring consistency and correctness of all statistical output.
