Build and maintain advanced VBA solutions, including Excel add-ins used by external clients

Design and manage Power Automate workflows; explore opportunities to leverage Copilot/AI agents
Utilize SQL/Alteryx for recurring data extracts and process automation
Analyse end-to-end business processes to identify automation opportunities
Oversee all automation projects, ensuring timely delivery, documentation, and centralization
Prioritize and structure initiatives to align with business needs and resource capacity
Ensure knowledge transfer and continuity through clear, accessible documentation
Act as a key point of contact between business teams and technical solutions
Prepare and deliver presentations for client meetings (including banks across the EU)
Build strong client relationships by understanding business needs and presenting solutions in a clear, professional manner
Support internal teams by translating technical concepts into actionable business outcomes
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, IT or an equivalent combination of education, with at least 2 years’ professional work in a multinational environment
Excellent written and oral communication skills in English
Advanced proficiency in VBA (Excel add-ins, complex coding, arrays, optimization)
Intermediate to advanced Python skills (automation, reporting, email generation)
Experience with Power Platform (Power Automate essential, Power Apps a plus)
Knowledge of Alteryx or similar workflow tools and SQL is an advantage
Interest and willingness to learn emerging automation tools and Copilot agents
Strong process analysis and problem-solving abilities
Proven experience managing multiple projects end-to-end, with strong prioritization skills
Excellent client relationship management skills, including preparing and delivering presentations
Structured, proactive, and able to work independently while aligning with team objectives
Exceptional attention to detail and analytical skills, with ability to prioritize and deliver tasks on time while maintaining high quality and consistent results
Strong planning and organizational skills, with the ability to manage and prioritize to meet deadlines

Company info
At BlackRock, we are all connected by one mission: to help more and more people experience financial well-being. Our clients, and the people they serve, are saving for retirement, paying for their children’s educations, buying homes and starting businesses. Their investments also help to strengthen the global economy: support businesses small and large; finance infrastructure projects that connect and power cities; and facilitate innovations that drive progress.
