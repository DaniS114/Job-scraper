Stručná charakteristika spoločnosti
Allfred.io is an all-in-one management tool built for creative agencies. We help them cut the admin chaos, run projects smoother, and actually make money instead of losing sleep over spreadsheets.
From proposals and budgeting to time tracking and reporting, Allfred automates the boring stuff so agencies can focus on what they do best — creating great work. With real-time insights into project progress and financial health, teams finally get the clarity they need to stay profitable.
Features like one-click time tracking, dynamic resource planning, and simple task overviews keep everyone on the same page. Plus, our intuitive onboarding makes implementation effortless — no endless setup, no headaches.
Agencies like Triad, MullenLowe GGK, or MUW Saatchi & Saatchi are already on board, and we’re growing fast across Europe — from Lithuania and Romania to Italy, Bulgaria, and the UK.
We believe agencies should spend their brainpower on creativity, not on tracking hours in Excel. That’s why we built Allfred — and why we’re always looking for talented people who want to help agencies work smarter, not harder.
