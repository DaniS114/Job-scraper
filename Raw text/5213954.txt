Job description, responsibilities and duties
Join Our Team as a Software Test Engineer!
Are you ready to put your testing skills to the ultimate test? At our company, you’ll dive deep into the world of software quality – where every bug squashed and every feature refined brings us closer to outstanding digital experiences.
As a
Software Test Engineer
, you’ll be the guardian of quality – blending manual testing know-how with automated testing expertise to ensure our applications exceed expectations. You’ll work closely with talented colleagues to analyze, test, and fine-tune our platforms, making sure every release hits the mark.
​Here’s what you’ll be working on:​
Perform manual and automated testing of new and existing software features with minimal supervision
Design, develop, and maintain automated test frameworks and test cases
Analyze business and technical requirements and translate them into effective test scenarios
Execute regression, integration, system, performance, and user acceptance testing
Identify, document, and track software defects, and support their resolution
Prepare test plans, define testing scope, objectives, risks, and deliverables
Track and report test metrics, results, and overall quality status
Collaborate closely with developers, product owners, and other stakeholders to resolve issues and support enhancements
Support integration testing with internal systems and third-party services
Ensure testing activities are repeatable, efficient, and delivered on time
Actively contribute to improving testing processes, tools, and automation coverage
