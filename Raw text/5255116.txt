Job description, responsibilities and duties
Advise national and international customers on network architecture, modernization strategies, and security concepts
Design IT and OT network architectures, including segmentation, zoning, Zero Trust principles, and high-availability models
Prepare architecture blueprints, technical design documents, and migration strategies
Serve as subject-matter expert for OT-related network topics in complex environments
Provide architectural guidance and operational support to our internal global IT team
Participate in international, cross-functional project teams
Contribute to the development of standards, guidelines, and best practices for network architecture
Support the implementation of IT and OT network solutions
Conduct troubleshooting, performance analysis, and optimization of existing network environments
Collaborate on security and compliance projects
Contribute to hardening measures, operational concepts, and risk-mitigation strategies for mixed IT/OT infrastructures
