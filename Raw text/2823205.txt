Company info
Would you like to participate in international projects (Software development related projects mainly in wholesale) within MOL Group IT Organization on the Wholesale/Asset/HSE area?
Would you like to use your creativity, proactivity, curiousity and your coordination skills in your daily work with support of (Downstream) Wholesale, Asset and EHS Solutions Delivery team?
Would you like to work in a international team of 4 that is a supportive and helpfull and you can learn from. You will have a mentor who will be able to help you.
Are you ready to have high visibility and impact on business and IT areas?
Then your place is here with us as an Junior/medior IT Business Analyst – Wholesale based in Budapest MOL Campus, Hungary
Tasks
Act as a key connection with business stakeholders, super users, architects, and developers.
Gather and document business requirements for your assigned sub-domain.
Contribute to functional specifications and process modelling (“as-is”, “to-be”).
Coordinate testing activities (integration and UAT), track defects, and validate fixes.
Support master data processes and change management activities.
Oversee development requests and smaller projects in an agile method.
Monitor market trends and share best practices within the team.
Requirements
University degree (IT, engineering, or economics).
1–3 years of relevant IT business analyst experience, preferably in a multinational environmentStrong communication and presentation skills in Hungarian and English.
Structured mindset, independence, stakeholder management skills, and strong time management.
Fast learner with solid analytical skills.
Proficiency in MS Office.
Advantage: SAP, SAP Solution Manager, Azure DevOps, ERP/CRM systems (Salesforce), test management tools.
