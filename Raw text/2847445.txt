Description
The Leidos AIE team has an opening for a Cybersecurity Manager with a SECRET security clearance for our Cybersecurity Team. This opening supports our program that is being deployed to Army installations around the world. Our home base is in Edgewood MD which houses the program team, warehouse operations, system integration lab, 24x7 Help Desk operation, engineering center and full-scale replicas the system.
Primary Responsibilities:
This position is responsible for all aspects of the AIE Cybersecurity program and compliance with Department of Defense policies and
guidance.
Primary duties include:
Ensure the system obtains and maintains Authorization to Operate (ATO)
Ensure compliance of the system with STIGs and DoD policies
Develop and update System Security Plans (SSPs) consistent with FISMA requirements
Develop and maintain SSP, POA&Ms and other artifacts for the Accreditation process
Create documentation as project deliverables
Support Command Cyber Readiness Inspections and accreditation efforts
Review vulnerability scan results and recommend remediations
Review all published CTO’s; recommend and track compliance actions where applicable, ensure reporting requirements are met
Support all product teams in security requirements definition, application, and risk reduction efforts
Basic Qualifications
Candidate must have expert knowledge related to cybersecurity management, assessment and analysis for strategic information technology direction to comply with FISMA, FedRAMP, Privacy Act, the NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF), CNSSI 1253 and other applicable regulatory guidelines.
Must possess a Secret clearance.
Typically requires BS degree and 8 – 12 years of prior relevant experience.
Must be intimately familiar with the DoD Risk Management Framework (RMF) process.
Must possess a CISSP or CISM certification
Must have experience using Enterprise Mission Assurance Support Service (eMASS)
Preferred Qualifications
Experience working with Host Based Security System (HBSS)
Experience reviewing Assured Compliance Assessment Solution (ACAS) results
Experience working with DISA STIGs, STIGViewer, and eMASSter
Familiarity with, and experience collaborating with, various Army Cyber teams (NETCOM, ARCYBER, GCC Secure, AESS, etc.)
