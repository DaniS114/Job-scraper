Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
#We have a hybrid set up with possibility to work from Bratislava or Zilina office#
Would you like to leave your mark in the truly booming #cloud-native & fin-tech industry#? Would you like to show your ideas and bring them to life?
Ohpen is on the lookout for #a skilled Senior Java Developer# to join the team and help us build and maintain the product and its applications. There is where you come into the picture!
#Briefing#
You would be joining one of our self-governing squads in Bratislava or Zilina, which owns the domain of our platform from start to finish and where most decisions are made internally – an exciting place for skilled developers who can bring in new ideas.
You will get the freedom to come up with new solutions and be in an ever-evolving role where you learn new things in the exciting world of Java.
Since #English# is our daily working language, we expect you to be comfortable communicating effectively in English on a professional level.
#About You#
You have #4+ years# with backend programming languages such as #Java, Rust, C++# and willingness to code in #Java/SpringBoot#
You have semi-advanced knowledge or interest in #AWS (EC2, Lambda, LB)#
You have experience with #Terraform, GitHub + workflows#
You have working experience with or an interest in #Developing Cloud Systems, Build platform-integrated pipelines or Lead software delivery automation#
#How We Work#
We don’t do sprints or ticket factories. We deliver whatever we deem best value/effort, release biweekly (for now), and constantly work to shorten feedback loops. Developers write code, tests, and deploy. Each team has one QA and one DevOps - no handovers, no walls to throw things over.
Everyone is expected to contribute to #architecture and design#. Bureaucracy is kept to a minimum. If something slows us down, we fix it.
#About Us#
We’re building a real-time, cloud-native core banking platform for two major markets. No legacy systems. Just real customers, real code, and real impact. You’ll work in small, strong teams of engineers who care about their craft.

Brief description of the company
Ohpen delivers a cloud-based software solution for financial institutions. With offices in Slovakia, the Netherlands, UK, and Spain we help financial institutions get rid of their legacy.
