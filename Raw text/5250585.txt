Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for
C# Software Developer
for our software platform
Syngo
.
Syngo
is an intelligent imaging software for multi-modality reading for all key clinical fields, used from general radiology to oncology, from cardiovascular to neurology. It provides excellent base for optimal diagnosis and therapy decisions. See more on the Medical Imaging - Siemens Healthineers
Your team will be working on challenging tasks to enable software platform for multiple medical devices and processing of high-throughput as well as big volume of data, real-time diagnoses and data visualization.
As a part our the team you will participate on agile software development including defining requirements, implementation of automated tests, implementation and maintenance of product code, technical documentation, bugfixes, communication with stakeholders and contribution to the team and company strategic goals.
Requirements:
Minimum 3 years of hands-on experience in software engineering preferably with C#/.NET, Angular/Typescript
Experience in using and applying OOD, OOP, design patterns and UML. Solid understanding of the software development life cycle (SDLC) and engineering practices such as TDD, CI, code review, etc.
Basic knowledge of  Containerization / Kubernetes is nice to have
Good written and verbal communication skills in English
Analytical, problem solving skills and aptitude for learning new technologies
Ability to work in a team environment
