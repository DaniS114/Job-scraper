Responsibilities
YOUR FUTURE ROLE
As a Senior Development Consultant (f/m/d), you are passionate about emerging technologies such as AI, SAP BTP, and ABAP Cloud and eager to drive innovation. You will design and deliver modern, cloud-based solutions on SAP BTP, working closely with development, sales, and presales teams. Leveraging your consulting experience, you identify digitalization opportunities and help customers optimize and future-proof their business processes — including the integration of AI-driven capabilities. Agility, strong communication skills, and a collaborative mindset are key, as you will work with customers worldwide. If you are excited to shape how organizations leverage SAP BTP and AI, we look forward to your application.
What you'll do:
Take end-to-end responsibility for complex projects or defined areas of ownership
Act as a senior expert on technical and business topics, driving high-quality solutions
Work independently, set priorities, and resolve complex cross-functional challenges
Contribute to strategic concepts and influence future system and process design
Bring several years of relevant experience with strong technical and/or business expertise
Build trusted relationships with customers and internal stakeholders
Communicate clearly, provide transparent updates, and collaborate effectively across teams
Requirements
YOUR PROFILE
You hold a Bachelor's degree or higher in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field and bring several years of experience in implementation projects. You are experienced in agile development methodologies and have successfully delivered SAP BTP projects or Proof of Concepts.
You have strong technical and analytical skills in SAP development tools and methodologies, including the creation of technical specifications. You are comfortable working with innovative and fast-evolving technologies and understand complex system landscapes, business processes, and SAP's technology strategy — particularly in the context of cloud technologies and market trends.
You are open to customer-facing engagements and travel across Europe and globally. Fluent German and English skills complete your profile.
What you bring:
Strong programming skills (ABAP, Java, JavaScript) with a cloud-first mindset
Development on SAP BTP using SAP CAP and/or SAP RAP
Experience integrating AI capabilities and data-driven services into SAP solutions
Strong UI development skills (HTML5, SAPUI5, Fiori Elements, React)
Solid understanding of SAP S/4HANA extensibility and clean core principles
Experience with modern development environments (ADT, BAS, VS-Code)
Knowledge of SAP's AI portfolio and emerging technology trends
Excellent communication skills in both German and English
What we offer
What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.
Location: Garching or Walldorf
