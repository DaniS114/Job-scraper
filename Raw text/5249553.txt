Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are part of Swiss Re’s P&C Digital & Technology Re organization and are looking for an enthusiastic AI Engineer who is passionate about applying cutting-edge AI techniques to complex, real-world risk challenges. We build global AI-driven products that support underwriting, pricing, claims, and risk management across Property & Casualty Reinsurance.
While we value a strong foundation in machine learning, we place equal emphasis on robust engineering practices, scalable cloud-native infrastructure, and deploying AI solutions into reliable production systems.
If you join us, you will be able to participate in a variety of impactful initiatives. For example, you will:
-Design and deploy AI solutions to extract insights from complex underwriting and claims documents using Generative AI
-Build and operationalize machine learning models supporting risk selection, pricing, and portfolio steering
-Develop AI tools to help automate and make our business more efficient
We are a highly skilled and diverse team working on global initiatives across Swiss Re. Our passion for learning connects us. We continuously invest in upskilling, knowledge sharing, and improving how we engineer AI products. You will be encouraged to explore new technologies and deepen your expertise while contributing to high-impact solutions.
By joining us, you will have the opportunity to develop your skills in the technologies that power modern AI products — especially cloud computing, distributed systems, data platforms, and MLOps — while solving some of the most complex risk challenges in the reinsurance industry. This role offers a unique chance to grow as an AI engineer in a collaborative, high-impact environment.
