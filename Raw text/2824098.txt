Company info
We help our clients with industry-specific services to create value and manage risks. Our wide-ranging client portfolio enables you to gain real-life experience in industries such as telecommunications, energy, IT, finance or sports, and to work on ground-breaking projects.
What we offer:
competitive salary and a wide range of cafeteria elements
private health insurance, life and accident insurance
company laptop and iPhone to support efficient work
employee wellbeing programs: discounted gym membership and other sports opportunities
working with a motivated, highly skilled senior expert and dynamic, helpful junior team
working with a wide selection of prestigious clients on exciting assignments
professional development opportunities to strengthen or broaden your expertise
hybrid and flexible working arrangements
opportunity to participate in volunteer activities throughout the year, with additional paid leave provided
active community life and regular team‑building events
What You would do at KPMG:
taking part in large business and IT projects as a Test Manager
planning and implementing test‑related processes and activities
preparing and implementing test strategies
creating test plans
supporting and coordinating preparation for tests (e.g. ensuring that test data, test environments and test cases/scenarios are available),
setting up and managing test cases and bugs in test management tools (e.g. Jira)
defining metrics for monitoring and controlling test activities
monitoring and reporting test progress and bug fixing to relevant stakeholders and forums
identifying, assessing and mitigating risks
handling escalations, resolving issues and ensuring timely actions
collaborating with other stakeholders and workstreams within projects
continuously improving and optimising test‑related processes
What brings you aboard:
a university or college degree in Economics with a strong interest in IT, and/or a degree in IT with a strong interest in Economics
3–5 years of relevant experience in an IT organization role, preferably as a Test Manager
a deep understanding of testing methodologies and hands‑on experience in applying them
experience in SAP testing (preferred)
practical experience with test management tools and preferably test automation tools
experience in test environment management
strong conceptual skills, including the ability to understand complex scenarios and develop creative solutions
strong English skills and native Hungarian proficiency (must‑have)
the ability and willingness to learn and deliver in a challenging environment
strong analytical and presentation skills
excellent communication and interpersonal skills
strong problem‑solving skills and creativity
strong client and stakeholder management skills and a proactive personality
