Brief description of the company
Who we are
We are a team of dedicated software developers, data scientists, telecom experts and business professionals. It is a workplace where you can influence important decisions, affecting international operators directly. Our Polystar team is growing quickly, and seek team members who want to grow with us. With an innovation mindset, we are working with novel tools, to create automation and analytics that give insight and solve customer, service, and network issues.
What we do
Polystar is a growing international software business that delivers automation, AI and analytics for network operators globally. Our vision is to provide Communication Service Providers (CSPs) with solutions that enable them to run completely self-driving networks. With the background of being Finland’s largest CSP, Polystar’s solutions are developed in a live network, with the benefits for network operators in mind.
