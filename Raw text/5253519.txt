We are looking for a Senior Cloud Engineer who can build and operate the technical foundation of the Open Telekom Cloud (OTC) Academy. As an engineer specializing on backend development, you develop APIs, services and data structures that support training paths, onboarding flows and platform automation. You work closely with the frontend developer, product teams and an offshore development team to deliver stable backend functionality and keep all services secure and reliable. The onboarding solution is based on ServiceNow CSM, so you need to understand how workflows, data models and integrations behave and ensure that backend logic supports these flows. Half of the role focuses on development, the other half on operations to maintain performance and stability.
What will you do?
Develop backend services, APIs and data models for learning paths, user progress and content delivery
Implement ServiceNow CSM and NEOS based related integrations such as case updates, workflow triggers and data exchange
Work with the offshore team to coordinate development tasks and maintain backend code quality
Support the automation of lab environments and platform components through IaC
Monitor backend performance and resolve issues that affect user flows or data accuracy
Manage CI/CD pipelines for backend services and support smooth release cycles
Ensure backend security, logging and audit readiness
You will succeed if you:
Experience in backend development with languages such as Python, Java or Node.js
Ability to design and build APIs and data structures for dynamic learning and onboarding flows
Understanding of ServiceNow CSM workflows, tables, data models and integration points
Experience with cloud environments, platform services or comparable enterprise systems
Familiarity with IaC tools and automation methods as well as the integration of NEOS based setups
Ability to coordinate with offshore developers and maintain consistent delivery quality
Experience with CI/CD pipelines, DevOps and troubleshooting backend issues
Strong problem-solving skills with attention to real user scenarios
Background in B2B or enterprise environments is helpful
