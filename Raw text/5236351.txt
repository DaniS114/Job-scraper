Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you excel at translating complex business needs into actionable technical solutions? We're seeking a talented IT Business Analyst to join our Finance Data CoE in Bratislava, where you'll play a crucial role in shaping our financial data landscape. This position offers the perfect blend of technical challenge and business impact, allowing you to leverage your analytical expertise to drive strategic initiatives that transform how our organization manages and utilizes financial data.
About the Role
In this position, you'll be instrumental in implementing our Finance Target Architecture using Azure Databricks while ensuring seamless data flows across our financial systems. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to capture requirements, design solutions, and deliver high-quality implementations that meet evolving business needs. This role requires someone who can navigate complex data models, communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders, and maintain a relentless focus on quality and efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
•	Configure the quarterly submission out of the Enhanced Finance Repository
•	Drive Finance IT business analysis towards the implementation of our Finance Target Architecture (Azure Databricks), enabling end-to-end Finance data flows
•	Work closely with business counterparts to capture accurate business needs and translate them into implementable work items in Azure DevOps
•	Team up with software development teams within the Data & Analytics Product Area and collaborate with other Finance IT teams
•	Analyze, manage, and document the as-is status of business/finance data and provide gap analysis
•	Specify source system data derivation rules with high quality, including proposing feasible solutions
•	Model data and integrate with the existing data landscape
•	Provide test data and execute the testing process in collaboration with implementation developers
•	Coordinate and support UATs, including creation/enhancement of training documentation
•	Monitor and update proposed solutions under changing assumptions
•	Support Go live and the establishment of the BAU process
•	Provide on-call support during quarterly financial close periods to ensure smooth data processing and reporting
About the Team
The Finance Data Center of Excellence (CoE) team is a vital part of the Finance Data Foundation Product Area. We optimize data strategy and technical architecture while minimizing waste through automation and building customer trust. Our team ensures end-to-end product vision coherence, shapes solutions for onboarding applications into the Finance Data Foundation landscape, and enables accurate reporting through reusable data assets. We provide subject matter expertise in Finance Data, manage Finance Data Models, and consult on implementing new data analytics capabilities.
