Responsibilities
Requirements elicitation:
Collaborate with business stakeholders to gather, challenge and document underlying business needs, translating these into scope, objectives and business goals for the Mortgage product
Requirements development and management:
Produce detailed scope, process and business requirements. Translate business requirements into features and functional requirements to allow Mortgage delivery team to develop technical solutions
Developing the Mortgage business:
Support the continuous development and improvement of the Mortgage product, processes, and related technology solutions
Solution assessment & validation:
Understand, validate and articulate the effects of proposed solutions. Engage with a diverse group of stakeholders including 3rd party suppliers to ensure the solution design meets business goals and the intended requirements
Solution implementation support:
Develop cross business and service user acceptance plans and communications to support implementation of solutions within the Mortgage programme
Change management and governance:
Understand the bank's change process, principles and approach. Apply this understanding and ensure solutions comply with the bank policy & regulatory requirements
Stakeholder management:
Engage a variety of stakeholders across business areas, owning and gathering specification input, delivering quality work and maintaining relationships. Act as a link between business and IT
Requirements
Educated to bachelor's degree
Several years practical experience of methodologies and techniques in Business and IT Analysis,
Experience in designing and documenting IT solutions
Experience in managing (supplier) relations within a professional environment
Good knowledge of Agile methodology as well as JIRA, Confluence, MS Office and MS Visio
Strong verbal and written communication skills in German and English
Banking background is a plus
