Responsibilities
As a member of the Automation Engineering Support Team,
the Software Engineer is responsible for the technical availability, optimization, and expansion of automation systems (DeltaV/Simatic/SIS) for buildings, production, and infrastructure facilities, considering internal and regulatory requirements. As a gatekeeper of the software system, standards are developed and implemented. The role also ensures that external software work is executed according to local standards.
Accountabilities:
Responsible for Emerson DeltaV process control system and bus and third-party systems
Creation and implementation of comprehensive technical automation concepts (Emerson DeltaV)
Development of libraries, engineering templates, DeltaV code, graphics, and recipes
Responsible for updating software documentation
Programming of control and visualization systems (DeltaV, SIS, Simatic S5/S7 advantageous)
Contact person for troubleshooting, recipe control, and program modifications in existing facilities
Support production in process optimization
Implementation of investment projects for DeltaV software from the Opex portfolio
Subject matter expert and recipient of DeltaV software work from Capex projects
Creation and compliance with DeltaV software specifications
Implementation of global DeltaV software standards at the Muttenz site and participation in cross-site collaborations
Support of Team Leader Automation and stakeholders; ensuring safety-relevant requirements in DeltaV; support of OSI-PI, Seeq, and alarm management
Requirements
Technical degree, preferably in Electrical/Automation or Chemical Process Engineering
Minimum 5 years experience in industrial automation, preferably in chemical/pharmaceutical industry and with DeltaV systems support
Languages: German and English written and spoken (French advantageous)
Good knowledge of MS Office and other standard programs, SAP-PM and COMOS knowledge advantageous
Comprehensive DeltaV knowledge (system architecture, coding, application) - mandatory (DeltaV training certificate); Siemens DCS and Simatic control advantageous
Familiarity with state-of-the-art technology, methodology, modern standards, and current trends in process automation
Proven experience in software development, troubleshooting, and problem-solving
Project experience in matrix organizations; project management advantageous
Basic undestanding of project management, documentation of technical changes, risk analyses, scope definition, URS creation, and implementation of DeltaV software projects
On-call duty readiness required
