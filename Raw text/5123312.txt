Embark on a thrilling tech adventure as our Field Support Specialist! You'll be the superhero of our local infrastructure, wielding your IT powers to maintain and optimize our digital realm. Get ready to dive into the exciting world of internal processes, where you'll craft reports that'll make quality metrics dance with joy!
What will you do?
Become the Ticket Master! Dive into the world of problem-solving, change-making, and incident-crushing. You'll be the ITSM wizard, conjuring solutions that'll make our internal customers cheer!
Play tech Santa for our newcomers! Prepare and maintain shiny new hardware that'll make their eyes light up like Christmas morning.
Channel your inner software sorcerer! Install OS images, anti-virus shields, and other magical software to keep our digital kingdom safe and sound.
Be the App Whisperer! Maintain and troubleshoot applications like a tech therapist, soothing their digital woes.
Don your lab coat for some hardware and architecture testing! It's like being a mad scientist, but with fewer explosions (we hope).
You will succeed if you:
Have Tech Superpowers
Hardware Whisperer: Basic ability to communicate with laptops and servers
Microsoft Sorcerer: Novice-level spells for Microsoft software products
Network Ninja: Beginner skills in DNS, DHCP, LAN, and MAN (no capes required)
Active Directory Apprentice: Just starting your journey into the realm of AD
Antivirus Alchemist: Mixing up basic digital potions to keep the bad guys at bay
Have Secret Identity Skills
Communication Charisma: Ability to charm both humans and machines
Customer Service Superpower: Making users feel like VIPs in our tech universe
Project Management Magic: Juggling tasks like a circus performer (without dropping the balls)
