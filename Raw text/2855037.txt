Responsibilities
Senior DevOps Engineer 80 - 100 % (w/m/d)
For more than 70 years, Hamilton Medical has shaped the future of mechanical ventilation. We develop intelligent ventilation solutions that support clinicians in critical moments.
Our HAMILTON ventilators are used in intensive care units, during emergency transport, and in MRI environments. They help medical teams care for adult, pediatric, and neonatal patients worldwide.
Here, your work has real impact. Every improvement you make supports better respiratory care for patients around the globe.
How you can make a difference:
Design, build, and operate a platform where the source code repository is the single source of truth
Manage infrastructure and applications in a fully declarative manner
Implement and maintain staged deployments (Dev ? Test ? Prod) with automated validation
Package internal and external software in a reproducible and traceable way
Build and evolve CI pipelines with a strong focus on validation and traceability
Write and automate system-level and integration tests
Develop tooling to migrate, validate, and manage data
Provide reproducible development environments, including isolated service landscapes for developing and testing new services
Act as a technical partner to product development teams across the organization, supporting them in adopting new tools and processes
That's what you bring along:
Senior-level experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, or Site Reliability Engineering
Strong experience and proven ability to work with declarative systems
Solid Linux expertise
Experience designing, building, and operating CI/CD pipelines
Familiarity with functional or immutable system design concepts
Strong security and reliability mindset
Ability to work independently, make architectural decisions, and communicate clearly across teams
