Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Architecting complex AWS infrastructures and zero-day compliant landing zones
Understanding customers’ business problems and providing technology consulting to identify the best-fit solution
Owning the end-to-end solution as a key decision-maker and driving the delivery team
Describing infrastructures using Terraform (Terragrunt) or AWS CloudFormation
Migrating customer workloads from on-prem/GCP/Azure/Heroku environments
Reviewing and remediating customers’ cloud solutions applying AWS Well-Architected Framework’s principles and best practices
Designing, building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines in multiple integration and test environments
Staying up-to-date with the AWS innovations and achieving Professional/Specialty level certifications based on personal interest
Contributing to AWS Competency and Service Delivery Program to support SnapSoft’s AWS Partner journey
Requirements
A bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
Technical leadership experience for coordinating projects from a technical side
Client facing skills for driving refinements and planning sessions with the customer
4+ years of hands-on experience in designing, deploying, and managing cloud infrastructure using AWS
In-depth knowledge of standard AWS services such as VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, ECS, IAM, SNS, SQS, API Gateway, EKS, CloudFront, EFS/EBS, SecurityHUB, Guard Duty, CloudWatch, AWS Application Migration Service, KMS, PCA and similar
3+ years of experience in at least one Infrastructure as a Code technology like Terraform (preferred), AWS CloudFormation, Ansible, AWS CDK or similar
3+ years of experience in building and managing CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab, Bitbucket or similar)
2+ years of experience in creating and running container-based applications using Docker and Kubernetes/EKS
Strong understanding of networking concepts (TCP/IP, DNS, load balancers, firewalls, VPN) and related AWS Services (CloudFront, API Gateway, VPC, Route 53, ELB)
Strong understanding of security concepts (IAM, PoLP, Access Control, Endpoint Security, Data Encryption)
Excellent problem-solving skills for use-case-specific architecture design and implementation
Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work effectively in a team environment
AWS Professional/Specialty Certification is preferred

Company info
SnapSoft is a company performing end-to-end delivery from architecture design to implementation, constructing the cloud infrastructure of international companies, mainly in fintech, aviation, telco and edtech industries. With expertise, they help customers achieve and maintain robust availability, resilience and scalability in their cloud-based products while ensuring their infrastructure's security and compliance.
