Information about the position
️  We are seeking a Microsoft Security Expertto join our virtual Cyber Security team. This is a unique opportunity to work hands-on with Microsoft’s most advanced security capabilities, including Copilot for Security and MXDR, going well beyond a standard E5 implementation.
️   The successful candidate will own the implementation, configuration, and operational support of the Microsoft E5 security stack, with a strong emphasis on Microsoft Purview, Microsoft Defender, and Microsoft Sentinel. This is a hands-on role working closely with our CISO, IT, and security teams to maximize the value of our Microsoft security investments.
️  Who we are? https://www.cme.net/
️  Key Responsibilities:
• Platform Ownership:Act as the subject matter expert for Microsoft E5 security, designing, implementing, and continuously improving security controls across our cloud services.
• Microsoft Purview: Implement and operate Purview for data security, including data classification, DLP, and Insider Risk Management, and support related incident response.
• Microsoft Defender:Manage the full Microsoft Defender suite (Endpoint, Office 365, Identity, Cloud Apps), tuning detections and collaborating with the SOC on threat response.
• Microsoft Sentinel: Support the operational use of Microsoft Sentinel (SIEM/SOAR) by assisting with analytics rules, automation, and threat hunting activities.
• Collaboration & Governance:Work with security and IT teams to align tools and processes, ensure configurations meet compliance requirements (GDPR, NIS2), and act as a trusted advisor on Microsoft security best practices.
