Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the Role
You will work as part of a trusted and experienced team to drive innovation across on-prem, private cloud, and public cloud environments, with a strong focus on automation, stability, security, and cost efficiency. The team builds and operates self-service database platforms business units, enabling reliable and scalable database services at enterprise scale.
The Database as a Service (DBaaS) squad is looking for a Database Engineer who can take technical ownership of database platforms and actively shape their long-term evolution. The role combines engineering, operations, and platform responsibility, working on backlog items within an agile setup to deliver predictable and sustainable outcomes.
Operational on-call coverage is limited (approximately once every two months and decreasing as the team grows) and applies only to high-priority incidents. The team follows a “you build it, you run it” philosophy and actively strives to automate operational activities wherever possible.
Responsibilities
- Take technical ownership of enterprise and mission-critical database platforms
- Influence architecture, HA/DR strategies, and operational standards
- Act as a technical point of contact for complex database topics and escalations
- Support and guide other engineers through technical challenges
- Work closely with international and geographically distributed teams
- Share knowledge, ideas, and best practices across the team
- Maintain a strong customer orientation and understanding of business requirements
- Proactively identify risks, issues, and opportunities during backlog refinement
- Coordinate with your team to support hybrid working arrangements
- Operate with integrity and strong ethical standards
