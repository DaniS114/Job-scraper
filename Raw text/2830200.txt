Responsibilities
Support Our Test Automation
You'll work with our development teams to build and maintain automated tests in Playwright. Whether it's implementing new tests, updating existing ones, or helping debug issues, you'll provide hands-on support wherever test automation is needed.
Keep Our Tests Documented
You'll document test cases in Gherkin format so everyone can understand what's being tested. You'll help maintain clear, up-to-date documentation that reflects our actual test coverage.
Learn While You Work
You'll collaborate with experienced developers who'll guide you as you learn modern test automation practices. You'll gain practical experience with Playwright, CI/CD pipelines, and frontend testing while balancing your studies.
Requirements
Currently enrolled at a German university with at least 12 months remaining in your studies
Available for at least 12 months and 20h/week
1+ year of experience with TypeScript/JavaScript
Genuinely interested in software testing and excited to dive deeper into Playwright
Proactive learner who asks questions and seeks to understand, not just complete tasks
Structured working style and able to work independently on clear assignments
English communication skills (German is a bonus)
Nice to have:
Experience with Playwright, React, Git, or CI/CD pipelines
Familiarity with test management in Jira
Knowledge of accessibility (A11y) best practices or web performance optimization
