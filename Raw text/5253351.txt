Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Unlock the Power of Data with Us! In a world where data is ubiquitous, we're seeking talented professionals to provide the structure and processes that unleash the full potential of our inhouse data, powering cutting-edge AI-based applications. Join our dynamic team and help shape the future of data governance!
About the Role
In this role, you will be using a combination of technical expertise and leadership skills to:
• Drive engineering excellence by defining and support of coding & architectural best practices
• Design and develop specialized upskilling programs for data, ML, and AI domains
• Create internal tools used by technical teams to improve and monitor project development processes and quality
• Conduct independent technical reviews for projects and provide architectural guidance to development teams
• Foster a culture of data & engineering quality through stakeholder engagement, training, and consulting
• Guide engineering teams in implementing data governance policies and collaborate on policy creation
• Work closely with internal Tech and Business stakeholders at all levels to integrate quality standards
• Stay current with the latest technology trends and regulatory changes impacting data & engineering quality
About the Team
You'll join a diverse and impactful team that sits at the intersection of AI technology, policy, and engineering excellence. As the global focus point of data governance in the company, we shape how Data & AI is governed across the organization, ensuring our data landscape is discoverable, up-to-date, and follows engineering best practices. We support hybrid and international collaboration, working closely with engineering teams across locations including Zurich, Bratislava, Bangalore, and beyond.

Personality requirements and skills
We understand that nobody meets 100% of requirements. If you meet some of the criteria below and are genuinely curious about the world of data engineering and governance, we'd be delighted to connect with you.
We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements:
• Degree in Computer Science, Information Management, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field, or equivalent experience. We welcome different levels, including graduates.
• Knowledge of Python and/or TypeScript for backend and/or full-stack tool development.
• Ambition to promote software engineering best practices in production grade data ingestion and transformation pipelines.
• Commitment to staying updated with the latest industry trends, technologies, and regulatory changes.
These are additional nice to haves:
• Experience in distributed computing/databases (e.g., Spark)
• Experience in building/designing data processing pipelines and data dashboards
• Interest in working closely with data engineers, data scientists, and domain experts
• Experience engaging with stakeholders at all levels, including senior management and external partners
• Drive to promote adherence to data governance policies and actively participate in defining these policies
If you don't have some or any of these qualifications but are eager to learn, we still encourage you to get in contact with us!
