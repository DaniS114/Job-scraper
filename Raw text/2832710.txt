Responsibilities
Lead strategic and operational projects from initial requirements to deployment
Serve as a primary point of contact for project-related communication and facilitate meetings, manage expectations, and act as a “translator” between technical and non-technical teams
Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand their goals, processes, and challenges
Translate these business needs into clear, detailed, and actionable technical requirements and functional specifications
Work with the product owner and solution architects to design effective technical solutions
Develop and execute test cases to validate that delivered solutions meet the specified requirements
Manage project planning, timelines, and provide regular status updates to stakeholders
Create and maintain comprehensive documentation, including use cases and process flows to ensure a shared understanding across all teams
Requirements
3+ years of experience as a Business Analyst in a tech environment.
Strong project and process management skills incl. collaborating with cross-functional teams
Solid understanding of business processes and the ability to translate them into technical requirements
Strong analytical skills, with a keen eye for detail and the ability to perform data analysis and solve complex problems
Strong technical curiosity and understanding of how systems and architectures work
Excellent communication skills in English (written and spoken)
