Job description, responsibilities and duties
As an IT Business Analyst, you will bridge the gap between business needs and technology solutions. Your primary focus will be to analyze processes, define requirements, and ensure the successful delivery of systems and functionality that drive business value. You will collaborate with stakeholders, configure SaaS/COTS applications, and support testing and adoption to enable efficient, scalable solutions.
This is maternity cover role.
Key Responsibilities
Lead discovery sessions to capture business processes, requirements, and improvement opportunities.
Perform gap analysis and recommend actionable solutions aligned with business objectives.
Translate business needs into clear functional specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
Model processes using BPMN and define future-state workflows.
Coordinate and execute application testing to ensure quality and compliance.
Configure and optimize SaaS/COTS applications to support business processes.
Develop user documentation and training materials for smooth adoption.
Provide operational support, troubleshoot issues, and perform root cause analysis to maintain system stability.
Monitor system performance and proactively recommend enhancements.
