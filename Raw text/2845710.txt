Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Architect and implement end-to-end cloud data solutions, including data lakes, data warehouses, and real-time analytics platforms.
Design, build, and troubleshoot data acquisition pipelines from databases, flat-files, and REST APIs.
Apply medallion architecture (bronze/silver/gold) for scalable, secure solutions.
Design star schema dimensional models and implement semantic models optimized for Power BI analytics.
Configure incremental refresh and partitioning strategies to optimize performance.
Apply and promote best practices in governance, scalability, and deployment standards.
Implement RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) with a zero-trust security approach.
Troubleshoot pipeline failures, query performance issues, and semantic model errors using monitoring tools and optimization techniques.
Create and maintain technical documentation, data flow diagrams, and mapping documents.
Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior team members.
Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Requirements
5+ years’ experience in data engineering, databases, or software development.
3+ years’ experience with Python and/or SQL for data engineering.
Proven expertise with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP).
Strong experience with dimensional modeling, SCDs, and medallion architecture.
Solid problem-solving skills including troubleshooting performance issues and optimizing queries.
Track record of delivering complex ELT/ETL projects in agile environments.
Ability to create prototypes to meet evolving business needs.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
What we offer
Competitive base salary (up to $135k, depending on experience).
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision benefits.
401K plan with full employer contribution.
Training and continuous learning opportunities.
Wellness programs, fitness perks, and a collaborative work environment.
Hybrid/onsite flexibility in Indiana with strong team culture and support.
