Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Artificial intelligence (AI) and data science are a one of major pillars of Arval's strategy. You will contribute to Arval's growth by identifying use cases that bring value to different functions (sales, marketing, operations, finance, risks, etc.) and implementing them with AI methodology.
What will be Your responsibilities ?
Understand business needs and identify opportunities for analytics, automation, and machine‑learning solutions
Analyze, prepare, and explore data using Data Hub and approved data sources
Extract meaningful insights from internal and external datasets to support operational and strategic decision‑making
Build predictive models (classification, regression, forecasting) and pattern‑detection models (clustering, dimensionality reduction)
Develop clean, well‑documented, production‑ready Python code with focus on quality, interpretability, and maintainability
Ensure model interpretability, monitor performance, detect bias, and validate alignment with business needs
Collaborate with IT, business stakeholders, and data experts to continuously improve analytical solutions
Solve business issues using advanced analytics and machine learning techniques
Contribute to Arval’s data‑driven strategy and follow best practices in data management, data protection, and AI governance
Stay up to date with emerging Data Science methods and tools
Technical Requirements
Python (advanced) – model development, data processing, production‑grade code
ETL processing in Dataiku – design and build data pipelines
Domino – model development, experimentation, and model management
GitLab – version control, documentation, CI/CD workflows
MS Office (especially Excel) and BI tools – visualization and presentation of insights
Compliance with internal standards – AI governance, data protection, model‑risk management
We are looking for candidates with:
Engineering degree or an equivalent university degree (Master's level) with a specialization in Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics, Applied Statistics
Expertise in Machine Learning
Proficiency in Python Data/ML libraries focused on data analysis and machine learning
Strong analytical skills and attention to details
Good communication and presentation skills
Fluent English
