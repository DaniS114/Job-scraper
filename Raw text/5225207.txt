Team description
Application & System Manager
for the
ADB (Auftragsdatenbank)
application, responsible for agile product integration, operational stability, and lifecycle management.
The role acts as the central coordination point between business, customers, and IT, ensuring requirements are implemented, services are stable, and the application is continuously operated and improved.
What will you do?
Coordinate communication with all involved parties (i.e. Dynamic platform services, Service delivery manager, Development, customer, Telekom IT application operation, etc.) of the application
Overlook quality aspects of operation (performance, availability, stability, security)
Perform verification of critical business impact during incidents (major incidents calls, operational stability calls)
Responsible for quality assurance in terms of defined key performance indicators according to service level agreement
Plan provision of resources incl. order process management
Prepare rules and regulations for operating processes (incident/change/problem management)
Ensure the use of CITSD-compliant software
Bear commercial responsibility for budget- plan and monitor budget
Make commercial assessment of actual, planned and forecast figures based on technical concept regulations
Organize and lead application expert group or similar service meetings
Plan and steer deployment installations
Coordinate Release Container with all stakeholders
Coordinate und perform “Antest” of the deployments after Release Container
Submit operational interests in D2O- demand to operation and D2S-demand to solution
Update application-related data in SACM systems (CAPE, Darwin)
Support Risk and Escalation Management
Report and monitor PRIO1 issues
On Call duty support
