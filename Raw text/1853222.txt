Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner, Hungary's largest agricultural machinery manufacturing company, is looking for new colleagues for Agile Quality Engineer (Mobile – iOS & Android) position.
Act as a quality advocate within the mobile team, helping shape stories,acceptance criteria, and early risk discussions.
Collaborate closely with mobile developers to test features continuously, not just at the end.
Build and maintain strong working relationships with web development teams, aligning mobile testing with shared business logic and APIs.
Design, execute, and continuously improve exploratory, functional, and non-functional tests for mobile applications.
Contribute to the continuous improvement of testing strategies, tools, and team processes.
Support test automation efforts where they add value, working closely with developers and CI/CD pipelines.
Analyse customer defect reports to identify recurring issues, root causes, and improvement opportunities.
Provide fast, actionable feedback that helps the team learn and improve.
Actively participate in agile ceremonies (refinement, planning, reviews, retrospectives).
Continuously learn and introduce modern testing practices into the team.
Elvárások / Requirements
Strong quality mindset and a clear drive for personal improvement.
Solid experience as a Quality Engineer in agile, product-oriented teams.
Hands-on experience testing mobile applications on iOS and Android.
Experience with test automation for mobile apps, APIs, and integrations.
Good understanding of mobile-specific risks (devices, OS versions, performance, usability, connectivity).
Experience working closely with developers and QA engineers from other teams rather than in isolation.
Proven ability to build strong cross-team relationships, especially with web and backend teams.
Deep understanding of agile testing principles, including:
○ Whole-team ownership of quality
○ Shift-left testing
○ Continuous feedback and learning
Ability to clearly communicate testing insights to both technical and non-technical
stakeholders
