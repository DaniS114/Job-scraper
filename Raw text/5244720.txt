Job description, responsibilities and duties
Design and implement scalable data architectures that meet the organization’s expanding data demands, while building and maintaining backend web services, mission-critical ingestion pipelines, and automated data workflows.
Take ownership of systems within a cloud-first data ecosystem, contributing efficiently to production-grade codebases and continuously monitoring, troubleshooting, and optimizing pipelines to ensure high availability and strong performance.
Develop and enhance core datasets within a SQL-based data warehouse, and create new data solutions tailored to both internal teams and external clients. Ensure data accuracy and reliability through comprehensive validation, testing, and monitoring practices.
Work closely with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into clear technical specifications and actionable execution plans. Additionally, mentor and support team members, fostering their technical expertise and professional development.
