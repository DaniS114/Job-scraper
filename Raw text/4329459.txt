Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for Linux enthusiasts to join an exciting automotive project in Stuttgart, Germany. As part of a specialized team of DevOps engineers, you will be responsible for maintaining the customer’s global R&D Linux infrastructure.
Approximately 98% of the systems run on Ubuntu LTS, followed by AlmaLinux.
Your main responsibilities will include:
• Managing client and backend systems using SaltStack, GHE, Jira, and Jenkins, while adhering to Infrastructure as Code (IaC) methodologies and best practices
• Collaborating with our offshore support team to oversee day-to-day operations using Prometheus, InfluxDB, Grafana, and ServiceNow-based reporting
• Planning and implementing infrastructure-related maintenance, updates, and upgrade tasks
• Supporting the rollout of new solutions
• Onboarding new internal RD and non-RD/global clients
