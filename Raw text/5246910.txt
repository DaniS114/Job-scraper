Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are seeking a seasoned professional to orchestrate complex projects that bridge the gap between logistics infrastructure and IT solutions. In this role, you will be the driving force behind operational efficiency and strategic growth.
Core Responsibilities
End-to-End Project Lifecycle:
Oversee all stages of project management, from initial conception and resource planning to execution, performance tracking, and formal closure.
Supply Chain Transformation:
Lead optimization initiatives, including the strategic launch of new sites and the modernization of existing operations.
IT System Integration:
Coordinate IT-centric projects in collaboration with the IT Manager, ensuring seamless upgrades and continuous system improvement.
Performance & Optimization:
Proactively identify operational bottlenecks, analyze data patterns, and implement lean solutions to enhance productivity.
Governance & Reporting:
Maintain rigorous control over project budgets, timelines, and scope while ensuring full compliance with ISO standards and legal regulations.
