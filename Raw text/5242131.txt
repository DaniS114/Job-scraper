Senior Automation Engineer (Agentic AI focus)
To our Smart Automation Team of digital experts, we are searching for a colleague who will join us on our digitalization journey and will help us design and build the next generation of automation solutions using RPA, AI and Agentic AI technologies.
You will work with cutting-edge concepts, explore new AI capabilities, and help shape our future automation architecture. If you enjoy innovation, hands-on development, and solving real business problems together with great colleagues, we would be happy to welcome you to our team.
Contract type
full-time
Place of work
Bratislava, Slovak Republic
Start date
ASAP
Wage (gross)
From 2 900 EUR/month
About
the position
Analyse business needs and design smart automation solutions, combining RPA, AI, and emerging agentic technologies.
Take full ownership of End-to-End delivery: requirement analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and support.
Develop agentic AI prototypes, autonomous workflows, and intelligent assistants.
Apply and enhance our automation solution portfolio (e.g. Blue Prism / ABBYY / ChatGPT and other AI tools).
Participate in creating our future AI architecture, standards, guidelines, and best practices.
Drive research and development: evaluate new technologies, test emerging frameworks, and create reusable components for internal use.
Implement improvements that increase process efficiency, accuracy, and scalability.
Provide expert consulting for automation and AI topics, including training sessions for internal stakeholders.
Build positive, lasting relationships with internal customers and support them on their digitalization journey.
Contribute to complex projects and support the team with technical expertise in AI.
Contribute to DevOps/MLOps tasks, including monitoring, optimization, and continuous improvement of AI solutions.
We work in a HYBRID STYLE of working with the possibility of home office, but twice a week we meet in the office for meetings.
The published salary is the minimum possible offer. The starting salary may be higher depending on the extent of fulfillment of the employee's requirements (education, language skills, required practice, personality assumptions and skills).
