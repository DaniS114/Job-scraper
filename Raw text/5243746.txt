Procurement Data Steward – Master Data
We are looking for a detail-oriented Data Steward to ensure the accuracy, consistency, and reliability of procurement master data across our systems.
In this role, you will drive data standards, support procurement processes with high-quality data, and collaborate closely with key stakeholders to enable effective sourcing decisions and reliable reporting. Acting as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for procurement data-related topics, you will contribute to data governance, process improvement initiatives, and the continuous enhancement of the procurement data model.

About
the position
Key Responsibilities:
Guard procurement data quality, consistency, and accuracy across all systems, ensuring reliable use in S2C/P2P processes and reporting.
Define, maintain, and evolve procurement data standards, attribute definitions, naming conventions, classification rules, and validation logic.
Develop and maintain the procurement data model, including attribute structures and cross‑object dependencies.
Coordinate master data updates and corrections with the master data team and ensure timely, high‑quality execution.
Serve as SPOC for procurement data issues; analyze root causes, identify gaps, and coordinate fixes with IT and master data teams.
Identify process improvement opportunities and support digitalization and automation initiatives in procurement data management.
Ensure procurement data readiness for reporting and analytics, including definition of required attributes and data structures.
Collaborate with Category Managers to align master data with category strategies and ensure cross‑process consistency.
Support creation and maintenance of procurement data documentation, rulebooks, guidelines, and process descriptions.
Conduct data validation checks, identify inconsistencies or gaps, and enforce data governance requirements.
Work with SAP MM and (ideally) SAP MDG‑M to support data‑related changes, testing, and system improvements.
Contribute to cross‑functional cooperation with procurement, analytics, IT, P2P, and governance teams.
