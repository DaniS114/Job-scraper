Job description, responsibilities and duties
We’re looking for a skilled Frontend Developer to join our team where you’ll work on technology shared across the entire SYNOT Games portfolio — more than 200 games, live in 30+ countries, enjoyed by millions of players worldwide.
This is not a single-project role. Your work will directly impact all our games, shaping the foundations they’re built on. You’ll contribute to our game engine, internal tools, and shared libraries, and collaborate closely with developers, designers, testers, and product teams.
If you enjoy writing clean, scalable code, care about performance, and want your work to have real, visible impact at scale, you’ll feel right at home here.
What you’ll be working on
Developing and maintaining the frontend core of our web-based games using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript
Building shared features and libraries used across our entire game portfolio
Participating in the full software development lifecycle — from design and implementation to testing and bug fixing
Collaborating with frontend and backend developers, graphic designers, testers, and product managers
Contributing estimates and helping plan development work
Communicating progress, challenges, and ideas with the team and management​​
