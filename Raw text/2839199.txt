Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries and employs over 30,000 professionals. The firm is committed to helping organizations improve performance and achieve lasting impact through data-driven insights and innovative solutions.
