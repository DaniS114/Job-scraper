Responsibilities
Build impactful products: Develop and deliver high-quality solutions that exceed client expectations.
Collaborate internationally: Work in a multicultural environment with global clients.
Contribute to quality: Share your knowledge through code reviews and continuously improve codebases.
Requirements
2+ years of experience with React and TypeScript.
Solid understanding of Clean Code, OO programming, and SOLID principles.
Experience with Unit Testing, Integration Testing, and exposure to TDD.
Proficiency in modern web standards (HTML5, CSS) and Git.
Active English proficiency and a valid Hungarian work permit.
Nice-to-have
Bonus points for experience with NestJS, Flutter, or Elixir., Open-source contributions or experience with Sass, PostCSS, CI/CD or E2E testing., Familiarity with Agile practices like Scrum or Extreme Programming., Be part of a team that’s shaping the digital landscape with your skills and ideas., Enjoy a workplace that combines fun and productivity – from movie nights to enriching team events., Advance your career in a company that invests in your personal and professional growth.
What we offer
Innovative technologies: Work with React and TypeScript in an agile, polyglot environment.
Flexibility: Enjoy remote work, flexible hours, and self-management opportunities.
Growth opportunities: Courses, conferences, coaching, and mentoring junior developers.
Supportive team: Shared breakfasts, team-building events, office massages, and a multicultural environment.
Health benefits: Premium health insurance for your peace of mind.
How to apply
You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
