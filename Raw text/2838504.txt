Senior Security Engineer / DevSecOps EngineerAbout Mettler ToledoMETTLER TOLEDO is a global leader in precision instruments and services. We are renowned for innovation and quality across laboratory, process analytics, industrial, product inspection, and retailing applications. Our sales and service network is one of the most extensive in the industry. Our products are sold in more than 140 countries, and we have a direct presence in approximately 40 countries. For more information, please visit .Our Opening and Your ResponsibilitiesOur Software Development Center (SWDC) engages in the research, design, and development of software applications across all divisions. We are looking for a passionate and experienced security expert who thrives in a dynamic environment and enjoys tackling challenging problems. For our global operating headquarters in Zurich, Greifensee, we are looking for a motivated:Senior Security Engineer / DevSecOps Engineer 100% (all genders)In this role, your responsibilities would include:You lead the design, implementation, and maintenance of DevSecOps pipelines and infrastructure.You integrate security practices seamlessly into the CI/CD process, including automated security testing and vulnerability management.You collaborate closely with development, security, and operations teams to promote a culture of shared responsibility for security and quality.You drive the adoption of best practices, tools, and frameworks related to DevSecOps across the organization.You identify risks, provide mitigation strategies, and ensure compliance with industry standards and regulations.You monitor system performance and security incidents while implementing necessary corrective measures.You develop and perform project-specific security risk assessments together with local subject matter experts.You review feature designs to identify potential security flaws in technical designs involving C#, JavaScript (Vue), SQL Server, Kubernetes, Docker, and Azure DevOps.What You Need to SucceedFollowing requirements are necessary for this role:You possess a Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related technical field.You have at least 5 years of experience in application security, DevSecOps, or risk management.You have deep expertise in security practices, including threat modeling, vulnerability assessment, and incident response.You possess additional certifications such as CISSP or CISM (or you are highly motivated to obtain them).You have excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset, enabling you to act as a bridge between technical teams and security stakeholders.You are fluent in English, and German is a significant advantage.Our Offer to YouFlexible working hours (depending on the role), a hybrid work model,
