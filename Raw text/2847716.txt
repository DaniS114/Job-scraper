Responsibilities
Review SoC solution architectures and develop micro-architecture specifications
Implement RTL designs, identify and fix bugs, perform design checks, generate power intent, and support implementation constraint creation
Collaborate with verification teams to review test plans and debug design issues
Work closely with performance and power analysis teams to evaluate and optimise system efficiency
Continuously improve design methodologies to drive innovation and efficiency
Support project management activities including planning, scheduling, and progress tracking
Requirements
Hands-on experience in RTL design for complex SoCs using Verilog and/or SystemVerilog
Understanding of static design checks, synthesis, timing analysis, power management, and clock domain crossing
Exposure to full design lifecycle: concept, specification, implementation, power & performance analysis, optimisation, testing, documentation, and support
Ability to produce clear specifications and documentation for complex designs
Familiarity with shell scripting or programming (Tcl, Perl, Python, etc.)
Nice-to-have
Experience with Arm-based designs or Arm System Architectures, Development and integration of subsystems such as PCIe, UCIe, DDR/LPDDR/HBM, Ethernet, High-level programming in C/C++ and/or assembly, Knowledge of embedded OS, device drivers, microprocessor architectures, and embedded system hardware

Company info
Arm’s Solution Engineering division develops cutting-edge SoCs for diverse application segments, leveraging the latest IP from Arm and other vendors. We are seeking a creative and driven SoC Design Engineer to join our Sheffield team and contribute to the specification, design, and verification of advanced SoC products. You will collaborate closely with dedicated engineers across Sheffield and Manchester, as well as multiple teams across Arm, to help define the next generation of compute solutions. This is a unique opportunity to bring innovation and expertise from across the semiconductor industry to a role that directly shapes the future of Arm devices.
