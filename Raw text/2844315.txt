Assist in the implementation of ServiceNow solutions, including system configuration and development of custom features
Support technical consulting and quality assurance activities

Understand business needs and help standardize processes in design and deliverables
Requirements
At least 2 years of experience in frontend development with a focus on JavaScript
ServiceNow CSA and Scripting in ServiceNow Fundamentals certificate / completed training
Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail
Fluent in English; knowledge of German is an advantage
Eagerness to learn and grow in a dynamic environment
Legal right to work in Hungary without requiring visa sponsorship
What we offer
Competitive compensation package including cafeteria budget, private health care, family-oriented benefits, AYCM card, and company shares at a discount price
Extensive home office opportunity and a nice office to meet with your team members
Wide range of leading-edge trainings, including all available ServiceNow certifications and a variety of language courses
Employee assistance program: Psychologist support, legal or financial advice

Accenture is a leading global professional services company and a recognized Global Elite Partner of ServiceNow with over 5500 professionals and over 17000 ServiceNow certifications.
