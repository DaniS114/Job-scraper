Responsibilities
Lead and mentor a team of developers, fostering a culture of excellence, collaboration, and innovation
Technical strategy: Drive architectural decisions, coding standards, and best practices across the stack
Work closely with product managers, designers, and stakeholders to define and deliver technical solutions that align with business objectives
Design, develop, and maintain scalable, high-performance web applications on both the front-end and back-end
Architect and implement complex systems to ensure scalability, reliability, and security
Conduct and lead code reviews, ensuring quality, performance, and maintainability across the platform
Develop and maintain automated tests, including unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
Collaborate with internal teams to identify feature requirements, enhance workflows, and deliver high-quality solutions
Take end-to-end ownership of features and projects, from concept to deployment and beyond, while maintaining high technical standards
Work seamlessly with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment between technical development and business goals
Requirements
Strong experience with modern frontend frameworks such as VueJS, SolidJS, ReactJS, or AngularJS, with a focus on TypeScript and JavaScript development
Advanced proficiency in Node.js with Express for building APIs and server-side applications, along with familiarity with MongoDB as a preferred database solution
Hands-on experience in designing scalable and distributed systems, leveraging tools like Docker, Terraform, and AWS Cloud for deployment and management
Proficient in working within a monorepo setup (e.g., Rushstack) and version control systems like GitLab for collaborative development
Excellent communication skills in English, with the ability to effectively articulate ideas, collaborate across teams, and contribute to a positive team culture
Demonstrated ability to work effectively in agile teams and collaborate across remote settings, fostering open communication and innovation
A strong sense of ownership and enthusiasm for embracing new technologies, solving complex technical challenges, and delivering high-quality results
What we offer
Great creative freedom within the secure background of a broad-based successful company, enabling you to pursue ambitious entrepreneurship, interdisciplinary networking and bold innovations
A wide range of benefits, such as subsidized meals at work, company pension allowance, bicycle leasing program, company health management, discounts at gyms, sabbaticals, exclusive employee discounts and much more
Free magazines and discounts on magazine subscriptions and over 300 brands in areas such as technology, fashion, sport and culture
Personal and professional development through a wide range of internal and external training opportunities and coaching
We take responsibility, contribute to the well-being of our society with our foundations and support you in your passion project with initiatives such as “Burda moves”
30 days of vacation, flexible working hours & work locations (by arrangement), up to 20 days of workation in other EU countries for your ideal work-life balance
Our employees are important to us: we offer you a permanent employment contract and encourage internal transfers
A smartphone and laptop for professional and private use
