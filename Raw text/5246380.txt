Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced
HRIT Project Manager
to lead the implementation of modern HR technology solutions. In this role, you will be responsible for managing projects from initiation through deployment, coordinating stakeholders, and ensuring successful delivery of HR IT initiatives. You will play a key role in aligning business needs with technology solutions while maintaining clear communication across teams.

Lead the implementation of new HR software and digital HR solutions
Plan, organize, and facilitate project kick-offs, workshops, and stakeholder meetings
Prepare, manage, and maintain project documentation and implementation blueprints

User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Track project milestones, timelines, risks, and deliverables to ensure successful completion
Serve as the main point of ownership for project execution and stakeholder communication
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including HR, IT, and external vendors
