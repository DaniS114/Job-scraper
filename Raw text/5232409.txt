Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are seeking a
motivated Full Stack Developer focused on PHP and Angular
to join our
eCommerce team
. eCommerce is a web-based portal used by customers of Siemens Healthineers to buy spare parts, components and services for their medical devices.
You will be a part of an international team of passionate people working on feature development, maintenance, and operational aspects of our eCommerce platform.
Key Responsibilities:
Development of new features using agile methodology
Maintain and enhance existing functionalities to ensure optimal performance
Work closely with cross-functional teams to align development efforts with business objectives
Ensure the application meet high standards of quality, security, and user experience
Required Technical Skills & Qualification:
Expertise in software development with a focus on web applications
Proficiency in PHP and TypeScript (preferably with Symfony and Angular frameworks)
Proven experience with MySQL and MariaDB
Practical knowledge with unit testing (PHPUnit, Karma, Jasmine)
Problem-solving skills and attention to detail
Ability to communicate effectively as a part of a team as well as work independently
