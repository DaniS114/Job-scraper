Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
We are working on a sales portal for a successful insurance company, which we built entirely from scratch. We engage with customers from eight different countries, both within Europe and beyond.
Currently we are expanding our Bratislava team and that’s the reason why we are searching for a Fullstack Developer.
What can you expect in this role?
You will work on web illustration system used for creation of the insurance.
Our team consists of 28 members (Developers, Testers/QA Engineers, Scrum Masters, Project Manager, ….). We are divided into four smaller independent teams, but we are used to meet at least twice a week in the office to make it easier to discuss things and share our knowledge and experience.
You’ll operate in an agile environment surrounded by other Fullstack Developer who are always ready to assist or advise.
Techstack:
The application is divided into several different modules/microservices.
You willl use technologies such as Kotlin, Java 11, Spring, REST API + DB technologies - Hibernate, QueryDsl, Liquibase, MS SQL.
Frontend is written in Angular 16. Some parts of the application still use the “old” frontend written in AngularJS, but we are currently migrating those to Angular 16.
Our role requires:
Working history using Kotlin/Java on commercial projects.
A solid grasp of other technologies mentioned above.
Experience with frontend technologies such as Angular, React, or TypeScript.
Coming to the office in Bratislava at least twice a week.
Ability to communicate in English.
