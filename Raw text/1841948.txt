Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner is bringing their impressive global success story to Hungary, building a brand-new Global Business Center in Budapest. They are looking for a
Data and Reporting Manager
to lead their Business Intelligence initiatives.
They offer a unique environment that prioritizes an inclusive culture and service excellence from day one. In this role, you will turn data into decision-making power within a collaborative, supportive, and social international team. It is a rare chance to join a community that values teamwork, celebrates success, and genuinely enjoys working together to build a fresh operation.
Business Intelligence & Reporting
Lead the design, development, and maintenance of enterprise-level Power BI dashboards.
Transform complex datasets into clear, actionable insights that support strategic business decisions.
Collaborate with business heads to define KPIs and reporting requirements.
Data Governance & Quality
Establish and enforce data governance standards to ensure data accuracy, consistency, and security.
Oversee data warehousing initiatives (e.g., Snowflake), ensuring structured and accessible data for analysis.
Identify and resolve data quality issues, championing a culture of data integrity.
Team Leadership & Strategy
Mentor and lead a team of data analysts, fostering technical growth and business acumen.
Drive the adoption of modern data analytics tools and practices (e.g., predictive analytics, AI-driven insights).
Elvárások / Requirements
Proven experience in leading Data/BI teams in an enterprise environment.
Expert-level proficiency in SQL and Power BI (DAX, data modeling).
Experience with modern cloud data platforms, specifically Snowflake or Azure Data Factory.
Strong understanding of Data Governance principles.
Business language fluency in English.
