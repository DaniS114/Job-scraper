Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the Master Data Management (MDM) Team
•	We are a team of expert business analysts and software engineers, spread across multiple locations around the globe, driving the MDM function at Swiss Re.
•	We are responsible for driving all stages of software development including business analysis, solution design, application architecture, development, testing, roll-out and operational platform management for nearly 1000 master and reference data sets.
•	We are the main contact for our internal clients and business partners for the MDM processes, technology platform and knowledge.
About the Role
•	You would be working with Senior data managers within the MDM team and internal business partners across the globe to analyse the business processes and requirements and translate them into functional and technical specifications for the development team.
•	Collaborating with various partners to define new processes, develop data models, drive the information governance frameworks, improve data quality and create maintain the data mapping specifications
•	You will be required to own and handle incidents, problems and change requests for the data assets under your ownership.
•	Work within a small squad to deliver assigned projects in the above-mentioned fields; also comfortable in working on technical, configuration and process related tasks independently.
•	Support software releases, including communication with clients and other interfacing applications/teams, coordination of user testing and training. Incorporate their feedback into the product backlog for constant improvements.
•	Communicate and collaborate with Senior data managers, data owners/leaders, enterprise architects, other application owners to ensure MDM conversations/topics find the right place in Swiss Re's technology and data landscape.

Personality requirements and skills
About You
•	You have a master's or bachelor's degree in information management, computer science, business administration or equal.
•	Over 5 years of working experience as Business Analyst or data analyst and solution design, preferably in a data function (MDM, Data-Warehouse, Data Governance, Meta-Data, Data Catalog etc.).
•	Marvelous social skills and a good grasp of the English language (spoken and written). Strong story telling skills (using MS PowerPoint etc.) are an added advantage!
