Company info
SEON is a company founded in 2017 with $187mn in funding and over 290 team members. SEON strives to help online businesses reduce the cost, time and challenges faced due to fraud. Whether serving global financial leaders or small eCommerce startups, SEON's solution simplifies fraud management so customers can focus on growing and scaling their companies. The company encourages open communication, transparency, and innovation, fostering a customer-obsessed, resilient and humble culture. SEON operates offices in the Americas, EMEA and Asia-Pacific, serving thousands of companies worldwide across multiple time zones.
