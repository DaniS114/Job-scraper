Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
At KPMG, we are committed to delivering innovative solutions to clients. Join our team as an SAP Analyst specializing in Finance & Controlling and help shape the future of SAP implementations.
As an SAP Analyst Finance & Controlling – Assistant, you will support senior consultants in delivering SAP Finance & Controlling solutions, focusing on optimizing financial operations for clients. You will be involved in various phases of SAP projects, from requirements gathering to post-deployment support.
Key Responsibilities:
Support the configuration and implementation of SAP Finance & Controlling solutions.
Assist in mapping client business processes and ensuring they align with SAP functionality.
Participate in solution testing, troubleshooting, and issue resolution.
Provide documentation support for SAP implementations, including configuration guides and training materials.
Collaborate with other SAP team members to deploy best practices in Finance & Controlling processes.
Learn and adapt to new SAP technologies and methodologies.
