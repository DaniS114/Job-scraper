Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Leading and mentoring cloud engineering teams, fostering collaboration, and ensuring delivery excellence
Providing direct line management, coaching, and professional development for a team of cloud engineers and migration specialists
Fostering a high-performing, technically excellent team focused on secure and scalable migrations
Owning the end-to-end solution as a key decision-maker and aligning customer needs with technical outcomes
Facilitating customer workshops, refinement sessions, and planning discussions to define clear delivery goals
Driving recruitment, onboarding, and performance management for your team
Championing engineering best practices, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement
Architecting and implementing complex AWS infrastructures and zero-day compliant landing zones
Reviewing and remediating customer solutions using the AWS Well-Architected Framework
Driving migrations from on-premises and other cloud providers (GCP, Azure, Heroku) into AWS
Designing, building, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines across multiple environments
Defining infrastructures as code with Terraform (Terragrunt preferred) or AWS CloudFormation
Staying up to date with AWS innovations and driving SnapSoft’s AWS Partner journey by contributing to Competency and Service Delivery Programs
Requirements
Excellent problem-solving skills for use-case-specific architecture design and implementation
Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to lead teams and engage customers effectively
Proven technical leadership experience in coordinating engineering projects and guiding teams
Strong client-facing skills to lead refinements, planning, and technology discussions
Experience in building and scaling engineering teams, including recruitment and talent development
Ability to influence stakeholders and align engineering outcomes with business goals
A master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
8+ years of experience in the field of software development
4+ years of hands-on AWS infrastructure design, deployment, and operations experience
In-depth knowledge of AWS services: VPC, EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, ECS, EKS, IAM, API Gateway, CloudFront, SecurityHub, GuardDuty, CloudWatch, KMS, PCA, and others
3+ years of experience with Infrastructure as Code tools (Terraform preferred, CloudFormation, Ansible, AWS CDK, etc.)
3+ years of experience in building and managing CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab, Bitbucket, or similar)
2+ years of experience with container-based applications (Docker, Kubernetes/EKS)
Strong knowledge of networking concepts (TCP/IP, DNS, VPN, load balancers, firewalls) and related AWS services
Strong understanding of cloud security concepts (IAM, PoLP, access control, encryption, endpoint security)
AWS Professional/Specialty Certification (preferred)
Nice-to-have
Node.js + AWS + Terraform course available during first month with opportunity to obtain AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate certifications

Company info
SnapSoft is a company that performs end-to-end delivery from architecture design to implementation, constructing the cloud infrastructure of international companies, mainly in fintech, aviation, telco, and edtech industries. With expertise, they help customers achieve and maintain robust availability, resilience, and scalability in their cloud-based products while ensuring their infrastructure's security and compliance.
