Responsibilities
Work with C-suite executives to problem-solve on their key issues and provide actionable recommendations leveraging technology know-how and business sense
Play an active role in all aspects of a client project, including gathering and analyzing information, formulating and testing hypotheses, and developing and communicating recommendations for client presentations
Present results to client management and advise on their implementation in collaboration with client team members
Work in teams of 3 to 5 consultants alongside experts and partners including developers, designers, data engineers, agile coaches and others
Work on projects across all industries and functions, integrated with the global firm
Help clients deliver breakthrough products, experiences, and businesses on technology and non-technology topics
Requirements
Degree (Bachelor or Master) in technical subjects (e.g., computer science, engineering, IT, hard sciences)
Up to 2 years of experience (internships or full-time) in technology/IT strategy roles
Knowledge in areas like digital transformation, IT architecture, IoT, e-commerce, AI, robotics, cloud, big data, analytics, or cybersecurity is advantageous
Outstanding track record of academic and professional achievements
Combination of strong strategic and analytical abilities with a passion for technology
Proven track record of leadership through extracurricular activities
Fluency in both English and Polish or Hungarian (depending on the location)
Willingness to travel

Exceptional training and frequent coaching and mentoring

A voice that matters from day one
Global community with colleagues across 65+ countries and over 100 nationalities

Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that brings together the best of McKinsey’s Tech & AI capabilities to help clients use technology to transform their businesses. The firm operates in over 65 countries with a diverse workforce of over 100 nationalities, committed to driving positive, enduring change in the world.
