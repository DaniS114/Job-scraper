Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Provide technical support for the internal quotation calculation system (NBA)
Analyze cases where a quote was not generated for a customer’s client
Execute and test Python scripts in a Jupyter Notebook environment using test data
Debug existing processes to identify the root cause of issues (not primarily bug fixing)
Evaluate input data and identify potential inconsistencies or errors
Communicate findings to the team (e.g., ST), including clear explanations of why a quote was not calculated
Collaborate on extending existing solutions (e.g., adding new conditions or modifying logic)
Work primarily in Python on a long-running system
The job will offer you opportunities to participate in international projects in Central and South-Eastern Europe (job location is Slovakia).
We are looking for people who share with us the passion that working with data and people is fun and a “mission”, not a “job”.
