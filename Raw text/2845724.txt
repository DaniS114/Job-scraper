Responsibilities
Architect and design cloud solutions across AWS, GCP, and Azure, ensuring scalability, performance, and security.
Collaborate with engineering and product teams to translate business needs into technical architecture.
Lead cloud migration and modernization projects, including infrastructure redesign and automation.
Define and implement best practices for cloud deployment, monitoring, and governance.
Support DevOps and Infrastructure teams in building reliable CI/CD pipelines and optimizing cost efficiency.
Prepare technical documentation, solution blueprints, and proposals for internal and client projects.
Evaluate emerging cloud technologies and recommend innovations aligned with business goals.
Work directly with customers to understand requirements and provide expert guidance on cloud strategies.
Requirements
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
5+ years of experience in cloud architecture, DevOps, or infrastructure engineering.
Proven hands-on experience with AWS, GCP, and/or Azure (multi-cloud background preferred).
Expertise with Infrastructure-as-Code tools (Terraform, CloudFormation, etc.).
Deep understanding of Kubernetes, Docker, and cloud networking principles.
Experience designing high-availability, cost-efficient architectures for SaaS or enterprise clients.
Strong communication skills with the ability to engage technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Cloud certifications (AWS Solutions Architect, GCP Professional Cloud Architect, or Azure Solutions Architect) are a plus.
