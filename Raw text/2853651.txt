Responsibilities
Assess and clarify Customer’s functional requirements related to the system/software jointly with business consultants
Develop and modify applications or part of the application, prepare related documentation
Work out and develop solutions, concepts, procedures, operational method or mathematical operation applied as the basis of the software interface
Test applications and systems, establish and maintain test environments
Implement and roll-out of hardware and software
Prepare technical documentation of the completed developments
Ensure and monitor the continuous and smooth operation of the software and hardware
Eliminate errors on the user side, modify data and write status reports on the resolution of incidents and problems
Engage in pre-sales activities: respond to requests for proposals and put together bids, prepare presentation materials and project plans
Coordinate, mentor and manage peers working in similar positions from professional point
Participate in the Solution Architecture of the overall Oracle Retail implementation
Execute against development plans and advises management when changes are needed
Lead technical requirements gathering, data conversion and integration design sessions
Create, design and implement the Oracle Retail batch schedule
Requirements
College degree in programming, software development or information technology
Strong written and spoken English
Development tools: Java, SQL, PL/SQL, Pro*C
Operation systems: Windows, Linux, Unix
Skills to cooperate with others/to work in teams, practice-oriented mind-set, proactivity, diplomacy, good organizing skills, accuracy, customer-oriented attitude, objectivity, imaginative thinking, creativity and agility
Application maintenance and support or system analysis is preferred
3+ years of experience in software development is preferred
Experience in providing consultancy services is an advantage
Technical and development background of Oracle Retail modules is advantage
