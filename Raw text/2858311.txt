Responsibilities
Take ownership of the end-to-end design process, from concept to final implementation, ensuring a seamless user experience.
Partner closely with Product Managers and Engineers to define and execute product design strategies that align with business objectives.
Conduct user research, usability testing, and data analysis to inform and validate design decisions.
Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity UI designs using Figma and contribute to the evolution of SEON’s design system.
Iterate rapidly based on user feedback and stakeholder input to refine product experiences.
Collaborate with engineering teams to ensure high-quality implementation and consistency across all product touchpoints.
Requirements
5+ years of experience in product design, preferably within SaaS or enterprise B2B environments.
A strong portfolio showcasing UX/UI design for complex, data-driven products.
Proficiency in Figma, including design systems and prototyping.
Experience conducting user research, usability testing, and data analysis to drive design decisions.
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to simplify complex workflows.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with experience working in cross-functional teams.
Familiarity with agile development processes and working closely with engineering teams.
Strong attention to detail and a systematic approach to design.
Experience designing mobile-first and responsive web applications.
English language proficiency at a professional level.
