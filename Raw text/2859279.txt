Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Defining requirements for reporting and analytical (incl. data science) products
Identifying and mapping source systems
Preparing detailed functional and data-level specifications for the Data Engineering team (incl. supporting data ingestion, transformation logic, and semantic model needs)
Creating dashboard wireframes
Supporting BI and data science teams during development
Supporting data validation and testing
Driving UAT
Ensuring data product readiness for go-live and adoption
Providing post-go-live support & stabilization (monitoring, end user support)
Requirements
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
Experience in requirements gathering and business process understanding
Ability to translate business needs into detailed functional and data specifications
Familiarity with data platforms, data ingestion, and transformation concepts
SQL knowledge or prior exposure to data exploration
Experience with source system analysis, data mapping, and documentation for Data Engineers
Experience designing dashboard wireframes or mock-ups
Nice-to-have
Prior BA experience in a data/analytics environment, Developer experience with BI tools (preferably Power BI), Experience with data modeling, Domain knowledge in aviation, commercial analytics, customer engagement, or pricing and revenue management, Experience supporting data science projects (feature definition, model validation, data preparation), Understanding of data governance, metric standards, and data lineage, Experience with data platform migration, report reverse engineering
Company info
Az Adatkezelő információ-technológiai szolgáltatással foglalkozó gazdasági társaság, amely a megbízók részére keres a megbízók által megadott feltételeknek megfelelő szakembereket informatikai projektek elvégzésére. E tevékenysége során az Adatkezelő kezeli az érintettek azon személyes adatait, amelyek az elküldött/feltöltött önéletrajzban kerültek megadásra, továbbá kezeli a jelölttel folytatott telefonbeszélgetést, és az ennek során esetlegesen megadott további személyes adatokat.
