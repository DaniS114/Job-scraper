Responsibilities
What you'll build
Production Planning and Detailed Scheduling (PP/DS) in SAP S/4HANA empowers companies to easily create feasible and optimized production plans and procurement proposals. PP/DS performs finite capacity planning, which means it considers real-world limitations and constraints, such as available machinery capacity or raw materials.
To accelerate the improvement and modernization of our solution, we are establishing a new development team in Walldorf following the Harmonized Product Operating Model.
As a developer, you will participate in all phases of application development.
Contribute to the design, development and maintenance of PP/DS in SAP S/4HANA
Collaborate with architects, product managers, and team members to translate business and technology requirements into solutions
Ensure code quality, scalability, and performance through best practices and code reviews
Leverage agile software engineering and cloud mindset to ensure we can deliver high-quality software continuously
Develop your own concepts and designs together with product management, UX designers and architects
Support the team by taking over additional technical or organizational tasks
Where you belong
Our organization is responsible for the development and enhancement of Production Planning and Detailed Scheduling (PP/DS) within SAP S/4HANA. You will join a dynamic new scrum team which will contribute to the success of SAP's solutions around Supply Chain Planning.
Requirements
What you bring
Required Skills
University degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
Solid programming skills in at least one language like ABAP, JAVA, C++, or similar (3 - 5+ years in this role)
Familiarity with agile methodologies and test automation
Analytical thinking, problem-solving attitude, and good communication skills
Growth mindset: eagerness to learn ABAP, RAP and HANA/CDS; proactive and accountable
Interest in technology and eagerness to learn and apply new skills
Language: English (fluent), German language skills are a bonus
Preferred Skills
Hands-on expertise: ABAP, RAP, CDS, OData, Fiori/UI5
Familiarity with supply chain management concepts, IBP, APO or similar products
Experience with development principles for cloud products
Experience with AI tools
What we offer
What's in it for you? Constant learning, skill growth, great benefits, and a team that wants you to grow and succeed.
In our collaborative environment, innovation and technical excellence are highly valued. This role offers opportunities for both personal and professional growth within a global organization. You will collaborate closely with other scrum teams and product management across various locations, including Walldorf, Budapest and Bangalore.
This position allows you to engage in the conceptualization, design, prototyping, and development of core PP/DS processes, providing you with a diverse and enriching work experience.
