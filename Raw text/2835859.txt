Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, implement, and operate scalable web applications and services based on Java and Spring – from idea and implementation to deployment, monitoring, and optimisation in production.
Work in a team to deliver features end-to-end, breaking work into incremental, shippable pieces and aligning with team and business goals.
Ensure high code quality through practices like Pair Programming, TDD, code reviews, and regular refactoring, documenting decisions and trade-offs where needed.
Apply DevOps principles: contribute to CI/CD pipelines (GitLab), use container-based deployments, monitor your services in production, and follow up on incidents and issues.
Share your knowledge with peers and actively reach out for feedback to grow together
Requirements
Several years of experience building scalable web applications using Java and Spring
Solid fundamentals in software engineering (Clean Code, object-oriented design, modern architectures such as microservices or service-oriented systems).
Hands-on experience in agile development (e.g. Kanban or Scrum), including breaking down tasks, estimating effort with guidance, and iterating based on changing scope and priorities.
Strong focus on quality and maintainability: automated tests, readable code, and familiarity with Git and CI/CD pipelines in a production environment.
Very good communication skills in English (spoken and written)
Preferred Qualifications
Practical experience with TDD, Pair Programming, and systematic code reviews as part of your daily workflow
Knowledge Experience in developing interfaces to Apps/Frontend
Leading small technical initiatives (e.g. refactoring, process or tooling improvements).
Strong interest in augmenting your daily professional life with AI – for example using AI to improve productivity, testing, and monitoring.
Good German language skills are a plus to collaborate smoothly with stakeholders and colleagues across the organisation.

Company info
With revenues of more than 375 million Euros and nine apps, ParshipMeet Group is one of the three leading international Dating and Video companies. Our more than 500 employees help users all over the world to meet others – whether it's like-minded people, an exciting date, or love for life. True to the motto: Meet – Date – Fall in Love.
As a Java Engineer (all genders) you will work on our matchmaking products Parship, ElitePartner and eharmony. You will be part of a cross-functional engineering team and take responsibility for designing, implementing, and evolving features that our customers use daily. We develop and operate our platforms ourselves, work in an agile Kanban setup, and value ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
