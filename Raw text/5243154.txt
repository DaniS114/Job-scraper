Job description, responsibilities and duties
The Senior System Administrator is responsible for operating, configuring, and maintaining Kubernetes-based infrastructure and cloud environments while supporting international customers and internal IT systems.
Key Responsibilities:
Administer and configure Kubernetes clusters
Manage and maintain AWS cloud infrastructure (primarily Kubernetes-related)
Develop and modify automation scripts using Terraform and Bash
Deploy new environments and support system deployments
Troubleshoot hardware, configuration, and infrastructure incidents
Maintain servers and internal infrastructure
Apply server and client patches
Manage VPN, Active Directory, and access control
Support databases, application servers, and web servers when required
Deliver customer training and prepare technical documentation
