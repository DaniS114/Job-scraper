Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Define and execute the regional enterprise applications strategy, including rationalisation and consolidation of existing systems.
Maintain a transparent overview of the application portfolio and actively reduce complexity and redundancy.
Prepare regional business entities for a future ERP implementation based on IFS Cloud.
Align business processes, data structures, and integrations with global standards and target architecture.
Collaborate closely with global ERP leadership to define and establish the regional rollout approach.
Own the full lifecycle management of enterprise applications, ensuring stability, performance, and scalability.
Provide direction and decision-making during ERP-related projects, upgrades, and enhancements.
Lead and develop the regional enterprise applications team and integrate it into group-wide delivery and support models.

Business-oriented mindset with the ability to translate technical complexity into practical outcomes.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills across business and IT functions.
Ability to manage multiple priorities, dependencies, and initiatives in parallel.
