Purpose
The Senior Microsoft BI Software Engineer / Consultant is a key member of the Common Data Intelligence (CDI) Hub, responsible for the end-to-end development, delivery, and operation of enterprise Data Warehouse and Business Intelligence solutions. The role combines deep hands-on development with solution ownership, technical leadership, and mentoring, ensuring BI solutions are scalable, secure, reliable, and aligned with business needs.
Team / Project Description
The Common Data Intelligence (CDI) Hub is an enterprise analytics organization responsible for building and operating standardized Data Warehouse and BI solutions across multiple business domains. The team works in an agile, collaborative environment, delivering high-quality analytics products used by international stakeholders.
What will you do?
Design, develop, and maintain enterprise Data Warehouse and BI solutions from concept through production and support.
Take end-to-end ownership of BI solutions, leading implementation, deployment, and continuous improvement.
Develop advanced SQL solutions on Microsoft SQL Server, including performance-optimized queries and data models.
Translate business requirements into scalable, maintainable, and secure BI architectures.
Implement and continuously enhance security, access control, and data protection concepts within BI solutions.
Drive solution optimization initiatives, improving performance, reliability, and maintainability.
Supervise acceptance testing and ensure high-quality delivery standards are met.
Oversee end-user support and operational stability, ensuring reliable production systems.
Act as a senior technical leader within the CDI Hub, providing guidance, mentoring, and knowledge sharing for junior developers.
Collaborate closely with other engineers, analysts, and stakeholders to solve complex data challenges.
Work in an agile development environment, actively managing backlog, tasks, and delivery using Jira.
Contribute to continuous improvement of development standards, best practices, and ways of working.
Support solution design and architectural discussions as a senior BI expert.
