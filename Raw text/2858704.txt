About us
L2 Operational Support in German
Location: Budapest, Hungary
Job Type: Full Time
Working model: Hybrid (2 days in the office)
Responsibilities:
Provide advanced troubleshooting for complex mobility incidents escalated from L1 support
Diagnose and resolve device enrollment, configuration, compliance and application deployment issues
Support mobile OS upgrades, patching strategies and device lifecycle events
Ensure minimal disruption to business users through rapid root cause identification
Strategic Mobility Platform Management
Own the technical roadmap for all MDM platforms including JAMF Pro, IBM MaaS360 and Ivanti
Evaluate OS changes from Apple and Android and assess enterprise impact
Proactively recommend enhancements, automation opportunities and platform optimizations
Lead platform upgrades and mobility transformation initiatives
Governance & Compliance
Enforce security policies across managed mobile devices
Monitor encryption, certificate management and conditional access controls
Generate compliance dashboards and executive reporting
Ensure adherence to corporate security policies and regulatory requirements
Problem Management
Conduct structured deep-dive analysis of recurring Business mobility issues
Perform root cause analysis and deliver formal documentation with long-term remediation plans
Collaborate with security, infrastructure and regional IT teams to drive permanent fixes
Knowledge Management
Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation
Develop knowledge articles, user guides and mobility FAQs
Maintain a structured and searchable knowledge repository for global IT teams
Training & Enablement
Design and deliver periodic training sessions for regional IT teams
Enable local support teams with platform updates, OS changes and best practices
Provide advisory support for mobility-related initiatives across business units
Your profile:
Required Skills & Experience:
experience in Enterprise Mobility Management
Strong hands-on expertise in JAMF Pro for iOS and iPadOS
Experience managing IBM MaaS360 for Android and corporate mobility
Working knowledge of Ivanti mobility capabilities
Experience with Apple Business Manager, Automated Device Enrollment and Android Enterprise
Deep understanding of mobile OS security controls and compliance frameworks
Experience handling escalated L2 incidents in global environments
Strong documentation and reporting skills
An advantage if you have experience in:
JAMF 200 or 300 certification
ITIL certification
Experience supporting Business or geographically distributed workforce
Exposure to regulated industries such as pharma or healthcare
Core Competencies that we request:
Strong analytical troubleshooting capability
Structured problem-solving approach
Proactive platform ownership mindset
Ability to operate independently while coordinating with global stakeholders
