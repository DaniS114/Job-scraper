Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the Role
We are seeking a Junior Automation Engineer to join our global Solutions team in Bratislava. In this role, you will contribute to the evolution and support of Magnum, Swiss Re’s market-leading automated underwriting solution, currently deployed with more than 70 clients worldwide.
You will play a key role across the full solution lifecycle — from design and development through testing, validation, and continuous improvement. Working closely with product, underwriting, and technology stakeholders, you will help ensure Magnum continues to deliver reliable, scalable, and best-in-class automated underwriting capabilities that support our clients’ digital transformation journeys.
Magnum is a strategic Swiss Re product, combining advanced risk knowledge with modern automation technologies to enable efficient, consistent, and high-quality underwriting decisions across Life and Health markets.
Key Responsibilities
- Participate in the design, development, and delivery of automation solutions aligned with product strategy and client requirements
- Produce clear and well-structured functional and technical specifications
- Develop and maintain underwriting rules, automation logic, and supporting components
- Contribute to ongoing Magnum product enhancements and innovation initiatives
- Apply automation and RPA technologies to streamline and optimize business processes
- Design, execute, and maintain comprehensive test plans, including regression test packs
- Validate development outcomes against defined business and client requirements
- Identify, track, and resolve defects throughout the full development lifecycle
- Actively participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) cycles and release validation activities
- Design, develop, and maintain automated testing frameworks and test suites
- Continuously improve test automation approaches to enhance coverage, reliability, and efficiency
- Collaborate effectively with global stakeholders across multiple time zones
