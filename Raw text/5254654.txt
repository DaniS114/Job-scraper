DevOps Engineer for Open Telekom Cloud Landing Zone (T-cloud)
Place of work
Košice, Slovakia (Job with occasional home office)
Contract type
full-time
Wage (gross)
1 650 EUR/month
Final salary is negotiable.
We are offering base salary depending on seniority level and previous experience of candidate. In addition to base salary we provide variable part and other financial benefits. Base salary will not be lower than 1 650 EUR/month.
We are looking for a DevOps Engineer with 2+ years of hands-on experience in cloud environments to support the build and operation of our Open Telekom Cloud Landing Zone for the DT IT environment.
You will collaborate with cross-functional engineers and architects to design, implement, and operate infrastructure-as-code, networking, security guardrails, automation, and monitoring solutions in an enterprise-scale environment.
This role is suited for someone with solid cloud fundamentals who is ready to take ownership, deepen their technical expertise, and grow towards a Cloud Engineering or Architecture career path.
What will you do?
Work in an agile setup, collaborating closely within the team, across the Domain, and with Deutsche Telekom platform customers
Apply a DevOps and continuous improvement mindset to daily work
Contribute to building a reliable, scalable, and secure cloud platform in collaboration with senior engineers.
Design and maintain the Landing Zone in alignment with security and privacy requirements
Implement Infrastructure-as-Code (e.g., Terraform) and configuration management (e.g., Ansible) to deliver repeatable, production-grade environments.
Configure and troubleshoot networking components (VPCs, routing, VPN, load balancing, DNS, interconnectivity, distributed networks, and integration with cloud services) following established design patterns
Implement and maintain monitoring, logging, dashboards, and contribute to alert tuning and runbook improvements
Automate routine platform operations (CI/CD pipelines, scripts, tooling)
Maintain high-quality documentation and contribute to platform standards and guidelines
Continuously expand technical knowledge and proactively suggest improvements to the platform
You will succeed if you:
Minimum qualifications
English proficiency at B2+ level (team and hub language is English)
2+ years of experience in Cloud, Platform Engineering, DevOps, or SRE roles (or equivalent practical experience)
Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud provider
Proficiency in at least one programming language (Python, Go, Java, or Node.js) and solid shell scripting skills
Practical experience with Git and understanding of CI/CD principles
Hands-on exposure to Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform or similar)
Hands-on experience with configuration management and automation tools (e.g., Ansible).
Ability to work in complex environments with ownership and accountability
Ability to clearly communicate and visually present technical concepts (e.g., architecture diagrams)
Nice to Have
Hands-on labs or project experience on GCP, AWS, or Azure
Entry-level cloud certifications (completed or in progress), such as:
Google Associate Cloud Engineer
AWS Solutions Architect – Associate
Azure Fundamentals
Experience with cloud networking (VPC, routing, security groups, load balancers)
Understanding of security and IAM concepts
Experience with monitoring and observability tools (Cloud Monitoring/Logging, Prometheus, Grafana)
Databases (SQL/NoSQL)
Growth & Support
Dedicated mentorship from senior cloud architects and platform engineers
Learning time and certification budget (target: Associate-level certification within 6–12 months)
Opportunity to present architectures, POCs, and contribute to platform standards
Success Indicators (First Months)
Deliver reviewed and production ready Terraform code
Contribute to CI/CD pipelines used in production
Implement monitored and documented configurations aligned with security and compliance standards
Demonstrate ownership of defined platform components
