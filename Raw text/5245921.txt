Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
Are you passionate about connecting investment technology with business needs? Join our Investment Platforms Team where you'll play a crucial role in managing and evolving key investment systems that power our global asset management operations. This position offers an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of finance and technology, translating complex business requirements into effective solutions.
About the role:
As a Business Analyst for Investment Platforms, you'll be the functional bridge between Investment teams, Operations, and IT, ensuring our BlackRock Aladdin and eFront platforms operate efficiently while continuously evolving to meet business needs. You'll manage user requests, implement functional changes, and drive platform enhancements in a dynamic, global environment.
Key Responsibilities:
- User Support & Analysis: Serve as the primary contact for investment platform users, solving complex challenges and developing sustainable solutions that enhance platform stability
- Change Implementation: Drive the implementation of new functionalities from assessment through deployment, ensuring seamless integration that elevates our investment capabilities
- Platform Evolution: Monitor platform roadmaps and guide the adoption of new features, keeping our systems aligned with evolving business needs and market requirements
- Process Optimization: Identify and implement valuable automation opportunities that reduce manual work and improve efficiency across our investment platforms
- Stakeholder Management: Connect diverse global teams, facilitating effective communication between Investment, Operations, Finance, and IT to ensure successful technology outcomes
About the team
We are a small, focused technology team, part of Group Digital & Technology (GDTO) serving the Asset Management domain within Swiss Re. The team is located in Bratislava, collaborating with business and IT teams in numerous locations worldwide including New York, London and Zurich.
