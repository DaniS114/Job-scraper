Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Comprehending complex needs: Understanding both business and customer needs in complex energy-related domains, driving product scoping to a level of detail that enables development, and helping deliver the best possible customer experience for our clients
Consulting & advising clients: Leading discussions with clients in the energy space, identifying opportunities to innovate, and driving real results and measurable benefits in critical areas
Product discovery & planning: Driving the planning and discovery phases of energy-related software products, and continuously prioritising product roadmaps in close collaboration with our clients
Stakeholder management & client engagement: Engaging in in-depth discussions with clients on use cases, feature opportunities, and business processes, and translating these into user journeys and clear requirements for development and design teams
Collaborating with teams: Facilitating product ideation sessions and working closely with Technical Project Managers, Architects, UI/UX Designers, and Business Analysts to translate product requirements into clear specifications
Being the voice of the customer: Deeply understanding customer needs and market trends in order to effectively influence product direction and development
Requirements
At least 5 years of experience in the energy industry: Proven experience and strong business understanding of interconnectors, TSOs/DSOs, energy trading, grid asset management, or similar domains
Adaptability to different situations: The ability to navigate change effectively, applying strong change-management skills when dealing with unforeseen edge cases and new features
An innovative, customer-focused mindset: A background as an energy industry professional with hands-on experience in digital product development
Excellent communication skills: The ability to clearly explain how functions work to non-technical audiences, while also translating business requirements into actionable input for technical teams
Strong business acumen: A solid understanding of both business and customer needs within the energy industry
A commercial mindset: A proactive and curious approach to identifying and exploring new business opportunities in close collaboration with our clients
What we offer
Opportunities to learn and develop including conferences, trainings, mentoring, and hands-on experience with digital projects
Wellbeing benefits including pension scheme with Aviva, company mobile package, Cycle to Work scheme, 25 days holiday plus Bank Holidays
Vibrant, open-minded culture with biannual all-expenses-paid team events in Hungary and local community events in London
Cosy office environment in central London with perks, plus a personal MacBook for work
Company info
Supercharge is a digital product consultancy that partners with ambitious companies to create innovative digital products and services. Founded in 2015, Supercharge operates internationally with a focus on delivering impactful digital solutions across various industries including energy, healthcare, finance, insurance, mobility, and the public sector. The company is committed to fostering a vibrant, open-minded culture that encourages curiosity and creativity, supporting continuous learning and development for its employees.
