IT Vendor Management Specialist, 80-100% (ID1958)
Short Description
The IT Vendor Managemnet Specialist is responsible for the strategic, operational, and financial management of all external IT partners, service providers, and technology vendors of the AO IT organization. The position ensures that all vendor delivered services meet the AO's expectations in quality, security, cost efficiency, compliance, and performance.
The role collaborates closely with Procurement, Security, IT Architecture, Infrastructure, IT Business Applications, Service Desk, and key business stakeholders to guarantee end to end service continuity and a professional vendor ecosystem.
Main Responsibilities
Vendor & Third Party Management:
Develop, implement, and continuously improve the AO's vendor management framework, including policies, governance, and lifecycle management
Drive vendor consolidation
Strongly support and drive changing vendors delivery method from a staff augmentation to a managed work approach
Leads the full lifecycle in collaboration with internal stakeholders: sourcing, evaluation, onboarding, contracting, performance management, productivity, renewal, and offboarding
Ensure compliance with AO information security and regulatory requirements, including periodic vendor audits and assessments
Leading Microsoft capability within IT
Contract, Commercial & Financial Management:
Oversee contract negotiations in cooperation with procurement to secure cost effective and high quality services
Manage vendor related budgeting, financial forecasting, spend tracking, and cost optimization with related counterparts in the IT organization
Ensure alignment of all contracts with AO legal, procurement, and compliance requirements.
SLA, KPI & Performance Management:
Define service levels, KPIs, OLAs, and performance measures for all external providers.
Conduct structured performance reviews with vendors, including escalations and corrective action plans.
Ensure transparency of service quality through standardized reporting and dashboards.
Risk, Security & Compliance:
Ensure that all vendors adhere to AO's Information Security Policy, IAM requirements, data protection standards, and incident response expectations
Work with the IT Security Manager to manage vendor related risk assessments, security audits, and continuous improvement measures
Maintain a vendor risk register and ensure mitigation actions are implemented
Cross Functional Collaboration with:
IT Infrastructure on operational performance, SLA compliance, and service reliability
IT Security on third party security assessments, IAM obligations, and audit readiness
Service Desk & Workplace regarding partner activities, escalations, and operational alignment
IT Architecture to ensure vendor solutions align with AO architectural principles
IT Business Applications in relation with vendors for Application and Software developments
Relationship, Communication & Stakeholder Management:
Act as the primary strategic contact for all key IT vendors and service providers
Ensure transparent and structured communication between vendors and AO stakeholders
Facilitate business stakeholder engagement for vendor strategy, performance, and service improvements
Reporting & Governance:
Provide regular performance, financial, and risk reporting to IT Leadership and relevant governance bodies
Ensure documentation, contracts, vendor records, and audit materials are maintained and up to date.
Contribute to AO IT strategic planning through insights into vendor capabilities, risks, and market developments.
Main Requirements
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or a related field
Additional training in negotiation, contract law, or financial management is an advantage
Minimum 4 years of experience in IT vendor management
Proven experience managing complex vendor ecosystems, including cloud, managed services, workplace, and infrastructure providers
Experience in audit driven environments and security sensitive operational contexts
Fluency in English (written and spoken).German is a strong asset.
Required time in Davos AO Headquarter, minimum 3 full working days.
Core Skills / Competencies:
Strong expertise in IT vendor management, IT contract negotiation, and commercial governance
Excellent knowledge of IT service management frameworks (ITIL), SLA models, and performance management
Solid understanding of IT security, IAM, compliance standards, and risk management
Experience with Mircrosoft license models (Azure, MS365)
Strong communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills
Analytical mindset with the ability to interpret complex operational and commercial data
Ability to manage multi vendor environments and resolve escalations in a structured and collaborative manner
An interesting and varied job in an exciting and innovative organization
The opportunity to be part of a highly committed international team
Modern infrastructure
High degree of flexibility regarding working hours and location (depending on operational requirements)
Generous pagacke of social benefits, including supplementary vacation days and pension scheme contributions
