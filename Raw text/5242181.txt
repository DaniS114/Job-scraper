Job description, responsibilities and duties
Who We Are
At Rankacy AI, we're not your average tech company - we're the ones training AI models on over 500TB of gaming data while others are still using pre-trained models and calling it innovation. We've built AI systems that analyze millions of Counter-Strike scenarios, serving tens of thousands of users who demand real-time insights. Our secret sauce? PhD researchers who've traded academic comfort for startup adrenaline, working alongside battle-tested engineers who solve problems others won't even attempt.
Now we're ready for the next frontier: computer vision.
What You'll Be Doing
Building Vision Systems
– Design, train, and deploy computer vision models for game analysis, action recognition, and tactical understanding
Transformer Mastery
– Implement and optimize transformer architectures (video-focused and multimodal models) for our specific use cases
Training at Scale
– Develop models on massive gaming datasets using our custom training infrastructure. You'll have serious compute and PhD researchers to collaborate with
Production Engineering
– Take models from research to production. Optimize inference, handle edge cases, ensure reliability for thousands of concurrent users
Pushing Boundaries
– Contribute to technical architecture decisions and help define our computer vision roadmap across gaming and defence applications
Key Challenges
Real-Time Performance
– Processing high-resolution game footage with complex transformer models while maintaining sub-second latency
Domain Adaptation
– Making vision transformers work for gaming scenarios that differ significantly from standard datasets
Scale vs. Speed
– Balancing model complexity with training time and inference costs when working with millions of scenarios
Multimodal Fusion
– Combining visual information with game state data, player actions, and temporal context for intelligent analysis
What You Need to Bring
Deep Computer Vision Expertise
– You've built and shipped production CV systems. You understand CNNs, transformers, attention mechanisms, and when to use each
Python & ML Frameworks
– PyTorch or TensorFlow mastery. You can implement architectures from papers and debug training dynamics
Production Mindset
– You've deployed models real users depend on. You think about latency, throughput, error handling, and monitoring
Systems Thinking
– You understand the full stack from data pipelines to model serving
Bonus Points
– Action recognition, 3D computer vision, published research, gaming/esports background
