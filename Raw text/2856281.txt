Responsibilities
IT TAKES REAL INTELLIGENCE TO PIONEER THE ARTIFICIAL KIND.
Networks are about more than just IT. They are about connecting people who make things possible. Close teamwork is key to understanding how IT can help integrate processes into systems. We put this insight into practice, and it's part of our success.
We drive the AI transformation of the BMW Group with innovative platform services. Our team develops and operates secure, scalable solutions for the entire lifecycle of AI models, enabling sustainable, efficient, and compliance-compliant AI use cases.
What awaits you?
You take strategic ownership of an AI Moderation Layer as a hardened platform service: define architecture standards, security policies, SLAs/SLOs, and ensure global scalability and integration with AI platform services.You design and lead a multi-layered defense strategy against jailbreaks, prompt injection, data exfiltration, and tool misuse through advanced input/output scanners, safety filters, and autonomous agents.
You establish and continuously improve the security lifecycle for LLMs/Agents: threat modeling, attack simulations, red teaming, LLM-specific pentests, automated security assessments, and incident response frameworks.
You act as a trusted advisor for legal, compliance, and data protection teams, shaping governance and regulatory alignment for AI security.
You drive operational excellence: define KPIs, implement canary rollouts, optimize cost/performance, scale to additional data sources, and ensure 24/7 reliability.
You mentor and guide an agile team of engineers and data scientists, fostering best practices in architecture, security, and platform engineering.
You ensure enterprise-grade quality of platform services regarding architecture, code, IT security, compliance, documentation, and operational KPIs.
Requirements
What should you bring along?
University degree in computer science, mathematics, or a comparable field with an IT focus, or equivalent qualification.
8+ years of experience in software or platform security and architecture in cloud environments (Azure/AWS), including containerized microservices, CI/CD pipelines, GitOps, Infrastructure as Code, service mesh, SLOs, high availability, and disaster recovery.
Deep expertise in LLM and agent security: advanced protection against jailbreaks, prompt and indirect injection, input/output scanners, policy engines, and moderation strategies.
Proven leadership in security lifecycle and compliance: threat modeling, red teaming, LLM-specific pentests, automated security assessments in CI/CD, telemetry, alerting, and incident response.
Strong engineering background with architectural decision-making: Python, C# or TypeScript, REST and event-driven systems, observability practices, and experience with agile methods.
Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills; business fluent English required, German is a plus.
Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional initiatives and mentor teams in complex, high-security environments.
