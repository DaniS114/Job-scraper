Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Develop and execute test plans and verification methodologies for SoC-level designs
Complete functional verification to the required quality standards and schedules
Collaborate with project leads and management on planning, scheduling, and quality checkpoints
Ensure progress and quality of SoC verification activities
Work closely with teams across architecture, design, implementation, modelling, performance analysis, silicon validation, FPGA, and board development
Mentor and guide junior verification engineers (for senior engineers)
Requirements
Proven experience in embedded C/C++ based SoC verification environments
Strong understanding of assembly language (preferably ARM)
Proficiency in hardware verification languages such as SystemVerilog, and scripting languages (Tcl, Perl, Python, etc.)
Hands-on experience in UVM, and at least one of formal verification, low power, or emulation
Exposure to all verification stages: requirements collection, methodology and test plan creation, testbench development and test case implementation, documentation and support
Practical experience verifying processor-based system designs
Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences
Nice-to-have
Nice to have skills: solid understanding of Arm system architectures, Experience with power-aware design and Clock Domain Crossing (CDC) verification, Familiarity with embedded OS, device drivers, and hardware/software integration, Experience verifying subsystems and interfaces such as PCIe, LPDDR, HBM, UCIe, and Ethernet, Experience in developing and deploying large SoCs on emulation or FPGA platforms, 10x mindset guides how we engineer, collaborate, and grow
What we offer
Hybrid working environment designed to support high performance and personal wellbeing
Flexibility in hybrid working patterns depending on team needs
Accommodations during recruitment process for candidates who need adjustments
Competitive salary with benefits beyond salary to reward dedication and contributions
Support for professional and personal development
Inclusive and diverse work environment
Company info
Arm’s Solutions Engineering team builds subsystems and full SoC designs for a wide range of application segments, leveraging the latest IP from Arm and other vendors.
