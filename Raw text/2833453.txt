Responsibilities
You’ll support the operation and improvement of mission-critical regulated-gaming infrastructure while joining us on an exciting journey to modernize our technology stack. Working under the guidance of a senior DevOps engineer and experienced IT leadership, you’ll have the opportunity to learn how modern platforms are built, operated, and continuously improved in a real production environment.
This role is ideal for a junior or graduate engineer who wants to build hands-on experience in DevOps, automation, containerization, CI/CD, and production operations while contributing to the ongoing evolution of our systems. We are looking for someone eager to learn, grow, and take part in shaping how our future platform is delivered and operated.
What You’ll Work With (Tech Stack)
Containers & Runtime
Docker and Docker Compose
Service packaging and environment consistency practices
CI/CD & Artifact Management
Jenkins or Bamboo pipelines
Nexus artifact repositories
Maven builds in the Java ecosystem
Automation & Infrastructure
Ansible for provisioning and configuration
Python or other scripting languages for automation tasks
Automation testing and quality checks
Observability & Operations
Grafana / Kibana and related monitoring/logging tools
Operational support, troubleshooting, and runbook-driven processes
Security
Secrets and certificate management practices
Basic TLS / certificate handling
Data & Streaming
Exposure to Kafka, PostgreSQL, and Oracle-related deployment or support tasks
Day-to-Day Responsibilities
Assist with containerizing services and helping move systems toward more modern runtime and deployment patterns
Support CI/CD pipelines and help improve build, test, and release processes
Help maintain infrastructure and configuration automation using Ansible and scripting
Assist with deployments across development, test, and production environments
Monitor systems, dashboards, and alerts, and support troubleshooting activities
Work with senior team members to investigate issues across Linux systems, pipelines, containers, and supporting services
Contribute to the modernization of our tooling, processes, and operational practices over time
Learn and follow operational, security, and release management best practices
Contribute to documentation, runbooks, and team knowledge sharing
Requirements
A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, IT, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
Good understanding of Linux fundamentals
Basic knowledge of containers, especially Docker
Familiarity with scripting or programming, preferably Python
Understanding of Git and collaborative development workflows
Interest in CI/CD, automation, infrastructure, and platform engineering
Willingness to learn tools such as Jenkins/Bamboo, Ansible, Nexus, Grafana, and Kibana
Enthusiasm for learning modern DevOps practices and contributing to technology modernization
Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive attitude
Good communication skills and eagerness to work closely with experienced engineers
A genuine interest in developing a career in DevOps and platform engineering
Company info
OmniLogic (
https://www.omnilogic.io)
delivers mission-critical regulated-gaming and lottery platform solutions, including high-performance omni-channel sportsbook platforms, PAM and wallet systems, risk and trading infrastructure, data pipelines, and legacy modernisation for World Lottery Association members and Tier-1 operators.
As we continue to scale our operations, we are also on a mission to modernize our technology stack, tooling, and engineering practices. We are looking for a Junior / Graduate DevOps Engineer to join our team and support this journey across our test and production environments.
This role is designed for someone at the start of their DevOps career who is excited to learn from experienced professionals, grow into our technology stack, and play a hands-on role in helping us build the next generation of our platform.
There’s also a slightly stronger version of this message that makes the role sound more ambitious and appealing to graduates, using language like “join us in modernizing and shaping the future of our platform.”
