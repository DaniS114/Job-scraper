About the role
As an SAP ABAP Fiori Developer at DXC Technology in the Czech Republic, you will play a pivotal role in designing, developing, and implementing SAP applications that enhance user experiences through Fiori interfaces. Your responsibilities will encompass the entire software development lifecycle, including analysis, design, coding, testing, and deployment of custom ABAP developments. You will collaborate closely with functional consultants and other developers to deliver high-quality solutions tailored to client needs.
Daily challenges
Develop and maintain SAP Fiori applications using SAPUI5, OData services, and related technologies.
Implement and test custom ABAP developments, ensuring optimal performance and adherence to best practices.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
Provide technical support and troubleshooting for existing SAP applications.
Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.
eXperience and skills required
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
5+ years of experience with SAP ABAP programming. SAP Fiori is a bonus.
Advanced knowledge of ABAP OO, ALV, Enhancement Framework, BAPI, and BAdi concepts.
Practical experience with one or more of the following technologies: ABAP on HANA, OData services, REST services, SAP UI5, WebDynpro, Business Objects (BOR), SAP Workflow, Adobe Forms, SmartForms.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
Proficiency in English
Preferred Skills:
Experience with SAP S/4HANA, SAP Fiori, and SAP UI5.
Familiarity with additional programming languages such as JavaScript, RAP, HTML, and CSS.
Ability to work effectively in a team environment and manage multiple priorities.
Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to identifying and resolving issues.
