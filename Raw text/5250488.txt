Ready to shape the future of legal tech? Freshfields is launching a new IT hub in Bratislava’s Shared Services Centre (SSC)—and we’re looking for talented professionals to join us. Be part of an innovative, international team working on transformative digital solutions. Build your career with Freshfields and make an impact on a global scale!
We are looking for a skilled and motivated Software Developer to join our Application Platforms team.
You’ll collaborate with product specialists, business analysts, and stakeholders to deliver secure, scalable, and maintainable solutions that align with our technology roadmap. Whether you're designing new systems or enhancing existing ones, your work will directly support the firm’s operational excellence and innovation goals.

Develop and maintain scalable, high-performing applications with a focus on user experience.

Contribute to solution architecture and Agile sprint delivery.
Continuously improve systems and workflows through automation and optimisation.
