Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Plan and lead the technical implementation of ServiceNow solutions, including system configuration and development of custom features.
Architect and design scalable and robust ServiceNow solutions.
Provide advanced technical consulting and support quality assurance activities.
Ensure adherence to implementation standards and technical best practices.
Understand complex business needs and help standardize processes in design and deliverables.
Mentor and guide junior developers and team members.
Requirements
Minimum of five years of experience in ServiceNow development and architecture.
Deep expertise of at least one ServiceNow module; experience with several modules is an advantage.
Multiple ServiceNow certifications.
Excellent command of English; knowledge of German is an advantage.
Demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team.
Leadership and mentoring skills.
What we offer
Competitive compensation package including cafeteria budget, private health care, family-oriented benefits, AYCM sport pass, and company shares at a discount price.
Extensive home office opportunity and a nice office to meet with your team members.
Wide range of leading-edge trainings and a variety of language courses.
Company-sponsored ServiceNow certificates, including the ArchX and CTA certification.
Employee assistance program: Psychologist support, legal or financial advice.
Company info
Accenture is a leading global professional services company and a recognized Global Elite Partner of ServiceNow with over 5500 professionals and over 17000 ServiceNow certifications. Our market position and partnership with ServiceNow enables us to be at the forefront of innovation in ServiceNow development and implementation. We help organizations worldwide streamline services across their enterprises, enhancing speed, agility, and efficiency at scale.
