Responsibilities
Product Strategy:
You own the end-to-end product vision and road­map for all non-financial ERP capa­bilities across MB Energy (SAP/Implico, OTIS and CODAS), aligned with your business stake­holder demands.
Platform Operations:
You manage day-to-day opera­tions, stability, and perfor­mance of the SAP / Implico platform after go-live, inclu­ding incident, change, and release management.
System Ownership:
You act as the overall IT product owner for addi­tional ERPs (OTIS, CODAS), coordinating vendors and ensuring systems remain fit for pur­pose for their respective busi­ness models.
Process Excellence:
You analyze, design, and con­tinuously improve end-to-end busi­ness processes from product supply and order management to delivery planning, dispatching, and final delivery. Utilize AI capabi­lities where it makes sense.
Digital Transformation:
You identify, prio­ritize, and imple­ment process digi­tization and auto­mation oppor­tunities across the ERP landscape (e.g., integrations, workflows, data flows).
Requirements
Education:
You hold a university degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Enginee­ring, Business Adminis­tration or a related field; an advanced degree is a plus.
Experience:
You bring several years of experience in IT Consulting or as an IT Product Manager within a non-digital / asset-heavy environ­ment, with a strong focus on ERP and order-to-cash / order-to-delivery processes.
Product Management:
You have a proven track record in managing end-to-end product lifecycles, defining roadmaps and KPIs/OKRs, and driving implemen­tation, opera­tions, and continuous improvement.
ERP and
Process Expertise:
You possess strong analy­tical skills and hands-on expe­rience with ERP systems (ideally SAP-based and/or industry solu­tions such as Implico, OTIS, and CODAS) as well as inte­gration and automation tools.
Stakeholder and Team Collaboration:
You work effec­tively in multidisciplinary teams and influence stake­holders across functions and hierarchies without direct authority.
Language:
You are fluent in German (mandatory) and have very good English skills; addi­tional languages are an advan­tage.
With these characteristics, you fit perfectly with us:
Strategic and Communi­cative:
You combine stra­tegic thinking with strong communi­cation skills to influence stake­holders at all levels.
Integrity and Responsibility:
You act with integrity, take respon­sibility for sensi­tive topics, and maintain a clear stance in critical situa­tions.
Adaptability:
You remain flexible and effec­tive in a dynamic, fast-changing environ­ment.
Even if you do not fulfill all of the above require­ments, we encourage you to apply if you believe your expe­rience will add value to our team.

Company info
MB Energy,
founded in 1947 and head­quartered in Hamburg, is an inde­pendent and integrated energy company with operations in Europe, the United States, and Singapore. The company is active in the import, storage, distri­bution, and marketing of petroleum products, liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), chemicals, and biofuels. With decades of industry experience and an inno­vative approach, MB Energy plays an active role in the global energy landscape, supporting its customers in the transition to lower-emission energy sources by deve­loping and pro­viding forward-looking alter­natives.

Senior IT Product Manager – ERP-Systems / Marketing & Sales (m/f/d)
Here's what you can look forward to:
In this role, you own the end-to-end product lifecycle for all non-financial ERP aspects within the Marketing and Sales Busi­ness Unit of MB Energy – from order cap­ture and delivery plan­ning to dis­patching and final delivery. As part of the global Commercial IT and AI Trans­formation func­tion, you help build a scalable, data-driven ERP landscape that drives opera­tional excel­lence, margin improve­ment, and a strong customer expe­rience.
