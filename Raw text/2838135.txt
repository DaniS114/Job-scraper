Responsibilities
Development of the "Quality Management" domain according to the Area Consulting CoE Digital Supply Chain and Corporate IT Strategy. Ensuring relevant business functions through the efficient use of applications, interfaces, and technologies - with the goal of reducing complexity in the long term
"Quality Management" Domain Architect must deeply understand business needs and translate them into scalable, secure, and valuedriven IT solutions. They are expected to contribute to feasibility analyses, business cases, and strategic roadmaps, aligned with Area Consulting CoE Digital Supply Chain and Corporate IT Strategy
"Quality Management" Domain Architect acts as advisor, guiding business units through ideation, process optimization, and technology scouting. This includes facilitating workshops, interviews, and stakeholder meetings to clarify needs and define value
"Quality Management" Domain Architect should be proactive in identifying emerging technologies (e.g., AI, cloud, automation) and evaluating their relevance for business transformation in the own domain and area of expertise
Ensuring the adherence to the Enterprise Architecture Management (EAM) framework and principles within Liebherr-IT Services GmbH (LIS) and the entire Liebherr Group - focusing on the domain "Quality Management"
Cross-Divisional coordination of the "Quality Management" domain, organizing regular meetings and workshops with domain architects, exchanging with working groups and departments, aligned with Area Consulting CoE Digital Supply Chain and E2E (End-To-End) Process Excellence, from idea to operations
Analyzing and improving the domain's maturity level. Identifying harmonization potentials and strategic fields of action. Defining a target architecture and documenting it in LeanIX
Requirements
Completed studies in Computer Science, (Business) Informatics, Information Technology, Information Management, or a comparable qualification (e.g., in the field of Quality Management)
Several years of IT Strategy & Consulting experience as an (Domain) Architect in an international environment or larger mid-sized company or as Global Solution Architect in the field of Quality Management
Advanced Problem-Solving skill, adopting a holistic, technologyagnostic perspective and enabling strategic clarity and cross-domain insights
Effectively shape decision-making without formal authority by articulating direction grounded in technical feasibility, cost-effectiveness, and business impact
Strategic Thinking & Business Understanding in the own domain • E2E (End-To-End) cross-functional thinking, mindset and solution conceptualization
Strong Communication skills & Stakeholder Engagement
Advisory and Facilitation (including presentation) skills
Adaptability & Continuous Learning, stay current with industry trends and regulatory changes
Living in Germany (no sponsorship foreseen)
Fluent in German and English, any other European language B2 level is a plus
TOGAF certification is a plus
