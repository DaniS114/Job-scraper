About
We are looking for a Medior/Senior Identity and Access Management specialist to design and manage on-premises Active Directory environments, including AD tiering, RBAC, DNS, and related services. The role involves automation of identity lifecycle processes using PowerShell and Python, as well as working with Azure AD, Microsoft Defender, and Intune to strengthen security and compliance. It requires a proactive, agile-minded professional with strong IAM/PAM expertise.
