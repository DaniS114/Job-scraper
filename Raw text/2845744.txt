Responsibilities
Support the development and delivery of skills-based technology training for attorneys and staff.
Expand and maintain the firm’s eLearning library by creating engaging instructional materials, tutorials, and guides.
Assist with onboarding training programs for new employees and ongoing learning initiatives for current staff.
Coordinate training logistics, including scheduling, communications, and reporting.
Support user adoption strategies for new software rollouts and technology upgrades.
Provide one-on-one coaching or small group sessions to enhance productivity and technology confidence.
Collaborate with the IT and HR departments to assess training needs and evaluate program effectiveness.
Maintain accurate documentation of all training activities and materials.
Occasionally travel to other firm offices to deliver on-site training.
Requirements
2+ years of experience in a training, technology support, or instructional design role, preferably within a law firm or professional services environment.
Strong presentation and communication skills — able to simplify complex topics for non-technical users.
Excellent interpersonal and customer service abilities, with a proactive, solution-oriented mindset.
Ability to learn and teach new software applications quickly.
Experience creating eLearning content (using tools like Articulate, Captivate, or similar) is a plus.
Strong organizational and documentation skills, with attention to detail.
Associate’s or bachelor’s degree in legal studies, information technology, or a related field — or equivalent professional experience.
Willingness to travel periodically to other firm offices.
What we offer
Join a collaborative and respected firm that values professional growth and innovation.
Opportunity to shape the firm’s digital training experience and directly impact efficiency across departments.
Competitive compensation package and comprehensive benefits.
Hybrid work model offering flexibility and work-life balance.
How to apply
You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
