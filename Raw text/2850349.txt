Responsibilities
Perform analysis of prevalent user journeys and build an understanding of user needs, preferences, and pain points
Translate findings into user-centric design solutions that enhance the overall gambling experience
Create intuitive and visually appealing user interfaces for digital applications
Develop wireframes and interactive prototypes to communicate design concepts and validate ideas
Iterate and refine designs based on research, insights, and best practices
Consistently review the live product and actively participate in continuous product improvements
Work closely with Product Managers, Engineers, and other stakeholders throughout the product development lifecycle
Provide design expertise and contribute to product strategy discussions
Keep track of industry trends and perform competitor analysis to identify opportunities for improvement and innovation
Work closely with the Central Research teams to conduct user testing and gather insights
Contribute to the development and maintenance of a design system to ensure consistency across different product features and platforms
Requirements
3+ years of experience in user interface design, user experience design, interaction design, and information architecture
Strong design portfolio highlighting UX/UI skills in various areas, examples in gaming and/or fintech are an advantage
Deep understanding of user-centered design principles, usability, and accessibility standards
Experience with using metrics, data, and research to drive product design decisions
Proficiency with design and prototype tools such as Sketch, Figma, Adobe XD, InVision, Webflow, Axure RP, or similar
Strong written, verbal, and presentation skills (English)
Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work with agile and remote development teams
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced and evolving environment, meeting tight deadlines while maintaining attention to detail
Experience with user research methodologies, including conducting interviews, surveys, usability testing, and A/B/n Testing
Ability to work independently with minimal supervision whilst also being a strong team player
Experience working with a design system or pattern design library
Experience within the online gambling industry and a passion for creating responsible and enjoyable gaming experiences is an advantage
Formal qualification(s) in interaction design, human-machine interaction, or similar will be considered a plus
Prior experience with graphic design illustration using tools such as Photoshop & Illustrator will be considered a plus
