Job description, responsibilities and duties
Our opportunity
Cloud Data Analyst #LI-Hybrid
Lead and support data-centric IT product delivery (REDS), focusing on cloud-based (Databricks/Azure) solutions and legacy .NET/SQL platform transition. Ensure alignment with regulatory, operational, and data governance standards.
Your role
As an Cloud Data Analyst, your main responsibilities will include:
Capture and analyze user requirements from legacy .NET/SQL platforms; translate into user stories and migration plans.
Design and document scalable data pipelines for cloud platforms (Databricks, Azure).
Analyze, transform, and integrate complex data sources for business and regulatory needs.
Document data flows, business logic, and integration points for migration and knowledge transfer.
Ensure data quality, lineage, and governance; set standards for accuracy and completeness.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams (risk modelers, data architects, developers).
Facilitate agile ceremonies and support UAT/release planning.
Act as a liaison between business and technical stakeholders, driving platform consistency.
Your Skills and Experience
As a
Cloud Data Analyst
,
your skills and experience will ideally include:
Expert-level T-SQL for querying and performance tuning.
Experience with Azure or other cloud data solutions, such as AWS or similar platforms
Strong experience in data modeling (conceptual, logical, physical) and data transformation.
Experience with data pipelines/ETL tools (Azure Data Factory, Databricks, Kafka, etc.).
Proven ability to design and support data flows in cloud environments.
Ability to analyze legacy systems, run workshops, and drive migration.
Solid stakeholder management in a matrixed environment.
Track record with agile delivery (Scrum/Kanban).
Fluency in English; additional languages (e.g., Spanish) advantageous.
Experience coordinating UAT, releases, and performance/security validation.
Nice-to-Have:
Hands-on with Databricks, Apache Spark, Delta Lake.
Understanding of regulatory reporting and risk frameworks.
Familiarity with insurance data, risk modeling, or exposure data is a plus.
Knowledge of Basel risk metrics, credit/risk exposure, or actuarial data.

Brief description of the company
Zurich is a leading multi-line insurer that serves its customers in global and local markets. With about 60,000 employees it provides a wide range of property and casualty, life insurance products and services in more than 200 countries and territories. Zurich’s customers include individuals, small businesses, and mid-sized and large companies, as well as multinational corporations.
Zurich Slovakia is a Competence Center based in Bratislava and Kosice providing highly specialized services for Zurich's headquarter in Switzerland and all Zurich Business units around the globe. Our services are focusing on all types of actuarial services – Life and Non-life, pricing and reserving, on data science and engineering, risk management, business intelligence, information technology, software asset management as well as insurance network management and technical accounting. Our Competence Center employs around 400+ staff from 20+ different countries.
At Zurich Insurance, we recognize the importance of fostering an open-minded, safe and inclusive environment for everyone. We stand with diversity and respect different backgrounds and lifestyles.
