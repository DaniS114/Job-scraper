Responsibilities
Keep data moving: Build, run, and refine data pipelines and workflows (Python, Airflow/Cloud Composer) so they're reliable, fast, and easy to change
Shape our data models: Use BigQueryand Cloud Spanner to design wellstructured, highperformance datasets for analytics and machinelearning use cases
Privacy by design: Apply leastprivilege access, encryption, audit logging, and dataquality checks to meet GDPR/CCPA.
Make things observable: Create clear dashboards and alerts, take part in an international oncall rotation, and run calm, constructive postmortems.
Requirements
Several years of experience as a Data Engineer (all genders) working with productiongrade, distributed data systems
Handson GCP experience: BigQuery, Cloud Storage, Airflow/Cloud Composer, and identity & access management
Strong Python (tested, readable code), experience with Athena and advanced SQLskills
A careful approach to data security and privacy, especially with personal data
A practical mindset, clear communication, and a strong sense of ownership
Comfortable working asynchronously with colleagues from the US Eastcoast
Excellent English skills to contribute within an international company
Preferred Qualifications
CI/CD for data (e.g., GitLab CI or Cloud Build),Terraform, and Docker
Experience with dbt or a similar transformation framework
Data catalog/lineage tools (DataHub, Collibra,BigQueryData Catalog)
Streaming data ingestion (Pub/Sub, Dataflow, Kafka) or realtime feature stores
Experience withTableau,MongoDB,PostgreSQL, and costefficient query tuning onBigQuery

We're looking for a Senior Data Engineer (all genders) to own, improve, and scale our data platform on Google Cloud. Your work keeps data flowing for BI, analytics, and product features. You'll make sensible architecture choices, set good engineering practices, patiently mentor juniors, and help others do their best work.
