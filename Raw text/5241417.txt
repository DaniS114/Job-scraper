Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We’re seeking a skilled and reliable PHP Developer to join our growing CapitalBox tech team. If you have a solid background in PHP and want to build impactful, secure, and scalable solutions for SME lending—this role is for you.
Your focus:
Develop and maintain robust PHP applications using OOP principles.
Work with XML, SQL, and JSON data formats.
Use REST and SOAP APIs to support integrations with external systems.
Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code.
Apply test-driven development (TDD) and version control (Git) best practices.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality features on time.
Utilize Linux-based development tools and basic command-line knowledge.
Support technical improvements and performance optimization.

At Multitude, we’re more than just a listed European FinTech company—we’re a team driving financial innovation. We provide digital lending and online banking services that empower consumers, SMEs, and FinTechs to grow and succeed.
