Leading the team of PSE & Support Engineers that will be responsible for supporting the installation base of Server Systems, SDS solutions, AI cluster sold in different counties (case handling, hands-on trainings, certification, compliance with vendor validation requirements)

5+ years of work experience with system integrator, either distributor or vendor
Deep understanding of modern IT technologies and trends (servers, storage, virtualization,container, backup, cloud, HPC, SDS, HCI, AI designs, Linux operating systems, databases, networking or security)
Deep knowledge of a product line and hands-on experience with servers and storage systems of at least one of the A-brands & HW leading manufacturers (e.g., Dell EMC, HPE, Nvidia, SuperMicro, Lenovo, IBM, Hitachi Vantara, NetApp, Cisco, Juniper, Arista, etc.)
Some knowledge of leading virtualization technologies (VMware, Microsoft, Citrix), and containerized approaches with according to vendors’ certifications are a strong plus

Good technical presentation skills, oral and written communication skills

Experience working in international / installation / cluster deployment environment is a strong plus
Ability to adapt to new technologies and love to learn quickly with new approaches & designs coming into IT market

Ability to multitask and work under project deadlines & limits to meet sometimes tight schedules
