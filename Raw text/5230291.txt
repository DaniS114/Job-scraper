Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a new member to join our testing team at a fast-growing technology company that is revolutionizing recruitment in the environmental sector. The team develops highly specialized software solutions and automated systems for job seekers, which are already being used successfully by hundreds of partner companies.
Requirements
Practical experience with automated testing (Tosca is highly preferred)
Responsibility for all aspects of analysis, test design, and creation of scenarios for automated tests
Execution and monitoring of automated test results (script integration and CI support)
Creation, maintenance, and error handling related to automated testing
Preparation of test data required for automated testing
Knowledge of at least one programming language (Java, JavaScript, Python, ...)
Basic knowledge of SQL/API testing
Understanding of the software development lifecycle, testing methods, and techniques
Experience with tools for managing and executing test scenarios/tickets (e.g., Jira)
Knowledge of agile development (SCRUM, LEAN, KANBAN) and use of AI/ChatGPT in testing
Documentation and reporting of results and identified defects
Strong attention to detail and result-oriented mindset
Work either in a team or independently and collaborate effectively with developers/clients
Good communication skills and ability to interpret team outputs
Good command of the German language (B2 level), as the interview will be conducted in German
