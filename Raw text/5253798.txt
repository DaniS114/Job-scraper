Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a Junior Frontend Developer to join our team on an interesting project for one of our clients operating in the heavy-machinery industry. The project is primarily web-based and offers a great opportunity to grow under the guidance of experienced developers while contributing to the frontend part of the application.
The role includes participation in meetings with US colleagues, typically scheduled between 17:00 and 19:00 CET, with flexibility to adjust working hours accordingly.
As a Junior Frontend Developer with us you will:
Contribute to the development and maintenance of web applications using Angular.
Work closely with senior developers on implementing new features.
Learn and apply frontend best practices.
Collaborate with backend developers, QA engineers, and product stakeholders.
Participate in code reviews and continuously improve code quality.
Assist in debugging and fixing issues.
Take part in agile ceremonies such as planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives.
Actively develop your technical skills and grow within the team.
