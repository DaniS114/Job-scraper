Responsibilities
We are looking for a
research-driven Product Manager with a strong Software Engineering background
to help shape the future of our products. In this role, you will explore new product opportunities, translate research insights into technical product capabilities, and work closely with engineering teams to bring innovative solutions to market.
This position is ideal for someone who combines
deep technical expertise with product thinking
and enjoys turning complex research findings into impactful product features.
Key Responsibilities
Conduct
market and technology research
to identify new product opportunities, innovations, and enhancements.
Translate research insights into
product features, specifications, and technical requirements
.
Define and maintain the
product vision and roadmap
for strategically important products.
Collaborate closely with
engineering, product development, marketing, and sales
teams to deliver successful product solutions.
Analyze
customer needs, industry trends, and competitive landscape
to guide product decisions.
Build
business cases, value propositions, and go-to-market strategies
.
Prioritize product features and improvements based on
customer value, technical feasibility, and business impact
.
Monitor product performance and define
KPIs
to measure success.
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree and 8+ years of relevant experience
(Master’s degree preferred).
Strong Software Engineering background
with the ability to understand and evaluate technical architectures and product capabilities.
Experience working with
Agile / Scrum development environments
.
Strong
research, analytical, and problem-solving skills
.
Ability to translate technical
insights into business value and product strategy
.
Excellent
communication and stakeholder management skills
.
