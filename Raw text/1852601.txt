Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our client empowers organizations worldwide to strategically acquire and optimize software investments, embrace cloud and AI technologies, and drive innovation through enhanced efficiency. They are a trusted advisor in strategic software acquisition, continual IT estate optimization, and maximizing returns on investments in cloud, data, and AI.
Cloud Platform Engineers work with our clients to develop a robust and scalable infrastructure with automation and CI/CD practices. Engineers report to the Head of Platform Engineering, they work in close collaboration with our customers DevOps and IT Security Teams. The delivery method is agile, most of the time in remote working (customer visits might be required occasionally).
AWS Landing Zone setup following vendor best practices and customer requirements
Gather requirements and provide continuous feedback loops between IT security and the DevOps teams
Design and implement Infrastructure as a Code deployment pipelines
Automate everything
Document your work and keep the documentation up to date

Experience (5+ years) working in a large complex multinational environment (Financial Services preferred)
Understanding of Identity and Access Management principles and tools
In depth experience with AWS and especially AWS Networking
Experience with networks (OSI layers, TCP/IP, Routing, VPN)
Experience with Firewalls and IT security best practices
Experience with CI/CD tooling like (GitHub, Azure DevOps or Jenkins)
Experience with scripting languages (Bash, Powershell)
Experience with programming languages like Python is nice to have
Experience with Infrastructure as a Code, especially Terraform
Experience with Docker/Kubernetes
Experience with Linux (RHEL) and Windows based environments
