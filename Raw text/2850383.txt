Support and coordinate a diverse project portfolio across construction, energy, and infrastructure sectors

Requirements
A degree in business, economics, engineering, project management or a comparable field
Strong command of English and German
Experience in project management, PMO or consulting, ideally in complex, multi-stakeholder environments such as infrastructure, energy, or construction
A proactive mindset with ability to identify gaps and support teams with practical solutions
Systems thinking and flexibility to understand connections and adapt to change
Reliability and ownership with pride in work and responsibility
