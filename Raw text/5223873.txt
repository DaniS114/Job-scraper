Job description, responsibilities and duties
About the role
At Swiss Re, our mission is to make the world more resilient by helping society understand, manage, and transfer risk. In the Specialty reinsurance business, this mission takes a very concrete form: Swiss Re supports the coverage of complex, high-severity risks such as aviation, marine, energy, cyber, and political risk – expo-sures that are essential to global trade, infrastructure, and economic stability, yet difficult or impossible to carry without reinsurance.
As Lead Specialty Architect, you play a key role in enabling this business through technology. By shaping, evolving, and safeguarding the application landscape that supports Specialty underwriting and client engage-ment, you help ensure that these risks can be assessed, managed, and transferred reliably and at scale. In doing so, your work contributes directly to the stability and effectiveness of Swiss Re’s Specialty business and, ultimately, to the resilience of the systems and industries it supports.
Key responsibilities
As Lead Specialty Architect, you are the architectural owner of the Specialty application landscape within P&C Re, with end-to-end accountability across all the architectural disciplines:
•	Own the application and technical architecture for all Specialty business applications, covering their full lifecycle from design to operations.
•	Oversee the development, maintenance, and evolution of both internal underwriting applications and externally facing solutions used by Swiss Re’s Specialty reinsurance clients.
•	Shape the Specialty business application landscape in close collaboration with Business Architecture, ensuring clear domain alignment and long-term sustainability.
•	Design and evolve the Specialty data architecture together with P&C Re Data Architecture, including data models, integration patterns, and analytical foundations.
•	Define and govern cloud-native architectures on Microsoft Azure, ensuring scalability, security, cost efficiency, and operational robustness.
•	Ensure operational stability and resilience of the application portfolio by working closely with the central Level-2 support team and the Reliability & Resilience Architecture function.
•	Actively contribute to the P&C Re Architecture chapter, helping define standards, reference architec-tures, and application blueprints.
•	Own and lead one or two cross-cutting architectural topics relevant across all P&C Re IT product are-as.
•	Foster strong, trust-based collaboration between application development teams and the Architec-ture function.
Why this role matters
This is a role for someone who wants real impact. The decisions you make will influence how Specialty reinsur-ance is underwritten, managed, and scaled at Swiss Re – and, by extension, how complex global risks are ab-sorbed and mitigated.
If you are looking for an architect role with genuine ownership, strategic relevance, and a direct connection to Swiss Re’s purpose, we would be pleased to hear from you.
