Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Build impactful products from ideation to market-ready solutions in an agile team.
Lead and inspire as a technical architect, mentor peers, and foster continuous improvement.
Collaborate and grow by sharing knowledge, learning from others, and thriving in coding challenges.
Requirements
2+ years of experience in Flutter or native iOS/Android development.
Passion for Clean Code, testing practices, and modern web standards (HTML5, CSS).
Proficiency in code reviews and maintaining high-quality standards.
Active English skills and a valid Hungarian work permit.
Nice-to-have
Bonus points for familiarity with React, Nest.js or Elixir., Experience with agile practices like Scrum or Extreme Programming., Enthusiasm for creative coding projects or open-source contributions.
What we offer
Innovate with cutting-edge technologies: Work with Flutter, React, Nest.js or Elixir on exciting projects that matter.
Flexibility and trust: Manage your time, enjoy remote work and achieve work-life balance.
Growth opportunities: Access to courses, conferences, coaching, and English lessons.
Supportive community: Shared breakfasts, team-building events, office massages, and a multicultural team.
Health benefits: Premium health insurance to keep you at your best.
How to apply
You can submit your application on the company's website, which you can access by clicking the „Apply on company page“ button.
