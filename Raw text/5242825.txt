Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an
IT Applications Administrator
to take ownership of the administration and continuous improvement of our portfolio of business and enterprise applications used across the company. In this role, you will ensure our applications are reliable, secure, well-configured, and fully aligned with business processes.
You will provide deep, hands-on administration for some platforms, while taking lighter operational ownership of others, with the application mix evolving over time. Atlassian Cloud (Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence) is currently a key platform in scope, alongside a range of other common SaaS and internal applications supporting IT, R&D, HR, Customer Care, Finance, and Operations.
You can join us in the
Prague
or
Bratislava
office.
What You'll Do:
Own the administration and operational stability of a
portfolio of business applications
across the company
Act as technical owner for assigned applications, including configuration, access management, and documentation
Ensure applications are secure, reliable, and aligned with internal standards and business processes
Administer
Atlassian Cloud (Jira, Jira Service Management, Confluence)
as a major platform:
configure workflows, issue types, permissions, automations, and service          project
maintain reporting, dashboards, and basic platform governance
Support and gradually take ownership of
additional SaaS and enterprise applications
beyond Atlassian
Onboard new applications into the environment and assume operational responsibility
Work with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into application configurations
Manage users, roles, and permissions in line with SSO, identity, and security policies
Identify access, configuration, or process risks and support audits or reviews when required
Maintain clear, up-to-date application documentation, standards, and operating procedures
Coordinate with infrastructure, security, and vendors to resolve issues and implement changes
What You'll Bring:
Proven experience owning and administering
multiple business or enterprise applications
in a corporate environment
Hands-on administrative experience with
Atlassian Jira, Jira Service Management, and Confluence
(Cloud preferred)
Ability to independently take ownership of new applications and bring them into a stable, governed state
Solid understanding of service-oriented processes and ITSM principles
Strong grasp of access control, role design, and permission models
Analytical, structured mindset with the ability to translate business requirements into sustainable application configurations
Fluent English (spoken and written)

Brief description of the company
We are Sygic and we have been changing the way people travel since 2004.
We are known for the Sygic GPS Navigation mobile application, the first navigation app on the Appstore and the second on Google Play, but today we are much more! After all these years, we extended, but the focus and start-up mentality remain.
Our users are considered part of our development family. We listen to their suggestions and try to ensure that our product still brings them added value.
As part of the Eurowag Group, which comprises a dynamic portfolio of innovative companies, including us – Sygic but also fireTMS, Inelo, WebEye, Princip, KomTeS, and CVS, we collectively play pivotal roles in creating sustainable financial and technological solutions. Together we work to simplify the life of fleet companies worldwide.
At Eurowag Group, we are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace. We encourage women, men, and individuals of all backgrounds to apply and join our team.
