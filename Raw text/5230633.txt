Job description, responsibilities and duties
Do you love breaking things—so they can be built better?
We’re looking for an experienced
QA Tester / Test Engineer
who has a sharp eye for detail, a passion for quality, and the curiosity to dig deep into how software really works. If you enjoy collaborating with developers and helping deliver reliable, high-quality products, we’d love to meet you.
What You’ll Be Doing
As part of our team, you’ll play a key role in ensuring our software meets the highest standards:
•	Test web and/or mobile applications from the user’s perspective
•	Validate backend services through API testing
•	Design, create, and maintain clear test scenarios and test cases
•	Perform both manual and automated tests
•	Identify, document, and track defects, working closely with developers, analysts, and fellow testers
•	Actively contribute ideas to improve our testing practices and processes
