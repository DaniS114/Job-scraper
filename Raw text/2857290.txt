Project Planning & Delivery
: Plan, execute, and monitor projects by defining objectives, roadmaps, milestones, and KPIs to ensure successful delivery
Process & System Transformation

Stakeholder Management
: Collaborate closely with internal departments (e.g. Procurement, Sales, Logistics, IT) as well as external service providers to ensure effective coordination and knowledge transfer
