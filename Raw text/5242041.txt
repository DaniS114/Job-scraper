Job description, responsibilities and duties
At Siemens Healthineers, we pioneer breakthroughs in healthcare. For our Development Center in Bratislava, we are looking for an experienced and motivated
Scrum Master
to join our
Diagnostics - Point of Care Testing
business unit.
In this role, you will support two cross-functional Scrum teams developing and maintaining critical software solutions used in medical diagnostics. Your work will directly contribute to reliable, high-quality healthcare technology used worldwide.
We are looking for a colleague to cover a maternity leave, with the possibility of continuing in the position based on proven performance.
Your Responsibilities
Act as Scrum Master for
two Scrum teams
:
Team 1: Development of a modern
web application (C#, Angular)
Team 2: Maintenance and enhancement of a
legacy Windows-based application (C++)
Facilitate Scrum ceremonies (Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Planning, Reviews, Retrospectives)
Guide teams in Agile best practices and foster a culture of continuous improvement
Support teams in removing impediments and improving delivery flow
Ensure effective backlog management and transparency using
Azure DevOps
Collaborate closely with Product Owners, developers, testers, and stakeholders
Promote self-organization, accountability, and cross-team collaboration
Support the adoption and continuous improvement of software development processes
Your Profile
Proven experience as a
Scrum Master
in a software development environment
Good understanding of the
software development lifecycle (SDLC)
Experience with or solid overview of Agile methodologies and frameworks
Practical experience with
Azure DevOps
(boards, backlogs, sprint tracking)
Strong facilitation, coaching, and communication skills
Ability to work with multiple teams simultaneously
Fluent in
English
(spoken and written)
Nice to Have
Scrum certification (CSM, PSM, or equivalent)
Experience in regulated environments (medical, healthcare, diagnostics)
Experience working in an international, distributed environment
Experience, or an overview on:
Web technologies (C#, Angular) - awareness level is sufficient
Legacy systems or Windows-based applications - advantage
If you are passionate about Agile practices and want to contribute to innovative diagnostic solutions that impact patients worldwide, we look forward to receiving your application.
