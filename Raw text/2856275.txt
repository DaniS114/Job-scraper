Responsibilities
The intern will support the Fleet Energy Performance team in developing innovative solutions for energy optimization and emissions reduction. The role includes significant front-end development responsibilities and integration of advanced AI models to enhance user experience and decision-making.
Front-End Development & AI Integration:
Design and implement modern front-end interfaces for energy performance dashboards and AI-driven applications
Develop interactive visualizations and compelling data storytelling components for business stakeholders
Collaborate closely with data scientists to integrate machine learning models into user-facing tools
Ensure seamless data flow between back-end and front-end systems through API integrations
Support testing and deployment of AI-powered features in production environment
Contribute to iterative development cycles with a focus on scalability and user experience
Innovation & Technical Support:
Assist in building and optimizing digital solutions for energy monitoring and emissions analysis
Support the continuous improvement of existing analytics tools and dashboards
Work with modern development environments and version control systems
Contribute ideas for enhancing AI applications and user interaction concepts
Requirements
Currently enrolled in or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field
Strong front-end development skills, particularly in JavaScript and React (or comparable frameworks)
Experience working with Git and development environments such as VS Code
Solid understanding of data-driven applications and attention to detail
Excellent English communication skills, both written and verbal
Ability to translate complex data into clear and engaging narratives for business stakeholders
A curious mindset, quick comprehension skills, and a collaborative team spirit
Nice to Have:
Experience with prompt engineering for LLMs
Familiarity with AI and data products
Basic knowledge of SQL, Python, or Power BI
Interest in fuel consumption, energy efficiency, and emission
Tools & Technologies:
Front-end: JavaScript, React, HTML/CSS
Back-end: Python, SQL
Git, VS Code

Company info
TUI Cruises GmbH
is one of the leading cruise operators in the German-speaking world. The joint venture of TUI AG and the US cruise company Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd. employs around 750 people in Hamburg and Berlin. On board of the ships, 136 positions are managed directly by TUI Cruises GmbH. A fleet of eight ships sails for the brand
Mein Schiff®
. Since the end of 2020, the brand
Hapag-Lloyd Cruises
has also been part of TUI Cruises GmbH with a fleet of currently five ships. The Mein Schiff® fleet offers contemporary vacation cruises at sea in the premium segment (Mein Schiff 1 to Mein Schiff 7 and Mein Schiff Relax). Hapag-Lloyd Cruises is the leading luxury and expedition cruise brand in the German-speaking world with a 130-year history. (MS EUROPA, MS EUROPA 2 & HANSEATIC nature, HANSEATIC inspiration and HANSEATIC spirit). TUI Cruises operates one of the most modern, environmentally and climate-friendly fleets in the world. In the process of sustainable growth, one more new ship is planned by 2026.
We live the values of trust, appreciation, and sustainability. We are therefore looking for employees who internalize these values just as much as we do and bring them to life.
Thanks to our organizational culture, we treat each other as equals, from interns to management, with openness and respect.
TUI Cruises GmbH is looking for a full-time employee to start on March 1, 2026, for a fixed term of six months at its Hamburg site:
Intern – Front-End Developer (all gender) (Fleet Energy Performance)
