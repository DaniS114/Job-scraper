Responsibilities
We are seeking a Field Applications Engineer (FAE) to support our customers in Central Europe, with a primary focus on MCU hardware and software solutions. The ideal candidate will have a strong technical background in microcontroller technology and a passion for helping customers integrate our solutions into their designs. This role is based in Munich, Germany, and requires close collaboration with sales, product, and engineering teams to ensure customer success. German language proficiency is a plus.
Key Responsibilities
• Provide technical support to customers in Central Europe, helping them design and implement GigaDevice MCU solutions.
• Work closely with R&D and product teams to ensure successful customer integration and resolve technical challenges.
• Conduct technical presentations, hands-on training, and product demonstrations to customers, partners, and distributors.
• Develop and maintain strong customer relationships, understanding their technical requirements and recommending optimal solutions.
• Collaborate with sales teams to drive customer engagements and support design wins.
• Stay up to date with industry trends and competitor products to provide strategic technical insights.
Requirements
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field (equivalent to a 4-year college degree).
• Experience in MCU hardware and software development, including embedded system design, firmware development, and debugging.
• Strong understanding of microcontroller architectures, peripherals, and development tools.
• Experience with C/C++ programming, RTOS, and embedded system debugging tools.
• Excellent problem-solving and communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to customers.
• Ability to work independently and travel within the Central Europe region as required.
• Fluency in English is required; German language proficiency is a plus.
What we offer
Be part of a global leader in semiconductor technology with a strong reputation for innovation.
• Work on cutting-edge MCU and semiconductor solutions that impact industries worldwide.
• Collaborate with a diverse and talented global team.
• Competitive compensation, benefits, and career development opportunities.
If you are a technically skilled and customer-focused professional looking to make an impact in the semiconductor industry, we encourage you to apply!
Company info
GigaDevice is a global leader in semiconductor technology, specializing in high-performance Flash memory, microcontrollers (MCUs), and advanced semiconductor solutions. With a strong presence worldwide, we are committed to innovation and providing best-in-class support to our customers.
