Company info
Epiroc is an important part of a sustainable society and a global productivity partner for mining and infrastructure customers. With groundbreaking technologies, Epiroc develops and delivers innovative and safe equipment such as drilling rigs, rock excavation and construction equipment, and tools for surface and underground applications. In addition, the company offers first-class service and other customer support, as well as solutions for automation, digitalization, and electrification. Its dedicated employees support customers in more than 150 countries and work closely with them.
