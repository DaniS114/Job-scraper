Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an consultant responsible for accounting analysis, ERP implementation, and financial process setup for various clients (manufacturing, trade, e-commerce, services). The role is fully remote.
Key Responsibilities
Analyze clients’ accounting and financial processes
Design and optimize accounting workflows based on business type
Implement and configure Odoo economic and accounting modules
Ensure proper accounting and financial setup in Odoo
Test new Odoo versions and validate accounting functionality
Provide accounting implementation or accounting services if required
Advise clients on best practices in accounting and ERP configuration
Work with multiple clients (some accounting directly in Odoo)
Company on whose behalf the position is being filled
Financial consultancy, intermediation of financial services and insurance
Slovakian accounting company.
