Responsibilities
Work closely with internal project teams, customers, and partners, acting as the primary technical point of contact for assigned engagements
Own and lead technical project planning for assigned scope, including estimations, feasibility assessments, dependencies, and risks
Drive and define solution and architecture design, translating business and technical requirements into scalable, maintainable implementation concepts
Ensure the customer's technical environment is enabled to meet deployment milestones and solution requirements
Take end-to-end ownership of assigned implementation areas, ensuring adherence to architecture, best practices and project goals
Provide technical leadership and mentoring to (junior) consultants throughout build, test and deployment phases
Lead solution validation activities and ensure customers fully test and understand solution capabilities within project scope
Own and drive issue resolution including hands-on correction, re-design/re-build, change management, and coordination of bug reporting and escalations
Lead production rollout activities including technical migration, hypercare, handover to support and project closeout
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in a technical field or equivalent professional experience
Good knowledge of professional services delivery methodologies (Agile, Waterfall) and ability to lead technical delivery within these frameworks
Strong experience leading customer discovery, technical workshops and requirements analysis
Deep technical expertise in at least one programming or scripting language (e.g. Java, .NET, Python, PHP) with architectural understanding
Advanced experience with ESB / digitalization platforms and middleware-based solution architectures
Advanced knowledge of deployment and integration environments (APIs, containers, operating systems, databases, networking, virtualization, cloud services)
Proven experience in troubleshooting complex issues, performance tuning, and escalation management
Ability to mentor and guide technical delivery teams and ensure adherence to architecture and best practices
Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office and Atlassian tools (Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket); experience with design and project planning tools is a plus
Fluent Business English required; additional languages and international project experience strongly preferred
Company info
For over 25 years, SoftProject GmbH has been developing smart solutions for the digital world. With our low-code platform, we simplify business processes across industries, making them faster, more efficient, and easier to manage. Our customers digitize workflows effortlessly using intuitive graphical tools—programming skills? Not required.
Today, over 300 companies worldwide rely on our product and our team of more than 200 colleagues continues to drive SoftProject's success in Germany, Spain, and France. You can expect exciting projects, modern technologies, and a workplace where ideas matter, team spirit thrives, and innovation is fun.
Every day, in every part of our company, we combine excellence, precision, and creativity with passion. We work across Europe in diverse teams - yet still as one ONECompany. Different perspectives, common goal: creating solutions that really make a difference.
As a Senior Consultant, you act as the technical driving force behind complex customer engagements, translating solution architectures into robust, scalable implementations. Leveraging deep expertise and a trusted-advisor mindset, you guide customers and internal teams to deliver high-quality solutions while resolving technical challenges in close collaboration with R&D, Support, and Delivery.
