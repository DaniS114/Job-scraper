We are part of Erste Group – the largest banking group in Central and Eastern Europe with more than 2,500 branches and over 45,000 employees. Our more than 2,000 IT experts and enthusiasts are the bank's Digital Muscle.
We’re growing our internal CRM team and are looking for an experienced Dynamics 365 CRM expert who can independently deliver high-quality solutions across the Sales and Customer Insights Journeys modules. You’ll work on complex customizations, integrations and continuous improvement of our CRM organizations with exposure to financial industry use cases.
What to do:
Provide Operational Support (Incident and Problem Management) and Change Management for Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Insights Journeys.
Do CRM customizations and develop custom web resources (JS, HTML) and plugins.
Enhance and maintain integrations (SSIS integration packages, Cloud Flows).
Work on Power BI paginated reports.
Contribute to agile delivery using Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and CI/CD practices.
Explore new release wave features, including Copilot, and proactively propose improvements.
Diagnose and reverse-engineer existing logic to deliver stable and scalable solutions.
Collaborate with business stakeholders to translate requirements into robust technical designs.
You check these boxes:
You bring solid hands‑on experience with Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Power Automate, Microsoft SQL Server, and Google Cloud Platform.
You’re confident in plugin development, JavaScript/TypeScript, and building SSIS integrations.
You can dive deep into technical problems, analyze them effectively, and deliver clean, well‑tested solutions.
Experience in the financial industry is a plus.
You hold (or are working toward) certifications such as Power Platform Functional Consultant Associate, Power BI Data Analyst Associate, or Power Platform Developer Associate.
You take a structured, detail‑oriented approach to development and own tasks end‑to‑end, while staying curious, exploring new technologies, and collaborating smoothly with the team even when working independently.
Fluent English, German is a plus.
