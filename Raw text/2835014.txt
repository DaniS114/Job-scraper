Responsibilities
Development of complex testing technology and strategies (hardware/software) for optical sensors in accordance with the specification of the development order and in consultation with the project manager PLT
Planning and adherence to deadlines, costs and quality in the project tasks processed
Collaboration in project teams in the sense of simultaneous engineering between product and equipment development
Collaboration in optimizing the product specification with regard to the requirements of measurement/testing technology
Creation of test specifications
Creation of requirement specifications for the external assignment of test equipment production
Test equipment analyses and approvals and provision of the determined test data to the development department
Handover of test equipment/systems to production and training of production staff
Collaboration on FMEA, creation of the process validation plan and pilot series releases
Creating project schedules in coordination with project manager PLT and other specialist departments
Advising the industrial engineer on the creation of the production concept with regard to the requirements of test engineering (incl. occupational safety, economic efficiency, binding standards, etc.)
Timely involvement of the target TPUs in test equipment modification and documentation to take account of local conditions and requirements
Training of employees of the target TPUs
Support at service level 1 for problems with test systems in production operations
Adaptation of existing measuring/testing/calibration equipment to changed testing requirements (e.g. when changing the product specification) and with the aim of reducing manufacturing costs
Creating test equipment documentation (test instructions, parts lists, maintenance plan, etc.) for the test systems provided on the basis of the applicable P+F documentation standards
Requirements
Degree in electrical engineering, or mechatronics engineering with relevant experience in electronics and electrical engineering or with a secondary-level qualification in electrical engineering and relevant experience
Several years of specialist professional experience in the development and commissioning of complex measurement/testing technology and optical calibration systems
Good knowledge of electrical measurement technology
Good knowledge of C/C# programming languages
Good knowledge of statistical process control and test equipment approval
Tasks are processed according to general guidelines with a wide scope of action

Company info

Our company has been operating in Hungary since 1997 as a member of the international Pepperl+Fuchs group. At our Veszprém site, nearly 450 employees are engaged in the production and sale of plastic parts and cable assemblies.
