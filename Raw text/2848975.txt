Responsibilities
Lead and mentor a distributed team of test engineers
Design and optimise CI/CD pipelines using GitLab and related tools

Collaborate with development teams to diagnose issues and improve software quality
Requirements
Experience leading software testing or QA for embedded systems
Proficient in C programming and common scripting languages such as Python
Experience with system validation, debugging, and automation frameworks
Analytical attitude with focus on continuous improvement and quality metrics
Nice-to-have
Hands-on experience with embedded Linux internals, boot flow, and system bring-up, Familiarity with integrating hardware or simulators into CI pipelines, Knowledge of embedded build systems such as Yocto, Buildroot, or CMake, Interest or involvement in open-source development

Company info
Working in software engineering at Arm is about shaping a future of technology for which we don't yet have words. Our engineers lead innovation in physical IP, AI and machine learning, cloud architecture, automotive tech, and every aspect of computing that matters.
Spanning software's full spectrum, from compilers to AI-powered IoT and beyond, your code can enable virtually anything to be produced on silicon. Whether you're developing award-winning VR gaming or life-enhancing medical equipment, you can improve the lives of millions by being your brilliant self at Arm.
