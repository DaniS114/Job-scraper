Purpose
Senior Camunda Developer holds a lead role within a SCRUM team and is responsible for designing, developing, and implementing workflow-based solutions using Camunda. The focus is on automation and orchestration of business processes across distributed systems.
The role covers end-to-end process lifecycle: BPMN modeling, DMN decision design, REST integrations, job worker implementation, error handling, versioning, and deployment governance.
The developer provides independent technical expertise, ensures high-quality and resilient process solutions, mentors team members in workflow best practices, contributes to architectural decisions, and continuously drives innovation in business process automation.
What will you do?
Design and development of complex workflow-based IT solutions using Camunda
Analysis of business requirements and translation into BPMN/DMN process models
Implementation of automation solutions using Camunda and related technologies
Development in Agile (SCRUM) environment
Creation of technical and process documentation
Designing scalable and resilient process architectures
Participation in workflow automation R&D initiatives
Close collaboration with developers, analysts, and business stakeholders during implementation and testing
Training users on process-driven solutions
Mentoring junior developers and supporting onboarding activities
You will succeed if you:
Have several years of experience in enterprise IT, preferably in workflow and business process automation
Are advanced experience in process-driven design using BPMN, DMN, and service orchestration
Have strong SQL knowledge and understanding of workflow persistence and data modeling
Have experience integrating distributed systems via REST APIs and job worker patterns
Have experience with Git and Maven
Have understanding of Cloud-native architectures, DevOps, containers, and CI/CD
Have good knowledge of IT infrastructure relevant to workflow engines
Have solid communication skills and experience collaborating directly with business stakeholders and customers
Are able to work independently and in Agile (SCRUM) teams
Are self-motivated with strong ownership and communication skills
Possible specialization:
Advanced experience in Camunda 8 (Zeebe, Operate, Tasklist, Optimize)
Advanced experience in BPMN 2.0 modeling
Advanced experience in DMN / FEEL
Advanced experience in REST API integration
Advanced experience in Job Worker implementation
Advanced experience in process resilience and error handling
Advanced experience in process versioning and deployment governance
