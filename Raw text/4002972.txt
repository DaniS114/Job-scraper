Information about the position
Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced Systems Engineer to join our team. You will be responsible for designing, deploying, and maintaining server, network, and virtualization environments across on-premise and cloud platforms. Your mission is to ensure stability, performance, security, and scalability of all infrastructure services.
The ideal candidate has deep hands-on experience with enterprise-grade systems, strong troubleshooting skills, and a methodical approach to infrastructure management. We value reliability, attention to detail, and the ability to work both independently and as part of a team.

Personality requirements and skills
- Minimum 3 years of experience as a Systems Engineer / Infrastructure Engineer / System Administrator
- Advanced knowledge of Linux (Ubuntu, Debian or RHEL)
- Experience with virtualization platforms (VMware, Proxmox)
- Knowledge of containerization (Docker)
- Solid understanding of networking (TCP/IP, VLANs, routing, switching, VPN)
- Experience with storage technologies (NAS, RAID, Ceph, or ZFS)
- Knowledge of Radius, LDAP, DNS, DHCP

- Scripting skills: Bash, Python
- Database administration experience (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MSSQL, MariaDB)
