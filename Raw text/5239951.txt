Job description, responsibilities and duties
Our client is a globally focused fintech company based in Bratislava that aims to transform the way people understand and manage their finances.
Your Role
As a Senior Fullstack Developer, you’ll be driving development across the entire stack—building polished frontend experiences while ensuring backend systems are robust, scalable, and high-performance. You’ll work closely with CTO, product manager, scientists and other colleagues. You’ll also mentor team members and help define coding standards and workflows.
What You’ll Do
Develop end-to-end implement new product features end-to-end
Build modern, responsive frontend interfaces (Vue, React)
Maintain backend services (Laravel, FastAPI, Flask, PHP) powering the app
Collaborate on architecture and technical decisions
Write clean, well-tested, and maintainable code
Improve team workflows, CI/CD pipelines, and project processes
Ensure the application is fast, secure, and reliable
