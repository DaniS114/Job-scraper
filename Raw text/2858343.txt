Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Redesigning the VR video player experience (navigation, controls, usability, interaction patterns)
Adapting short-form video experiences into VR (think “TikTok-like flow”, but in spatial environments)
Improving core UX across web and mobile apps
Redesigning UI elements such as player icons, controls, and key product screens
Contributing to and scaling the design system (components, states, consistency across platforms)
Use research, usage data, and qualitative insights to understand user behaviour and inform design decisions
Build interactive prototypes (especially for new VR interaction patterns) to test ideas and align with Engineering
Collaborating closely with Product, Engineering, and stakeholders to validate solutions and ship improvements fast
Keep an eye on emerging VR/spatial UI patterns and apply relevant best practices to the product
Requirements
4+ years of product design experience, ideally in in-house product teams
Strong portfolio with shipped work in consumer products and complex UI flows (e.g., content platforms, streaming, subscriptions, social/video, gaming, fitness)
Confident Figma user (UI design, prototyping, component libraries)
Hands-on experience building or maintaining scalable design systems across platforms
Experience designing for multiple surfaces (VR and/or web and/or mobile) and the ability to adapt patterns between them
Strong collaboration with engineers (handoff, constraints, iteration) and comfort working in short feedback loops
Ability to translate product goals and user needs into clear solutions, and explain your reasoning to stakeholders
Exposure to VR/XR or spatial UI principles
Adobe tools and/or motion / 3D / animation workflows
Leadership/mentoring experience (ideally 3+ designers)
Comfortable working in an environment with ambiguity and fast iteration
Comfortable working with products that include age-restricted (18+) sections
English С1 (spoken) and Russian fluency
What we offer
A long-term B2B engagement with flexible working hours.
A hands-on role with real ownership: your design decisions ship fast and directly affect a large, active user base.
Work on a product at the intersection of VR, interactive media, and next-gen content consumption — a space that’s evolving rapidly and still has few established patterns.
Clear growth track into Design Lead once you ramp up and start driving outcomes independently.
Remote-first setup with collaboration aligned to European working hours and flexible scheduling.
A culture built around craft, accountability, and collaboration — ideas are discussed openly and decisions are made quickly.
Close collaboration with Product and Engineering, with short feedback loops and the ability to test and iterate without heavy bureaucracy.
Tools and equipment support from day one (everything you need to work effectively).
The opportunity to shape how immersive UX is done in practice — not just “design screens”, but influence interaction patterns and product direction.
