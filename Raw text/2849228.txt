Company info
Shiwaforce is a rapidly growing 200-person technology company with a startup spirit in the domestic market of innovative online solutions. It builds or transforms online and mobile services, enriched with new user experiences, in accordance with the expectations of the constantly changing digital environment. Its domestic and international clients include leading large companies and organizations such as OTP, Magyar Telekom, T-Systems, 4iG, Yettel, MBH, or Central Bank of Hungary. Its leading product is the now 8th generation Shiwa CMS, which helps large companies to increase their online potential and effectively reach their million customer base.
