Responsibilities
Build enterprise cloud infrastructure that provides European data sovereignty and hyperscaler-grade capabilities. You'll work on SAP Cloud Infrastructure, solving complex distributed systems challenges at scale: multi-region networking, container orchestration, observability, and the APIs that connect them.
You will contribute to building a sovereign, privacy-first AI cloud for the European market that makes it easy to securely train, fine-tune, and deploy foundation and domain models with strict data residency and isolation.
You'll develop solutions using Go, Kubernetes, and cloud-native technologies, tackling problems like: How do you autoscale thousands of workloads across clusters? How do you build resilient control planes and service meshes? How do you design APIs that handle massive traffic spikes?
Your work will power SAP's production systems and thousands of customer environments. You'll contribute to infrastructure that enables organizations to run mission-critical applications with the performance and reliability they expect from leading cloud platforms.
In your role as Developer, you'll contribute to building SAP Cloud Infrastructure through hands-on development of enterprise cloud services that provide European data sovereignty and hyperscaler-grade capabilities. You'll implement features and components for distributed systems that handle significant scale, working on Kubernetes orchestration, networking, and storage solutions that power customer environments.
You'll develop and maintain cloud-native infrastructure components using Go, Kubernetes, and related CNCF technologies, solving practical challenges in multi-region deployments, API development, and system integration. Your work will involve implementing features for auto-scaling clusters, building resilient workloads, and creating APIs that handle substantial traffic loads.
Contributing to production systems that serve SAP's customer base, you'll help build infrastructure that enables organizations to run mission-critical applications with enterprise-grade performance and reliability. Your code will support European organizations in maintaining data sovereignty while accessing powerful cloud capabilities that compete with leading cloud platforms.
Requirements
What you bring
Programming Mastery: Strong experience developing in Go or Python, with a solid understanding of concurrent programming, APIs, and distributed systems design
Kubernetes and/or Openstack expertise:
Kubernetes (satisfies requirement): Hands-on experience operating and developing for Kubernetes in production (cluster management, workload orchestration, and debugging distributed applications); CKA/CKAD/CKS and experience building Kubernetes operators/controllers are strong pluses.
OpenStack (satisfies requirement): Strong OpenStack development/operations background (e.g., Nova/Neutron/Octavia/Cinder/Keystone, networking/storage troubleshooting, upgrades/automation); experience integrating with Kubernetes is a plus. Hands-on experience deploying, tuning, and operating Ceph in production, ideally as storage backends for OpenStack services.
Cloud-Native Ecosystem: Familiarity with the broader CNCF landscape (Helm, Prometheus, Flux, Istio, etc.), Linux fundamentals, and container networking
Cloud Expertise: Familiarity with at least one of the multiple major cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), including advanced features and best practices
Advanced Development Practices: Expertise in Git workflows, end-to-end CI/CD pipelines, and extensive experience with test-driven development and code reviews
Growth Mindset: 5+ years of software development experience, with proven adaptability and a strong focus on continuous learning, especially in distributed systems
Collaboration Skills: Excellent communication abilities and extensive experience leading agile development teams, fostering a culture of collaboration and mentorship
Open-Source Contribution: Strong enthusiasm for contributing to open-source projects
Security and Compliance: Awareness of industry standards related to security and compliance and experience integrating regulatory requirements into architectural patterns
Cultural Adaptability: Experience working across cultures and languages with strong communication skills for diverse, distributed teams
Soft skills: Excellent presentation, communication and problem-solving skills, have a thorough way of working with accuracy, take proactive initiatives, self-dependent and structured mode of working, high level of commitment and reliability, ready to work in a constantly changing, floating environment, fun working in a multinational team
