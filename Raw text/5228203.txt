Brief description of the company
Ventum Consulting is an international technology and management consulting company with more than 170 employees across Austria, Germany, Poland and China. We support organizations in the strategic development of their business processes and strengthen IT as a key driver of innovation.
A core focus of our consulting services lies in Identity & Access Management (IAM) and IT Security. Working in interdisciplinary teams, we combine business expertise, technological competence and entrepreneurial thinking to deliver sustainable and future-proof solutions for our clients.
Our employees work in an environment characterized by flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and a high degree of individual responsibility and creative freedom. Personal development, continuous knowledge sharing and collaboration on equal footing are central elements of our company culture.
