Henkel operates worldwide with leading brands and technologies in two exciting business areas: Consumer Brands (Laundry & Home Care and Beauty Care) and Adhesive Technologies. Our success is built on constant innovation and people who strive for excellence. Working at Henkel is much more than just a job. It's a passion. Have you got what it takes?
Your role
Join a great agile team in the area of IT solutions for data and analytics, where you manage agile mindset and frameworks like Scrum, SAFe and Design Thinking and develop the team in self-organization.
Play a key role in shaping Henkel’s Digital Research and Development Platform within a scaled agile environment
Use Azure DevOps effectively to set clear standards that strengthen the team’s shared understanding of product goals and user stories
Guide the team by visualizing progress and ensuring transparency toward sprint goals
Act as a servant leader who supports the team and stakeholders, aligning people, processes, and tools while solving conflicts when they arise
Support and coach the product owner and development team to deliver customer value
Foster a collaborative, motivated, and focused team culture where product goals and continuous improvement matters
Shape your own career through training, development programs, and internal mobility
Your skills
Bachelor’s degree and 5+ years of IT project management experience, including work as a Scrum Master and/or Agile Project Manager
Strong foundation in Agile frameworks such as Scrum, SAFe (with experience supporting multiple ARTs), Kanban, and BizDevOps
Exceptional communication and facilitation skills, thriving in a highly international and intercultural business environment
Change advocate who actively drives adoption of new ways of working and supports execution of complex IT initiatives
Proficient in Azure DevOps, virtual whiteboard tools (e.g., Miro, Mural), and Microsoft Office
