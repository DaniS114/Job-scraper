Responsibilities
Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications and services supporting shared tools such as our logging and monitoring tool and our ticket and request handling
Develop integrations and APIs connecting these services with other internal systems to improve automation, observability, and collaboration
Set technical requirements and contribute to design for shared automation tools, including the Workshop Suite internal chatbot
Ensure reliability, performance, and cost efficiency of the shared development environment in collaboration with AWS infrastructure specialists
Build intuitive internal tools and user interfaces that simplify workflows and provide visibility for development and support teams
Implement CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and monitoring to ensure high quality and maintainability across shared systems
Drive continuous improvement in architecture, tooling, and processes to keep TRATON's development environment modern and sustainable
Requirements
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience
Proven experience as a Full Stack Developer in cloud-based environments (AWS or similar)
Strong skills in C#, .NET Core, and modern frontend frameworks (e.g., Angular, React, TypeScript)
Experience with DevOps practices including CI/CD, Docker, and Terraform
Familiarity with monitoring and observability tools (e.g., DataDog, CloudWatch, Grafana) and with service management systems such as ServiceNow
Competence in CI/CD, automation, containerization, and infrastructure-as-code tools (Docker, Terraform, etc.)
Service-minded, communicative and collaborative, focused on providing reliable tools and systems that help others work effectively
Curious and solution-oriented, enjoys exploring and implementing new technologies that improve internal efficiency and balances modernization with stability and long-term sustainability
Proactive and self-driven, takes ownership of shared services from design to operation

Your working environment
TRATON Research & Development (R&D) is undergoing a major transformation to establish a strong, global Group R&D organization. Our goal is to deliver a time-critical roadmap, deliver outstanding customer value, and drive innovation across our brands—Scania, MAN, INTERNATIONAL and Volkswagen Truck & Bus. This transformation will strengthen our competitiveness and position Group R&D as a key business partner within TRATON Group. We are now looking for you to be part of this exciting change journey, shaping the future of Group R&D.
At TRATON R&D, you are an important part of something bigger. Joining us means gaining access to the ins and outs of the entire transportation industry. As part of a global team of industry experts, you get to think bigger, experience more, and reach further. Being bigger also means being stronger. Together with our brands, we have the collective power to transform the transportation.
We see the individuality of our employees as a strength and welcome diverse applications from people with different backgrounds. If you need support during your application, please contact us.
