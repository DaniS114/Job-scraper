Collaborate closely with the Solution Architect to design, document, and specify frontend implementation concepts aligned to business requirements.
Develop, customize, and integrate SAP Commerce frontend components, ensuring high code quality through comprehensive unit and integration tests.
Lead debugging, troubleshooting, and frontend performance optimization efforts to enhance user experience.
Actively contribute to team meetings and discussions, mentoring junior developers and driving frontend best practices.
Support the global rollout, operation, and continuous evolution of the e-commerce platform with a strong focus on the frontend user interface and customer journey.
