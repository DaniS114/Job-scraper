Product Owner%Are you a hands-on product builder who thrives on turning complex challenges into functional solutions? Then join Neho as a Product Owner and take the lead in delivering high-impact tools that redefine the real estate experience for thousands of Swiss homeowners and buyers.Neho is one of Switzerland's leading real estate brokerage companies, redefining the industry through innovation. We combine human expertise with powerful digital solutions to deliver a faster, more transparent, and more efficient real estate experience. Our approach has been recognised with prestigious awards, including the SEF AwardWhat truly sets us apart is our fixed-price model, offering clients a modern alternative to traditional commission-based services, powered by advanced technology.If you are bold, adaptable, and thrive under complex challenges, you are in the right place. Our environment is fast-paced, entrepreneurial, and driven by continuous learning. As Product Owner, you will play a pivotal role in shaping the digital ecosystem that empowers both our customers and our teams to navigate the complexities of property transactions with efficiency and ease.Your impact will extend far beyond Neho: together, we aim not only to lead the market, but to fundamentally reinvent how real estate works.Location: This role can be based at our HQ in Lausanne or our office in Zurich, with a flexible hybrid work arrangement.Your Tasks:Backlog Management: Ensure the team always has a well-prioritized, clearly described backlog with properly defined user storiesDelivery & Execution: Remove obstacles, troubleshoot immediate technical hurdles, and ensure successful completion of sprint and release goals through a hands-on approach.Release Planning & Communication: Lead your team's product release cycle, set clear expectations, and publish relevant release notes and user guidesStakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with designers, commercial roles, and end-users to deliver solutions that meet real-world needsStrategic Contribution: Act as a sparring partner to shape the broader product vision, strategy, and innovation projectsMarket & User Research: Monitor user satisfaction and analyse key performance metrics to drive continuous improvementQuality Assurance: Collaborate closely with our QA team to ensure new developments meet our standardsProduct Communication: Together with designers, write clear and concise UI texts and translations to enhance the user experienceWhat we offer:Enjoy full end-to-end ownership of your product with the authority to drive implementation, challenge the status quo, and own the outcome from conception to launch of new featuresA unique career opportunity to join a fast-growing and award-winning scale-up within the real estate industryShape the future of real estate tech in Switzerland: Have a direct impact on Neho's growth, and deliver efficient, and scalable products that give Neho a competitive edgeEnjoy flat hierarchies, quick decision-making processes, and the freedom to work autonomously and leave your markBe part of an energetic, forward-thinking team spanning our offices in Lausanne and Zurich as well as our TechLab in RomaniaThrive in an entrepreneurial, innovative culture that values transparency, fairness, and strong team supportCompetitive benefits including 5 weeks of vacation + extra days based on tenure, fully paid maternity/paternity leave, remote work options, regular team events, and more!What you bring:Outcome-Oriented Execution: 2+ years of experience as a PO or in a similar product role. You thrive on solving ambiguous challenges, focusing on shipping practical solutions that work rather than shiny products and overly formalized processesHands-on Ownership: While you understand product strategy, you excel at the tactical PO level: Managing backlogs, working closely with squads, and staying happy to dive into the weeds to solve small bugsCustomer & UX Focus: Direct experience building consumer-facing tech (web/mobile) with a strong understanding of UX and user research; experience within the Swiss B2C market is considered an assetCross-Functional Collaboration: You excel at bridging the gap between developers, designers, and commercial teams.
