Céginformáció
At our client, innovation isn’t just a value – it’s part of their DNA. Their mission to enable a more sustainable use of the world’s food resources is driven by a dedicated global R&D team working across international innovation hubs. They develop industry-leading analytical solutions that empower food and agricultural businesses worldwide – and their journey of innovation continues to grow.
They are now looking for a professional to take on a key role as a New Product Development (NPD) Project Manager, leading the next wave of innovation within their NPD programs. This is an opportunity to drive the development of advanced, user-friendly food analysis solutions with real-world impact.
For their innovation center in Pécs, they are seeking a new colleague for the following position:
NPD PROJECT MANAGER (Supporting the Soft Flow NPD Programs)
Feladatok
Lead complex, cross-functional product development projects (hardware, software, digital integration, prototyping, certification, production setup)
Translate customer needs into product specifications and ensure an outstanding user experience
Plan and manage projects (milestones, budgets, risks, resources)
Closely collaborate with local and Danish R&D, Marketing, Sales, Service, and international teams
Ensure smooth handover to production and support successful market launch
Elvárások
Degree in Engineering or a scientific field (e.g., MSc in Engineering)
Minimum 3 years of experience in leading complex instrument or product development projects
Knowledge of hardware and software development, manufacturing, and certification processes
Experience working in a multidisciplinary environment (collaboration with R&D, production, quality)
Strong business acumen and strategic thinking
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
Structured yet flexible working style in complex environments
Juttatások
International company with a stable background
Real innovation projects with global impact
Modern working environment in Pécs
International collaboration and professional development opportunities
Flexible working hours and occasional home office option
Support for work-life balance
Occasional international travel opportunities
Place of work: Pécs
Working hours: Monday to Friday, 8 hours per day
Előnyt jelent
Experience in an international project environment
Knowledge of agile methodologies
Experience in food industry or analytical instrument development
