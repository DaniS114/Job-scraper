DESCRIPTION
Join Accenture Digital and drive high performance for our clients by enabling interactions with digital Accenture Advanced Technology center is looking for technology enthusiasts ready for a challenging IT career and focus on application development, systems administration work, and software maintenance providing practical programming and technology implementation for business.
You could have the opportunity to help clients develop applications with a cloud-first, digital- first mindset, generate the next wave of innovative technology in our state-of-the-art Accenture Labs, and provide end-to-end service and project delivery across all our Accenture Delivery Center engagements using rapid development principles such as Agile and DevOps to make software faster, flexible and more liquid
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
•	Design, develop, test, document, and deploy Salesforce solutions using Einstein 1 Platform, Lightning Web Components, and modern Apex
•	Analyze requirements and define data models
•	Build custom Salesforce applications (Apex, Triggers, LWC, Flows, integrations)
•	Evaluate new CRM technologies, product releases, and add ons (e.g., Data Cloud, DevOps Center, Salesforce Functions, Agentforce) and plan adoption
•	Monitor Agentforce capabilities for workflow automation and AI extensions
•	Support, optimize, troubleshoot, and maintain existing customizations
•	Share expertise in Salesforce development and integration patterns (secure Apex, LWC patterns, CI/CD, modular architecture, performant Flows, integration patterns)
•	Leverage experience with .NET, Java, web services, and/or SQL Server to understand or build scalable integration points between Salesforce and external systems using REST, SOAP, Platform Events, or middleware
WHAT WE
EXPECT
•	Experience in Salesforce development (Apex, LWC, declarative automation) (at least 3+ years)
•	Bachelor’s or Master’s in IT or related field
•	Background with Agentforce, AI Agents, Prompt Builder, Einstein Copilot preferred
•	Hands-on Java or C# experience and solid understanding of HTML, JavaScript, CSS, modern JS frameworks (ES6+, Node)
•	Strong communication, analytical skills, teamwork, and advanced English (B2 or above)
•	Fluent in Slovak (C1-C2)
NICE TO HAVE
•	Experience with jQuery or other JavaScript frameworks is a plus, though modern JS and LWC experience is preferred
•	Salesforce Technical or Consultant Certification (e.g., Platform Developer I/II, JavaScript Developer I, AI Specialist, Data Cloud Consultant) is a strong advantage
•	DevOps Center, Git, CI/CD, automated testing experience
•	German language is a plus
