SAP Integrations Consultant (CPI / PO/PI)
Place of work
Remote work

POSITION
DESCRIPTION
We are looking for a skilled SAP Integrations Consultant to join our growing team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining integration scenarios across our SAP landscape. Your primary focus will be on SAP Cloud Platform Integration (CPI), with additional work on SAP Process Orchestration / Process Integration (PO/PI). Exposure to S/4HANA environments is highly beneficial.
This is a great opportunity for someone with solid hands‑on experience who is ready to take ownership of integration flows, contribute to solution architecture discussions, and collaborate closely with business and technical teams.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
•Design, implement, and support integration scenarios primarily using SAP CPI (iFlows, API-based integrations, middleware orchestration).
•Maintain and enhance existing integrations on SAP PO/PI.
•Participate in integration architecture discussions and contribute to technical solution designs.
•Analyze business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
•Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to ensure seamless end‑to‑end integration.
•Support ongoing transition projects, including migrations from PO/PI to CPI.
•Work with S/4HANA integration frameworks and understand the underlying data models and communication protocols (IDocs, BAPIs, OData, SOAP, REST).
•Troubleshoot integration issues and provide 2nd/3rd line support as needed.
•Ensure adherence to integration standards, documentation, and best practices.
WHAT WE
EXPECT
•Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in IT or a related field
•3–5 years of experience in SAP Integration.
•Strong hands‑on expertise with SAP CPI (Cloud Integration).
•Practical experience with SAP PO/PI
•Exposure to S/4HANA projects, especially in integration contexts.
•Good understanding of APIs, message formats (XML/JSON), and integration protocols.
•Ability to analyze and debug integration flows across systems.
•English language proficiency at upper intermediate (B2) or advanced (C1) level
Nice-to-Have:
•Experience with API Management or API Gateway solutions.
•Knowledge of SAP BTP services.
•Experience with migration from PO/PI to CPI.
•Understanding of event-driven integration patterns.
