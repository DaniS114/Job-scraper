Responsibilities
Supporting the development of a stable and scalable Kotlin and Spring Boot back-end from the ground up
Collaborate closely with our Customer Support (L1) and Infrastructure Support (L2) teams to efficiently manage escalations- helping identify, troubleshoot, and resolve operational issues that keep our clients running smoothly
Participate in a rotating on-call schedule to ensure reliable 24/7 client support paired with appropriate compensation for your time and expertise
Crafting code that adheres to the highest industry standards while ensuring seamless integration with our Cl tools
Collaborating effectively within a dynamic team environment on exciting, cutting-edge tech initiatives while working closely with fellow engineers and supporting Tech Leads and Architects
Bringing an architectural perspective to the planning process
Guiding and mentoring junior developers to support their professional growth
Requirements
5+ years of experience in Java ecosystem, familiarity with Java 17, 21
Deep understanding of Spring Boot
Experience with an observability platform (Grafana / LGTM or ELK stack or NewRelic)
Proven ability to troubleshoot under pressure, quickly identify root causes, recommend impactful solutions, and ensure thorough follow-through
Hands-on experience with SQL or NoSQL databases (Postgres, Redis)
Experience with Kubernetes
Familiarity with at least one public cloud provider (preferably AWS)
Familiarity with IaC, such as Terraform, Helm is a plus
DevOps mindset of continuous improvement by following processes, sharing knowledge, and learning from feedback
A strong OOP mindset and a commitment to upholding SOLID principles
A genuine interest in staying up-to-date with the latest industry trends
A passion for writing clean, maintainable code, adhering to best practices
Kotlin experience is a huge plus, as this is our choice of language on the JDK
At least an intermediate level of English language skills since our team operates internationally

Company info
Our vision is simple yet powerful: we want to create a place where engineers thrive, taking on exciting projects and using the latest technologies, free from the shackles of legacy solutions.
Thanks to the diverse range of industries we work with, our days are never boring: from world-class apps to AR solutions or even programming robots, we’ve got it all covered. We don’t just write code for our screens; rather, we develop impactful products used by millions every day: let them be large-scale public transport applications, leading fintech platforms, or storage solutions contributing to a greener future.
We are a team of 100+ engineers (Java, JavaScript, Android, iOS, NodeJS and dotnet platforms) and QA engineers with our coding fingers at the ready working on the next big thing. We value openness, modesty, hard work, and simple yet great solutions.
After focusing on our International expansion for years, we felt that it was time to think locally and open a second office in Hungary, in the vibrant city of Szeged. With its bustling tech and social scene, stunning architecture, and laid-back atmosphere, the city has all that a true Supercharger needs. We are here to create a place where IT professionals can thrive, find international challenges, opportunities to grow, and amazing company culture.
