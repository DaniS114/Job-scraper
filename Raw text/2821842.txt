Responsibilities
Digital Value Enablement
: You will work closely with national and international DSOs, business stakeholders, and Digital Technology to elevate IT service excellence, unlock the full value of digital transformation, and accelerate the transformation of Energy Networks
IT Service Excellence Strategy:
You will shape and implement the strategic framework for IT service excellence, enabling both current and future business transformations and driving the evolution of our digital business platforms (Rocks)
Digital Thought Leadership:
As a key member of the Energy Networks Digital & IT Leadership Team, you will lead functional the Expert Team, structure and moderate meetings with the PMO expert, and contribute forward‑looking strategic concepts and recommendations
Cross-Functional Delivery:
You will manage cross-functional teams and ensure the successful delivery of strategic initiatives
Enterprise Standardization:
Together with the Digital & IT Leadership Team, you will define and steer standardization and optimization decisions across Energy Networks
Digital Ambassadorship:
You will act as a trusted expert and ambassador for Digital & IT in internal and external forums, presentations, and strategic dialogues
Requirements
Professional qualifications:
A university degree in IT or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
Professional experience:
At least 3 years of relevant professional experience, with expertise in IT Operations, User ExperienceDesign,IT infrastructure, Application Management, agile frameworks (e.g., SAFe), cloud technologies, and ITIL
Specific expertise:
Ideally, knowledge of the European energy industry, and a passion for leveraging digital technology to drive efficiency, innovation, and transformation
Working method:
Strong problem-solving, research, and project management skills, with the ability to align technical solutions with business strategies and processes
What sets you apart:
Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written, with the ability to convey technical information clearly and work collaboratively across teams and departments
Language skills:
Business fluency in both German and English to engage effectively within the E.ON Group and with Distribution Systems Operations

Flexible working:

Company info
E.ON is one of Europe's largest operators of energy networks and infrastructure and a provider of innovative energy solutions. Our employees are constantly working to digitise the energy world, achieve net zero and connect everyone to good energy. To achieve this, we are focusing our actions on sustainability, digitalisation and growth. We strongly believe that differences make us stronger and welcome all people into our inclusive and diverse culture.
