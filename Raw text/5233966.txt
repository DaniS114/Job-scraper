Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for an experienced Reporting & BI Specialist to take ownership of the company-wide reporting area, with a strong focus on Power BI and close cooperation with our ERP (Microsoft Dynamics NAV / Business Central) support team.
The role combines hands-on report creation with responsibility for evaluating the most appropriate way to deliver insights – whether through Power BI or directly from Dynamics NAV / Business Central.
You will play a key role in improving performance, data consistency, and long-term sustainability of our reporting landscape.
Your role will include:
Owning and overseeing the reporting landscape across the company
Reviewing existing Power BI reports and datasets:
identifying obsolete or unused reports
consolidating and optimizing data models
improving performance and maintainability
Designing shared and reusable data models instead of isolated report-level datasets
Actively deciding whether a reporting requirement should be:
implemented in Power BI, or
delivered directly via Dynamics NAV / Business Central
Close collaboration with the NAV / Business Central support team to:
understand data structures and business logic
ensure data correctness and consistency
Supporting business users in defining meaningful reporting requirements
Proposing improvements, simplifications, and best practices in reporting and BI usage
Participating in BI-related architecture decisions and future development
