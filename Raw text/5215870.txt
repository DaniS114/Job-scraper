Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are looking for a skilled Fullstack Developer at Medior or Senior level to join the development team and participate in the design, development, and maintenance of modern web applications. You will work in an agile environment, taking responsibility for both frontend and backend parts of the application.
Develop and maintain frontend applications using React, Next.js, and TypeScript
Build and manage reusable UI components with Storybook in collaboration with UX/UI designers
Design and implement backend services using Node.js (Python is a plus)
Develop and integrate RESTful APIs
Work with relational and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL...)
Deploy and operate applications in cloud environments
