Náplň práce, právomoci a zodpovednosti
Do you enjoy building reliable delivery platforms that make engineering teams faster? We are looking for a CI/CD Engineer to join Deutsche Telekom’s Technology Delivery International (TDI). You will take end-to-end ownership of the MagentaCI/CD Local Cache—a critical on-premises extension of our central platform designed to ensure resilient, low-latency software distribution across European markets, even in air-gapped scenarios.
Key Responsibilities
Build & Operate: Design and evolve the Local      Cache architecture, focusing on high availability for GitLab and JFrog      Artifactory Edge.
Data Strategy: Optimize data      synchronization and replication mechanisms (mirroring, federated      resources) between central and local nodes.
Platform Automation: Drive full-lifecycle      automation (provisioning, updates) using Kubernetes and GitOps      (ArgoCD/Flux).
Developer Enablement: Improve      the developer experience through self-service templates, documentation,      and standardized "golden paths."
Reliability: Maintain SLIs/SLOs      (hit rate, latency) and lead root-cause analysis for complex      infrastructure incidents.
What we are looking for (Must-haves)
CI/CD Expertise: Strong hands-on experience with      GitLab CI and artifact management (Artifactory/Nexus).
Cloud-Native Skills: Solid experience operating      production workloads on Kubernetes (networking, storage, and HA patterns).
Coding/Scripting: Proficiency in Python, Go, or      Bash.
Network Fundamentals: A strong      understanding of network protocols (TCP/IP, HTTPs, DNS) and secure      communication (TLS/PKI).
Mindset: A proactive      problem-solver who enjoys owning a product from the first line of code to      the on-call rotation.
Nice to have
Experience with GitOps tools (ArgoCD/Flux) and      OpenStack.
Observability experience (Prometheus/Grafana,      ELK).
Security knowledge      (Supply-chain security, SBOM, vulnerability scanning).

Stručná charakteristika spoločnosti
Deutsche Telekom Cloud Services (DTCS) is a technology hub within Deutsche Telekom Group. Our team is headquartered in Bratislava and located in Slovak Telekom premises. Established in 2015, the unit is a part of Product & Technology Board Area and part of Technology Delivery International (TDI).
TDI bundles expert teams across all our NatCos (national companies) in Europe. We build and scale modern cloud and network platforms that deliver best-in-class technology solutions for our customers and drive automation across our European footprint.
Our experts work in chapters and tribes, continuously developing their skills and contributing to the Group’s strategic priorities of digitalization, cloudification and automation.
DTCS offers a dynamic international environment, strong technical expertise and a culture of continuous learning - all with the ambition to support Deutsche Telekom’s vision of becoming the Leading European Telco.
