Database EngineerAbout Mettler ToledoMETTLER TOLEDO is a global leader in precision instruments and services. We are renowned for innovation and quality across laboratory, process analytics, industrial, product inspection, and retailing applications. Our sales and service network is one of the most extensive in the industry. Our products are sold in more than 140 countries, and we have a direct presence in approximately 40 countries. For more information, please visit .Our Opening and Your ResponsibilitiesAs a division within the METTLER TOLEDO Group, Process Analytics MTPRO is a leading provider of field instruments for analyzing liquids and gases in the process industry. At our headquarters in Urdorf, we develop and produce high-quality sensors for the control of production processes, which we sell worldwide through our own market organization. To strengthen the team, we are looking for an enthusiastic person asDatabase Engineer 100% (all genders)In the role of Application Engineer, you are part of our vision towards Automation and Digitalization in Production. You will be part of Software Engineering team within Industrial Engineering in Operations. In collaboration with members of other divisions (Automation, Production, Quality and Process Engineering), you will help shape and develop the future direction in terms of process automation & data management and drive forward the digitalization & standardization of the Urdorf site.In this role, you will be responsible for the following activities:You manage as administrator software solutions, interfaces applications for data management, databases, and MES/ERP interfaces in production with the aim of new modern systems integration and standardization.You are responsible for the creation and management of databases in test and production environment.You are responsible for the printing and labeling applications, layouts and printing server maintenance and support.You are responsible for the ERP/MES data configuration, administration modifications, and users' management system.You manage the software repositories, versioning system and interfaces with other applications.You support projects for the industrialization on new/retrofit equipment in production for software, applications, and databases.You act as 2nd level support for production issues and disturbances related to software, production application, databases, and equipment connectivity.You ensure complete documentation, management, and maintenance of software solutions in production environment.What You Need to SucceedWe have the following requirements for this position:You have a degree in computer science, preferably with experience in databases management and development and a fist relevant professional experience in an industrial environment.You have a good understanding and a high affinity for IT solutions, SQL databases, and first experience with IoT (communication protocols such as OPC-UA, Modbus) and SAP systems.You have very good knowledge of database systems (e.g., with MSSQL, ODBC, MS Access etc.)Your experience in Azure DevOps and git management is a big plus.You have very good experience in management of data and databases, interfaces and applications.You have a strong organizational personality with strong problem-solving, communication and analytical skills and enjoy interdisciplinary teamwork.You have very good written and spoken English and German skills.Our Offer to YouWe offer:Flexible working hours (depending on the role), a hybrid work model, and a wide portfolio of training opportunitiesA 40-hour work week with at least 25 vacation days per year, plus 4 to 7 additional days off between bank holidaysFree parking and direct access to public transportEmployees in Urdorf can purchase monthly lunch checks and enjoy healthy meals at a reduced price, either externally or in our cafeteriaA range of additional attractive benefits, including employee discounts at select area businessesYou can expect challenging, interesting, and varied tasks in an innovative and international environment, with the opportunity to network globally and achieve your career goals.Have we raised your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your complete application at our Job portal.Please note that we do not accept any application documents via e-mail, only applications submitted on our job platform due to our data protection guidelines. Furthermore, we do currently not accept applications from recruitment agencies for this role.If you have any question, please do not hesitate to contact our Talent Acquisition Team
