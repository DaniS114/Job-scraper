Keep contact with functional teams and designers, build professional relationships with clients
Requirements
At least 5-6 years of experience as an SAP ABAP or Integration Developer
Understanding of SAP development concepts, guidelines and SAP architecture
Good communication skills, ability to quickly adapt new technologies
Advanced English skills (German knowledge is an advantage)
Proven knowledge/experience with S/4HANA is an advantage

Constant career development with personalized internal mentorship
