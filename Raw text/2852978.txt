Company info
About Digital Factory
At Digital Factory, we pair the agility of a startup with the scale of a global company. As part of MOL Group’s 2030 Enter Tomorrow strategy, we’re transforming Consumer Services into a digital‑first, data‑driven mobility and retail business.
We’ve built a centralized Data Lake, deployed advanced analytics (across forecasting, pricing, category management, workforce optimization), and launched MOL MOVE – our digital loyalty platform used by millions across 9 countries.
Now, we’re expanding our data ecosystem even further, strengthening our engineering capabilities, and looking for a Senior Data Architect to help us build data products that scale across the region.
About the Role
We are looking for a
Senior Data Architect
who will shape the foundations of our data products and platforms. You’ll contribute across the entire data lifecycle – from conceptual architecture to hands‑on model design – ensuring our Data Squads deliver high‑quality, scalable solutions.
This role combines architecture, technical leadership, and coaching: you’ll design data structures, guide transformation logic, review code, ensure data quality, and support our transition to modern cloud‑based technologies like Synapse, Databricks, and Delta Lake.
If you’re passionate about data architecture, cloud platforms, and helping teams deliver robust analytics solutions, this role is for you.

Main Recruitment Steps
Initial call:
You will be contacted by MOL Recruitment via phone for a short chat.
Interview:
You can expect two or a maximum of three interview rounds, where you can meet your direct manager, some experts from the team, ask your questions and present your skills and career path. We always make sure you receive timely feedback after each round.
Technical Assessment:
You will either receive a take-home assignment after the first interview or be asked to solve an exercise on the spot at one of the interviews.
