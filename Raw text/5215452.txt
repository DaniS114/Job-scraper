Tosca Test Automation Lead
Place of work
Remote work
Contract type
full-time
POSITION
DESCRIPTION
We’re looking for a hands-on Test Automation Lead with deep Tricentis Tosca expertise to drive the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of our test automation strategy. You will own Tosca framework architecture, integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines, and partner closely with cross functional teams to assure quality across the SDLC.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
•	Define and own the Tosca test automation strategy, standards, and governance for products and programs.
•	Build and lead a small team of automation engineers, coach and mentor on Tosca best practices and test management discipline.
•	Establish automation KPIs (coverage, stability, execution time) and report progress to stakeholders.
•	Design, implement, and maintain Tosca-based automation frameworks (modules, business components, test data, recovery scenarios).
•	Create scalable, maintainable automated test suites for functional, regression, and smoke testing.
•	Optimize test assets for reliability and speed; perform root cause analysis of flaky tests and environment issues.
•	Integrate Tosca suites with CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Azure DevOps/GitHub/GitLab) to enable continuous testing, gated releases, and quality gates.
•	Automate test execution, reporting, and artifact publishing from pipelines.
•	Use test management tools (JIRA, HP ALM, TestRail) to plan, track, and report testing activities.
•	Manage test cases, data, and environments; ensure traceability from requirements to test results.
•	Coordinate defect triage and quality risk mitigation with product owners and engineering leads.
•	Drive “shift left” testing and in sprint automation, aligning with our internal RACI and role definitions.
•	Produce clear test plans, automation design docs, and execution reports; communicate status and risks to stakeholders.
WHAT WE
EXPECT
•	At least 5 years of experience in Test Automation engineering
•	Proficiency in Tricentis Tosca Testsuite: expert in test design, module creation, test data management, and execution.
•	Proven experience designing Tosca automation frameworks and maintaining large automation suites.
•	Hands on CI/CD integration of automated tests; experience with build pipelines and artifact/report publishing.
•	Strong command of test management tools (JIRA, HP ALM, TestRail) and test assets lifecycle management.
•	Solid understanding of SDLC and Agile/DevOps practices; ability to identify and mitigate testing risks.
•	Analytical and problem-solving skills: debugging test scripts, environment issues, and optimizing performance/stability.
•	Communication & collaboration skills: effective with developers, BAs, PMs; concise documentation and stakeholder reporting.
•	Experience training/mentoring teams on Tosca and testing standards.
•	Professional working proficiency in English (B2+), German is nice to have.
•	Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, information technology, or related field
Nice to have
•	ISTQB or Tricentis Tosca certifications.
•	Experience with API/non web automation in Tosca; familiarity with test data virtualization.
