Állás leírása
Responsibilities
Design, develop, and execute test plans, cases, and scripts for Odoo modules including Finance, Inventory, Sales, and Procurement.
Conduct functional, integration, regression, and user acceptance testing.
Validate Odoo customizations, configurations, and third-party integrations.
Ensure accuracy of data migration and verify workflows across multiple modules.
Identify, document, and track defects; collaborate with developers and consultants for resolution.
Contribute to the continuous improvement of QA processes and standards.
Requirements
3+ years of experience in software QA, ideally with ERP systems (Odoo strongly preferred).
Strong knowledge of QA methodologies, tools, and best practices.
Hands-on experience in ERP testing, API testing, and data validation.
Familiarity with the Odoo testing framework is a strong advantage.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and documentation skills.
Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams.
Nice-to-have
High attention to detail and accuracy., Strong troubleshooting and analytical mindset., Proactive, adaptable, and results-oriented., Ability to thrive in collaborative and dynamic environments.
What we offer
Opportunity to work with a leading ERP implementation in a global business environment.
Competitive compensation package.
Career growth in a supportive, innovative, and cross-functional team.
Company info
Our hiring partner, a global organization implementing Odoo ERP, is seeking a QA Specialist to ensure the quality, reliability, and performance of their ERP systems.
