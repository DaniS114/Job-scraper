Pozíció leírása / Job description
Our partner, Hungary's largest agricultural machinery manufacturing company, is looking for a new colleague for the
IT Security Expert
position.
Operate, develop and implement PKI solutions to secure services, applications, and data.
Manage and monitor certificates and keys, including creation, validation, and renewal.
Conduct security analyses and risk assessments related to PKI and cryptographic requirements.
Collaborate with various departments to identify security needs and develop appropriate solutions.
Continuously monitor security incidents and formulate preventive and remedial measures.
Train and raise awareness among employees regarding IT security topics, especially concerning PKI.
Update and maintain security policies and procedures.
Elvárások / Requirements
Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, or equivalent qualification.
Several years of experience in IT security, specifically with PKI systems and Public/Private Key cryptography.
Strong knowledge of common cryptographic algorithms, protocols, Hardware Security Modules (HSM) and different Operating Systems (Linux and Windows)
Experience with implementing and managing Certificate Authorities (CAs)
Ability to clearly communicate complex technical concepts.
Problem-solving skills and analytical thinking.
Team-oriented with a high degree of responsibility
