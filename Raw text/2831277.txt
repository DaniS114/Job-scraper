About the role
We are looking for a IoT Developer (m/f/x) to start as soon as possible.
Your responsibilities
Work within the Magna Seating IT/OT team to support Digitalization strategy in Seating Europe Group
Support development and implementation of digitalization initiatives in Seating Europe divisions
Manage code deployments, fixes, updates and related processes
Create IoT applications that run on-premises and in the cloud
Support creation of technical guidelines, standards and templates for digitalization projects to be scaled across Seating Group
Support creation of an OT architecture for Seating divisions
Interface with Group office and support divisions to implement digitalization projects
Receive and provide training to divisional teams related to digitalization
Develop KPIs, metrics, and other monitoring tools to track I4.0 production environments
Provide detailed specifications for proposed solutions including materials, external services and internal resources required
Develop new techniques to connect legacy equipment to modern infrastructure

Company info
Group Summary
Magna is an innovative leader in the development and manufacturing of high quality complete seating systems. Our solution includes seating structures, mechanisms and hardware, as well as foam and trim products for global automotive, heavy truck and bus industries. Our capabilities include market and consumer research; full concept development; design and engineering capabilities; testing, validation and world-class manufacturing. Our award-winning seating solutions are developed from consumer research, finding the ease of operation to be comfortable, convenient and connected.
