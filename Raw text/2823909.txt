Company info
McKinsey & Company is a global management consulting firm that serves leading businesses, governments, non-governmental organizations, and not-for-profits. Founded in 1926, McKinsey operates in more than 65 countries with a diverse workforce of over 30,000 professionals. The firm is committed to helping organizations across industries solve their most complex problems and achieve lasting impact through data-driven insights, strategic advice, and implementation support.
