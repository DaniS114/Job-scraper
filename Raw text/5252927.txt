Job description, responsibilities and duties
Step into a role where quality truly matters.
As a
Software Test Engineer
, you’ll play a key role in ensuring the reliability and performance of modern applications, working on complex systems and contributing directly to high-quality software delivery.
What will you do?
• Perform manual and/or automated testing of features and applications
• Design and execute test cases based on business requirements
• Work with regression, integration, performance, and UAT testing
• Identify, document, and track defects through resolution
• Collaborate closely with Product Owners, developers, and stakeholders
• Contribute to test planning, scope definition, and risk identification
• Analyze complex technical problems and support their resolution
• Track and report testing results, metrics, and overall quality status
• Ensure testing is efficient, repeatable, and aligned with quality standards
What’s important for the role:
• Ability to work independently on complex systems and tasks
• Strong analytical thinking and attention to detail
• Experience with test management and defect tracking tools
• Ownership mindset and proactive approach to problem-solving
• Good communication with both technical teams and business stakeholders
