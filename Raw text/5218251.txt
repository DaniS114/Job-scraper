Who we are
​
Joining NetCore means becoming part of a motivated team dedicated to building software that impacts the world of networking and global connectivity. We develop specialized network automation and management software, used worldwide in environments ranging from local networks to regional ISPs, data centers, and national carriers. Trusted by major industry players, our platform has over 3000 deployments globally. This year, we’re taking it even further by launching a new product to expand our platform.
At NetCore, our team is built on trust, and we strive to create a workplace where everyone feels empowered, supported, and able to do their best work. If you’re eager to grow as a designer and want to work on real software products used worldwide - come join us!
Who we are looking for
We are looking for a Junior or Medior Graphic Designer who enjoys working with visuals across digital and print channels and wants to grow in a real product-focused environment. In this role, you will support the creation and maintenance of our visual assets and gradually take more ownership over graphic and UI design tasks related to our products, marketing, and brand.
What you will be doing
Assist with creating web banners, social media visuals, and other marketing-related graphics.
Prepare print materials such as business cards, banners, flyers, and other promotional assets.
Create brand and promotional materials including presentations, merchandise visuals, and campaign graphics.
Work in Figma and collaborate with the team on maintaining visual consistency across products and  marketing outputs.
Support the execution of marketing deliverables and ad-hoc design requests.
Collaborate on developing and maintaining our visual identity and applying it consistently across all design outputs.
What we are looking for in you
A portfolio showcasing graphic or UI-related work for digital products (school, personal, or commercial projects).
Experience with both digital and print design in the field of digital products.
Basic to intermediate knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign).
Basic experience with Figma and willingness to deepen UI-related skills.
An eye for detail and a sense for visual consistency and branding.
Good communication skills and a collaborative mindset.
Motivation to learn, grow, and gradually take more responsibility.
How we work
We work in small teams - yours will consist of one senior designer, but you’ll collaborate closely with all departments.
Core hours are 10:00 - 16:00. Outside of these hours, it’s up to you when you work.
We don’t play titles. Everyone knows their role, and when something doesn’t work, we talk openly - informal but professional.
