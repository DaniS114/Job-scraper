Responsibilities
Understand and document data models, entities, and relationships across systems
Analyze data flows and integrations between applications and databases

Identify and investigate data quality issues (completeness, consistency, validity, accuracy)
Conduct root cause analysis of problematic or inconsistent data
Explain data issues and findings to technical and non-technical stakeholders
Design, test, and execute data correction scripts in a controlled and auditable way

Strong SQL skills (Oracle and PostgreSQL)
Solid understanding of relational data models

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Confident English knowledge

Experience with data governance or data quality frameworks
