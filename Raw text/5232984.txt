About the Role
We are looking for a Head of Cloud to take end-to-end ownership of Alma Media’s group-level cloud capability. In this role, you will lead the Cloud team with a strong focus on people leadership, prioritization, and delivery outcomes. You will be accountable for cloud cost control, security posture, reliability, and governance across the company, while ensuring the team operates sustainably and with clear focus.
You will have supervisory responsibility for the Cloud team and report directly to the Chief Technology Officer.
Key Responsibilities
Lead and develop the Cloud team as a high-performing engineering function with clear goals, roles, and ways of working.
Own prioritization and trade-offs across cloud cost, security, reliability, and enablement needs at group level.
Carry end-to-end responsibility for cloud cost optimization, financial transparency, and long-term cost efficiency.
Own cloud security posture, risk management, and compliance in close collaboration with security and IT operations.
Act as the primary interface between the Cloud team and business units, balancing competing demands and protecting team focus.
Ensure the reliability and operational continuity of cloud platforms, including incident readiness, escalation, and post-incident learning.
Set direction for cloud standards, guardrails, and platform evolution without acting as a primary individual contributor.
Provide clear executive-level reporting on cloud costs, risks, reliability, and team progress.
You will succeed in this role if you have:
Proven experience as an engineering manager, platform lead, or similar leadership role in a cloud-heavy environment.
Sufficient technical depth in cloud platforms (especially AWS) to guide decisions, challenge assumptions, and support the team — without needing to be hands-on day to day.
Experience owning outcomes such as cost control, security posture, platform reliability, or operational maturity at scale.
Strong ability to prioritize under pressure and manage constant trade-offs across multiple stakeholders.
Demonstrated people leadership skills, including coaching, feedback, role clarity, and building sustainable team practices.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with confidence operating at technology director level in an international organization.
Experience working closely with security, operations, and development teams in shared ownership models.
Relevant higher education degree or equivalent practical experience.
