Job description, responsibilities and duties
Position Overview
We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Developer with Team Lead responsibilities to join our engineering team focused on developing and enhancing our ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) solutions. This role combines hands on Java development with leadership responsibilities, guiding a small team of developers and contributing to architectural decisions that shape our core business systems.
Key Responsibilities
Technical Development
Design, develop, and maintain ERP modules using Java as the primary programming language.
Implement high quality, scalable, and maintainable code following best practices and coding standards.
Integrate ERP systems with internal and external applications (APIs, databases, industrial systems).
Participate in system architecture design, code reviews, and technical decision making.
Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues across the full software stack.
Team Leadership
Lead and mentor a team of software developers (2–6 people depending on project).
Coordinate development tasks, manage workload distribution, and ensure timely delivery.
Support team members through technical guidance, coaching, and performance feedback.
Collaborate closely with Product Owners, Project Managers, QA, and stakeholders.
Promote a culture of continuous improvement, knowledge sharing, and engineering excellence.
Project & Process Responsibilities
Participate in requirement analysis and translate business needs into technical solutions.
Contribute to sprint planning, estimation, and agile ceremonies.
Ensure documentation of system architecture, modules, and development processes.
Work with QA to ensure high-quality releases and stable production environments.
Support deployment, monitoring, and maintenance of ERP applications.
