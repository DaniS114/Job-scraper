Job description, responsibilities and duties
We are seeking a
responsible and reliable Trading Desk Specialist – Monitoring & Incident Response
to support the development and operation of virtual power plant solutions within our growing organization. This role offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the technical, operational, and data-driven aspects of distributed energy resource aggregation, energy trading, and market operations.
Key Responsibilities
Daily monitoring and       verification of market orders on OKTE and OTE
Ad-hoc nomination corrections:       Swiftly identify and correct any discrepancies in nominations to maintain       operational integrity
Supporting incident response       related to trading and operational deviations
Monitoring virtual power plant       operations and market activities
Acting as first-line support for       operational and technical issues
Identifying incidents, analyzing       root causes, and escalating when necessary
Comprehensive reporting: Prepare       and deliver accurate reports on trading activities, positions, and market       data.
