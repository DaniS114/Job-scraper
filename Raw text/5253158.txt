HOW do we achieve our goals:
Our Design Engineers for I&C (Instrumentation & Control) systems deal with specification of safety systems used in nuclear power plants worldwide.
The goal of Safety I&C (Instrumentation & Control) systems is to execute the plant shut down or countermeasures to prevent and minimize consequences of accidents which may occur during plants operation.
You'll be part of the Safety I&C Design section within the worldwide Framatome I&C Department. You'll be in collaboration with national and international teams in:
Elaboration of basic design documentation related to planning and specification starting from collection of functional and non-functional needs
Contributing to specification of design solutions covering various aspects of systems design as system architecture, sizing, implementation of safety functions, handling of interfaces, monitoring, periodic testing
Detailed specification of Safety I&C functions in a graphical form complemented with design data
