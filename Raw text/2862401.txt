Responsibilities
Your Mission
Are you passionate about technological innovation and ready to make a real impact? We are looking for an experienced
IT Project Manager
to join our Technology Organization. In this role, you will lead the deployment of cutting-edge solutions across key functional teams at BMG, driving efficiency and tangible business results. You will bridge the gap between technical teams and business stakeholders, overseeing seamless integrations and ensuring the smooth execution of strategic initiatives. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and are excited about using technology to transform workflows, we want to hear from you. Please note:
This is a temporary position as a replacement during parental leave, initially until 03.04.2027.
What You'll Be Doing
Roadmap Management:
Lead the definition and implementation of the technology roadmap, ensuring all projects are aligned with broader business priorities.
Stakeholder Liaison:
Serve as the central point of contact between engineering, product teams, and business units to facilitate collaboration.
Framework Development:
Design and implement frameworks for the seamless adoption of new technologies; track and report on KPIs to validate success.
Continuous Improvement:
Foster a data-driven culture of experimentation, utilizing feedback loops to optimize workflows and tools.
Strategic Insight:
Monitor emerging technology trends to enhance our IT strategy and manage effective communication regarding innovation updates.
Requirements
What We Are Looking For
Background:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Engineering, or a related field.
Experience:
A minimum of 5 years of professional experience in IT project or product management.
Execution:
Demonstrated ability to manage technology rollouts and integrations effectively across various teams.
Soft Skills:
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to manage expectations and align diverse stakeholder groups.
Bonus Qualifications:
Prior experience in the music, media, or publishing sectors, as well as initial leadership experience, is desirable but not mandatory.
Work Style:
A proactive problem-solver who is adaptable and performs well in a dynamic, high-speed environment.

Company info
At BMG, we recognize the power of music and are dedicated to ensuring it thrives. We are a community of artists, songwriters, and partners, working together to create music and build lasting careers. Here, music creators find not just a business partner, but a trusted home where their work shapes culture and leaves a legacy. Together, we evolve and grow.
We believe that creativity flourishes when people feel they belong. That means building a culture where everyone can show up as themselves, contribute freely, and be treated with fairness and respect - whatever their background, identity, or life story. We're committed to creating a workplace where individuality is valued, inclusion is intentional, and everyone has the chance to grow. All applicants to BMG will receive equal treatment regardless of age, disability, gender identity or expression, marital or civil partner status, pregnancy or maternity, race, colour, nationality, ethnic or national origin, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation.
