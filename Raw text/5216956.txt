Business Analyst - Strategy & Consulting
Place of work
Bratislava, Slovakia (Job with occasional home office)
Contract type
full-time
POSITION
DESCRIPTION
Are you a forward-thinking, analytical, and tech-savvy professional eager to build a career in consulting, strategy, and tech-driven business? Join our Strategy & Consulting team as a Business Analyst and contribute to multinational, large-scale transformation programs for clients across industries such as Telecommunications, Financial Services, Utilities, and EU Institutions.
We work very closely with our clients to support them in making data-driven decisions that move their business forward. We focus on developing and delivering leading solutions while leveraging our strong connections across Accenture’s European and global practices. This role is designed for professionals who are eager to gain hands-on experience in business consulting and accelerate their career growth through exposure to a wide range of projects across Europe. You will work alongside our professionals, helping clients address complex business challenges, identify growth opportunities, and optimize their technology utilization – including emerging AI solutions.
WHAT WILL
YOU DO
Support strategic advisory to large and renowned companies, identifying and driving initiatives that help improve performance and achieve strategic objectives.
Participate in transformative projects across diverse industries and support clients with strategic decisions, organizational redesigns, and change management efforts.
Analyze business processes and data to support the design of solutions in areas such as customer relationship management, supply chain management, and finance.
Focus on Systems Integration Consulting, which connects and aligns the client company’s processes with information technologies and software applications.
Contribute to the development of client-ready analyses, recommendations, and presentations within project teams.
Work with consultants and client teams to gather inputs, clarify requirements, and support the delivery of project outcomes.
WHAT WE
EXPECT
University degree (Bc., Mgr., MBA, Ing. or equivalent).
1-2 years of experience in consulting or a relevant industry role; the position is also suitable for fresh graduates.
Advanced English (B2+) and fluent Slovak (C1+).
Advanced proficiency in MS Office (PowerPoint and Excel).
Ability to think out of the box and offer unique perspectives to the client.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills & the ability to structure business problems and derive insights from data.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills & the ability to convey complex and technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders in a clear and concise manner.
Desire to work on a variety of strategic and operational projects focused on addressing the business requirements of the client.
High degree of ownership, reliability, and independence.
Willingness to meet client travel requirements when applicable.
Bonus points if you have:
Previous experience with SAP (procurement, financial controlling, sales and distribution, or other relevant areas, with a focus on practical usage rather than technical expertise).
Prior experience in consulting or relevant industries such as Communications, Media, Technology, Utilities, or Resources is highly valued.
Advanced German (B2+).
