Responsibilities
Key Responsibilities
1. Onboarding & Project Management
Guide the Transition : Lead the implementation of EPOS digital solutions for our existing and new B2B clients.
Process Design: Beyond managing clients, you will design the workflows, onboarding journeys, and internal procedures that will become the standard for our digital department
Training Excellence : Conduct personalized training sessions to ensure funeral directors and their staff are confident using our software.
Goal Alignment : Ensure the software setup reflects the specific operational needs of a German/Austrian funeral home.
2. Ongoing Support & Satisfaction
Technical Support : Serve as the day-to-day functional expert, responding to client requests via phone, email, and chat.
Issue Management : Identify and resolve level-1 technical incidents, acting as the bridge to the specialized product teams.
Proactive Success : Monitor "Account Health" to identify clients who aren't fully utilizing the tools and step in to help before they become dissatisfied.
3. Strategic Account Management
Relationship Building : Maintain the high level of trust Elektronik Printing is known for through regular follow-ups and business reviews.
Growth Identification : Detect opportunities for clients to benefit from additional Simplifia modules (upsells) and share these insights with the Sales team.
Operational Excellence: You will have a seat at the table to decide how we scale our support and success functions, turning your daily field experience into company-wide strategy.
Requirements
Your Profile
Industry Mindset : You understand that our clients operate in a sensitive industry; you possess high empathy, patience, and professional communication skills.
SaaS Experience: You have a 2 to 5 years background in Customer Success, Project Management, or Technical Support, ideally within a software environment.
Organizational Talent: You are autonomous and capable of managing multiple client rollouts simultaneously.
Tech-Savvy: Comfortable navigating CRM systems (like HubSpot) and modern workspace tools.
Language: Native German is essential. Fluency in English is required for collaboration with the Simplifia team in France.
What we offer
Stability meets Innovation: Work for a well-established company with a solid reputation while enjoying the energy of a tech-focused market launch.
Impact: You will be a founding me mber of our digital success team, directly shaping how the DACH funeral industry modernizes.
Direct Growth: A cross-functional role with significant visibility and the opportunity to travel across Germany and Austria.
Company info
The Mission: Pioneering the Digital Shift at Elektronik Printing
Elektronik Printing has long been the gold standard for professional printing services in the German and Austrian funeral industry.
Today, we are entering a high-impact new chapter. Following our acquisition by Simplifia, we are spearheading a bold strategy to digitalize the DACH funeral market.
By integrating our game-changing EPOS platform, we aren't just evolving, we are revolutionizing the DACH funeral market.
We are merging 30 years of trust with disruptive technology to transition from a traditional partner into the undisputed digital leader, setting a new gold standard for the entire industry.
EPOS, what is that?
It's a comprehensive SaaS ecosystem designed specifically for the funeral industry. It consists of three major pillars:
The Management Software:
A professional platform that helps funeral directors manage their activity. It's designed to make their work easier, more efficient, and more modern.
The Digital Showcase (Growth Tools):
Beyond management, we provide tools to help funeral homes increase their visibility and reputation online.
Family Services:
A digital and human support ecosystem to simplify post-death administration and provide meaningful tools for remembrance.

You are not just joining a team; you are the key architect shaping our future operations and the driving force behind the success of our product launches in the DACH market
