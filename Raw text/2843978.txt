Responsibilities
ARE YOU PREPARED FOR THE FUTURE? AI, AI SIR!
Artificial Intelligence and machine learning are at the cutting edge when it comes to shaping next-gen mobility in areas like automated driving. But it takes real intellectual leadership and expertise to identify and then harness the AI trends and technologies that will transform the way people travel for good.
Requirements
What should you bring along?
University degree (Master's or PhD) in computer science, robotics, electrical engineering, or related field.
5+ years in robotics or applied ML with production deployments in manipulation or mobile robotics.
Deep expertise in foundation models, specifically, multimodal, reasoning and vision-language-action models, diffusion models, and modern finetuning methods (PEFT, DPO, RLHF).
Strong programming skills in Python and C++ and experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch or JAX, CUDA).
Hands-on experience with industrial or research robots and simulators (e.g., ROS 2, Isaac Sim, MuJoCo); knowledge of kinematics, motion planning, and control.
Publications, patents, or open-source contributions are a plus.
Find out more about Artificial Intelligence at the BMW Group here.
